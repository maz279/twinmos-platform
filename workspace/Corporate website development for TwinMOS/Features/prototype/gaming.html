    Tuning

    Flip the switch: XMP 3.0 & EXPO

    What is XMP?

    Enable your rated speed

    1. Restart into BIOS/UEFIPress Del or F2 during boot.
    2. Open the memory tuner“AI Tweaker / XMP” on Intel boards, “EXPO” or “A-XMP” on AMD.
    3. Enable Profile 1The module’s factory-validated settings load automatically.
    4. Save & verifyBoot, then confirm the speed in Task Manager → Performance → Memory.
    XMP 3.0 · P1 — 6000 MT/s · CL36-46-46-86 · 1.35VEXPO — validated for AM5

    Why 6000 is the sweet spot

    On Ryzen 7000/9000, DDR5-6000 keeps the memory controller 1:1 with Infinity Fabric (FCLK 2000MHz) — the lowest-latency ratio for AM5. On Intel, 6000MT/s XMP profiles are a one-toggle upgrade on 12th Gen and newer.

    Fallback timings (JEDEC) run CL36-36-36-68 — stability first, speed on demand.

    ⚠ Enabling XMP/EXPO does not void your lifetime DRAM warranty. Confirm stability with a memory stress test after tuning.

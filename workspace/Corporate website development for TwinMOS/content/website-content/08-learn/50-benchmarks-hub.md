# Performance Benchmarks: TwinMOS in the Real World

Specifications tell part of the story, but real-world performance is what matters. The TwinMOS benchmark library provides transparent, reproducible testing that shows how our products perform in the scenarios you actually care about — gaming, content creation, and everyday productivity.

## Our Benchmarking Philosophy

### Transparent Methodology
We publish complete test configurations, software versions, and environmental conditions. Our results can be reproduced by anyone with similar hardware.

### Real-World Focus
Synthetic benchmarks have their place, but we prioritize tests that reflect actual user experiences. Game load times matter more than sequential read scores.

### Controlled Conditions
All benchmarks run in climate-controlled environments with standardized system configurations to eliminate variables.

- **GPU**: NVIDIA RTX 4090
- **OS**: Windows 11 Pro (latest updates)
- **Cooling**: 360mm AIO liquid cooler

## Understanding Benchmarks

Benchmarks provide useful comparisons but have limitations:
- **System dependency**: Your results may vary based on CPU, motherboard, and configuration
- **Software updates**: New drivers and patches can change performance
- **Sample variation**: Individual units may vary slightly within specifications
- **Thermal conditions**: Ambient temperature and airflow affect sustained performance

Use benchmarks as directional guidance, not absolute guarantees.

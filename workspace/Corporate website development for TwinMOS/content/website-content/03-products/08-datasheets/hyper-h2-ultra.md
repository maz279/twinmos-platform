### Product Overview

The TwinMOS Hyper H2 Ultra is a premium 2.5-inch SATA III solid-state drive that maximises the throughput ceiling of the SATA 6 Gb/s interface. Combining a Silicon Motion (SMI) SATA controller with Micron or SK Hynix 3D TLC NAND flash, the Hyper H2 Ultra achieves sequential read speeds of up to 580 MB/s and sequential write speeds of up to 550 MB/s — saturating the SATA interface and delivering a transformative upgrade over mechanical hard drives in both laptops and desktops.

Available from 128 GB to 2 TB, the Hyper H2 Ultra suits operating-system drives, secondary storage, and NAS/DVR applications where SATA connectivity is standard. The slim 7 mm z-height (with an optional 9.5 mm spacer) ensures compatibility across thin-and-light laptops and standard 2.5" drive bays. An industry-leading MTBF of 1,500,000 hours and a generous TBW endurance rating make the Hyper H2 Ultra a reliable long-term storage solution for professional and consumer environments.

### Key Features

1. **SATA III Interface Saturation** — Sequential performance at up to 580 / 550 MB/s R/W delivers the maximum practical throughput available over a SATA 6 Gb/s link.
2. **Silicon Motion (SMI) Controller** — Robust enterprise-derived SATA controller incorporating global wear levelling, bad-block management, and thermal protection.
3. **3D TLC NAND from Tier-1 Suppliers** — Micron and SK Hynix NAND provides consistent endurance across the product lifecycle, backed by TwinMOS's supply chain quality program.
4. **7 mm Slim Profile with 9.5 mm Spacer** — Ultra-slim z-height fits thin-and-light laptops; included spacer adapts the drive to 9.5 mm bays for universal compatibility.
5. **Multiple Finish Options** — Available in Dark Grey, Rose Gold, and custom Laser Engraved variants to meet OEM and retail aesthetic requirements.
6. **1,500,000-Hour MTBF** — Best-in-class MTBF rating for a consumer SATA SSD demonstrates robust component selection and firmware-driven reliability management.
7. **LDPC ECC with End-to-End Data Protection** — Comprehensive data path integrity from the host interface to the NAND array guards against silent data corruption.

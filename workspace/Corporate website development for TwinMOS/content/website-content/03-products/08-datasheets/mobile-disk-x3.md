### Product Overview

The TwinMOS Mobile Disk X3 is a USB 3.2 Gen 1 flash drive designed to balance compact portability with fast data transfer performance. Engineered for everyday file transfer, system backup, and media playback, the Mobile Disk X3 achieves read speeds of up to 130 MB/s — more than 3× the throughput of typical USB 2.0 flash drives — enabling rapid transfer of large documents, video files, and software packages.

Available in 64 GB and 128 GB capacities, the Mobile Disk X3 features a compact pendrive body in a retractable or cap-protected design that safeguards the USB Type-A connector when not in use. Backward compatible with USB 3.0 and USB 2.0 hosts, the drive requires no driver installation and is recognised immediately as a USB Mass Storage Class device on Windows, macOS, and Linux. The exFAT (>32 GB) and FAT32 (≤32 GB) pre-formats ensure out-of-the-box compatibility with computers, smart TVs, car infotainment systems, and set-top boxes.

### Key Features

1. **USB 3.2 Gen 1 SuperSpeed** — 5 Gbps interface bandwidth delivers read speeds up to 130 MB/s, enabling rapid transfer of HD and 4K video files, software installers, and compressed archives in a fraction of the time of USB 2.0 drives.
2. **Compact and Portable Design** — Retractable or cap-protected connector keeps the USB plug safe during transit; the slim profile fits any keychain, bag, or shirt pocket without adding noticeable bulk.
3. **Plug-and-Play — No Driver Required** — Recognised as a USB Mass Storage Class device on all major operating systems including Windows XP and later, macOS 10.6+, and Linux 2.6+; no software installation required.
4. **Broad Backward Compatibility** — Fully operational on USB 3.0, USB 2.0, and USB 1.1 hosts; performance adapts automatically to the host interface speed.
5. **exFAT / FAT32 Pre-Formatted** — Ships pre-formatted for immediate cross-platform compatibility; exFAT (128 GB) supports files larger than 4 GB for video and disk image transfers.
6. **Compact Weight (~8–12 g)** — Ultra-lightweight form factor suitable for long-term attachment to laptop bags, key rings, or lanyards without inconvenience.
7. **Wide Device Compatibility** — Supported by smart TVs, car audio systems, set-top boxes, gaming consoles (media mode), and embedded systems with standard USB Type-A ports.

### Product Overview

The TwinMOS M.2 2280 SATA III SSD delivers high-performance SATA storage in the compact M.2 2280 form factor, making it the ideal upgrade for ultrabooks, small-form-factor (SFF) PCs, and embedded systems that provide a B+M key M.2 slot but do not support PCIe NVMe. Leveraging Micron 3D TLC NAND flash with an SMI SATA controller, the drive achieves sequential read speeds of up to 580 MB/s and sequential write speeds of up to 550 MB/s — the practical maximum of the SATA 6 Gb/s interface.

Unlike PCIe NVMe M.2 drives, the M.2 2280 SATA III SSD communicates via the SATA protocol over the M.2 connector's SATA signal pins. This means it is interchangeable with 2.5-inch SATA SSDs in terms of protocol, enabling straightforward OS migrations using standard cloning tools. The drive's slim 3.5 mm profile and 10 g weight make it an unobtrusive, power-efficient storage solution consuming only ~1.8 W under active load — a clear advantage in thermally constrained platforms.

> **Important Compatibility Notice:** This drive uses SATA protocol via M.2 B+M key interface. It is NOT compatible with M-key-only (NVMe-only) M.2 slots. Always verify that the host M.2 slot supports SATA protocol before purchase.

### Key Features

1. **SATA Protocol over M.2 Connector** — Enables SATA-class performance in ultra-compact M.2 form factor without requiring a PCIe NVMe slot, ideal for legacy-SATA-capable ultrabooks.
2. **Micron 3D TLC NAND** — Tier-1 NAND from Micron ensures consistent endurance ratings, tight cell binning, and supply chain traceability.
3. **Ultra-Low Active Power (~1.8 W)** — Significantly lower power draw than PCIe NVMe alternatives; extends battery life and reduces thermal load in passively cooled systems.
4. **B+M Key Connector** — The dual-notch B+M key connector fits both B-key and M-key M.2 sockets that support SATA signalling, maximising platform compatibility.
5. **DevSleep at ~2 mW** — Ultra-low DevSleep mode supports modern platform power policies (e.g., Intel Modern Standby) for near-zero idle drain.
6. **Standard SATA AHCI Protocol** — Full compatibility with OS-native AHCI drivers; no additional driver installation required under Windows, macOS, or Linux.
7. **Single-Sided, Slim PCB** — At 3.5 mm height and 10 g, the drive fits restricted-clearance M.2 slots where double-sided designs may not seat correctly.

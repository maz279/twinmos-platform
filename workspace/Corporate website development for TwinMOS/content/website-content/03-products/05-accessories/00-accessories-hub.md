# TwinMOS Accessories

TwinMOS accessories are engineered to complement our memory and storage ecosystem while solving the everyday connectivity and workflow challenges that modern computing demands. Every accessory in our lineup is built to the same quality and certification standards as our DRAM and SSD products—certified to CE, FCC, and RoHS requirements and backed by our global support network.

Our accessories portfolio currently covers two categories: USB hubs for expanding port connectivity, and memory card readers for fast media ingestion from cameras, drones, and mobile devices.

---

## EzeeHUB USB 3.0 Hub Series

The TwinMOS **EzeeHUB** is a family of USB hubs designed to eliminate port shortages on modern laptops and desktops. Whether you need a premium aluminum model for a professional workstation or a compact, ultra-light hub to throw in a travel bag, the EzeeHUB series has a configuration for your needs.

All EzeeHUB models are bus-powered, plug-and-play, and compatible with Windows, macOS, and Linux out of the box. No drivers, no power bricks required.

### EzeeHUB 34L-M — 4-Port USB 3.0 Hub, Aluminum (Flagship)

The flagship EzeeHUB model combines full-speed USB 3.0 performance across all four ports with a premium **aluminum alloy chassis** that dissipates heat, resists flex, and looks at home on any professional desk. Individual port overcurrent protection and per-port status LEDs make it the most capable hub in the EzeeHUB lineup.

## Why TwinMOS Accessories?

**Certified Quality**
Every EzeeHUB and card reader is certified to CE, FCC, and RoHS requirements. Individual port overcurrent protection on the EzeeHUB guards your devices against electrical faults.

**Broad OS Compatibility**
TwinMOS accessories work across Windows (from Windows 2000 to Windows 11), macOS, Linux, and Chrome OS. No third-party drivers are needed—connect and go.

**Plug-and-Play Simplicity**
All TwinMOS hubs and card readers are bus-powered and driver-free. Plug them into any available USB port and they are immediately recognized by the host system.

**Built to Last**
Aluminum alloy housing on the EzeeHUB 34L-M and 23L dissipates heat during sustained use and resists the dents and cracks that plastic shells develop over time. The compact EzeeHUB 34S and card readers are built tough for travel.

**Affordable, Practical Upgrades**
TwinMOS accessories are priced to be practical additions to any workstation, not premium-tier luxuries. You get the connectivity you need without overspending on features you will not use.

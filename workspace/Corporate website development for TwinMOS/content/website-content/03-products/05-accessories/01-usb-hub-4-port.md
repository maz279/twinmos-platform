# EzeeHUB USB 3.0 Hub Series

Modern laptops are thinner and more powerful than ever, but the trend toward slim profiles has come at a cost: fewer USB ports. Many ultrabooks and business notebooks ship with only two USB ports total—one occupied by a mouse, the other by a charger. The TwinMOS **EzeeHUB** series solves this in seconds. The EzeeHUB family spans four models to match different priorities: a premium aluminum flagship, a compact travel hub, and two mixed USB 2.0/3.0 models for users who need broad device compatibility. All are bus-powered, certified to CE, FCC, and RoHS standards, and compatible with every major operating system from Windows to macOS to Linux.

### Key Features

**Aluminum Alloy Chassis**
The metal housing is more than an aesthetic choice. Aluminum conducts heat away from the internal hub controller during sustained use, keeping operating temperatures within range. It also resists the cracks, warps, and connector loosening that plastic housings develop over months of repeated insertion and removal.

**USB 3.0 on All Four Ports**
Every port on the EzeeHUB 34L-M runs at USB 3.0 speeds—there are no USB 2.0 fallback ports. Connect a fast external SSD on one port, a USB flash drive on another, and still have two ports free for a wireless dongle and a phone charging cable.

**Individual Port Overcurrent Protection**
Each of the four ports has its own overcurrent protection circuit. If a connected device draws excessive current—a faulty cable, a failing peripheral, an unexpected power surge—the protection circuit trips that single port without affecting the others or the host system.

**Per-Port Status LEDs**
Four individual LEDs confirm that each port is active, connected, and transferring data. A single power indicator confirms the hub itself is receiving bus power from the host. This makes it easy to diagnose a connection issue without guessing.

**Optional External Power Support**
The 34L-M runs bus-powered in most configurations, drawing power from the host USB port. For workstations with power-hungry USB devices—external hard drives, charging multiple phones simultaneously—an optional 100–240 VAC DC adapter eliminates any risk of the hub under-powering attached devices.

**Plug-and-Play, Zero Configuration**
The EzeeHUB 34L-M requires no driver installation on any supported operating system. Plug it in and your OS recognizes four new USB devices immediately. It works on Windows (every version from 2000 to 11), macOS, and Linux without any additional software.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do all four ports share the 5 Gbps bandwidth, or does each port get its own 5 Gbps?**
The 5 Gbps is the total upstream bandwidth shared across all four ports. In practice, this is only a limiting factor when multiple ports are simultaneously transferring large files to fast storage devices. For typical mixed workloads—one drive plus a mouse, keyboard, and phone charger—you will not notice any bandwidth contention.

**Do I need to plug in an external power adapter?**
No. The EzeeHUB draws all operating power from the host USB port. The optional external power adapter is only needed when connecting multiple high-current devices simultaneously, such as charging multiple phones while also running an external hard drive.

**Is the hub compatible with USB 2.0 devices?**
Yes. All EzeeHUB models are backward compatible with USB 2.0 and USB 1.1 devices. Older devices will simply operate at their native speeds rather than USB 3.0 speeds.

**Can I use the hub to charge my phone?**
Yes. The USB 3.0 ports provide bus power for charging. Charging speed depends on the phone's supported charging standard and the power draw of other connected devices sharing the bus.

**Does the hub work on macOS without drivers?**
Yes. macOS supports USB hubs natively with no third-party software required.

**Will the hub work on a PlayStation 4 or PlayStation 5?**
Yes. The EzeeHUB is recognized by PlayStation consoles for connecting USB peripherals such as keyboard, mouse, and USB audio adapters.

**What is the difference between the 34L-M and the 34S?**
Both have four USB 3.0 ports and the same per-port overcurrent protection. The 34L-M has an aluminum alloy housing, per-port status LEDs, and supports newer Windows versions (through Windows 11). The 34S uses a lighter plastic housing and is optimized for travel and minimal desk footprint.

# CoreX M.2 PCIe Gen 4.0

The TwinMOS CoreX M.2 is a performance-oriented PCIe Gen 4.0 NVMe SSD designed for users who want a significant speed upgrade over SATA and Gen 3.0 drives without the premium cost of flagship Gen 4.0 models. With sequential reads up to 5,000 MB/s and writes up to 4,800 MB/s, the CoreX M.2 delivers responsive, reliable storage for laptops, small-form-factor PCs, and mainstream desktops.

## Product Overview

What sets the CoreX M.2 apart is its single-sided design. By placing all NAND flash and controller components on one side of the PCB, TwinMOS ensures maximum compatibility with thin laptops, ultrabooks, and compact systems where dual-sided drives may not fit. This design also simplifies thermal management by concentrating heat on a single surface that can be cooled by the system's M.2 heatsink or chassis airflow.

Available in 1TB capacity, the CoreX M.2 provides ample space for operating systems, applications, games, and media libraries.

## Key Features

**Single-Sided Design**
The single-sided PCB is a critical advantage for laptop and SFF compatibility. Many ultrabooks and compact mini-ITX cases only have clearance for single-sided M.2 drives. The CoreX M.2 fits where bulkier dual-sided drives cannot.

**Balanced Performance**
While not the fastest Gen 4.0 drive on paper, the CoreX M.2's 5,000 MB/s read speed is still approximately 8–9× faster than SATA III SSDs and 40–50× faster than mechanical hard drives. For everyday computing, gaming, and professional tasks, this difference is transformative.

**Power Efficiency**
The CoreX M.2 controller is optimized for low power consumption, making it ideal for laptops where battery life matters. Reduced power draw also translates to lower heat generation, improving thermal stability in thin chassis.

**Broad Compatibility**
The CoreX M.2 works with any M.2 slot supporting PCIe Gen 3.0 or Gen 4.0. When installed in a Gen 3.0 slot, it operates at Gen 3.0 speeds, making it a future-proof upgrade for older systems.

## Use Cases

**Laptop Upgrades**
Replacing a laptop's stock SATA SSD or HDD with a CoreX M.2 NVMe drive dramatically improves boot times, application responsiveness, and file transfer speeds. The single-sided design ensures fitment in slim ultrabooks.

**Small-Form-Factor PCs**
Mini-ITX and compact HTPC builds often have limited M.2 clearance. The CoreX M.2's thin profile makes it an ideal choice for these space-constrained systems.

**Mainstream Gaming**
Gamers on a budget who want faster load times and smoother open-world streaming will find the CoreX M.2 an excellent upgrade path. It provides most of the real-world benefits of faster Gen 4.0 drives at a more accessible price.

**Professional Productivity**
Office workstations, developer machines, and creative systems all benefit from the CoreX M.2's low latency and consistent throughput.

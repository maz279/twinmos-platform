# TwinMOS SSDs

TwinMOS solid-state drives deliver the speed, reliability, and capacity modern users demand. From the blistering performance of PCIe Gen 5.0 NVMe to the universal compatibility of SATA III, our SSD portfolio covers every use case—from professional content creation to budget-friendly upgrades.

## NVMe SSDs

NVMe (Non-Volatile Memory Express) SSDs connect directly to the PCIe bus, bypassing the limitations of older SATA interfaces. TwinMOS NVMe SSDs are available across three PCIe generations to match your platform and performance needs.

### PCIe Gen 5.0

The CoreX Pro M.2 represents the cutting edge of consumer storage, with sequential read speeds up to 14,000 MB/s. Designed for professionals and enthusiasts with Gen 5.0 platforms, these drives dramatically reduce load times in large projects and accelerate data-intensive workflows.

### PCIe Gen 4.0

Our Gen 4.0 lineup includes the Xtreme M.2, CoreX M.2, and Xtreme Pro M.2 families. With read speeds ranging from 5,000 MB/s to 7,500 MB/s, these drives offer an excellent balance of performance and price for gamers, creators, and power users on modern platforms.

### PCIe Gen 3.0

The Alpha Pro M.2 and TW300 M.2 bring NVMe performance to older platforms and budget builds. With speeds up to 3,600 MB/s, these drives are still many times faster than SATA SSDs and represent a massive upgrade from mechanical hard drives.

## SATA III SSDs

For legacy systems, secondary storage, and cost-sensitive deployments, TwinMOS SATA III SSDs provide reliable flash storage with broad compatibility.

### Hyper H2 Ultra

The Hyper H2 Ultra is a 2.5" SATA III drive available in dark grey and rose gold finishes. With read speeds up to 580 MB/s, it is an ideal upgrade for laptops and desktops with only SATA connectivity.

### M.2 2280 SATA

The M.2 2280 SATA III SSD offers the compact M.2 form factor with SATA interface compatibility, perfect for slim laptops and small-form-factor PCs.

## Warranty Coverage

# Xtreme M.2 PCIe Gen 4.0

The TwinMOS Xtreme M.2 is a high-performance PCIe Gen 4.0 NVMe SSD designed for users who demand top-tier storage speed without the premium price of Gen 5.0. With sequential read speeds up to 7,500 MB/s and write speeds up to 6,800 MB/s, the Xtreme M.2 accelerates gaming, content creation, and professional workflows on modern Gen 4.0 platforms.

## Product Overview

The Xtreme M.2 combines a proven SMI controller with Micron/Hynix 3D TLC NAND to deliver sustained performance and reliability. A graphene heatsink helps manage thermal output, while the 2TB model includes 2GB of DRAM cache for consistent random I/O under heavy workloads.

Available in 512GB, 1TB and 2TB capacities, the Xtreme M.2 strikes an optimal balance between speed, capacity, and cost for performance-minded users.

## Key Features

**Gen 4.0 Speed Leadership**
At 7,500 MB/s sequential read, the Xtreme M.2 approaches the theoretical limits of PCIe Gen 4.0 x4. This enables lightning-fast boot times, instant application launches, and rapid file transfers that leave SATA SSDs far behind.

**SMI Controller**
The Silicon Motion (SMI) controller is renowned for its power efficiency, thermal management, and broad compatibility. It ensures stable performance across a wide range of workloads and platforms.

**Premium TLC NAND**
Micron and Hynix TLC NAND provides high endurance and consistent performance. Advanced error correction and wear-leveling algorithms protect data integrity over the drive's lifespan.

**Graphene Heatsink**
The integrated graphene heatsink dissipates heat efficiently while maintaining an ultra-thin profile. This allows the drive to fit in laptops and under motherboard heatsinks without clearance issues.

**DRAM Cache (2TB)**
The 2TB model's dedicated DRAM buffer ensures that random read and write performance remains strong even under sustained loads, such as database operations, video editing, and large file compilation.

## Use Cases

**Gaming**
Modern games with large asset libraries load significantly faster on the Xtreme M.2. Open-world titles benefit from reduced texture pop-in, while competitive gamers enjoy shorter matchmaking and map load times.

**Content Creation**
Video editors, photographers, and musicians working with large media files experience smoother timeline scrubbing, faster import/export, and quicker project backups.

**Productivity**
Power users running multiple virtual machines, large datasets, or development environments appreciate the Xtreme M.2's responsiveness and throughput.

**PlayStation 5**
The Xtreme M.2 meets Sony's PS5 speed requirements and can be installed as expanded internal storage with the included graphene heatsink or an aftermarket heatsink.

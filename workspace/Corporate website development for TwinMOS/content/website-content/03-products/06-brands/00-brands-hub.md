# TwinMOS Brand Directory

TwinMOS has been building memory and storage solutions since 1998. Today, the portfolio spans eleven distinct brands — each engineered for a specific use case, performance tier, and audience. From flagship DDR5 gaming memory to everyday USB flash drives, every TwinMOS brand carries the same commitment to quality, reliability, and value that has made TwinMOS a trusted name in over 93 countries.

Use this directory to find the right brand for your build, upgrade, or project.

---

## Memory Brands

### VOLTX — Flagship DDR5 Performance
**For:** Enthusiasts, Gamers, Content Creators, Professionals  
**Platform:** Intel LGA1700 (12th–14th Gen), AMD AM5 (Ryzen 7000/9000)

VOLTX is TwinMOS's flagship DDR5 memory brand. Operating at speeds from 4800MHz up to 6000MHz with Intel XMP 3.0 and AMD EXPO dual certification, VOLTX modules are purpose-built for next-generation platforms. Every kit ships with on-die ECC — a DDR5 architectural advancement that provides built-in single-bit error correction at the chip level. Available as standard U-DIMM, RGB U-DIMM, and SO-DIMM (laptop) form factors.

The 6000MHz CL36 tier is particularly significant for AMD AM5 users: at 6000MT/s, the Infinity Fabric clock runs at exactly 2000MHz in a 1:1 ratio with memory, delivering the best possible latency and bandwidth without sub-timing compromises.

### TornadoX7 — DDR4 Desktop Memory, Standard and Pro
**For:** System Builders, Upgraders, Budget-Conscious Gamers, Office Systems  
**Platform:** Intel LGA1700 (DDR4 models), AMD AM4 (Ryzen 5000 and below)

TornadoX7 is TwinMOS's DDR4 desktop memory brand, spanning two performance tiers: TornadoX7 (DDR4-3200 CL22, JEDEC-standard reliability) and TornadoX7 Pro (DDR4-3200 CL16, performance-binned for lower latency). The CL16 advantage is real: at 3200MT/s, CL16 delivers a true latency of 5.0ns versus 6.875ns for CL22 — 37.5% faster command response.

In 2026, with DDR5 kit prices elevated by AI-driven DRAM demand, DDR4 builds on Intel 13th/14th Gen (Raptor Lake) and AMD Ryzen 5000 represent exceptional value. TornadoX7 serves the hundreds of millions of DDR4 platforms still in active daily use worldwide.

### Thunder GX — Gaming-Grade DDR4
**For:** Gamers, Esports, Streaming Rigs  
**Platform:** Intel LGA1700 (DDR4 models), AMD AM4

Thunder GX modules bring additional validation depth to the DDR4-3200 specification. Where standard memory is tested against JEDEC minimum requirements, Thunder GX modules undergo extended thermal and electrical stress testing designed to replicate the sustained, full-system loads that gaming workloads generate when GPU and CPU operate simultaneously at peak capacity.

### Concord — RGB DDR4 Gaming Memory
**For:** Gamers, PC Builders, Streaming & Content Setup Builders

## SSD Brands

### CoreX Pro — Flagship PCIe Gen 5 NVMe SSD
**For:** Video Editors, 3D Artists, VFX Professionals, Developers, Enthusiasts  
**Interface:** PCIe Gen 5.0 x4, NVMe 2.0

CoreX Pro is TwinMOS's fastest and most advanced SSD brand, operating on the PCIe Gen 5.0 platform with sequential read speeds up to 14,000 MB/s — roughly double the throughput of PCIe Gen 4 flagship drives. For professionals working with 8K RAW video, massive 3D asset libraries, or large-scale compile workflows, CoreX Pro is the tier where synthetic benchmark numbers translate directly into saved minutes per workday.

Built with premium Micron/Hynix 3D TLC NAND, a DRAM cache, and an integrated graphene heatsink, CoreX Pro is engineered to sustain peak performance where lower-tier Gen 5 drives throttle under prolonged load.

[Explore CoreX Pro →](/products/brands/corex-pro)

### Xtreme — High-Performance PCIe Gen 4 NVMe SSD
**For:** Gamers, Content Creators, Power Users, PS5 Expansion  
**Interface:** PCIe Gen 4.0 x4, NVMe 1.4

Xtreme is TwinMOS's PCIe Gen 4 SSD brand, delivering sequential reads up to 7,500 MB/s. In 2025–2026, PCIe Gen 4 is the established mainstream performance sweet spot: fast enough to eliminate storage bottlenecks across gaming, 4K video editing, and multi-application workflows — without the thermal management demands and premium pricing of Gen 5.

The Xtreme M.2 is compatible with Sony PlayStation 5 and supports Microsoft DirectStorage on Windows. It is the recommended choice for builders and upgraders who want proven, stable, high-performance storage.

[Explore Xtreme →](/products/brands/xtreme)

---

### Alpha Pro — Mainstream PCIe Gen 3 NVMe SSD
**For:** Budget Upgraders, Students, Office Systems, Legacy Platform Users  
**Interface:** PCIe Gen 3.0 x4, NVMe

Alpha Pro brings the transformative speed of NVMe storage to budget-conscious buyers. At up to 3,600 MB/s sequential read — six times faster than any SATA SSD — Alpha Pro enables Windows to boot in under 10 seconds and eliminates the HDD bottleneck that slows hundreds of millions of older systems worldwide. HMB (Host Memory Buffer) technology provides strong random I/O performance without the cost of onboard DRAM.

[Explore Alpha Pro →](/products/brands/alpha-pro)

### Hyper H2 Ultra — SATA III SSD
**For:** Legacy Laptop Owners, Budget Desktop Builders, Industrial Systems, Secondary Storage  
**Interface:** SATA III 6Gb/s

Hyper H2 Ultra is TwinMOS's SATA III SSD brand, delivering read speeds up to 580 MB/s — at the practical ceiling of the SATA III interface. Available in a 7mm ultra-thin 2.5" form factor that fits every laptop ever made with a SATA bay, Hyper H2 Ultra is the single most impactful upgrade for the billions of older laptops and desktops still running mechanical hard drives. Available in dark grey and rose gold finishes.

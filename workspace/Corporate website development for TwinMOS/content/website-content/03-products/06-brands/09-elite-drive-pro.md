# ELITE Drive Pro

ELITE Drive Pro is TwinMOS's premium portable SSD brand — engineered for people who carry their data as part of their work. With USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C connectivity, an all-solid-state design, and a form factor that fits in a shirt pocket, ELITE Drive Pro delivers fast, durable, genuinely portable storage for creative professionals, business travelers, students, and mobile workers.

Unlike portable hard drives, ELITE Drive Pro has no spinning platters, no read/write heads, and no fragile mechanical components. It is as durable as the data it carries, resistant to the bumps and drops of daily use, and fast enough to move large files without making you wait.

## Brand Philosophy

The ELITE Drive Pro name communicates exclusivity and professional purpose. These are not basic flash drives or novelty external drives — they are purpose-built tools for people whose data is their livelihood and whose storage cannot fail at a critical moment.

"ELITE" signals that ELITE Drive Pro occupies the performance and quality tier above standard portable HDDs. "Pro" signals that it is designed for users with real professional demands: photographers moving raw files from a shoot, consultants transferring confidential documents between sites, videographers backing up footage on location, developers with large project repositories.

Available in four finishes — dark grey, gold, rose gold, and black — ELITE Drive Pro also acknowledges that professional tools should look professional.

## Why Solid-State Portable Storage

The comparison between portable SSDs and portable HDDs comes down to four real differences:

| Feature | ELITE Drive Pro (SSD) | Portable HDD |
|---------|----------------------|--------------|
| Transfer speed (read) | Up to 450 MB/s | 100–160 MB/s |
| Shock resistance | >1,500G | ~350G |
| Noise | Silent | Audible (seek, spin) |
| Size | Pocket-sized | Larger, heavier |

**Speed**: ELITE Drive Pro transfers files 3–4x faster than portable HDDs. A 50GB video project that takes 5 minutes to copy from a portable HDD copies in under 2 minutes on ELITE Drive Pro.

**Durability**: Portable HDDs have spinning disks and read/write heads that are vulnerable to shock. A drop from desk height onto a hard floor frequently destroys hard drives and the data on them. ELITE Drive Pro's solid-state construction withstands drops and impacts that would destroy a portable HDD.

**Portability**: The slim, lightweight ELITE Drive Pro enclosure fits in a front pocket, attaches to a keyring lanyard, or sits flat in a laptop bag's accessory pouch without adding meaningful weight or bulk.

## Key Features

### USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C (10Gbps)
The USB 3.2 Gen 2 interface supports up to 10Gbps of interface bandwidth — more than sufficient for ELITE Drive Pro's sustained transfer speeds. The USB Type-C connector is reversible (no orientation frustration), and the included cable supports both Type-C-to-Type-C and Type-C-to-Type-A connections for compatibility across device generations.

### Cross-Platform Compatibility
ELITE Drive Pro works with every major operating system and device category:

- **Windows** (10, 11): Plug-and-play, no driver installation required
- **macOS**: Direct connect to USB-C MacBook and iMac models; via adapter on older Mac with USB-A
- **Linux**: Recognized as standard USB mass storage device
- **Android**: Supported on Android devices with OTG USB-C support
- **iPadOS**: Compatible with iPad Pro and iPad Air (USB-C models)
- **iPhone 15 Pro / 15 Pro Max**: Direct connection for photo backup, video file management, and Filmic Pro / direct ProRes capture to external storage

Pre-formatted in exFAT for universal read/write compatibility across all operating systems without reformatting. Can be reformatted to NTFS (Windows-only read/write), APFS (macOS-only), or ext4 (Linux) based on your primary workflow.

### Solid-State Durability
No moving parts eliminates the primary failure mode of portable HDDs — mechanical shock damage. ELITE Drive Pro withstands the routine jostling, drops, and vibrations of travel. Data stored on ELITE Drive Pro is significantly safer from physical damage than data on a portable hard drive.

### Compact and Lightweight
The slim enclosure profile fits in a front jeans pocket. At ~210g, it adds minimal weight to a laptop bag, camera bag, or briefcase. The design is intentionally understated — professional in size, weight, and finish.

### Bus-Powered
No external power supply is required. ELITE Drive Pro draws power directly through the USB cable from the connected device — laptop, desktop, or tablet. This single-cable simplicity is essential for portable use.

## Use Cases

### Creative Professionals
**Photographers**: Offload full camera cards after a shoot directly to ELITE Drive Pro. Move Lightroom catalogs, raw files, and exported JPEGs between studio and client without internet dependency. The 1TB capacity holds 50,000–100,000+ JPEG files or 15,000–25,000+ full-resolution RAW files.

**Videographers**: Back up raw 4K and 6K footage from camera cards on location. The 450 MB/s read speed enables proxy generation directly from the ELITE Drive Pro during edit sessions. A 1TB drive holds approximately 50–100 hours of 1080p H.264 footage or 10–15 hours of 4K ProRes 422.

**Graphic Designers and Illustrators**: Carry active project files, font libraries, and asset collections between studio, home, and client offices. ELITE Drive Pro's speed ensures Photoshop, Illustrator, and Figma projects load quickly even when opened from the external drive.

ELITE Drive Pro connects directly to iPhone 15 Pro for photo backup and management, iPad Pro for file access during presentations, and Android devices for media management — without cloud storage dependency or subscription costs.

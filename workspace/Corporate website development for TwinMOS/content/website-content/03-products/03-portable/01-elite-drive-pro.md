# ELITE Drive Pro Portable SSD

The TwinMOS ELITE Drive Pro is a compact, high-speed portable solid-state drive designed for professionals, creatives, and mobile users who need fast, reliable storage in a pocket-sized package. With USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C connectivity, sequential read speeds up to **450 MB/s**, and write speeds up to **410 MB/s**, the ELITE Drive Pro delivers four to five times the transfer performance of a USB 3.0 portable hard drive — in an enclosure compact enough to slip into a shirt pocket, camera bag, or laptop sleeve.

Unlike mechanical hard drives, the ELITE Drive Pro contains no spinning platters, no read/write heads, and no precision motors. There is nothing to seize, scratch, or crack under physical stress. The result is a drive that handles the shock, vibration, and temperature variations of daily travel far better than traditional external hard drives — whether you are boarding a flight, hiking to a remote shoot location, or working from a coffee shop.

## Key Features

### USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C — 10 Gbps Interface

USB 3.2 Gen 2 doubles the maximum theoretical bandwidth of USB 3.0 to 10 Gbps, giving the ELITE Drive Pro the headroom it needs to sustain fast sequential transfers during large file operations. In practical terms, a 10 GB video file that takes over a minute to copy to a USB 3.0 portable hard drive transfers in under 25 seconds on the ELITE Drive Pro.

The reversible USB Type-C connector eliminates the frustration of plugging in the wrong way. It is forward-compatible with USB4 and Thunderbolt 3/4 hosts. The included USB Type-A adapter ensures full compatibility with older laptops, desktops, and monitors that do not yet have USB-C ports.

### Solid-State Durability — No Moving Parts

The ELITE Drive Pro eliminates the mechanical failure modes that make conventional hard drives vulnerable to travel:

- No spinning platters that fracture under physical impact
- No read/write heads that crash during movement
- No spindle motors that degrade from temperature cycling

This inherent solid-state robustness means the drive can be safely jostled in a camera bag, bumped off a desk, or carried through airport security without compromising data integrity. For users who travel frequently with important data, the absence of moving parts is a fundamental advantage — not just a specification.

### Compact, Pocket-Sized Enclosure

The ELITE Drive Pro's metal enclosure is engineered for minimum bulk. It fits in a jacket pocket, a small camera pouch, or any laptop bag without adding meaningful weight. The premium finish options — Gold, Black, Dark Grey, and Rose Gold — reflect TwinMOS's ELITE brand identity: storage that is designed to be seen as well as used.

The metal casing also functions as a passive heat spreader, helping the drive maintain stable temperatures during sustained read/write operations.

### Plug-and-Play on Every Platform

No driver downloads, no utility software, no configuration. Connect the ELITE Drive Pro to any compatible device and it mounts immediately as a standard storage volume:

- **Windows** XP, 7, 10, 11 — appears in File Explorer
- **macOS** — appears on the Desktop and in Finder
- **Linux** — supported natively via kernel USB mass storage driver (kernel 2.6+)
- **Android** — connect via USB OTG on compatible devices; access via Files app or third-party file manager
- **iPadOS 13+** — direct USB-C connection supported natively

### Pre-Formatted in exFAT

The ELITE Drive Pro ships pre-formatted in **exFAT**, which provides full read/write access from Windows, macOS, and Linux without reformatting. Transfer between operating systems freely. Reformat in NTFS for Windows-only workflows, or in APFS / HFS+ for macOS-only use, if required.

## Getting Started

1. Remove the ELITE Drive Pro and cable from the packaging.
2. Connect the USB Type-C to Type-C cable between the drive and your computer's USB-C port. For USB-A ports, use the included adapter.
3. The drive mounts automatically within a few seconds. On Windows it appears in File Explorer; on macOS it appears in Finder and on the Desktop.
4. Drag and drop files, or configure backup software to use the drive as its destination.
5. Before disconnecting, use your operating system's **Eject** or **Safely Remove Hardware** function to ensure all write operations have completed.

**Tip:** Always connect to a USB 3.2 Gen 2 or USB 3.1 Gen 2 port — typically identified by a blue port, a USB-C port, or the SS+ symbol — for maximum transfer speed. Connecting to a USB 2.0 port will reduce speeds to approximately 25–35 MB/s.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between the ELITE Drive Pro and the ProDrive Ultra?**
The ELITE Drive Pro is a solid-state drive: faster (up to 450 MB/s), more shock-resistant, and more compact. The ProDrive Ultra is a mechanical hard drive: larger maximum capacity (up to 4TB), lower cost per terabyte, and better suited to bulk archival storage. See the full [comparison guide](/products/portable#comparison).

**Can I use the ELITE Drive Pro as a boot drive?**
Yes, on systems that support booting from USB storage. Boot performance will be constrained by the host system's USB controller. For daily-use boot drives, an internal SSD is still recommended for the best system responsiveness; however, the ELITE Drive Pro is fully capable as an emergency or portable boot volume.

**Is the ELITE Drive Pro waterproof or drop-rated?**
The ELITE Drive Pro is not certified to an IP ingress protection rating and has not been tested to MIL-STD-810H. Its solid-state construction provides inherent resistance to shock and vibration during transport, but it is not specifically ruggedized for field environments. For harsh-environment use, see the [Rugged Portable SSD](/products/portable/rugged-portable) (coming soon).

**Does the ELITE Drive Pro work with PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X?**
Yes. The ELITE Drive Pro can be used as extended storage for compatible console games. The drive ships in exFAT format, which both PS5 and Xbox Series X/S support. Format the drive through your console's storage management menu and begin installing titles. In-game loading speeds will be determined by the console's USB port bandwidth, not the drive itself.

**What happens if I disconnect the drive during a file transfer?**
Files already fully written to the drive remain safe. Files being actively written at the moment of disconnection may be incomplete or corrupted. Always eject the drive properly before unplugging to protect your data.

**Is there a warranty registration portal?**
Yes. Register your drive at [twinmos.com/support/warranty/register](/support/warranty/register) to record your purchase date and activate warranty coverage.

**Does the ELITE Drive Pro require encryption software?**
No encryption software is bundled. The drive operates as plain unencrypted storage. If you require hardware-level encryption for sensitive data, see the [Encrypted Portable SSD](/products/portable/encrypted-ssd) (coming soon), which is designed for regulated and security-sensitive environments.

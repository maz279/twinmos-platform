## Why Choose TwinMOS Memory?

### Limited Lifetime Warranty
Every TwinMOS DRAM module is backed by a limited lifetime warranty. This covers manufacturing defects and ensures long-term peace of mind for both consumers and system integrators.

### 100% Burn-In Tested
All modules undergo 100% functional testing before shipment, including compatibility validation, thermal stress testing, and burn-in at rated speeds. This eliminates DOA (dead on arrival) units and ensures stable operation from the first boot.

### XMP 3.0 & AMD EXPO Support
Performance modules ship with pre-programmed Intel XMP 3.0 and AMD EXPO profiles. Enabling these profiles in BIOS instantly configures optimal frequency, voltage, and timing settings — no manual tuning required.

### RGB Ecosystem Integration
RGB-enabled modules synchronize with ASUS Aura Sync, Gigabyte RGB Fusion, MSI Mystic Light, and ASRock Polychrome RGB. Create unified lighting themes across memory, motherboards, fans, and peripherals.

### Global Platform Validation
TwinMOS memory is validated for compatibility with Intel and AMD platforms worldwide. Our modules are tested across major motherboard brands including ASUS, Gigabyte, MSI, ASRock, and OEM systems.

### JEDEC Compliance
All modules comply with JEDEC standards for voltage, timing, and electrical specifications, ensuring plug-and-play compatibility on supported platforms.

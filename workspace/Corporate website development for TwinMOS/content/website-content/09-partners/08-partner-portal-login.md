# Partner Portal Login

Welcome to the TwinMOS Partner Portal — the central hub for authorized distributors, resellers, and system builders. Access real-time pricing, marketing resources, training modules, and order management tools all in one place.

### Security Notice
For your protection, the Partner Portal uses encrypted connections and multi-factor authentication (MFA). If you have not yet configured MFA, you will be prompted to do so after entering your password.

## Forgot Password?

[Reset Password] — A secure reset link will be sent to your registered business email address within minutes. For security reasons, password reset links expire after 24 hours.

### Account Locked?
If your account has been locked due to multiple failed login attempts, contact your account manager or email partner-support@twinmos.com for assistance.

## New Partner?

If you do not have a Partner Portal account yet, you must first complete the partner onboarding process:

1. [Apply to become an authorized partner](/partners/become-distributor)
2. Complete application review and due diligence
3. Sign the distribution or reseller agreement
4. Finish required product training and certification
5. Receive your portal credentials via secure email

*Portal access is granted only to authorized partners who have completed onboarding. Resellers receive limited access based on their distributor's authorization.*

## Portal Features

Once logged in, you can access:

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| [Partner Dashboard](/partners/partner-portal-dashboard) | At-a-glance view of orders, notifications, account status, and recent updates |
| [Marketing Assets](/partners/partner-marketing-assets) | Download co-branded banners, product images, brochures, datasheets, and brand guidelines |
| [Price Lists](/partners/partner-price-lists) | Access current distributor, reseller, and system builder pricing with quarterly updates |
| [Training Resources](/partners/partner-training-resources) | Complete certification courses, watch webinar recordings, and track team progress |
| [Events Calendar](/partners/partner-events-calendar) | View upcoming partner events, training sessions, product briefings, and distributor summits |
| **Order Management** | Place orders, track shipments, view invoices, and manage returns |
| **Support Tickets** | Submit technical or commercial inquiries directly to your account manager |
| **Market Intelligence** | Access competitive briefs, market trend reports, and product roadmap updates |

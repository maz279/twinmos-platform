# Partners

TwinMOS builds success through strong, lasting partnerships. For over 27 years, we have collaborated with distributors, resellers, system builders, and OEM partners across 93+ countries to deliver industry-leading memory and storage solutions to businesses and consumers worldwide.

## Partner Programs

Whether you are looking to distribute TwinMOS products in your region, integrate our components into your systems, or resell to end customers, we have a program designed for you.

| Program | Best For | Learn More |
|---------|----------|------------|
| **Distributor Program** | IT distributors with established regional networks seeking exclusive territory rights and volume pricing | [Become a Distributor](/partners/become-distributor) |
| **Reseller Program** | PC retailers, e-commerce stores, and IT solution providers wanting to sell genuine TwinMOS products | [Become a Reseller](/partners/become-reseller) |
| **OEM / ODM Program** | PC manufacturers, industrial computing vendors, and gaming hardware brands needing custom-branded or specification-tailored memory and storage | [OEM / ODM Program](/partners/oem-odm-program) |
| **System Builder Program** | Certified system integrators and PC builders demanding reliable, high-performance components with preferred pricing | [System Builder Program](/partners/system-builder-program) |

## Why Partner with TwinMOS?

Partnering with TwinMOS means aligning with a globally recognized brand trusted for quality, innovation, and competitive price-performance. Our partners benefit from:

- **Competitive margins** and volume-based incentive programs
- **Marketing development funds (MDF)** for co-investment in regional campaigns
- **Dedicated account management** and 24/7 partner support
- **Comprehensive training** through sales and technical certification programs
- **Co-branded marketing assets** and product launch support
- **Exclusive territory rights** for authorized distributors

[Explore All Partner Benefits](/partners/distributor-benefits)

## Partner Portal

Authorized partners gain exclusive access to the TwinMOS Partner Portal — a dedicated platform providing everything needed to succeed:

- Real-time price lists and promotional pricing
- Downloadable marketing assets, product images, and datasheets
- Sales and technical training modules with certification tracking
- Order management, shipment tracking, and inventory tools
- Event calendar and webinar registrations

# Become a Reseller

Sell genuine TwinMOS memory modules, SSDs, portable storage, and flash products — and earn competitive margins while offering your customers trusted, high-performance solutions backed by 27+ years of innovation.

## Why Become a TwinMOS Reseller?

### Product Access & Pricing
- **Genuine products at reseller pricing** — competitive margins on our full portfolio
- **Priority allocation** during new product launches and high-demand periods
- **Promotional pricing** — access to seasonal campaigns and bundle offers

### Sales & Marketing Support
- **Co-marketing opportunities** — joint campaigns, social media assets, and email templates
- **Point-of-sale materials** — shelf talkers, counter displays, and product spec cards
- **Product training** — online modules covering key selling points and competitive differentiation
- **Sales playbooks** — scripts, objection handling, and closing techniques tailored to TwinMOS products

### Technical Support
- **Priority technical support** — dedicated reseller helpdesk for pre- and post-sales queries
- **Warranty facilitation** — streamlined RMA processes for your customers
- **Compatibility guidance** — access to platform validation lists and technical documentation

## Who Should Apply?

We welcome reseller applications from a wide range of businesses:

- **PC retailers** — brick-and-mortar stores selling components and pre-built systems
- **E-commerce stores** — online marketplaces and dedicated tech retailers
- **System integrators** — custom PC builders and workstation assemblers
- **IT solution providers** — managed service providers and corporate IT suppliers
- **Local electronics shops** — neighborhood retailers serving upgrade and repair customers
- **Corporate resellers** — B2B suppliers serving SMB and enterprise accounts

## How Reselling Works

Resellers purchase TwinMOS products through an **authorized TwinMOS distributor** in their region. This ensures:

- Genuine, warrantied products with full traceability
- Competitive reseller margins and promotional access
- Direct support from both the distributor and TwinMOS
- Eligibility for co-marketing and training programs

[Find an Authorized Distributor](/where-to-buy/distributors)

### No Distributor in Your Region?

If there is currently no authorized TwinMOS distributor serving your country, you have two options:

1. **Apply to become a distributor** yourself and build the TwinMOS network in your region
2. **Contact our partner team** — we may be able to arrange direct supply on a case-by-case basis while distributor coverage is established

[Apply to Become a Distributor](/partners/become-distributor)

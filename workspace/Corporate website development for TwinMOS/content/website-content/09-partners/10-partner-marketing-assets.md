# Partner Marketing Assets

Access a comprehensive library of ready-to-use marketing materials to promote TwinMOS products effectively in your region. All assets are professionally designed, brand-compliant, and optimized for both digital and print use.

## Product Images

High-quality visual assets for use on websites, e-commerce platforms, catalogs, and advertising.

- **Hero product shots** — clean, high-resolution images on white and dark backgrounds (300 DPI, PNG/JPG)
- **360° product views** — interactive rotating views for key SKUs (WebP/MP4)
- **Lifestyle and in-use images** — products in real-world settings: gaming setups, workstations, laptops
- **Packaging shots** — retail box images for e-commerce listings
- **Detail close-ups** — heatsink textures, RGB lighting, connector ports, and PCB shots
- **Size comparison images** — M.2 form factors next to common objects for scale reference

*Formats: PNG (transparent), JPG (white background), WebP (web-optimized), TIFF (print)*

## Digital Banners & Ads

Pre-designed banner sets in standard IAB and social media sizes, ready for deployment.

### Web Banner Sizes
- **Leaderboard:** 728 × 90 px
- **Medium Rectangle:** 300 × 250 px
- **Wide Skyscraper:** 160 × 600 px
- **Half Page:** 300 × 600 px
- **Large Rectangle:** 336 × 280 px
- **Billboard:** 970 × 250 px

### Social Media Templates
- **Facebook / Instagram:** 1080 × 1080 px (square), 1080 × 1350 px (portrait), 1200 × 628 px (landscape)
- **LinkedIn:** 1200 × 627 px (single image), 1080 × 1920 px (stories/reels)
- **X (Twitter):** 1600 × 900 px
- **YouTube:** 2560 × 1440 px (channel art), 1280 × 720 px (video thumbnail)

### Email Marketing
- **Email header graphics** — branded headers for newsletters and promotional campaigns
- **Product announcement templates** — pre-designed HTML-friendly layouts
- **Call-to-action buttons** — branded CTA graphics in multiple sizes

## Print Materials

Professional print-ready materials for in-store display, trade shows, and direct mail.

### Brochures & Catalogs
- **Product line brochures** — DRAM, SSD, Portable Storage, Flash (A4, tri-fold, and bi-fold formats)
- **Full product catalog** — complete SKU listing with specifications and comparison tables
- **Line cards** — quick-reference one-pagers by product category

### Datasheets & Technical Documents
- **Individual product datasheets** — specifications, features, compatibility, and ordering information
- **Comparison guides** — side-by-side SKU comparisons within each product family
- **Application notes** — technical guidance for enterprise, gaming, and industrial use cases

### Point-of-Sale (POS) Materials
- **Shelf talkers** — branded shelf-edge displays for retail environments
- **Counter cards** — tabletop displays highlighting key products or promotions
- **Posters** — A2 and A3 promotional posters for in-store display
- **Window decals** — branded vinyl decals for storefronts

## Brand Guidelines

Ensure consistent brand representation across all your marketing activities.

- **Logo usage guide** — correct and incorrect logo applications, minimum sizes, clear space requirements
- **Color palette** — primary and secondary brand colors with Pantone, CMYK, RGB, and hex values
- **Typography** — approved font families for headlines, body text, and technical content
- **Photography style** — guidance on image tone, lighting, and composition for custom shoots
- **Co-branding templates** — layouts for combining TwinMOS branding with your company identity
- **Tone of voice** — messaging guidelines for product descriptions, social media, and press releases

## Video Content

Engaging video assets for use on websites, social media, and in-store displays.

- **Product unboxing videos** — 60–90 second unboxing and first-impression content
- **Installation guides** — step-by-step memory and SSD installation tutorials
- **Benchmark highlights** — performance comparison clips for gaming and productivity
- **Brand story videos** — TwinMOS heritage, manufacturing, and quality commitment
- **Event recaps** — COMPUTEX, GITEX, and partner summit highlight reels

*Formats: MP4 (H.264), MOV (ProRes), and embedded YouTube links*

## Request Custom Assets

Need region-specific, language-localized, or campaign-specific materials? Our marketing team can create custom assets tailored to your market.

### Custom Asset Request Process:
1. **Submit a brief** through the Partner Portal or email your account manager
2. **Specify requirements** — dimensions, language, product focus, campaign theme
3. **Review draft** — receive initial design within 5–7 business days
4. **Approve final** — receive production-ready files after revisions

### Typical Turnaround Times:
- **Social media graphics:** 2–3 business days
- **Banner ads (standard sizes):** 3–5 business days
- **Brochure / datasheet:** 5–7 business days
- **Video editing / localization:** 7–10 business days
- **Custom photography:** 10–14 business days

*Custom asset requests are subject to MDF allocation and campaign priority. Contact your account manager to discuss your needs.*

# Distributor Onboarding Process

Our streamlined onboarding process is designed to get you up and running as an authorized TwinMOS distributor quickly and efficiently. From initial application to first order, most partners complete onboarding within **2–4 weeks**.

## Onboarding Overview

| Step | Activity | Timeline | Your Action Items |
|------|----------|----------|-------------------|
| **1. Application** | Submit distributor application | Day 1 | Complete online form, attach business documents |
| **2. Review & Due Diligence** | Partner team evaluation | Days 2–7 | Respond to information requests, provide references |
| **3. Agreement & Terms** | Contract negotiation and signing | Days 8–12 | Review agreement, negotiate terms, execute contract |
| **4. Product Training** | Sales and technical certification | Days 13–17 | Complete online training modules, attend webinar |
| **5. Portal Access** | Partner Portal setup | Days 18–20 | Activate account, explore tools and resources |
| **6. First Order & Go Live** | Initial purchase and market launch | Days 21–28 | Place first order, launch marketing campaign |

---

## Step 1: Application

Submit your [distributor application](/partners/become-distributor) with comprehensive company details, market focus, and business credentials.

### Required Documents:
- Business registration certificate / trade license
- Tax identification number (VAT/GST/TIN)
- Bank reference letter
- List of current brand partnerships (if any)
- Warehousing and logistics capability statement
- Resume or company profile

### Application Review Criteria:
- **Market relevance** — alignment between your territory and TwinMOS target markets
- **Business credibility** — financial stability, operational history, and references
- **Technical capability** — ability to support TwinMOS products pre- and post-sales
- **Sales network** — breadth and quality of reseller/dealer relationships
- **Brand alignment** — commitment to representing TwinMOS professionally

## Step 2: Review & Due Diligence

Our partner team conducts a thorough evaluation of your application.

### What We Evaluate:
- **Financial health** — credit checks and financial statement review (for volume commitments over $50K)
- **Reference checks** — contact with 2–3 existing brand partners or suppliers
- **Market assessment** — analysis of your territory's demand for memory and storage products
- **Competitive landscape** — understanding of local competitors and pricing dynamics
- **Facility verification** — virtual or physical site visit for warehouse and office (where feasible)

### Your Role:
- Respond promptly to information requests
- Make key personnel available for a 30-minute introductory video call
- Provide additional documentation if requested

*Typical duration: 3–5 business days*

## Step 3: Agreement & Terms

Upon successful review, we proceed to formalize the partnership.

### Distribution Agreement Covers:
- **Territory definition** — exclusive or non-exclusive rights for specified geographic area
- **Product categories** — authorized product lines and any restrictions
- **Pricing schedule** — distributor buy prices, volume tiers, and promotional pricing access
- **Sales targets** — mutually agreed annual minimum purchase commitments
- **Payment terms** — Net 30/60/90, letter of credit, or other agreed structure
- **Marketing obligations** — minimum marketing spend and brand guideline compliance
- **Warranty and returns** — RMA procedures, defective product handling, and return policies
- **Term and termination** — contract duration (typically 12–24 months), renewal, and exit clauses

### Negotiation Process:
- Draft agreement shared within 2 business days of approval
- Up to two rounds of revision based on mutual feedback
- Final agreement executed via electronic signature

## Step 4: Product Training

Complete comprehensive sales and technical training to ensure your team can effectively represent TwinMOS products.

### Training Modules:
| Module | Format | Duration | Certification |
|--------|--------|----------|---------------|
| **TwinMOS Brand & Portfolio** | Self-paced online | 2 hours | Required |
| **DRAM Technology Fundamentals** | Self-paced online | 3 hours | Required |
| **SSD Technology & Interfaces** | Self-paced online | 3 hours | Required |
| **Competitive Positioning** | Live webinar | 1.5 hours | Required |
| **Sales Techniques & Objection Handling** | Self-paced online | 2 hours | Recommended |
| **Technical Troubleshooting** | Live webinar | 2 hours | Recommended |

### Certification Requirements:
- At least **one sales-certified** employee must be on your team
- At least **one technical-certified** employee is recommended for markets with complex enterprise sales
- Certifications are valid for 12 months and require annual recertification

[Access Training Resources](/partners/partner-training-resources)

## Step 5: Portal Access

Receive login credentials for the TwinMOS Partner Portal — your central hub for all partner resources.

### Portal Setup Includes:
- **Account activation** — unique username and temporary password via secure email
- **Profile configuration** — company details, shipping addresses, and notification preferences
- **Team member invites** — add colleagues to the portal with role-based permissions
- **Resource exploration** — familiarize yourself with price lists, marketing assets, and order tools

### Key Portal Features:
- Real-time price lists and promotional pricing
- Marketing asset downloads (product images, banners, brochures)
- Training module access and certification tracking
- Order placement, shipment tracking, and invoice history
- Event calendar and webinar registration
- Support ticket submission and account manager messaging

[Log In to Partner Portal](/partners/partner-portal-login)

## Step 6: First Order & Go Live

Place your first order and officially launch TwinMOS products in your market.

### First Order Recommendations:
- **Starter assortment** — curated SKU mix based on your market profile (typically 15–25 SKUs)
- **Launch quantities** — balanced between breadth of range and inventory investment
- **Promotional stock** — include units for demo, review samples, and launch giveaways

### Go-Live Support:
- **Launch kit** — physical and digital marketing materials for your debut campaign
- **Press release template** — announce your partnership with TwinMOS in local media
- **Social media toolkit** — graphics and copy for LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram announcements
- **Account manager check-in** — scheduled call within 48 hours of first order delivery

### Post-Launch:
- **30-day review** — assess initial sales, stock levels, and customer feedback
- **Quarterly business reviews** — strategic planning and performance assessment
- **Ongoing support** — continuous access to training, marketing assets, and technical assistance

## Onboarding Checklist

Use this checklist to track your progress:

- [ ] Submit distributor application with all required documents
- [ ] Respond to partner team information requests
- [ ] Participate in introductory video call
- [ ] Review and sign distribution agreement
- [ ] Complete required training modules and certifications
- [ ] Activate Partner Portal account and configure profile
- [ ] Place first order and receive inventory
- [ ] Execute launch marketing campaign
- [ ] Conduct 30-day post-launch review with account manager

---

*Typical onboarding timeline: 2–4 weeks from application to first order. Complex markets or large territories may require additional time.*

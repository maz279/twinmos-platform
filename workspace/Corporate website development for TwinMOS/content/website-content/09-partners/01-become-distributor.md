# Become a Distributor

Join TwinMOS's global distribution network and bring industry-leading memory and storage solutions to your market. With over 27 years of brand heritage and a presence in 93+ countries, TwinMOS offers distributors a compelling partnership built on quality products, competitive economics, and unwavering support.

## Why Become a TwinMOS Distributor?

### Financial Benefits
- **Exclusive territory rights** — protected market coverage in your authorized region
- **Competitive margins** — attractive pricing structures that reward performance
- **Volume incentives** — tiered rebate and reward programs for high-performing partners
- **Marketing Development Funds (MDF)** — co-investment in regional marketing campaigns

### Operational Support
- **Dedicated account manager** — personal support from our experienced partner team
- **Efficient logistics** — reliable supply chain and fulfillment from our Dubai HQ
- **Flexible inventory programs** — stocking and replenishment options tailored to your market
- **Priority technical support** — direct access to engineering and product specialists

### Marketing & Training
- **Co-branded marketing assets** — ready-to-use brochures, banners, product images, and digital kits
- **Product launch support** — coordinated go-to-market campaigns for new releases
- **Sales certification programs** — TwinMOS Certified Sales Professional training
- **Technical certification** — TwinMOS Certified Technical Specialist credentials for your team

[Learn More About Benefits](/partners/distributor-benefits)

## Who Should Apply?

We are seeking qualified distributors who can represent the TwinMOS brand with professionalism and drive growth in their markets. Ideal candidates include:

- **IT distributors** with established regional networks and dealer relationships
- **Companies with proven experience** in memory modules, SSDs, or storage solutions
- **Businesses seeking to expand** their product portfolio with a recognized global brand
- **Organizations with strong logistics capabilities** including warehousing and last-mile delivery
- **Firms with dedicated sales and technical teams** capable of supporting end customers

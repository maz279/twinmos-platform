# Partner Training Resources

Equip your sales and technical teams with the knowledge and credentials to sell, recommend, and support TwinMOS products with confidence. Our comprehensive training program covers product fundamentals, competitive positioning, technical troubleshooting, and industry trends.

## Training Formats

TwinMOS partner training is delivered through multiple formats to accommodate different learning styles and schedules:

- **Self-paced e-learning** — on-demand modules accessible 24/7 through the Partner Portal
- **Live webinars** — interactive sessions with Q&A, scheduled monthly
- **In-person workshops** — hands-on training at distributor summits and trade shows
- **Certification exams** — online assessments with immediate results and digital badges

## Technical Training

### Memory Technology Fundamentals
A comprehensive foundation in DRAM technology for technical staff.

- **DDR4 vs. DDR5 architecture** — key differences in speed, latency, power consumption, and channel structure
- **Memory form factors** — U-DIMM, SO-DIMM, ECC, and registered memory explained
- **Speed and timing** — understanding MHz, CAS latency, and real-world performance impact
- **Intel XMP 3.0 / XMP 2.0** — how memory profiles work and how to configure them
- **On-die ECC and PMIC** — DDR5-specific features and their benefits
- **Thermal management** — heatsink design, airflow, and temperature monitoring

### Storage Technologies
Deep dives into SSD and flash storage technologies.

- **NAND flash types** — SLC, MLC, TLC, and QLC — characteristics, endurance, and use cases
- **SSD interfaces** — SATA III, PCIe Gen 3.0, Gen 4.0, and Gen 5.0 — bandwidth, latency, and compatibility
- **Controller technologies** — SMI, Phison, and in-house controllers — features and performance profiles
- **Key SSD features** — S.M.A.R.T., TRIM, wear leveling, LDPC ECC, power loss protection
- **Form factors** — 2.5", M.2 2280, M.2 2230 — physical dimensions and installation considerations
- **Endurance and TBW** — understanding drive lifespan and warranty implications

### Compatibility & Validation
Ensure successful deployments with proper compatibility assessment.

- **Platform compatibility** — Intel and AMD motherboard validation lists
- **BIOS and firmware** — updating motherboard BIOS for optimal memory support
- **QVL (Qualified Vendor List)** — how to read and use manufacturer compatibility lists
- **Common incompatibility issues** — symptoms, diagnostics, and resolutions
- **Enterprise server validation** — RDIMM, LRDIMM, and ECC compatibility

### Troubleshooting
Systematic approaches to resolving common memory and storage issues.

- **No POST / boot failure** — diagnostic flowchart for memory-related boot issues
- **System instability** — identifying faulty modules, adjusting timings, and voltage tuning
- **SSD not detected** — BIOS settings, cable/connector checks, and firmware updates
- **Performance degradation** — TRIM status, thermal throttling, and wear-leveling assessment
- **RMA qualification** — determining warranty eligibility and gathering required documentation

## Certifications

TwinMOS offers two professional certifications for partner staff. Certifications are valid for 12 months and require annual recertification to maintain active status.

### TwinMOS Certified Sales Professional (TCSP)
**Target Audience:** Sales representatives, account managers, and retail staff

**Requirements:**
- Complete all sales training modules (approximately 8 hours)
- Pass the TCSP examination (40 questions, 75% passing score)
- Maintain active certification through annual recertification

**Certified Skills:**
- Product portfolio knowledge across all categories
- Competitive positioning and differentiation
- Customer needs assessment and solution recommendation
- Basic technical understanding for pre-sales conversations

**Benefits:**
- Digital badge for LinkedIn and email signature
- Listing on TwinMOS "Certified Professionals" directory (optional)
- Priority access to sales incentive programs

### TwinMOS Certified Technical Specialist (TCTS)
**Target Audience:** Technical support staff, system builders, and pre-sales engineers

**Requirements:**
- Complete all technical training modules (approximately 12 hours)
- Pass the TCTS examination (60 questions, 80% passing score)
- Complete one hands-on lab or virtual simulation
- Maintain active certification through annual recertification

**Certified Skills:**
- Deep technical knowledge of DRAM and SSD technologies
- Compatibility assessment and platform validation
- Advanced troubleshooting and diagnostics
- RMA processing and warranty administration

**Benefits:**
- Digital badge for LinkedIn and email signature
- Authorization to perform Level 2 technical support for end customers
- Access to advanced technical documentation and engineering contacts
- Eligibility for "Authorized Service Center" designation

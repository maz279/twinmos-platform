# Marketing Development Funds (MDF) Program

The TwinMOS Marketing Development Funds (MDF) program is a co-investment initiative designed to support authorized partners in executing high-impact marketing activities that drive mutual growth. By sharing marketing costs, TwinMOS and its partners can amplify brand visibility, accelerate product adoption, and strengthen market presence.

## Program Overview

TwinMOS allocates Marketing Development Funds annually to qualified distributors and strategic partners. Funds are released quarterly based on performance, marketing plan quality, and territory opportunity.

### Key Principles
- **Mutual investment** — TwinMOS typically co-funds 30–50% of eligible activity costs
- **Performance-linked** — MDF allocations are tied to sales targets and partnership tier
- **Results-focused** — partners must demonstrate measurable outcomes from funded activities
- **Transparency** — all MDF transactions are tracked and reported through the Partner Portal

## Eligible Activities

The following marketing activities are eligible for MDF co-funding:

### Trade Shows & Exhibitions
- Booth rental and build at local or regional technology exhibitions
- Demo unit procurement and shipping for show displays
- Promotional materials (banners, brochures, giveaways) for event use
- Staff travel and accommodation for TwinMOS-approved events

*Maximum co-funding: 50% of eligible costs, up to $10,000 per event*

### Digital Marketing Campaigns
- Search engine marketing (SEM) and pay-per-click (PPC) campaigns
- Social media advertising (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X)
- Display and retargeting campaigns on technology websites
- Influencer partnerships and sponsored content
- Email marketing campaigns to reseller and end-customer databases

*Maximum co-funding: 40% of media spend, up to $5,000 per quarter*

### Co-Branded Promotional Materials
- Custom brochures, catalogs, and line cards featuring TwinMOS products
- Co-branded merchandise (USB drives, mouse pads, apparel) for reseller incentives
- Custom packaging for bundle offers and promotional kits
- Localized marketing materials in regional languages

*Maximum co-funding: 50% of production costs, up to $3,000 per campaign*

### Partner-Hosted Events & Seminars
- Reseller training days and certification workshops
- Product launch events and press conferences
- Roadshows visiting multiple cities within your territory
- Customer appreciation events and networking receptions
- Gaming tournaments and esports sponsorships

*Maximum co-funding: 50% of eligible costs, up to $7,500 per event*

### Showroom & Demo Setup
- In-store display fixtures and branded shelving
- Interactive demo stations for memory and SSD products
- Showroom signage and window displays
- Product sample allocation for hands-on customer experience

*Maximum co-funding: 40% of setup costs, up to $5,000 per location*

## Activities Not Eligible for MDF

The following are generally not eligible for MDF co-funding:

- Routine operational costs (salaries, rent, utilities)
- Standard inventory purchases and stock replenishment
- Activities that do not prominently feature TwinMOS branding
- Marketing for non-TwinMOS products within the same campaign
- Costs incurred before MDF approval is granted
- Activities in territories outside your authorized distribution agreement

## How the MDF Process Works

### Step 1: Annual Planning
- Partners submit an annual marketing plan by December 15 for the following year
- Plan includes proposed activities, budgets, timelines, and expected outcomes
- TwinMOS reviews and allocates annual MDF budget by January 15

### Step 2: Quarterly Proposals
- Partners submit detailed proposals for specific activities at least 6 weeks before execution
- Proposals include activity description, budget breakdown, target audience, and success metrics
- TwinMOS reviews and approves within 10 business days

### Step 3: Pre-Approval (Optional)
For large activities (over $5,000 partner contribution), TwinMOS may offer pre-payment of the co-funded portion upon approval.

### Step 4: Execution
- Partner executes the approved activity according to the proposed plan
- TwinMOS brand guidelines must be followed for all co-funded materials
- Significant deviations from the approved plan require prior written approval

### Step 5: Reporting & Reimbursement
- Partners submit a post-activity report within 14 days of completion
- Report includes photos, attendance records, media coverage, and lead/sales metrics
- Reimbursement is processed within 30 days of report approval
- Unused quarterly allocations may be carried forward once with approval

## MDF Allocation Framework

### Annual Allocation by Partner Tier

| Partner Tier | Annual MDF Allocation | Minimum Sales Target |
|--------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| **Registered Distributor** | $5,000 – $10,000 | $100,000 |
| **Certified Distributor** | $10,000 – $25,000 | $250,000 |
| **Elite Distributor** | $25,000 – $50,000 | $500,000 |
| **Strategic Partner** | $50,000+ | $1,000,000+ |

*Actual allocations are determined based on territory size, market potential, marketing plan quality, and historical MDF utilization effectiveness.*

### Quarterly Release Schedule
| Quarter | Allocation | Proposal Deadline |
|---------|------------|-------------------|
| Q1 (Jan–Mar) | 25% of annual allocation | December 1 |
| Q2 (Apr–Jun) | 25% of annual allocation | March 1 |
| Q3 (Jul–Sep) | 25% of annual allocation | June 1 |
| Q4 (Oct–Dec) | 25% of annual allocation | September 1 |

## Success Metrics & Reporting

Partners receiving MDF support are expected to track and report the following metrics:

| Metric | How to Measure | Reporting Frequency |
|--------|---------------|---------------------|
| **Lead generation** | Number of inquiries, demo requests, or sample requests | Per activity |
| **Reseller engagement** | Number of resellers trained, certified, or activated | Per activity |
| **Media impressions** | Social media reach, ad impressions, PR coverage | Per activity |
| **Sales impact** | Revenue attributed to MDF-funded activities | Quarterly |
| **ROI calculation** | (Incremental revenue – MDF investment) / MDF investment | Annually |

## MDF Best Practices

Our most successful MDF partners follow these practices:

- **Plan early** — submit annual and quarterly proposals on time to secure maximum allocation
- **Integrate activities** — combine digital, events, and POS for maximum impact
- **Measure everything** — track leads, engagement, and sales to demonstrate ROI
- **Leverage co-branding** — prominently feature TwinMOS alongside your brand
- **Share learnings** — participate in partner summits to exchange successful campaign ideas
- **Follow guidelines** — adhere to brand standards and approval processes

## Submit an MDF Proposal

Ready to propose a marketing activity for MDF co-funding?

1. Log in to the [Partner Portal](/partners/partner-portal-login)
2. Navigate to Marketing > MDF Proposals
3. Complete the proposal form with activity details, budget, and expected outcomes
4. Attach supporting documents (vendor quotes, mockups, media plans)
5. Submit for review

*For questions about the MDF program, eligibility, or proposal guidance, contact your account manager or email mdf@twinmos.com.*

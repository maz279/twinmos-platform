## Certifications & Standards

TwinMOS products and manufacturing processes adhere to the highest international standards:

| Certification | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| **ISO 9001** | Quality management system compliance (manufacturing partners) |
| **FCC** | Federal Communications Commission certification (USA) |
| **CE** | European Conformity marking |
| **UKCA** | UK Conformity Assessed (post-Brexit) |
| **EAC** | Eurasian Conformity (Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan) |
| **RoHS** | Restriction of Hazardous Substances directive compliance |
| **REACH** | Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation & Restriction of Chemicals (EU) |
| **JEDEC** | DRAM standards compliance for DDR3, DDR4, and DDR5 modules |

## Quality Commitment

Every TwinMOS product undergoes rigorous testing before leaving our facilities:

- **Rigorous product testing** before delivery to customers
- **Environmental stress testing** under extreme temperatures and conditions
- **Performance and benchmark testing** against industry standards
- **Compatibility testing** across major motherboard and system platforms
- **Reliability testing** simulating real-world usage scenarios

Learn more about our quality assurance processes: [Quality Assurance](/about/quality-assurance)

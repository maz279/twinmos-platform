## Industry Insights

The global memory and storage market continues to evolve rapidly. DDR5 adoption is accelerating across consumer and enterprise segments, while PCIe Gen 5.0 SSDs are redefining storage performance benchmarks. TwinMOS remains at the forefront of these trends, delivering cutting-edge products that combine performance, reliability, and competitive value.

Key market trends shaping our industry:
- **DDR5 mainstream adoption** — Transition from DDR4 accelerating in 2025–2026
- **PCIe Gen 5.0 SSD growth** — Gaming and content creation markets driving demand
- **AI-powered computing** — Increased memory bandwidth requirements for AI workloads
- **Emerging market digitization** — Africa, South Asia, and Southeast Asia showing strong IT hardware growth

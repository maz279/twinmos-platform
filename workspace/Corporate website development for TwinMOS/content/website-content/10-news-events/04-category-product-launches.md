# Product Launches

Discover the latest innovations from TwinMOS. From next-generation DDR5 memory modules to breakthrough PCIe Gen 5.0 SSDs, our product launches reflect nearly three decades of engineering excellence and market-leading performance.

---

## Featured Launch

### [TwinMOS Launches CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSD](/news-events/2025-q3-corex-pro-gen5-launch)
**Q3 2025**
The flagship CoreX Pro Gen5 delivers unprecedented PCIe Gen 5.0 x4 performance with sequential read speeds up to 14,000 MB/s. Built with Micron/Hynix 3D TLC NAND, integrated graphene heat spreader, and a 5-year warranty, this SSD sets a new standard for enthusiast and professional storage.

### [TwinMOS Launches VOLTX RGB DDR5-6000](/news-events/2025-q4-voltx-rgb-ddr5-6000-launch)
**Q4 2025**
High-frequency DDR5 memory combining 6000MHz speed with stunning customizable RGB lighting. Full compatibility with ASUS Aura Sync, GIGABYTE RGB Fusion, MSI Mystic Light, and ASRock Polychrome. Intel XMP 3.0 and AMD EXPO certified for next-generation gaming builds.

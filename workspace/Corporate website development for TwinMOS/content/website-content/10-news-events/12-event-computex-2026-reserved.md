## About COMPUTEX

COMPUTEX TAIPEI is the world's leading technology trade show, attracting over 1,500 exhibitors and 40,000+ international visitors annually. The exhibition showcases the latest innovations in computing, AI, next-generation connectivity, consumer electronics, and high-performance hardware. For TwinMOS, COMPUTEX represents our most important global platform for unveiling new products and strengthening relationships with distributors, partners, and media from around the world.

## TwinMOS Participation Plans

TwinMOS intends to participate in COMPUTEX 2026, building on the success of our COMPUTEX 2025 showcase. We plan to demonstrate our latest innovations in memory and storage technology, including next-generation DDR5 modules, advanced PCIe Gen 5.0 SSDs, and expanded portable storage solutions.

### Expected Product Highlights

- **Next-Generation DDR5 Memory** — Expanded VOLTX lineup with higher frequencies and new form factors
- **Advanced PCIe Gen 5.0 SSDs** — CoreX Pro series expansion with additional capacities
- **Enhanced RGB Ecosystem** — New VOLTX RGB lighting integrations and software features
- **Portable Storage Innovations** — Next-gen portable SSDs and USB solutions

# TwinMOS Launches VOLTX RGB DDR5-6000

**Published:** Q4 2025  
**Category:** Product Launches

---

TwinMOS has launched the **VOLTX RGB DDR5-6000**, a high-speed DDR5 memory module designed for gamers, PC enthusiasts, and content creators who demand both uncompromising performance and striking visual customization. With speeds of up to 6000MHz, tight CL36 timings, and full RGB synchronization support, the VOLTX RGB DDR5 series brings next-generation memory performance to Intel and AMD platforms.

The VOLTX RGB DDR5 series is the latest addition to TwinMOS's award-winning gaming memory lineup, engineered to extract maximum performance from the latest Intel 12th/13th/14th Gen Core and AMD Ryzen 7000/9000 series processors while delivering stunning visual effects through seamless RGB ecosystem integration.

## Product Highlights

| Specification | Details |
|---------------|---------|
| **Speeds** | 5600MHz, 6000MHz |
| **Capacities** | 16GB, 32GB (single module); 32GB (2×16GB), 64GB (2×32GB) dual-channel kits |
| **Form Factor** | 288-pin U-DIMM |
| **Voltage** | 1.35V |
| **Heatsink** | Dark Maroon aluminum with premium thermal design |
| **RGB Sync** | ASUS Aura Sync, GIGABYTE RGB Fusion, MSI Mystic Light, ASRock Polychrome |
| **Warranty** | Lifetime Product Warranty |
| **Compatibility** | Intel XMP 3.0, AMD EXPO |
| **On-die ECC** | Yes |
| **PCB** | 8-layer high-performance design |

## Performance Profiles

The VOLTX RGB DDR5 modules ship with pre-configured XMP 3.0 and AMD EXPO profiles for easy one-click overclocking. Users can switch between performance and compatibility profiles directly from the BIOS:

| Profile | Speed | Timings | Voltage | Best For |
|---------|-------|---------|---------|----------|
| XMP Profile 1 | 6000MT/s | 36-46-46-86 | 1.35V | Maximum performance on Intel platforms |
| XMP Profile 2 | 5600MT/s | 36-36-36-68 | 1.35V | Balanced performance and compatibility |
| EXPO Profile 1 | 6000MT/s | 36-46-46-86 | 1.35V | Maximum performance on AMD platforms |
| EXPO Profile 2 | 5600MT/s | 36-36-36-68 | 1.35V | Broad AMD motherboard compatibility |

Both profiles are rigorously tested and validated to ensure stability under demanding workloads, from competitive gaming to 4K video rendering and large dataset processing.

## Key Features

- **High-Frequency Performance:** 6000MHz speed grade optimized for Intel 12th/13th/14th Gen and AMD Ryzen 7000/9000 series platforms, delivering up to 50% faster data transfer than DDR4-3200
- **Customizable RGB Lighting:** Dynamic lighting effects with 16.8 million colors, synchronized with major motherboard RGB ecosystems for a cohesive build aesthetic
- **Dual Platform Support:** Certified for both Intel XMP 3.0 and AMD EXPO for broad compatibility across the latest platforms
- **Premium Heatsink:** Dark Maroon aluminum heatsink with optimized thermal design ensures efficient heat dissipation and maintains peak performance under sustained loads
- **Lifetime Warranty:** Backed by TwinMOS's lifetime product warranty for complete peace of mind
- **On-die ECC:** Built-in error correction enhances data integrity and system stability for critical applications
- **8-Layer PCB:** High-quality printed circuit board design ensures signal integrity and reliable overclocking headroom

## About the Event

GITEX GLOBAL is the world's largest and most influential technology exhibition and conference, marking its 45th edition in 2025. The event gathered over 6,800 exhibitors, 2,000 startups from 180 countries, and 1,200 investors at the Dubai World Trade Centre and Dubai Harbour. GITEX serves as the premier platform for showcasing emerging technologies, forging strategic partnerships, and exploring new market opportunities across the Middle East, Africa, and beyond.

Key focus areas at GITEX GLOBAL 2025 included AI, cybersecurity, quantum computing, mobility, and sustainable technology — all sectors that drive demand for high-performance memory and storage solutions.

## TwinMOS Participation

TwinMOS participated in GITEX GLOBAL 2025 at the Conrad Dubai, hosting strategic meetings in **Meeting Rooms 8 & 9**. The engagement provided a valuable opportunity to connect with regional distributors, enterprise clients, government representatives, and technology partners from across the Middle East and Africa.

> *"GITEX GLOBAL is a cornerstone event for TwinMOS in the Middle East and Africa region. It allows us to strengthen our partnerships, explore new enterprise opportunities, and demonstrate how our memory and storage solutions address the evolving needs of businesses and governments across MEA."*
>
> — **TwinMOS Executive Team**

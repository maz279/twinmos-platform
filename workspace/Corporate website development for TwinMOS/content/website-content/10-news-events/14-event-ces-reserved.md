## About CES

The Consumer Electronics Show (CES) is the world's most influential technology event, attracting over 130,000 attendees, 4,000+ exhibitors, and media from across the globe. CES serves as the global stage for innovation, showcasing breakthrough technologies in consumer electronics, computing, automotive, health tech, and beyond. For memory and storage brands, CES represents a key opportunity to reach North American consumers, system builders, and media influencers.

## TwinMOS Evaluation

TwinMOS is currently evaluating participation in CES as part of our strategic expansion into North American markets. While our primary markets remain the Middle East, Africa, South Asia, and CIS regions, CES participation would support our long-term goal of building brand awareness in the USA and Canada.

### Strategic Considerations

- **North American Market Entry** — CES provides exposure to US retailers, e-commerce platforms, and tech media
- **Brand Building** — Opportunity to position TwinMOS alongside tier-1 memory and storage brands
- **Media Coverage** — Access to influential tech journalists, YouTubers, and industry analysts
- **Partner Recruitment** — Connect with potential US and Canadian distributors

### Potential Showcase Products

If TwinMOS participates in CES, the showcase would likely include:
- **CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSD** — Flagship PCIe Gen 5.0 storage
- **VOLTX RGB DDR5 Series** — Gaming-focused memory with RGB lighting
- **Portable Storage Solutions** — Elite Drive Pro and USB flash drives

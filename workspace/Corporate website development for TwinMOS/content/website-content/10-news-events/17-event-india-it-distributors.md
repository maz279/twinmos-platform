## About the Event

India represents one of the world's largest and fastest-growing technology markets, with a vibrant ecosystem of IT distributors, resellers, system integrators, and retail chains. TwinMOS is actively engaging with India's channel partner community through dedicated distributor events, product showcases, and partnership development meetings.

These events provide a platform to:
- Introduce TwinMOS's full portfolio of memory and storage solutions to the Indian market
- Train channel partners on product features, positioning, and sales strategies
- Strengthen relationships with existing distributors and onboard new partners
- Showcase TwinMOS's competitive advantages in price-performance and quality
- Discuss regional roadmaps, pricing, and promotional campaigns

- **Coverage:** National distribution network targeting consumer, SMB, and enterprise segments

### Market Opportunity
India's technology hardware market is experiencing rapid growth driven by:
- **Digital India initiatives** — Government push for digital infrastructure and connectivity
- **Gaming and esports boom** — Explosive growth in PC gaming driving demand for DDR5 and high-performance SSDs
- **PC upgrade cycle** — Massive installed base of aging systems requiring memory and storage upgrades
- **SMB digitization** — Small and medium businesses upgrading IT infrastructure
- **Education sector** — Schools and universities investing in computer labs and student devices

## Event Objectives

- **Partner Onboarding** — Introduce TwinMOS to new channel partners across tier-1 and tier-2 cities
- **Product Training** — Deep-dive sessions on CoreX Pro Gen5, VOLTX DDR5, and full product portfolio
- **Sales Enablement** — Equip partners with marketing collateral, competitive positioning, and sales tools
- **Relationship Building** — Face-to-face engagement with key decision-makers in India's distribution ecosystem

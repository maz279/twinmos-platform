## Featured Company News

### [TwinMOS Launches Anti-Counterfeit Program](/news-events/2026-q2-anti-counterfeit-program)
**Q2 2026**
TwinMOS has launched a comprehensive anti-counterfeit program to protect customers from fraudulent products and ensure the authenticity of every TwinMOS purchase. The initiative includes product verification tools, authorized retailer guidance, and a reporting mechanism for suspected counterfeit goods.

### [TwinMOS Website Relaunch Planned for Q2 2026](/news-events/2026-q2-website-relaunch)
**Q2 2026**
TwinMOS is undertaking a comprehensive corporate website relaunch to deliver improved user experience, updated product information, enhanced partner portal integration, and mobile-optimized design. The new website will serve as the primary digital touchpoint for customers, partners, and distributors worldwide.

## Corporate Highlights

### 27+ Years of Innovation
Founded in 1998 in Taipei, Taiwan, TwinMOS has grown from a memory module manufacturer to a global provider of comprehensive memory and storage solutions. Our headquarters in Dubai, UAE, serves as the strategic hub for operations across 93+ countries.

### Global Presence
- **5 continents** served
- **93+ countries** with active distribution
- **301–500 employees** worldwide
- **responsive customer service** and support

### Quality Commitment
- **Rigorous product testing** before delivery
- **ISO 9001** certified manufacturing partners
- **FCC, CE, UKCA, EAC, RoHS, REACH** certified products
- **JEDEC compliance** for all DRAM modules

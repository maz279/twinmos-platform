## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will TwinMOS come to my country soon?**  
TwinMOS is actively expanding its global distribution network. We evaluate new markets based on demand signals, distributor availability, and regulatory requirements. The best way to accelerate our expansion into your country is to [apply as a distributor](/partners/become-distributor) or encourage local IT businesses to apply.

**Q: Can I buy from a grey-market or reseller who imports TwinMOS unofficially?**  
We strongly advise against it. Unofficial imports carry significant risks:
- Products may be counterfeit, refurbished, or substandard
- No warranty coverage from TwinMOS
- No guarantee of compatibility with local power standards or regional firmware requirements
- No after-sales support

Always verify that your seller is listed in our [authorized retailer directory](/where-to-buy).

**Q: I found TwinMOS products on a local marketplace — are they genuine?**  
Not necessarily. Local marketplace sellers (social commerce, informal resellers) may not be authorized TwinMOS partners. Look for the TwinMOS hologram sticker on the packaging, check the box for the full warranty card, and verify the serial number through [TwinMOS support](/support) if in doubt.

**Q: What is the minimum order I can place directly with TwinMOS?**  
TwinMOS primarily sells through its distribution network. For direct corporate or bulk orders, [contact our sales team](/contact) — minimum orders are discussed case by case. Individual consumers are encouraged to purchase through the [official online store](https://www.twinmos.com/shop/) or an authorized online retailer.

**Q: How long does international shipping take from the official TwinMOS store?**  
Shipping times vary by destination and shipping method selected at checkout. Typical estimates: 7–21 business days for standard international shipping, 3–7 business days for expedited options where available.

## Buy with Confidence — Authenticity Guarantee

TwinMOS products sold through unauthorized channels may be counterfeit, refurbished, or carry no warranty. Protect yourself:

1. **Verify the seller** — look for "Authorized TwinMOS Dealer" or "Official TwinMOS Store" designations.
2. **Check the warranty** — all genuine TwinMOS products carry a 3-year manufacturer warranty.
3. **Use our store locator** — only retailers listed on this page are officially authorized.
4. **Report fakes** — contact [anti-counterfeit@twinmos.com](mailto:anti-counterfeit@twinmos.com) if you suspect a counterfeit product.

[Learn about our Anti-Counterfeit Program](/news-events/2026-q2-anti-counterfeit-program)

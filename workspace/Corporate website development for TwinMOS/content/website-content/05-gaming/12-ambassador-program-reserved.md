# VOLTX Ambassador Program

## What Is the VOLTX Ambassador Program?

The VOLTX Ambassador Program is an exclusive community of passionate gamers, builders, and content creators who represent the VOLTX brand at the grassroots level. Ambassadors are not just fans — they are advisors, testers, storytellers, and advocates who help shape the future of VOLTX gaming hardware.

Our mission is simple: **find the most dedicated gamers in our core markets and give them the tools, access, and platform to share their passion with the world.**

Whether you are a competitive player, a PC building enthusiast, a streamer, or a tech reviewer, the Ambassador Program offers a direct connection to the TwinMOS team and a voice in product development.

## Who We're Looking For

### Eligibility Criteria

We welcome applications from gamers and creators who meet the following criteria:

**1. Active Content Creator or Community Leader**
- Regular output of gaming or tech content (streams, videos, articles, social posts)
- Minimum 6 months of consistent activity
- Content quality that reflects positively on the VOLTX brand

**2. Genuine Product Enthusiast**
- Currently uses or has used TwinMOS/VOLTX products in your builds
- Can speak authentically about product performance and features
- Willing to provide honest feedback — both positive and constructive

**3. Geographic Presence**
- Based in one of TwinMOS's core markets: Middle East, Africa, South Asia, Southeast Asia, CIS, or Europe
- Understanding of local gaming communities and market dynamics

**4. Community Engagement**
- Active presence on at least one major platform (YouTube, Twitch, Instagram, Twitter/X, TikTok, or regional equivalents)
- Engagement rate matters more than follower count — we value authentic connection over vanity metrics
- Demonstrated ability to drive conversation and inspire action

**5. Professional Conduct**
- Represents the VOLTX brand with integrity and respect
- No history of toxic behavior, harassment, or controversial conduct
- Commits to content guidelines and brand voice standards

## Ambassador Responsibilities

### Content Requirements

| Frequency | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Monthly** | At least one piece of VOLTX-focused content (video, article, social post, or stream segment) |
| **Quarterly** | One in-depth review or build showcase featuring a current VOLTX product |
| **Per Product Launch** | Share launch announcement and create content within 14 days of receiving early access |
| **Ongoing** | Engage with TwinMOS social channels and community discussions |

### Content Guidelines

- **Authenticity First:** Honest reviews are mandatory. We do not require positive coverage — we require fair coverage.
- **Brand Voice:** Confident, technical but accessible, action-oriented. See [VOLTX Brand Story](/gaming/voltx-brand-story/) for voice guidelines.
- **Disclosure:** All sponsored or ambassador-created content must include appropriate disclosure per FTC/ local regulations.
- **Visual Standards:** Use high-quality images and video. Dark theme aesthetic preferred for gaming content.
- **Hashtags:** Use #VOLTX and #MyVOLTXBuild on social media. Tag @TwinMOS for potential amplification.

### Feedback & Collaboration

- **Product Feedback:** Submit structured feedback on early-access products via our ambassador portal
- **Beta Testing:** Participate in closed beta programs for firmware, software, and hardware
- **Community Support:** Answer questions and help fellow gamers in TwinMOS community channels
- **Event Participation:** Represent VOLTX at local events when possible

## Application Process

### Step 1: Submit Your Application

Complete the ambassador application form with the following information:

- Personal details (name, email, country, social handles)
- Content portfolio (links to your best work)
- Current and past PC builds (specs, photos, why you chose VOLTX)
- Audience demographics (platforms, follower counts, engagement rates)
- Why you want to be a VOLTX ambassador (200–500 words)
- Content ideas you have for the first 3 months

[Apply Now →](/contact/){ .cta-primary data-tracking="cta_ambassador_apply" }

### Step 2: Review & Screening

Our team reviews applications on a rolling basis:

- **Content Quality Assessment:** Review of submitted portfolio for production value and authenticity
- **Community Check:** Verification of engagement metrics and community reputation
- **Brand Alignment:** Evaluation of fit with VOLTX values and voice

**Timeline:** 14–21 business days from submission to decision.

### Step 3: Interview (Selected Applicants)

Shortlisted candidates are invited to a 30-minute video interview:

- Discuss your passion for gaming and PC building
- Share your vision for ambassador content
- Ask questions about the program and TwinMOS

### Step 4: Onboarding

Accepted ambassadors receive:

- Welcome package with VOLTX merchandise and current product lineup
- Access to the private Ambassador Discord server
- Brand guidelines and asset access
- First early-access product assignment
- Personalized referral link and affiliate dashboard

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many ambassadors will you accept?
We plan to start with 20–30 ambassadors in our first cohort, expanding to 100+ within the first year.

### Is there a minimum follower count?
No. We value engagement, authenticity, and community impact over raw numbers. A creator with 5,000 highly engaged followers is more valuable to us than one with 100,000 passive subscribers.

### Can I be an ambassador if I don't currently own VOLTX products?
Yes, but you must be willing to integrate VOLTX products into your builds upon acceptance. We provide seeding for this purpose.

### Is the ambassador program paid?
Ambassadors receive free products, affiliate commissions, and performance bonuses. There is no fixed salary, but top performers can earn significant income through the affiliate program.

### Can I leave the program?
Yes. The ambassador relationship is voluntary. Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice.

### Do I need to sign a contract?
Yes. Accepted ambassadors sign a simple agreement covering content guidelines, disclosure requirements, and intellectual property terms.

### How do I update my application?
Email gaming@twinmos.com with your name and "Ambassador Application Update" in the subject line.

# Submit Your Build

## Share Your Rig

We love seeing what the community builds with VOLTX. Submit your PC build for a chance to be featured in our Build Gallery, shared on our social channels, and celebrated by gamers worldwide.

---

## Submission Guidelines

Before submitting, please ensure your entry meets the following criteria:

- [ ] **TwinMOS Product Required:** Your build must include at least one TwinMOS or VOLTX product (memory, SSD, or portable storage)
- [ ] **High-Resolution Photos:** Minimum 1920×1080 pixels, clear focus, good lighting
- [ ] **Complete Specifications:** Full component list with model names
- [ ] **Build Story:** 100–500 words describing your theme, goals, and why you chose VOLTX
- [ ] **Original Content:** Photos must be your own work; no stock images or renders
- [ ] **Appropriate Content:** No offensive imagery, watermarks from other brands, or inappropriate material

**Prompt Suggestions:**

- What was your design theme or inspiration?
- What do you primarily use this PC for? (gaming, streaming, content creation, work)
- Why did you choose VOLTX memory or storage for this build?
- What was the most challenging part of the build?
- What would you upgrade next?
- Any tips for someone building a similar rig?

**Guidelines:**
- Minimum 100 words, maximum 500 words
- Be specific about your component choices

### Photo Upload

**Requirements:**
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Resolution:** Minimum 1920×1080 pixels per image
- **Maximum File Size:** 5MB per image
- **Maximum Images:** 5

**Recommended Shots:**
1. **Hero Shot:** Full build with RGB lighting on, front or angled view
2. **Interior Shot:** Clear view of components, cable management, memory modules
3. **Detail Shot:** Close-up of VOLTX product (memory or SSD) installed
4. **Ambient Shot:** Build in its environment — desk setup, monitor, peripherals
5. **Bonus Shot:** Any creative angle, thermal shot, or before/after comparison

**Photo Tips:**
- Use natural light or softbox lighting; avoid harsh direct flash
- Clean your case glass and components before shooting
- Turn off room lights to let RGB lighting stand out
- Use a tripod for sharp images in low light
- Shoot in RAW if possible for post-processing flexibility

## What Happens Next

| Step | Timeline | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **1. Submission Received** | Immediate | Confirmation email sent to your provided address |
| **2. Moderation Review** | 3–5 business days | TwinMOS team reviews photos, specs, and story for quality and guideline compliance |
| **3. Approval / Feedback** | Within 7 business days | Approved builds move to scheduling; rejected builds receive feedback with resubmission option |
| **4. Publication** | 1–2 weeks after approval | Build goes live in the gallery with full attribution |
| **5. Social Feature** | Ongoing | Selected builds are shared on TwinMOS Instagram, Twitter/X, and Discord |

**After Submission:**
- You will receive a "Pending Moderation" confirmation message on the site
- Check your email (including spam folder) for moderation updates
- Approved builds are published on a rolling basis; featured builds are selected monthly
- You may submit updates to your build (new photos, upgraded components) by emailing gaming@twinmos.com

## Privacy & Data Handling

TwinMOS Technologies is committed to protecting your personal information:

- **Email addresses** are stored securely and used only for moderation communication
- **Full names** are collected for verification but never published
- **Social handles** are optional and only displayed with your explicit consent
- **Photos** are stored on TwinMOS servers and may be backed up to CDN for performance
- **Data retention:** Submissions are retained indefinitely unless you request deletion by contacting privacy@twinmos.com

## Design Specifications

- **Layout:** Single-page form with progressive disclosure (personal info → specs → story → photos → terms)
- **Form Validation:** Real-time validation with inline error messages
- **Photo Upload:** Drag-and-drop zone with preview thumbnails, file type and size validation
- **Progress Indicator:** Step bar showing current section (Step 1 of 5)
- **Mobile:** Stacked form fields, native file picker, large touch targets
- **Theme:** Dark theme consistent with gaming hub

## Accessibility Notes

- All form fields must have associated `<label>` elements
- Error messages must be announced to screen readers via `aria-live`
- File upload must support keyboard activation and have visible focus states
- Required fields must be indicated with both text and `aria-required`
- Form must be fully navigable by keyboard (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space)
- Color must not be the sole indicator of validation state (icons + text)

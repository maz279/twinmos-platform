# Esports & Sponsorships

## TwinMOS in Esports

TwinMOS is committed to supporting the competitive gaming community at every level. From grassroots LAN tournaments across South Asia to international showcases at COMPUTEX Taipei, we partner with teams, organizers, and content creators who share our belief that performance wins games.

Our esports philosophy is simple: **equip competitors with hardware that removes bottlenecks, so skill is the only variable.** VOLTX DDR5 RGB and CoreX Pro Gen5 SSDs are designed for the split-second reactions and seamless asset streaming that competitive gaming demands.

### Brand Vision

- **Grassroots First:** We believe talent exists everywhere. Our sponsorship program prioritizes emerging regions — South Asia, Middle East, Africa, and Southeast Asia — where competitive infrastructure is growing rapidly.
- **Performance Over Hype:** We sponsor teams and creators who value substance. No pay-to-win. No inflated follower counts. Just genuine competitive drive.
- **Community-Driven:** Our partnerships are two-way streets. We listen to what players need, iterate on our products, and celebrate wins together.

## Partnership Tiers

### Team Sponsorships

For competitive esports teams with established rosters and tournament participation.

**What We Provide:**
- Full hardware support: VOLTX DDR5 RGB, CoreX Pro Gen5 SSDs, and portable storage
- Co-branded team jerseys and peripheral designs
- Tournament travel stipends and accommodation support
- Social media amplification of team achievements
- Access to pre-release hardware for testing and feedback

**What We Look For:**
- Active roster competing in ranked leagues or tournaments
- Demonstrable community engagement (social following, stream viewership)
- Alignment with TwinMOS values: performance, integrity, accessibility
- Geographic presence in our core markets (MEA, South Asia, SEA, CIS)

---

### Influencer Collaborations

For streamers, YouTubers, and content creators who produce gaming and tech content.

**What We Provide:**
- Product seeding: latest VOLTX memory and storage for review and integration
- Affiliate and referral programs with competitive commission structures
- Early access to new product launches (NDA-based)
- Co-marketing opportunities: featured on TwinMOS social channels and website
- Invitation to exclusive creator events and product briefings

**What We Look For:**
- Consistent content output (minimum 2 videos/streams per week)
- Authentic, honest review style — we value criticism that helps us improve
- Audience engagement over raw subscriber counts
- Content in English, Arabic, or Hindi (other languages considered)

---

### Event Sponsorships

For tournament organizers, LAN event hosts, and gaming convention partners.

**What We Provide:**
- Prize pool contributions: VOLTX hardware bundles, cash prizes
- Booth presence and product demonstrations at major events
- Branded tournament overlays, stage backdrops, and streaming assets
- Community-driven online competition prizes and giveaways
- Marketing support: social promotion, email blasts, website features

**What We Look For:**
- Proven track record of successful events (minimum 100 participants or 10K stream viewers)
- Professional production quality for streamed events
- Inclusive community policies and safe event environments
- Alignment with regional growth markets

# TwinMOS Refer & Earn — Coming Soon

> **Status:** This programme is in planning and will launch in a future phase. The structure below reflects the planned programme. Subscribe to [TwinMOS Insider](/newsletter/signup/) to be notified when Refer & Earn goes live in your region.

Share TwinMOS with the people you trust — and earn real rewards every time they upgrade their memory, storage, or portable drives.

**TwinMOS Refer & Earn** is a dual-sided programme: your friend receives a discount on their first qualifying order, and you earn store credit or exclusive rewards when they complete that purchase. The more you refer, the more you earn.

---

## How It Works

### Step 1 — Get Your Unique Referral Link
Once enrolled, you receive a personalised referral link tied to your TwinMOS account. This link is unique to you and tracks every referral you generate — accurately and automatically.

### Step 2 — Share It With Anyone Who Would Benefit
Share your link with friends upgrading their PC, colleagues sourcing office memory, gaming community members, social media followers, or business contacts making storage decisions.

**Ideal referral scenarios:**
- A friend building their first gaming PC who needs DDR5 RAM or an NVMe SSD
- A colleague whose laptop is running slow and could benefit from a RAM/SSD upgrade
- A small business owner sourcing USB flash drives or portable storage for their team
- A system builder or IT integrator who isn't yet a TwinMOS distribution partner

### Step 3 — Your Friend Gets an Exclusive Welcome Discount
When someone clicks your referral link and visits TwinMOS or a participating retailer, they receive an **exclusive new-customer discount** applied automatically at checkout.

**Planned referral discount for new customers:** **15% off** their first qualifying order (min. order value applies — final amount confirmed at launch).

### Step 4 — You Earn Rewards When They Buy
Once your referred friend completes a qualifying purchase, your reward is credited to your TwinMOS account within 7 days.

**Planned referrer reward:** **AED 20 / INR 500 / SAR 50** store credit per successful referral.
Credit is redeemable on any TwinMOS product through participating retailers.

## What Counts as a Qualifying Purchase?

A qualifying purchase is:
- A first-time purchase by a new TwinMOS customer (someone who has not previously purchased through the TwinMOS referral system)
- Completed through a participating retailer or directly via an authorised TwinMOS channel
- Meeting a minimum order value *(to be confirmed at launch — planned minimum: AED 75 / INR 1,500)*
- Verified as genuine within the reward validation window (typically 30 days from referral link click)

## Programme Rules (Planned)

- One referral account per person. Referrals using multiple accounts to self-refer are ineligible and may result in account suspension.
- Referral rewards are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Referral credits expire 12 months from the date of issue if unused.
- Existing TwinMOS customers are not eligible as "new customer" referrals.
- TwinMOS reserves the right to modify, suspend, or terminate the programme, including reward values, at any time.
- Final programme terms and conditions will be published on this page at programme launch.

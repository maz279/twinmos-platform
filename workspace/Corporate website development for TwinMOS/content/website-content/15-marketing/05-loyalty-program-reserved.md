# TwinMOS Rewards — Coming Soon

> **Status:** This programme is in active development and will launch in a future phase. The structure, tiers, and benefits outlined below represent the planned programme. Final details may change. [Subscribe to TwinMOS Insider](/newsletter/signup/) to be notified at launch.

We are building the **TwinMOS Rewards** programme — a loyalty system designed to recognise our most dedicated customers, system builders, and distribution partners with meaningful, compounding benefits.

---

## Why TwinMOS Rewards?

Loyalty programmes that deliver real value retain customers at 5x the rate of single-transaction discounts. TwinMOS Rewards is designed around one principle: **the more you invest in TwinMOS, the more TwinMOS invests in you.** Points alone aren't enough — our programme rewards relationships, knowledge, and advocacy, not just purchase frequency.

---

## Programme Structure: Consumer Tier

### How You Earn Points

| Activity | Points Earned |
|----------|--------------|
| Product registration (per SKU) | 100 points |
| Purchase via participating retailer (per AED/INR/SAR 10 spent) | 10 points |
| Write a verified product review | 50 points |
| Successful referral (friend completes qualifying purchase) | 200 points |
| Newsletter subscription | 25 points (one-time) |
| Birthday bonus (once per year) | 150 points |
| Attending a TwinMOS demo event | 75 points |
| Sharing TwinMOS content on social media (verified) | 25 points/post, max 100/month |

### Consumer Tiers

| Tier | Points Required | Tier Colour |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Member | 0–999 | Grey |
| Silver | 1,000–4,999 | Silver |
| Gold | 5,000–14,999 | Gold |
| Platinum | 15,000+ | Platinum |

### Consumer Tier Benefits

| Benefit | Member | Silver | Gold | Platinum |
|---------|--------|--------|------|----------|
| Birthday discount | — | 5% | 10% | 15% |
| Early access to new launches | — | 24 hrs ahead | 48 hrs ahead | 72 hrs ahead |
| Flash sale access | Standard | Early | Priority | Exclusive |
| Exclusive bundle pricing | — | — | Yes | Yes |
| Free expedited shipping (qualifying orders) | — | — | Yes | Yes |
| Dedicated support line | — | — | — | Yes |
| Invitation to TwinMOS events | — | — | Regional | Global |
| Points multiplier | 1x | 1.5x | 2x | 3x |
| Point redemption (per 100 points) | AED 1 / INR 25 | AED 1 / INR 25 | AED 1.25 / INR 30 | AED 1.50 / INR 40 |

### How to Redeem Points

Points can be redeemed in multiples of 500 for:
- **Store credit** toward purchases at participating online retailers
- **Exclusive merchandise:** TwinMOS branded accessories, merchandise bundles
- **Extended warranty upgrades** on registered products
- **Early access codes** for limited-edition and pre-release products
- **Event tickets** for TwinMOS-sponsored gaming tournaments and trade shows

*Minimum redemption: 500 points (= AED 5 equivalent). Points expire after 24 months of account inactivity.*

## Programme Structure: B2B / Trade Tier

Recognising that our distribution partners are the backbone of TwinMOS's market presence, the **TwinMOS Trade Rewards** programme runs as a parallel track with B2B-specific incentives.

### Trade Tier Levels

| Tier | Annual Purchase Volume | Programme Colour |
|------|----------------------|-----------------|
| Authorised Reseller | Entry | Registered |
| Silver Partner | AED 50,000+ / year | Silver |
| Gold Partner | AED 150,000+ / year | Gold |
| Platinum Partner | AED 400,000+ / year | Platinum |
| Elite Distributor | AED 1,000,000+ / year | Elite |

|---------|-----------|--------|------|----------|-------|

| Regional event sponsorship | — | — | — | Eligible | Priority |
| New product preview access | — | 2 wks | 4 wks | 6 wks | 8 wks |
| Exclusive SKU access | — | — | — | Yes | Yes |

### How to Enrol in Trade Rewards

1. Apply to the [TwinMOS Partner Programme](/partners/).
2. Receive authorisation confirmation from your regional account manager.
3. Begin tracking purchases against your nominated account.
4. Tier upgrades are reviewed quarterly based on trailing 12-month purchase volume.
5. Rebates are processed within 30 days of quarter close.

For B2B loyalty enquiries, contact our partner team at [sales@twinmos.com](mailto:sales@twinmos.com) or [submit an enquiry](/contact/sales/).

## Planned Technical Features

- **Points dashboard:** Real-time points balance, tier status, expiry countdown, and transaction history
- **Mobile-friendly account portal:** Manage everything from your phone
- **Retailer integration:** Points awarded automatically when purchases are linked via order number or registered email
- **Referral tracking:** Unique referral codes generated in-app for sharing
- **Tier progress indicator:** Visual bar showing how close you are to the next tier
- **Automated notifications:** Email/SMS alerts for tier upgrades, expiring points, and exclusive offers

## How to Redeem TwinMOS Promotions

### Consumer Promotions
Most consumer-facing promotions are applied automatically at participating retailers — online and in-store. Some promotions require a promo code distributed via our newsletter or partner retailer apps.

**Steps:**
1. Identify the eligible product(s) from the promotion listing above.
2. Visit a [participating retailer](/where-to-buy/).
3. At checkout, verify the discounted price is reflected. If not, show the promotion details to the sales associate.
4. For promo code offers: enter the code in the designated discount field at online checkout.

### Enterprise & Distributor Promotions
B2B and volume-based promotions require direct coordination with your TwinMOS account manager:

1. Contact TwinMOS Sales: [Submit a Sales Inquiry](/contact/sales/) or email **sales@twinmos.com**
2. Provide your distributor account number and estimated order volume.
3. Receive formal pricing confirmation and rebate terms in writing.
4. Submit proof of purchase within the stated claim window.

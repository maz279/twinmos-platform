# Technology Hub

## Innovation at TwinMOS

With 27+ years of memory and storage expertise, TwinMOS continues to push the boundaries of performance, reliability, and efficiency. From our founding in 1998 in Taiwan to our global headquarters in Dubai (DAFZA), innovation has been the driving force behind every product we create.

Our technology portfolio spans the entire memory and storage stack — from DRAM module design and 3D NAND flash architectures to advanced SSD controllers, thermal management solutions, and hardware-level data security. Every TwinMOS product is engineered to deliver measurable, real-world performance gains while maintaining the reliability standards that have defined our brand across 93+ countries.

## Technology Pillars

### Memory Excellence
TwinMOS DRAM modules leverage the latest JEDEC-compliant architectures, from DDR3 legacy support through cutting-edge DDR5 platforms. Our VOLTX DDR5 RGB series represents the pinnacle of consumer memory technology, featuring on-module PMIC power management, on-die ECC, and Intel XMP 3.0 / AMD EXPO overclocking support.

### Storage Leadership
From SATA III legacy upgrades to PCIe Gen 5.0 flagship drives, TwinMOS SSDs harness advanced 3D TLC NAND, industry-leading controllers from Phison and SMI, and proprietary thermal solutions to deliver class-leading speed and endurance.

### Reliability by Design
Every TwinMOS product undergoes rigorous validation across electrical, thermal, environmental, and compatibility dimensions. Our limited lifetime warranty on DRAM and multi-year SSD warranties reflect our confidence in the engineering process.

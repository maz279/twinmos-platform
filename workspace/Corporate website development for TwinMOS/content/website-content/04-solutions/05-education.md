# Education Solutions

## Reliable Technology for Learning Environments

Educational institutions face a unique procurement challenge: limited budgets, large device fleets, small IT teams, and the need for hardware that withstands daily classroom use across multi-year deployment cycles. TwinMOS offers a range of memory and storage solutions that address all of these constraints — with competitive volume pricing, standard form factors that work across device brands, and products engineered for longevity.

---

## The Education Technology Landscape in 2025–2026

Understanding the market is key to making the right procurement decisions:

- **K-12 global technology spending reaches approximately USD 30 billion annually**, with device procurement representing the largest share
- **Chromebooks hold 60.1% of the global education device market** and remain the dominant platform in K-12 settings
- **93% of US school districts intended to purchase Chromebooks in 2025**, with Windows laptop procurement also strong at 91%
- **Device refresh cycles average 3–5 years** in most districts — the pandemic-era rush purchases of 2020–2021 are creating a significant refresh wave in 2025–2027
- **Windows 11's minimum hardware requirements** (64GB storage, TPM 2.0, Secure Boot) are forcing storage upgrades in older Windows education laptops that cannot be refreshed on a full replacement budget

TwinMOS products are positioned precisely at the intersection of these forces: affordable, reliable, easy to deploy, and compatible with the full spectrum of education devices.

## Solutions by Deployment Segment

### Student Laptops

The most common education IT need — upgrading aging student laptops to extend useful life, meet Windows 11 requirements, or improve performance for modern coursework:

**Storage Upgrade (HDD → SSD):**
- Boot times drop from 60–90 seconds to under 15 seconds
- Application load times cut by 50–80%
- No moving parts — more durable than HDDs in student backpack environments
- **Hyper H2 Ultra SATA 2.5"** or **M.2 2280 SATA** are direct HDD replacements in older laptops

**Memory Upgrade (RAM):**
- Chrome OS devices with expandable RAM benefit from faster multitasking and tab management
- Windows laptops running Office, Teams, and browser-based learning platforms benefit significantly from 8GB → 16GB upgrades
- **DDR4 SO-DIMM** (2666MHz / 3200MHz) for the majority of current student laptop platforms
- **DDR5 SO-DIMM** for newer student devices on Intel 12th Gen / AMD Ryzen 6000+ platforms

**Recommended Student Laptop Products:**

| Product | Use Case | Form Factor |
|---|---|---|
| Hyper H2 Ultra SATA 128–512GB | HDD replacement | 2.5" SATA |
| M.2 2280 SATA 128–512GB | M.2 SATA slot upgrade | M.2 2280 |
| Alpha Pro Gen3 NVMe 256–512GB | Performance M.2 upgrade | M.2 2280 NVMe |
| DDR4 SO-DIMM 4GB / 8GB / 16GB | Laptop RAM upgrade | SO-DIMM |
| DDR5 SO-DIMM 16GB | Modern laptop RAM upgrade | SO-DIMM |

### Computer Labs & STEM Classrooms

School computer labs require consistent, standardized hardware that can be deployed in bulk and maintained with minimal IT resources:

**Desktop Lab Builds:**
- Standardized DRAM configurations eliminate compatibility variables across identical lab machines
- NVMe SSDs dramatically improve the experience for coding, design, and media coursework
- Bulk packs of DRAM and SSD allow rapid deployment without per-unit ordering complexity

**STEM & Design Labs (Higher Demand):**
- 3D design, video production, and engineering software require more capable hardware
- **Xtreme Gen4 NVMe + 32GB DDR4/DDR5** provides a capable workstation foundation for CAD, Blender, Adobe Creative Suite
- NVMe SSDs reduce project file load times and rendering waits that disrupt class time

### Campus IT Infrastructure

Universities and larger school districts manage more complex IT infrastructure — file servers, backup systems, staff workstations, and research computing:

**Administrative Workstations:**
- Staff running administrative software, databases, student information systems benefit from NVMe SSDs and 16–32GB RAM
- TwinMOS DDR4/DDR5 U-DIMM and Alpha Pro Gen3 NVMe serve this segment cost-effectively

**File Servers & Backup:**
- ProDrive Ultra 4TB HDD for bulk archive and backup storage
- Hyper H2 Ultra SATA SSD for file server OS and frequently-accessed data tiers

**Portable Storage for Faculty & Administration:**
- ELITE Drive Pro portable SSD for secure file transport between offices, classrooms, and home
- USB 3.2 Gen 2 speeds (up to 1,000 MB/s transfer) move large files — curriculum packages, media libraries, student work — quickly

**Research Computing (Universities):**
- University research labs with simulation, data analysis, or media production needs require workstation-class storage
- CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe and high-capacity DDR5 serve these demanding academic research workloads

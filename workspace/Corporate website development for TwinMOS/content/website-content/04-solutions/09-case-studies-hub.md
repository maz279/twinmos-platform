Strong submissions include:
- **A specific challenge** your organization faced before deploying TwinMOS products
- **The TwinMOS products** you selected and why
- **Quantified results** — boot time improvements, render time reductions, uptime figures, cost savings
- **A testimonial quote** from a team lead, IT manager, or decision-maker

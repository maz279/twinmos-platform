## What Happens Next

1. **Application Review** — Our HR team reviews your application within 2–3 weeks
2. **Screening** — Shortlisted candidates receive an email or phone call to schedule an initial screening
3. **Interviews** — Successful candidates proceed to technical/functional and final interviews
4. **Offer** — Selected candidates receive a formal offer with detailed compensation and benefits information
5. **Onboarding** — Welcome to the TwinMOS team!

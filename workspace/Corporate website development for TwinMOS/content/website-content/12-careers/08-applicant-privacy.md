## 2. Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data during the recruitment process:

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Contact Information** | Full name, email address, phone number, mailing address, city, country |
| **Professional Information** | Resume, CV, cover letter, work history, job titles, employers, dates of employment |
| **Educational Information** | Degrees, certifications, academic transcripts, institutions attended, graduation dates |
| **Application Details** | Position applied for, preferred department, preferred location, salary expectations, notice period, availability |
| **Skills & Qualifications** | Technical skills, languages spoken, professional certifications, portfolio links |
| **Referral Information** | Name of referring employee (if applicable) |
| **Diversity Information** | Gender, disability status (voluntary and used solely for diversity monitoring) |

### 2.2 Information Collected During the Recruitment Process

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Assessment Data** | Interview notes, test results, evaluation feedback, skills assessments |
| **Background Check Data** | Reference checks, employment verification, criminal background checks (where permitted by law) |
| **Communication Records** | Emails, phone call logs, and other correspondence related to your application |

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- Professional networking sites (e.g., LinkedIn) if you provide a profile link
- Recruitment agencies (if applicable)
- Employee referrals
- Publicly available professional information

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use applicant personal data solely for recruitment and hiring purposes, including:

| Purpose | Legal Basis |
|---------|-------------|
| Evaluating your qualifications and suitability for the role | Legitimate interest / Pre-contractual steps |
| Communicating with you about your application status | Legitimate interest / Consent |
| Conducting interviews and assessments | Legitimate interest / Pre-contractual steps |
| Conducting background and reference checks where required | Legal obligation / Legitimate interest |
| Complying with legal and regulatory obligations | Legal obligation |
| Diversity monitoring and reporting | Consent (voluntary) |
| Defending legal claims | Legitimate interest / Legal obligation |

We do **not** use applicant data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects.

---

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process applicant data based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

- **Consent** — Provided when you submit an application and agree to this privacy notice
- **Pre-contractual steps** — Processing necessary to enter into an employment contract
- **Legitimate interest** — Our legitimate interest in evaluating candidates, managing recruitment efficiently, and defending legal claims
- **Legal obligation** — Compliance with applicable labor, employment, and anti-discrimination laws

---

## 5. Data Retention

We retain applicant data in accordance with the following schedule:

| Scenario | Retention Period |
|----------|------------------|
| **Unsuccessful applicants** | Up to 24 months after the conclusion of the recruitment process |
| **Successful applicants (hired)** | Data becomes part of your employee record; retained per employee data retention policies |
| **General applications (no specific role)** | Up to 24 months, unless you request earlier deletion |
| **Legal holds** | Extended as necessary to comply with legal obligations or defend claims |

> **Note:** Local law may require a shorter or longer retention period in specific jurisdictions. We will comply with the most restrictive applicable requirement.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws (including GDPR for EU residents, PDPL for UAE residents, and similar frameworks), you may have the following rights:

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Access** | Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you |
| **Correction** | Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data |
| **Deletion** | Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten") |
| **Restriction** | Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances |
| **Objection** | Object to processing based on legitimate interests |
| **Data Portability** | Request transfer of your data to another controller |
| **Withdraw Consent** | Withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g., diversity data) |

## 7. Data Sharing and Transfers

### Internal Sharing

We may share your data internally with:
- Hiring managers and department heads
- HR personnel and recruitment coordinators
- Legal and compliance advisors
- Senior leadership (for senior roles)

### Third-Party Sharing

We do **not** sell applicant data. We may share data with:

| Third Party | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| **Recruitment agencies** | Facilitating the recruitment process (where applicable) |
| **Background check providers** | Conducting employment verification and reference checks |
| **Assessment platforms** | Administering skills tests and evaluations |
| **IT service providers** | Hosting and maintaining our recruitment systems |

All third parties are contractually bound to protect your data and use it only for the specified purposes.

### International Transfers

As a global company, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries where we operate, including the UAE, Taiwan, India, Germany, and the USA. We implement appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) to ensure your data receives adequate protection during international transfers.

## 8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including:

- Encryption of data in transit and at rest
- Access controls and authentication requirements
- Regular security assessments and audits
- Employee training on data protection
- Incident response procedures

While we take all reasonable precautions, no system is completely secure. If you have reason to believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

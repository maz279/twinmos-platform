# Benefits & Compensation at TwinMOS

**Rewarding Excellence, Supporting Wellbeing**

TwinMOS offers a comprehensive compensation and benefits package designed to attract top talent, reward performance, and support the wellbeing of our team members across engineering, sales, marketing, operations, quality, and support functions. We believe that when our people thrive, our products excel.

---

## Compensation

### Competitive Base Salaries
We provide market-competitive salary packages aligned with role responsibilities, experience level, and regional benchmarks. Our compensation philosophy ensures fairness and transparency across all locations.

### Performance-Based Incentives
Eligible positions receive performance bonuses tied to individual achievements, team goals, and company success. We recognize and reward those who go above and beyond.

### Recognition Programs
- **Spot Awards** — Immediate recognition for exceptional contributions
- **Annual Performance Reviews** — Structured evaluations with clear growth pathways
- **Long-Service Awards** — Celebrating loyalty and dedication at milestone anniversaries

---

## Professional Development

We invest heavily in growing our people because their growth drives our innovation.

- **Technical Training & Certifications** — Access to courses on DDR5 technology, NAND flash management, PCIe Gen 5 architecture, thermal design, firmware development, and industry-standard certifications
- **Cross-Functional Project Exposure** — Work on projects that span departments, building versatile skill sets
- **Mentorship from Senior Leaders** — Learn directly from executives and department heads with decades of industry experience
- **Conference & Trade Show Attendance** — Represent TwinMOS at COMPUTEX Taipei, GITEX Dubai, CES, and regional technology events
- **Online Learning Platforms** — Subscriptions to leading technical and business learning platforms
- **Tuition Reimbursement** — Financial support for further education relevant to your role (subject to approval)

---

## Health and Wellness

### Health Coverage
Health insurance offerings vary by location and role, in compliance with local labor regulations. Coverage typically includes:
- Medical insurance for employees and eligible dependents
- Dental and vision coverage (where available)
- Annual health check-ups
- Mental health support and counseling services

### Wellness Programs
- **Employee Wellness Initiatives** — Programs promoting physical and mental wellbeing
- **Fitness Support** — Gym membership allowances or on-site fitness facilities (location-dependent)
- **Stress Management Resources** — Access to mindfulness apps, wellness workshops, and work-life balance coaching

---

## Time Off and Work-Life Balance

### Leave Entitlements
TwinMOS observes local public holidays and provides annual leave entitlements in accordance with regional employment laws and company policies.

- **Annual Leave** — Generous paid time off based on location and tenure
- **Sick Leave** — Paid sick leave in line with local regulations
- **Parental Leave** — Maternity and paternity leave as per regional standards
- **Bereavement Leave** — Compassionate leave for family emergencies

### Flexible Work Arrangements
Select roles offer flexible, remote, or hybrid working arrangements depending on function, seniority, and location. We understand that productivity and creativity flourish when people have balance.

Benefit availability and specifics vary by country to comply with local regulations and market standards:

- **Dubai, UAE** — Comprehensive health insurance, annual air ticket allowance, end-of-service benefits
- **Taipei, Taiwan** — National health insurance, labor insurance, pension contributions

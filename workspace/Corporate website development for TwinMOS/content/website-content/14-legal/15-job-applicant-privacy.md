# Job Applicant Privacy Notice

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

This notice explains how **TwinMOS Technologies Middle East FZE** and its group entities (collectively, "TwinMOS") collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you apply for a position with us. This notice applies to all job applicants, regardless of the channel through which you apply (our website, job boards, recruitment agencies, or direct referrals).

Please read this notice alongside our main [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy/).

## 1. Data Controller

The TwinMOS entity in the country where the role is based acts as the data controller for your applicant data. For roles advertised by our headquarters or Middle East hub, the controller is:

**TwinMOS Technologies Middle East FZE**
C-9, Dubai Airport Freezone (DAFZA), P.O. Box 54278, Dubai, UAE
Email: privacy@twinmos.com

For roles based in India, the controller is TwinMOS Technologies (in-privacy@twinmos.com). For other regional roles, please contact privacy@twinmos.com to identify the relevant controller.

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

During the recruitment process, we may collect:

### 2.1 Application Data
- Full name, contact details (email address, phone number, postal address)
- CV, resume, cover letter, and portfolio materials
- Work history, employment dates, and names of previous employers
- Educational background and professional qualifications
- Professional certifications, training records, and skills assessments

### 2.2 Interview & Assessment Data
- Interview notes and assessments made by TwinMOS interviewers
- Results of technical assessments, aptitude tests, or work samples
- Video or audio recordings of recorded interviews (where applicable, with advance notice and consent)

### 2.3 Reference & Verification Data
- Reference checks from previous employers or professional contacts (with your prior knowledge)
- Background verification results (criminal records, identity, right to work checks) where required by law or role

### 2.4 Equal Opportunity Data (Voluntary)
- In some jurisdictions, we may invite you to voluntarily provide demographic data (e.g., gender, disability status, ethnicity) for equal opportunity monitoring. This data is held separately from your application, is not used in hiring decisions, and is reported only in aggregated form.

### 2.5 Communication Data
- Records of correspondence and communications between you and TwinMOS during the recruitment process

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use your applicant data to:
- Evaluate your qualifications and suitability for the role applied for
- Communicate with you about your application, interview arrangements, and outcomes
- Conduct reference checks and background verification (where applicable and with your knowledge)
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations (including right-to-work verification and tax record-keeping for offers)
- Maintain records required by applicable employment and data protection law
- Consider you for future roles (with your explicit consent, where required)

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

| Processing Activity | Legal Basis |
|---|---|
| Evaluating application and conducting interviews | Pre-contractual steps at your request / Contract (if you accept an offer) |
| Right-to-work checks and legal compliance | Legal obligation |
| Background checks and reference verification | Legitimate interest (or legal obligation where required by law) / Consent where required |
| Keeping your data for future opportunities | Consent |
| Equal opportunity monitoring | Consent (voluntary) |

## 5. Sharing Your Data

We may share your applicant data within the TwinMOS group (e.g., where a role requires collaboration across offices or where the hiring manager is based in a different entity). We may also share data with:
- **Recruitment agencies and job boards** through which you applied (to the extent necessary)
- **Background check providers** engaged to conduct verification (where applicable)
- **IT service providers** hosting our applicant tracking system, subject to data processing agreements

We do not sell your personal data or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 6. International Data Transfers

Your applicant data may be transferred to and processed in countries where TwinMOS operates, including the UAE, Taiwan, and India. For EU/EEA applicants, such transfers are governed by Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards in accordance with GDPR. For UK applicants, we use appropriate UK-approved transfer mechanisms. For India applicants, cross-border transfers comply with DPDP Act 2023 requirements.

## 7. Retention Periods

| Data Category | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Unsuccessful applicant data (without consent for future roles) | **24 months** from the decision date, then securely deleted |
| Unsuccessful applicant data (with consent for future roles) | Up to 36 months from consent, subject to renewal |
| Successful applicant data (employees) | Transferred to employee HR record; retained per employment retention schedule |
| Equal opportunity / demographic data | Aggregated/anonymized; individual data deleted after reporting cycle |
| Background check results | Per legal requirements in the relevant jurisdiction (typically 12–24 months after rejection or departure) |

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your applicant data:

- **EU/EEA/UK:** Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection to processing, and the right to withdraw consent at any time
- **UAE:** Access, correction, destruction/anonymization, and withdrawal of consent
- **India (DPDP Act 2023):** Access, correction and erasure, grievance redressal, and nomination. Contact our **Grievance Officer** at in-privacy@twinmos.com — acknowledgment within 48 hours, resolution within 7 working days
- **UK (DUAA 2025):** In addition to standard UK GDPR rights, note the phased implementation of new provisions under the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 throughout 2025–2026
- **Other jurisdictions:** Rights consistent with applicable local data protection law

To exercise any right, contact **privacy@twinmos.com** or the relevant regional privacy contact. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (typically 30 days).

## 9. Withdrawing Your Application

You may withdraw your application and request deletion of your data at any time by contacting **hr@twinmos.com**. Withdrawal of your application does not affect any data we are required to retain by law.

## 10. Changes to This Notice

This notice may be updated to reflect changes in law or our recruitment practices. The "Last Reviewed" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last updated. Material changes will be communicated to active applicants by email.

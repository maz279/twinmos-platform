# UKCA Marking & Declaration of Conformity

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

Following the United Kingdom's withdrawal from the European Union, products placed on the **Great Britain** market (England, Scotland, and Wales) are required to bear the **UKCA (UK Conformity Assessed)** mark, replacing the CE mark for Great Britain purposes.

## Great Britain — UKCA Marking

### Applicable UK Regulations

The UKCA mark confirms that TwinMOS products comply with the following Great Britain regulations, which substantially mirror the equivalent EU directives:

| UK Regulation | Equivalent EU Directive | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016** (SI 2016/1091, as amended) | Directive 2014/30/EU (EMC) | All electronic products |
| **Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016** (SI 2016/1101, as amended) | Directive 2014/35/EU (LVD) | Powered products |
| **Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012** (SI 2012/3032, as amended) | Directive 2011/65/EU (RoHS) | All EEE products |

### UK Responsible Person / UK Importer

Non-GB manufacturers are required to appoint a UK Responsible Person or importer established in Great Britain who is responsible for ensuring products comply with applicable UK conformity requirements and who can be contacted by UK market surveillance authorities.

### Technical Documentation

UKCA technical files for TwinMOS products are maintained at our Dubai headquarters and with our UK Responsible Person. These include:
- Product descriptions and specifications
- Electromagnetic compatibility and electrical safety test reports
- RoHS material declarations
- UK Declarations of Conformity (UK DoC)

UK DoCs are available to UK market surveillance authorities (Trading Standards, OPSS) on request, and to customers and distributors upon written request to legal@twinmos.com.

## Northern Ireland — CE or UK(NI) Marking

Products placed on the **Northern Ireland** market are subject to a distinct legal framework under the **Windsor Framework** (successor to the Northern Ireland Protocol).

Under the Windsor Framework:
- **Products in scope** of EU single market rules (manufactured to EU standards for sale in NI) must bear the **CE mark** and comply with applicable EU harmonization legislation
- **Products complying with UK GB rules** and destined for the NI retail market may be permitted to bear a **UK(NI) mark** under specific conditions, subject to UKCA and UK-EU agreement provisions
- Importers and distributors should verify which marking applies based on the specific product category and supply chain arrangement

TwinMOS monitors Northern Ireland regulatory developments and updates our marking and documentation accordingly. For Northern Ireland-specific documentation queries, contact legal@twinmos.com.

## Transition Period Status (2026)

The UK government has confirmed that CE marking (based on EU standards) will continue to be recognized in Great Britain for certain product categories during extended grace periods to facilitate supply chain continuity. TwinMOS monitors OPSS (Office for Product Safety and Standards) announcements for any extensions or changes to recognition periods affecting our product categories.

Where EU CE marking and UKCA marking requirements are currently equivalent (for our product types under the EMC, Electrical Safety, and RoHS regulations), TwinMOS's conformity assessment and technical documentation supports both marks.

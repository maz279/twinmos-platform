# EAC Compliance Statement

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

TwinMOS products bearing the **EAC mark** (ЕАС — Евразийское соответствие / Eurasian Conformity) comply with the applicable technical regulations of the **Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU)**, comprising Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, and Russia.

## Applicable Technical Regulations

EAC conformity is assessed against the following EAEU Technical Regulations (TR CU / TR EAEU) applicable to electronic equipment:

| Technical Regulation | Subject | Applicable To |
|---|---|---|
| **TR CU 020/2011** | Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC) | All electronic devices |
| **TR CU 004/2011** | Low Voltage Electrical Safety | Electrically powered products |
| **TR EAEU 037/2016** | Restriction of Hazardous Substances (RoHS equivalent) | EEE products |

## Conformity Assessment

EAC conformity is demonstrated through:
- **Declaration of Conformity (Декларация о соответствии):** For most TwinMOS electronic products, EAC conformity is confirmed through a self-declaration process supported by test reports from accredited EAEU testing laboratories
- **Certificate of Conformity (Сертификат соответствия):** Required for products in specific regulated categories; issued by an accredited certification body within the EAEU

EAC declarations and certificates include:
- The EAC mark and the registration number of the declaration or certificate
- The scope and period of validity
- References to the applicable Technical Regulations

## Product Labeling

TwinMOS products placed on EAEU markets bear the EAC mark on the product label and/or packaging, along with any additional markings required by applicable Technical Regulations (e.g., hazardous substance environmental use period under TR EAEU 037/2016).

## Local Representation

TwinMOS works with authorized local representatives and authorized importers within the EAEU to:
- Ensure proper registration and validity of EAC declarations and certificates
- Cooperate with EAEU market surveillance authorities
- Manage regulatory correspondence and post-market surveillance obligations

## Important Notice Regarding Russia

TwinMOS monitors applicable export controls, trade sanctions, and regulatory developments affecting the EAEU region. EAC compliance documentation is maintained for applicable markets in accordance with current regulations.

# EPEAT Environmental Compliance

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

TwinMOS Technologies participates in the **EPEAT** (Electronic Product Environmental Assessment Tool) registry for applicable products, demonstrating our commitment to environmentally responsible product design and lifecycle management.

## About EPEAT

EPEAT is a global ecolabel and rating system for electronic products, managed by the **Green Electronics Council (GEC)**. EPEAT evaluates products against comprehensive environmental criteria across the product lifecycle, from material selection and design through energy use, product longevity, and end-of-life management.

EPEAT registration is widely recognized by government and enterprise procurement programs worldwide, including the US Federal Government (Executive Order requirements), EU public procurement guidelines, and many corporate sustainability programs.

## EPEAT Product Categories Applicable to TwinMOS

The EPEAT registry includes the following product categories relevant to TwinMOS:

| EPEAT Category | Standard | Applicable TwinMOS Products |
|---|---|---|
| **Solid State Drives (SSD)** | IEEE 1680.1 (Computers) or SSD-specific criteria | NVMe M.2 SSDs, SATA SSDs, Portable SSDs |
| **Storage** | SSD and storage criteria | All SSD and HDD products |

EPEAT ratings (Bronze, Silver, Gold) are based on the number of optional criteria met beyond the mandatory required criteria.

## Environmental Criteria Addressed

TwinMOS products considered for EPEAT registration are evaluated against criteria including:

### Material Efficiency
- **Reduction of hazardous substances:** Compliance with and performance beyond RoHS requirements (see [RoHS Compliance](/legal/rohs/))
- **Recycled content:** Use of post-consumer recycled materials in product components and packaging where technically and commercially feasible
- **Recyclable materials:** Selection of materials to facilitate disassembly and recycling at end of life

### Energy Efficiency
- Low standby and active power consumption
- Power management features (dynamic power scaling in NVMe SSDs)
- Compliance with applicable ENERGY STAR® specifications where applicable

### Design for End-of-Life
- Products designed to facilitate disassembly, recycling, and responsible disposal
- Compliance with WEEE Directive take-back and recycling obligations (see [WEEE Compliance](/legal/weee/))
- Avoidance of flame retardants and plasticizers linked to environmental persistence

### Packaging
- Minimization of packaging materials
- Use of recyclable packaging materials
- Reduction or elimination of harmful inks and coatings in packaging

### Corporate Performance
- ISO 9001 Quality Management System certification (see [ISO 9001](/legal/iso-9001/))
- Environmental policy and sustainability commitments
- Supply chain transparency and conflict minerals due diligence (see [Conflict Minerals Policy](/legal/conflict-minerals/))
- Modern slavery and labor standards compliance (see [Modern Slavery Statement](/legal/modern-slavery-statement/))

## EPEAT Registration Status

EPEAT-registered TwinMOS products are listed in the official EPEAT registry at **epeat.net**. Registration status, product ratings, and declared criteria responses can be verified directly on the EPEAT website by searching for "TwinMOS" in the global registry.

EPEAT-registered products are subject to:
- **Post-market verification:** Periodic GEC surveillance testing to verify continued conformance with declared criteria
- **Annual re-registration:** Products must be re-confirmed against current criteria versions

## Continuous Improvement

TwinMOS is committed to progressively improving the environmental performance of its products. Our roadmap includes:
- Increasing EPEAT registration coverage across our SSD product lines
- Pursuing higher EPEAT rating tiers (Silver, Gold) for flagship products
- Reducing packaging weight and increasing recyclable content
- Engaging suppliers on lifecycle assessment and environmental impact reduction

## Our Recall & Safety Notification Framework

TwinMOS's product recall procedures are aligned with the **EU General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR)**, effective 13 December 2024, which replaced the General Product Safety Directive 2001/95/EC. The GPSR imposes enhanced obligations on manufacturers and online sellers, including mandatory notification to the EU Safety Gate portal and expanded consumer communication requirements.

### How We Monitor Product Safety

- Systematic post-market surveillance of field performance data, RMA returns, and warranty claims
- Analysis of failure modes reported through technical support channels
- Monitoring of regulatory databases and industry safety bulletins
- Assessment of component and supplier quality notifications
- Review of changes to applicable product safety standards

### When a Recall Is Triggered

A recall or safety notice will be initiated when:
- A product defect presents a risk of injury, fire, electrical hazard, or other safety risk to users
- A safety issue is identified that cannot be adequately addressed through a software or firmware update
- Required by a regulatory authority or market surveillance body
- A batch or production run is found to contain a manufacturing defect that affects product safety or integrity

### Our Recall Process

1. **Risk Assessment:** The nature and severity of the risk is evaluated by TwinMOS product safety and engineering teams
2. **Regulatory Notification:** We notify relevant market surveillance authorities (including the EU Safety Gate portal, UAE ESMA, and other applicable regulators) promptly and within mandatory timeframes
3. **Consumer Communication:** We communicate the recall through:
   - A prominent notice on this page and the TwinMOS homepage
   - Direct email notifications to registered product owners
   - Retailer and authorized distributor notifications
   - Press releases where the issue is widespread or involves significant risk
   - Social media announcements on TwinMOS official channels
4. **Remedy Provision:** We offer affected consumers a remedy appropriate to the nature of the issue, which may include:
   - Free repair or firmware/software update
   - Free product replacement
   - Full refund of the purchase price
5. **Progress Reporting:** We report the effectiveness of recall actions to regulatory authorities as required

### What to Do If a Recall Is Announced

If TwinMOS announces a recall or safety notice affecting a product you own:

1. **Stop using the product immediately** (unless the notice advises otherwise)
2. **Read the recall notice carefully** for specific instructions about your product and available remedies
3. **Contact TwinMOS** via the recall-specific contact information provided in the notice, or email **support@twinmos.com** with your product serial number and proof of purchase
4. **Follow the return, repair, or replacement instructions** provided

You do not need to pay for any recall-related service or return shipping.

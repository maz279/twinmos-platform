# Warranty Policy

> **Version 3.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

This Warranty Policy ("Policy") sets forth the terms and conditions under which **TwinMOS Technologies Middle East FZE** and its affiliates (collectively, "TwinMOS," "we," "us," or "our") warrant our products against defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. This Policy applies to all TwinMOS-branded products purchased from authorized channels, subject to the terms and conditions below.

**Statutory Rights:** Nothing in this Policy limits your statutory rights as a consumer under applicable mandatory consumer protection law. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional or different rights as summarized in Section 10 below.

## 1. Warranty Coverage Overview

TwinMOS warrants that its products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship when used under normal conditions and in accordance with the product documentation and specifications. If a valid warranty claim is confirmed, we will, at our sole discretion:
- Repair the defective product free of charge
- Replace it with an equivalent or comparable product (new or refurbished), or
- Issue a refund of the original purchase price

This limited warranty is non-transferable and applies only to the original end-user purchaser with valid proof of purchase from an authorized TwinMOS distributor, reseller, or retailer.

## 3. What Is Covered

This limited warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship that arise during normal use, including:

- Manufacturing defects in memory chips, NAND flash cells, SSD controllers, or PCB assemblies
- Component failures within specified operating conditions (temperature, voltage, humidity)
- Firmware defects present at the time of purchase that cause product malfunction
- Failure to meet published performance specifications (read/write speeds, capacity) under normal use
- Dead-on-arrival (DOA) products confirmed by TwinMOS support within the DOA period (typically 30 days from purchase)

## 4. What Is Not Covered

This warranty does not cover damage, defects, or failures resulting from:

- **Physical Damage:** Drops, impacts, crushing, bending, liquid damage, fire, or exposure to extreme environmental conditions (temperature, humidity, static electricity, dust) outside specified operating ranges
- **Unauthorized Modifications:** Any alteration, repair, or disassembly not performed by TwinMOS or an authorized service center, including the removal of warranty labels, stickers, or holographic seals
- **Improper Installation:** Incorrect insertion, incompatible systems, improper voltage supply, failure to follow installation instructions, or installation in systems not meeting minimum specifications
- **Write Endurance Exhaustion:** Gradual performance degradation inherent to flash memory technology when NAND write/erase cycles have exceeded the rated TBW (Terabytes Written) specification
- **Software & Data Loss:** Loss of data, software, or configurations stored on the product. Customers are solely responsible for maintaining regular data backups
- **Unauthorized Sellers:** Products purchased from unauthorized distributors, gray market sources, auction sites, unauthorized online marketplaces, or suspected counterfeit sources
- **Acts of God:** Natural disasters, power surges, lightning strikes, flooding, or other events beyond our control
- **Overclocking & Stress Testing:** Operation at voltages, frequencies, or temperatures exceeding published specifications; use in cryptocurrency mining operations beyond specified workload ratings
- **Cosmetic Damage:** Scratches, dents, discoloration, or other aesthetic changes that do not affect product functionality

## 5. RMA Process

To obtain warranty service, follow the Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) process:

### Step 1: Submit a Warranty Inquiry
Submit a request through our [Warranty Inquiry](/contact/warranty/) form or email **support@twinmos.com** with:
- Full name and contact information
- Product model name and part number (PN)
- Serial number (found on the product label)
- Date and place of purchase
- Detailed description of the defect or issue
- Proof of purchase (invoice, receipt, or order confirmation)

### Step 2: Troubleshooting & Remote Validation
Our support team will attempt remote troubleshooting. You may be asked to:
- Provide photographs of the product and any visible damage
- Run TwinMOS diagnostic software or third-party utilities and share results
- Confirm system specifications and installation configuration

### Step 3: RMA Authorization
If the issue is confirmed as a potential warranty defect, you will receive an **RMA number** and return instructions via email. The RMA number must be clearly written on the outside of the return package. Returns without a valid RMA number will be refused at our receiving center.

### Step 4: Product Return
Ship the defective product to the designated TwinMOS service center or regional RMA hub:
- The customer is responsible for outbound shipping costs unless mandatory consumer protection law requires otherwise
- Use a trackable shipping method and retain proof of shipment
- Pack the product securely to prevent additional damage in transit; TwinMOS is not responsible for damage incurred during return shipping due to inadequate packaging

### Step 5: Inspection & Resolution
Upon receipt, our technicians will inspect the product (typically 5–10 business days). Once verified, we will:
- Repair the product and return it, or
- Replace it with an equivalent or comparable product (new or refurbished at our discretion), or
- Issue a refund of the original purchase price if repair or replacement is not feasible

### Step 6: Return Shipment
Repaired or replacement products will be returned to you at TwinMOS's expense via standard courier. Expedited shipping is available at your additional cost upon request.

## 10. Jurisdiction-Specific Consumer Rights

This Policy does not limit or replace your rights as a consumer under applicable mandatory law:

### 10.1 European Union — Consumer Sales Directive 2019/771
EU consumers are entitled to a **minimum 2-year statutory warranty** against lack of conformity from the seller. The seller (not TwinMOS directly) is responsible for this statutory warranty. TwinMOS's own warranty is in addition to and does not replace any statutory rights you have against the seller.

### 10.2 United Kingdom — Consumer Rights Act 2015
UK consumers have statutory rights including that goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose, and as described. These rights are enforceable against the seller for up to 6 years (5 years in Scotland).

### 10.3 UAE — Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 on Consumer Protection
UAE consumers are entitled to a warranty document with each purchase. If a warranty repair exceeds 7 business days, TwinMOS will provide an equivalent product for use during the repair period free of charge. We maintain availability of spare parts for products sold in the UAE for a reasonable period.

### 10.4 India — Consumer Protection Act 2019
Indian consumers may bring claims against TwinMOS directly as the manufacturer for defective products and non-conformity with express warranties under the strict liability provisions of the Consumer Protection Act 2019.

### 10.5 United States — Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act
This warranty is provided as a "limited warranty" under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act. The warranty does not require you to use TwinMOS-branded accessories or service providers as a condition of warranty validity, except where free of charge or where an FTC waiver has been obtained. The informal dispute resolution procedure (warranty inquiry via support@twinmos.com) is available before resorting to formal proceedings.

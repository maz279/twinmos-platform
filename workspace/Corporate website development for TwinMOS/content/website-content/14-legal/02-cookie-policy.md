# Cookie Policy

> **Version 3.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how TwinMOS Technologies Middle East FZE and its affiliates (collectively, "TwinMOS," "we," "us," or "our") use cookies and similar tracking technologies on our websites, and the choices available to you regarding their use.

When you first visit our website, a cookie consent banner will appear. By clicking "Accept All," you consent to our use of all cookies. You may also choose "Manage Preferences" to accept only specific categories or "Decline Non-Essential" to limit cookies to those strictly necessary. You may update your preferences at any time via the [Cookie Preferences](/legal/cookie-preferences/) page.

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, tablet, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites function efficiently and to provide information to website owners. Cookies can be:

- **Session cookies** — deleted automatically when you close your browser
- **Persistent cookies** — remain on your device until they expire or you delete them

In addition to cookies, we may use other similar technologies:

| Technology | Description |
|---|---|
| **Web Beacons / Pixels** | Tiny images that monitor user activity and measure marketing campaign performance |
| **Local Storage** | Browser-based storage that persists across sessions and is not sent to our servers with every request |
| **Session Storage** | Similar to local storage but cleared when the browser tab or window is closed |
| **Device Fingerprinting** | Collection of technical device attributes to distinguish your browser from others without setting a cookie |

## 2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

| Purpose | Description |
|---|---|
| **Essential Operations** | To enable core website functionality, security features, and account authentication |
| **Performance & Analytics** | To analyze how visitors interact with our website, identify errors, and improve performance |
| **Functionality & Preferences** | To remember your settings, language preferences, and personalized configurations |
| **Marketing & Advertising** | To deliver relevant advertisements, measure campaign effectiveness, and prevent ad repetition |
| **Social Media Integration** | To enable social sharing features and embed social media content |

## 3. Cookie Categories

### 3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies
These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They enable basic features such as page navigation, access to secure areas, and account authentication. The website cannot function properly without these cookies; they cannot be disabled through our cookie preference center. No consent is required for these cookies.

### 3.2 Functional Cookies
Functional cookies allow the website to remember choices you make (such as your username, language, or region) and provide enhanced, personalized features. Disabling these cookies may reduce your browsing experience but will not prevent you from accessing the site.

### 3.3 Analytics & Performance Cookies
These cookies collect information about how visitors use our website—for example, which pages are visited most often and whether users receive error messages. Data collected is aggregated and anonymized where possible and is used solely to improve website functionality and user experience. These cookies require your consent.

### 3.4 Marketing & Targeting Cookies
Marketing cookies track visitors across websites and are used to display ads that are relevant and engaging to individual users. They may be set by us or by our advertising partners. These cookies require your consent. If you decline marketing cookies, you will still see advertisements but they will be less personalized.

## 6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

### 6.1 Cookie Preference Center

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time:
- **On first visit:** Use the cookie consent banner to accept all, decline non-essential, or customize by category
- **Any time:** Click "Manage Cookie Preferences" in the footer of any page, or visit our [Cookie Preferences](/legal/cookie-preferences/) page

### 6.2 Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We honor the **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signal. If your browser or browser extension sends a GPC signal indicating you do not want your personal information sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising, we will treat this as equivalent to declining marketing cookies for users in jurisdictions where this signal is legally recognized.

### 6.3 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. You can:
- View cookies currently stored on your device
- Delete existing cookies
- Block all cookies or block cookies from specific websites
- Set alerts for when a cookie is being set

Common browser cookie management links:
- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and Other Site Data
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
- **Edge:** Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions → Manage and Delete Cookies

Please note that disabling certain cookies may reduce website functionality or prevent personalization features from working.

### 6.4 Industry Opt-Out Tools

You can opt out of interest-based advertising through these industry tools:
- **Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA):** optout.aboutads.info
- **Network Advertising Initiative (NAI):** optout.networkadvertising.org
- **European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA):** www.youronlinechoices.eu

## 7. Data Collected via Cookies

The data we collect through cookies and similar technologies may include:

- Device type, operating system, and browser version
- IP address (truncated where applicable) and approximate geographic location
- Pages visited, time spent on pages, and click patterns
- Referral sources (how you arrived at our site) and exit pages
- Interaction with marketing emails and advertisements
- E-commerce activity (products viewed, cart additions, purchases)

This data is used in aggregated or anonymized form for analytics and is linked to identifiable information only where necessary for account functionality or with your consent for marketing purposes. We do not use cookie-derived data to make automated decisions that significantly affect you.

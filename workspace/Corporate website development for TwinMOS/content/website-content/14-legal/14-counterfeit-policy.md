# Anti-Counterfeit Policy

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

Counterfeit electronic components pose serious risks to consumers — including data loss, hardware damage, fire hazards, and exposure of personal information. They also damage the TwinMOS brand and undermine legitimate distributors and retailers who invest in providing genuine products with real warranty support.

TwinMOS Technologies actively combats counterfeiting through supply chain controls, legal enforcement, customs recordation, platform monitoring, and consumer education.

## 1. How to Identify Genuine TwinMOS Products

Genuine TwinMOS products can be verified through the following measures:

### 1.1 Purchase from Authorized Sources
- The most reliable protection is to **purchase only from authorized TwinMOS distributors and retailers**
- A list of authorized distributors by region is available on the [Where to Buy](/where-to-buy/) page
- Be cautious of unusually low prices, unverified marketplaces, or sellers with limited history

### 1.2 Packaging & Labeling
- Genuine TwinMOS packaging features consistent, high-quality printing with accurate typography and color
- Product labels include the TwinMOS logo, model name, part number (PN), and serial number
- Holographic security labels or serial number verification stickers are present on applicable product lines
- Packaging includes the correct certifications (CE, FCC, RoHS, JEDEC, etc.) applicable to the target market

### 1.3 Serial Number Verification
- Serial numbers on genuine TwinMOS products follow a defined format; contact support@twinmos.com to verify a serial number
- Duplicate serial numbers appearing on multiple units are a strong indicator of counterfeiting

### 1.4 Product Registration
- Register your TwinMOS product at [twinmos.com/register](https://www.twinmos.com/register) — our system will flag if a serial number has already been registered by another user, which may indicate a counterfeit
- Registration also provides access to TwinMOS warranty support, which is not available for counterfeit products

### 1.5 Performance Testing
- Genuine TwinMOS products will perform to or above their published specifications
- Use trusted benchmark tools (e.g., CrystalDiskInfo, CrystalDiskMark, MemTest86, or TwinMOS diagnostic utilities) to verify performance
- Significant discrepancies from published specifications may indicate a counterfeit

## 2. Our Anti-Counterfeit Enforcement Program

TwinMOS employs a comprehensive program to detect and take action against counterfeit products:

### 2.1 Trademark Registration & Customs Recordation
- TwinMOS trademarks are registered in key markets worldwide, providing legal basis for enforcement actions
- Our trademarks are recorded with customs authorities in key jurisdictions (including UAE Customs, China Customs, EU customs recordal systems, and US CBP) to enable border detection and seizure of counterfeit shipments
- We provide customs officials with detailed authentication guides and product identification materials

### 2.2 Trademark Watch Services
- We monitor new trademark filings globally for marks that are confusingly similar to TwinMOS marks
- We monitor domain registrations for brand-impersonating domains
- We monitor online channels including major e-commerce platforms for counterfeit listings

### 2.3 E-Commerce Platform Programs
- **Amazon Brand Registry:** TwinMOS is enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry, enabling automated detection and removal of counterfeit listings and unauthorized sellers on Amazon
- **Alibaba / 1688 Anti-Counterfeiting:** TwinMOS actively reports counterfeit listings through Alibaba's intellectual property protection program
- **Other Platforms:** We work with platform trust and safety teams on additional e-commerce marketplaces to remove counterfeit listings and suspended sellers

### 2.4 Industry Cooperation
- We cooperate with industry associations, customs authorities, and law enforcement agencies to conduct raids, seizures, and prosecutions of counterfeit networks
- We participate in information-sharing with other technology brands to identify cross-brand counterfeiting operations

## 3. Reporting Suspected Counterfeits

If you suspect you have purchased or encountered a counterfeit TwinMOS product, please report it promptly:

- **Email:** legal@twinmos.com
- **Subject Line:** "Counterfeit Report — [Product Name / Location]"
- **Information to include:**
  - Product model, part number, and serial number (if visible)
  - Seller name, location, website URL, or marketplace listing URL
  - Purchase receipt or order confirmation (if available)
  - Photographs of the product, packaging, and labeling
  - Description of any performance issues or discrepancies observed

All reports are reviewed and investigated by TwinMOS's legal team. Reporter information will be kept confidential where legally permissible.

## 4. Legal Action

TwinMOS reserves the right to pursue the full range of civil and criminal remedies available under applicable law against counterfeiters, including:
- Civil injunctions to stop production, sale, and distribution
- Claims for damages, including lost profits and unjust enrichment
- Criminal prosecution for trademark infringement, fraud, and consumer protection violations
- Cooperation with law enforcement for raids, seizures, and asset forfeiture
- Customs recordation and border enforcement actions

We cooperate actively with local law enforcement, customs authorities, and prosecutors globally.

## 5. Warranty Exclusion

**Counterfeit TwinMOS products are not covered by TwinMOS warranties or support services.** TwinMOS has no obligation to provide warranty service, repairs, replacements, or refunds for any product that is determined to be counterfeit. If you have purchased a counterfeit product, we encourage you to seek recourse from the seller under applicable consumer protection laws.

## 6. Notice to Distributors & Retailers

Authorized TwinMOS distributors and retailers are strictly prohibited from knowingly purchasing, stocking, selling, or facilitating the sale of counterfeit TwinMOS products. Violation of this prohibition will result in immediate termination of the distribution or reseller agreement and potential legal action.

# Data Deletion Request

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data under applicable privacy laws, including the EU GDPR, UK Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, UAE PDPL, India DPDP Act 2023, Nigeria NDPA 2023, South Africa POPIA, and other regional regulations recognized by TwinMOS.

## What "Deletion" Means

When your deletion request is verified and fulfilled, TwinMOS will delete or permanently anonymize your personal data from our active systems. In some cases, data may be retained in encrypted backup systems for a limited period (typically up to 90 days) before permanent deletion during routine backup purges.

## Before You Submit

Please be aware that deletion is permanent and may affect:

- Active support tickets or open warranty claims — deletion may prevent us from providing further support
- Registered product ownership records — deletion removes warranty registration history
- Subscription and marketing preferences
- Account access to TwinMOS portals and services

In some cases, TwinMOS is required by applicable law to retain certain data even after a deletion request. This includes:
- Financial and transaction records (UAE tax law, applicable accounting requirements — typically 7 years)
- Records required for pending or active legal proceedings
- Contractual records required to fulfil ongoing obligations

Where data must be legally retained, TwinMOS will inform you of the reason and the expected retention period.

## Your Deletion Rights by Jurisdiction

| Jurisdiction | Right | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **EU/EEA (GDPR)** | Right to erasure (Art. 17) | Applies where processing is no longer necessary, consent withdrawn, or processing was unlawful |
| **UK (DUAA 2025)** | Right to erasure equivalent to GDPR Art. 17 | Maintained under UK DUAA 2025 |
| **UAE (PDPL)** | Right to destruction or anonymization | Subject to legal retention requirements |
| **India (DPDP Act 2023)** | Right to erasure | Contact Grievance Officer for expedited handling |
| **Saudi Arabia (PDPL)** | Right to request destruction | Under lawful conditions |
| **Nigeria (NDPA 2023)** | Right to erasure | Where processing no longer lawful or consent withdrawn |
| **South Africa (POPIA)** | Right to request correction or deletion | Where processing not authorized by law |

## Required Information

To process your request, please provide:

1. **Full name** as associated with your TwinMOS account or correspondence
2. **Email address** associated with your account or used in communications with TwinMOS
3. **Country / region of residence** (to identify applicable law and processing location)
4. **Type of data** you wish to delete (e.g., account data, warranty registration, marketing subscriptions, all data)
5. **Reason for deletion request** (optional but helpful)
6. **Confirmation** that you understand that deletion may affect active services and warranty records

A government-issued ID may be required to verify your identity before we process the deletion. We will request this only where necessary and will not retain a copy beyond the verification process.

## How to Submit

**Option 1 — Email Request:**
Send your request to **privacy@twinmos.com** with the subject line "Data Deletion Request — [Your Name]" and include the information listed above.

**Option 2 — Written Request:**
Send a signed written request to:
Privacy Team, TwinMOS Technologies Middle East FZE, C-9, Dubai Airport Freezone (DAFZA), P.O. Box 54278, Dubai, UAE

**India Applicants:**
Contact our **Grievance Officer** at **in-privacy@twinmos.com** — we acknowledge within 48 hours and aim to resolve within **7 working days**.

## Processing Timeline

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgment of receipt | Within **2 business days** |
| Identity verification (if required) | Within **5 business days** of acknowledgment |
| Verification and deletion processing | Within **30 calendar days** of receipt of complete request (or shorter as required by applicable law) |
| Confirmation of deletion | Provided in writing upon completion |

If we require additional time to process your request (e.g., where the request is complex or we have received a large number of requests), we will inform you within 30 days and provide an expected completion date. EU/UK users are entitled to seek a supervisory authority review if the extended period appears unreasonable.

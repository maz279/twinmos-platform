# BIS India Compliance

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

TwinMOS Technologies is committed to compliance with the **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)** requirements for electronic products sold in India. BIS is India's national standards body, established under the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016, and is responsible for product certification, testing, and standardization activities in India.

## Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS)

Under the **Electronics and Information Technology Goods (Requirements for Compulsory Registration) Order** (most recently updated in 2023), a wide range of electronic and IT products must be registered under the BIS **Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS)** before they may be sold in India. The CRS applies to products whether manufactured in India or imported.

Products falling under the CRS must:
- Be tested by a BIS-recognized laboratory to verify compliance with the relevant Indian Standard (IS)
- Be registered under the applicant's CRS Registration Number issued by BIS
- Bear the **BIS Standard Mark** on the product and packaging, along with the Registration Number and R-Number (for imports)
- Meet ongoing compliance requirements, including periodic factory surveillance (for domestic manufacturers) or Import Report filing (for importers)

## Applicable Indian Standards

Key Indian Standards applicable to TwinMOS products include:

| Indian Standard | Title | Applicable Products |
|---|---|---|
| **IS 13252 (Part 1)** | Information Technology Equipment — Safety (based on IEC 62368-1) | SSDs, USB drives, USB hubs, memory modules |
| **IS 616** | Specification for Fuses for Voltages Not Exceeding 1000V AC / 1500V DC (where applicable) | Powered accessories |
| **IS 14165** | Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC) for IT Equipment (based on CISPR 32 / EN 55032) | All electronic devices |
| **CISPR 32 / EN 55035** | EMC Immunity for Multimedia Equipment (referenced in BIS testing) | All electronic devices |

The specific IS standard applicable to each product category is determined by the Mandatory Testing and Inspection Requirements (MTI) notified by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) for the CRS product category.

## TwinMOS BIS Compliance

TwinMOS products sold in India are registered under CRS with BIS-recognized laboratory test reports demonstrating compliance with applicable IS standards. Our India entity, **TwinMOS Technologies**, serves as the local registrant and authorized representative for BIS compliance purposes.

Registered products bear:
- The BIS Standard Mark (ISI mark for applicable standards)
- The BIS Registration Number
- R-Number (import registration number) as required
- Batch/serial number traceability as mandated

## Additional India Regulatory Context

Beyond BIS, TwinMOS products sold in India are also subject to:
- **E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022** — Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) obligations for end-of-life product take-back and recycling (administered by CPCB)
- **Quality Control Orders (QCOs)** — Periodic notifications from MeitY that may require additional BIS compliance for new product categories
- **India Consumer Protection Act, 2019** — Product liability and warranty obligations

Our India entity coordinates with BIS and MeitY on all applicable compliance requirements and maintains up-to-date registration status for all CRS-covered products in our India portfolio.

## Labeling Requirements

BIS-registered TwinMOS products sold in India carry:
- BIS Standard Mark with Registration Number
- MRP (Maximum Retail Price) label where applicable
- Country of Origin declaration
- Importer/local representative name, address, and customer care contact
- All labels in English and/or Hindi as required

# RoHS Compliance Statement

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

TwinMOS Technologies confirms that its products are designed and manufactured to comply with the **EU RoHS Directive 2011/65/EU on the Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment**, as amended by **Delegated Directive (EU) 2015/863** (commonly referred to as **RoHS 3**), which added four phthalate substances to the restricted list effective July 2019 for most product categories.

## Regulatory Scope

| Regulation | Status | Effective Date |
|---|---|---|
| EU Directive 2011/65/EU (RoHS 2) | Implemented | July 21, 2011 |
| Delegated Directive (EU) 2015/863 (RoHS 3) | In force | July 22, 2019 (Category 1–7, 10) |
| UK RoHS Regulations 2012 (as amended) | Applied for UK market | Ongoing |
| China RoHS (GB/T 26572-2011) | Applied for China market | Ongoing |

RoHS 3 added four phthalates to the restricted substances list. As of 2026, **no RoHS 4 proposals are currently in force**; the existing 10-substance restricted list remains stable.

## Restricted Substances

TwinMOS products placed on the EU and UK markets comply with the maximum concentration values (MCVs) for all **10 restricted substances**:

| # | Substance | Symbol | Maximum Concentration Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lead | Pb | 0.1% by weight (1000 ppm) in homogeneous materials |
| 2 | Mercury | Hg | 0.1% (1000 ppm) |
| 3 | Cadmium | Cd | 0.01% (100 ppm) |
| 4 | Hexavalent Chromium | Cr(VI) | 0.1% (1000 ppm) |
| 5 | Polybrominated Biphenyls | PBB | 0.1% (1000 ppm) |
| 6 | Polybrominated Diphenyl Ethers | PBDE | 0.1% (1000 ppm) |
| 7 | Bis(2-ethylhexyl) Phthalate | DEHP | 0.1% (1000 ppm) — added by RoHS 3 |
| 8 | Benzyl Butyl Phthalate | BBP | 0.1% (1000 ppm) — added by RoHS 3 |
| 9 | Dibutyl Phthalate | DBP | 0.1% (1000 ppm) — added by RoHS 3 |
| 10 | Diisobutyl Phthalate | DIBP | 0.1% (1000 ppm) — added by RoHS 3 |

## Product Scope

This statement applies to all TwinMOS electrical and electronic products placed on the EU, EEA, UK, and other RoHS-equivalent markets, including:
- DRAM memory modules (DDR3, DDR4, DDR5)
- NVMe and SATA solid-state drives (M.2 and 2.5" form factors)
- Portable SSDs and external HDDs
- USB flash drives and memory cards
- USB hubs and peripheral accessories
- Product packaging (where applicable under RoHS)

**Exemptions:** Some applications of lead-free solder in specific components may benefit from specific RoHS Annex III or IV exemptions where technically unavoidable alternatives do not exist. Such exemptions are identified and documented in our technical files.

## Technical Documentation

TwinMOS maintains RoHS technical files at our headquarters (Dubai, UAE) and with our authorized EU Responsible Person. These files include:
- Bill of Materials (BOM) with substance declarations
- Material Declaration Forms (MDFs) from component suppliers
- Third-party RoHS test reports from accredited laboratories
- Internal substance screening records

## Supplier Obligations

We require all component suppliers to:
- Provide substance declarations or material declarations conforming to IPC/JEDEC J-STD-709B (Full Material Declaration) or IEC 62474 standards
- Submit third-party RoHS test reports for any new component or material
- Notify TwinMOS of any changes in materials or processes that may affect RoHS compliance
- Maintain traceability records linking substance declarations to production lots

## Labeling

TwinMOS RoHS-compliant products bear the applicable compliance mark on the product or packaging as required by applicable law. Products sold in China carry the China RoHS environmental use period label in accordance with SJ/T 11364.

# Customer Feedback

Your feedback is invaluable. Whether you have praise, concerns, or ideas, we want to hear from you. Every submission is read by our team and used to drive continuous improvement across our products, services, and digital experience.

## What to Share

- **Product Performance** — Speed, reliability, compatibility, thermal behavior, or durability observations
- **Unboxing & User Experience** — Packaging quality, installation ease, documentation clarity
- **Website & Digital Experience** — Navigation, content accuracy, search functionality, or mobile usability
- **Support & Service Quality** — Responsiveness, helpfulness, and resolution effectiveness
- **Sales Experience** — Pricing transparency, order process, delivery, and communication
- **New Product Ideas** — Features, form factors, or product categories you'd like to see
- **Sustainability & CSR** — Thoughts on our environmental and community initiatives

## Form Fields

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Feedback Category | Yes | Product, Website, Support, Sales, Idea, Other |
| Product Model | No | If feedback is product-specific |
| Rating | No | 1–5 star rating for the experience |
| Feedback Details | Yes | Your detailed feedback (min. 50 characters) |
| Full Name | No | Optional; required if you want a response |
| Email Address | No | Optional; required if you want a response |
| Country / Region | No | Helps us understand regional trends |
| Would You Recommend? | No | Yes / No / Maybe |
| File Attachments | No | Screenshots or photos (max 3 files, 5MB each) |

## Your Privacy

- Feedback can be submitted **anonymously** — no contact details required
- If you provide contact information, we may reach out for clarification or to share how your feedback was actioned
- Personal data is handled in accordance with our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/)
- We never share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes

## How We Use Your Feedback

| Feedback Type | How We Action It |
|---|---|
| Product issues | Forwarded to R&D and quality assurance teams |
| Website suggestions | Reviewed by UX and content teams monthly |
| Support complaints | Escalated to customer service management |
| New product ideas | Shared with product strategy and planning |
| Positive testimonials | May be featured on our website (with permission) |

# Technical Support

Experiencing issues with a TwinMOS product? Our technical support team is here to help with troubleshooting, compatibility questions, firmware updates, and warranty guidance. We support our full product range including DDR4/DDR5 memory modules, PCIe Gen 3/4/5 NVMe SSDs, SATA SSDs, portable storage, and USB flash drives.

## Before You Submit a Ticket

Save time by trying these steps first:

1. **Check our [Knowledge Base](/support/kb-articles/)** — Articles cover common issues like RAM compatibility, SSD not detected, XMP/EXPO setup, and more.
2. **Verify firmware and drivers** — Ensure your motherboard BIOS, SSD firmware, and OS drivers are up to date.
3. **Test on a second system** — If possible, test the product on another PC to isolate the issue.
4. **Review our [Warranty Policy](/support/warranty-policy/)** — Confirm your product is within the warranty period and understand coverage terms.
5. **Search our [FAQ](/support/faq/)** — Quick answers to the most common questions.

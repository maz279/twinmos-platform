# Thank You — We've Received Your Inquiry

Your submission has been successfully received and logged in our system. A confirmation email has been sent to the address you provided. Our team is already reviewing your message and will respond according to the timeline below.

## What Happens Next?

| Inquiry Type | Typical Response Time | What to Expect |
|---|---|---|
| **General Inquiry** | 1–2 business days | A personalized response from the appropriate department |
| **Sales Inquiry** | 1–2 business days | Contact from your regional sales manager with pricing or next steps |
| **Technical Support** | Within 1 business day | Initial diagnosis, troubleshooting steps, or RMA instructions |
| **Distributor Application** | 5 business days | Partnership team review with possible follow-up questions |
| **OEM / ODM Inquiry** | 3–5 business days | Feasibility assessment and proposal outline |
| **Request a Quote** | 2–3 business days | Detailed quotation PDF with terms and conditions |
| **Media & Press** | Within 1 business day | Confirmation and next steps from our communications team |
| **Warranty Claim** | 1–2 business days | Coverage verification and RMA authorization (if approved) |
| **Customer Feedback** | 3–5 business days | Acknowledgment and information on how your feedback will be used |

## Reference Information

- **Your submission is tracked** — If you provided an email, you will receive updates automatically
- **Add us to your safe sender list** — Emails from @twinmos.com may land in spam folders
- **Quote requests** — Formal quotations are valid for 30 days from issue date
- **Warranty claims** — Do not ship products until you receive an approved RMA number

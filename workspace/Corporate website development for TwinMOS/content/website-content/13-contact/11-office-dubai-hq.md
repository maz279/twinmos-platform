## Role & Responsibilities

Our Dubai headquarters serves as the primary administrative, logistics, and sales hub for the Middle East, Africa, South Asia, and CIS regions. Key functions include:

- **Global Sales & Distribution Management** — Overseeing regional sales teams and distributor networks
- **Logistics & Supply Chain** — Central warehousing and shipping coordination
- **Legal & Compliance** — Corporate governance, contracts, and regulatory matters
- **Finance & Administration** — Invoicing, payments, and corporate operations
- **Customer Support Coordination** — Escalation hub for technical and warranty issues
- **Marketing & Communications** — Regional campaigns, events, and PR

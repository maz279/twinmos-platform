# Warranty FAQ

## How long is the warranty on TwinMOS products?

- DRAM modules: Limited Lifetime
- CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSD: 5 Years
- Xtreme Gen4 / Alpha Pro Gen3 NVMe SSDs: 3 Years
- Hyper H2 Ultra SATA SSD: 3 Years
- ELITE Drive Pro Portable SSD: 1 Year
- USB Flash Drives: 1–3 Years (varies by model)

## Do I need to register my product for warranty coverage?

No, warranty coverage is based on proof of purchase. However, registration expedites RMA processing and provides a digital record.

## Is the warranty transferable?

The warranty is generally limited to the original purchaser, unless local consumer protection laws state otherwise.

## What does the warranty cover?

Defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. It does not cover physical damage, misuse, overvoltage, or unauthorized modifications.

## What is not covered?

- Physical damage, cracks, burns, or liquid damage
- Damage from improper installation
- Overclocking beyond rated specifications without appropriate profiles
- Counterfeit or unauthorized products
- Data loss of any kind

## How do I claim warranty?

Contact support@twinmos.com with your serial number and proof of purchase. You may be instructed to submit an RMA request.

## Does enabling XMP or EXPO void the warranty?

No. Running memory within its advertised XMP 3.0 or EXPO profile does not void the warranty. Running beyond specified voltage or frequency limits may void coverage.

## What if I bought from an unauthorized seller?

Products from unauthorized sellers may not be eligible for warranty service. Use our [Serial Number Check](/support/serial-number-check/) to verify authenticity.

## Can I get a refund under warranty?

Warranty service provides repair or replacement at TwinMOS's discretion. Refunds are subject to the retailer's return policy.

## How long does an RMA take?

Typically 5–10 business days after the defective product is received at the service center, plus shipping time. See our [Support SLA](/support/sla/) for response-time details.

## What is proof of purchase?

Valid proof of purchase includes:
- Original sales receipt (physical or electronic)
- Invoice from an authorized retailer
- Order confirmation email with date and product details
- Bank or credit card statement showing the transaction

The document must clearly show the purchase date, product description, and seller information.

## Can I get a replacement if my product is discontinued?

If your exact model is discontinued, TwinMOS will provide a comparable replacement with equal or greater specifications. You will be notified of the replacement model before shipment.

## Does the warranty cover shipping costs?

- **Customer to Service Center:** The customer is responsible for shipping the defective product to the designated service center.
- **Service Center to Customer:** TwinMOS covers return shipping of the replacement product.

Some regions may have different arrangements. Check [Warranty by Region](/support/warranty-by-region/) for details.

## What happens if I modify my product?

- **Removing heat spreaders:** Voided if damage occurs during removal.
- **Custom water cooling:** Not covered if leaks cause damage.
- **Firmware modifications:** Voided if unofficial firmware is installed.
- **Standard XMP/EXPO use:** Fully covered.

## Can I transfer my warranty to someone else?

The TwinMOS warranty is generally non-transferable and applies to the original purchaser only. However, some regional consumer protection laws may grant warranty rights to subsequent owners.

## What if I live in a country without a service center?

TwinMOS serves customers globally. If there is no local service center:
1. Contact support@twinmos.com for guidance.
2. You may be directed to ship to the nearest regional center.
3. International shipping costs for RMA are typically the customer's responsibility.

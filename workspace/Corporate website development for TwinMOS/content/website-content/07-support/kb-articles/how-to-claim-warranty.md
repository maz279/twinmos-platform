# How to Claim Warranty

If your TwinMOS product fails due to a manufacturing defect within the warranty period, you are entitled to a repair or replacement. This guide explains the entire warranty claim process.

## Warranty Periods

| Product Category | Warranty Period | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| DRAM Modules | Limited Lifetime | Original purchaser only |
| CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe | 5 Years | Subject to TBW limits |
| Xtreme Gen4 / Alpha Pro Gen3 NVMe | 5 Years | Subject to TBW limits |
| Hyper H2 Ultra SATA | 3 Years | Subject to TBW limits |
| ELITE Drive Pro Portable | 1 Year | Varies by region |
| USB Flash Drives | 1–3 Years | Varies by model and region |

## What You Need

Before starting a claim, gather the following:
- Product model name
- Serial number (found on the product label)
- Proof of purchase (receipt, invoice, or order confirmation)
- Description of the defect or issue
- Your contact and shipping information

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Verify Warranty Status
Use the [Warranty Lookup](/support/warranty-lookup/) tool to confirm your product is within the warranty period.

### Step 2: Contact Support
Email support@twinmos.com or call +971-4-2996421/22. Provide:
- Serial number
- Proof of purchase
- Issue description
- Troubleshooting steps you have already tried

### Step 3: Receive RMA Authorization
If the support team determines the issue is covered under warranty, they will issue an RMA number and provide shipping instructions.

### Step 4: Ship the Product
Package the product securely in anti-static wrapping. Include a printed copy of the RMA authorization. Ship to the designated service center at your expense (unless local law requires otherwise).

### Step 5: Inspection and Replacement
The service center will inspect the product. If the defect is confirmed, a replacement or repaired unit will be shipped to you at TwinMOS's expense.

## What Is Not Covered

- Physical damage, liquid damage, or burns
- Damage from overvoltage or improper installation
- Products purchased from unauthorized sellers
- Counterfeit products
- Data loss

## Tips for a Faster Claim

- Register your product at the time of purchase.
- Keep your proof of purchase in a safe place.
- Be specific when describing the issue.
- Respond promptly to any follow-up questions from support.

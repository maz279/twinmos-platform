# Warranty by Region

TwinMOS honors warranty claims globally; however, specific terms, shipping procedures, and consumer rights may vary by region due to local laws and regulations. The following outlines general regional guidelines.

> **Select your region below** to view specific terms, service center information, and shipping details.

## Global Warranty Summary

| Product Category | Standard Warranty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| DRAM Modules | Limited Lifetime | Original purchaser only |
| CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSD | 5 Years | Subject to TBW endurance limits |
| Gen4/Gen3 NVMe SSDs | 3 Years | Subject to TBW endurance limits |
| SATA SSDs | 3 Years | Subject to TBW endurance limits |
| Portable Storage | 1 Year | Excludes physical damage |
| USB Flash Drives | 1–3 Years | Varies by product line |

## Europe

Consumers in the European Union benefit from mandatory consumer protection laws, including a minimum two-year conformity guarantee under EU Directive 1999/44/EC. TwinMOS warranty terms apply in addition to these statutory rights.

**Statutory Rights:**
- **Minimum Conformity Guarantee:** 2 years from date of delivery (EU Directive 1999/44/EC)
- **Extended Coverage:** TwinMOS product category warranties apply beyond the statutory minimum where longer
- **Reverse Burden of Proof:** For the first 6 months, the seller must prove the product was not defective at delivery

**Service:**
- **Local Representatives:** Available in select EU markets
- **Contact:** support@twinmos.com for local service center details
- **Business Hours:** Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 CET (UTC+1)

**Key Markets:**
Germany, France, UK, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Poland, Romania, Belgium, Greece, Portugal, Czech Republic

**Shipping:**
- EU customers may be directed to a regional EU hub or local partner
- Shipping costs governed by EU consumer protection regulations

## Americas

Products sold in North, Central, and South America through authorized channels are covered under standard TwinMOS warranty terms. Local distributors may offer additional support services.

**Key Markets:**
USA, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Chile, Peru

**Service:**
- **Primary Contact:** support@twinmos.com
- **Regional Partners:** Support provided via authorized local partners and distributors
- **Business Hours:** Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 EST (UTC-5)

**Shipping:**
- Customer responsible for inbound shipping to designated regional service center
- TwinMOS covers return shipping
- Service center location assigned based on customer location

## Local Consumer Protection Laws

| Region | Key Law / Directive | Consumer Rights |
|---|---|---|
| European Union | EU Directive 1999/44/EC | Minimum 2-year conformity guarantee |
| United States | Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act | Written warranty must be clear; implied warranties vary by state |
| United Kingdom | Consumer Rights Act 2015 | 6 years in England/Wales; 5 years in Scotland |
| Australia | Australian Consumer Law | Statutory guarantees for acceptable quality |
| UAE | Consumer Protection Law | 2-year warranty on electronics |

## Important Notes

- Warranty coverage is valid only in the region where the product was purchased through an authorized channel, unless otherwise stated.
- Gray-market or unauthorized imports may not be eligible for warranty service.
- Always retain your proof of purchase for warranty claims in any region.
- Local consumer protection laws may grant rights that supersede portions of the TwinMOS warranty policy.

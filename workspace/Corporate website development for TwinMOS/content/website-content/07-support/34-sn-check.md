# Serial Number Authenticity Check

Use this tool to verify that your TwinMOS product is genuine. Counterfeit products are not covered under warranty and may pose security and reliability risks.

## Why Verify?

- **Warranty Coverage:** Only genuine TwinMOS products are eligible for warranty service.
- **Performance:** Counterfeit products often fail to meet advertised specifications.
- **Data Security:** Fake storage devices may contain modified firmware that compromises data integrity.

## How to Check

### Step 1: Locate Your Serial Number
The serial number is printed on a label on the product itself and on the retail packaging. It is typically 12–16 alphanumeric characters.

| Product Type | Serial Number Location |
|---|---|
| DRAM Modules | Label on the heat spreader or PCB sticker |
| M.2 NVMe SSDs | Label on the top or bottom of the drive |
| 2.5-inch SATA SSDs | Label on the top casing |
| Portable Storage | Label on the enclosure or back panel |

## What If My Product Is Counterfeit?

If you suspect your product is counterfeit:
1. Stop using it immediately, especially for critical data.
2. Contact the seller for a refund.
3. Report the issue to support@twinmos.com with photos of the product and packaging.
4. Review our [Anti-Counterfeit Policy](/support/counterfeit-policy/) for more information.

## Tips for Buying Genuine Products

- Purchase from authorized TwinMOS distributors and retailers.
- Be wary of prices significantly below market rate.
- Check packaging quality — counterfeits often have poor print quality or missing holograms.
- Verify the serial number before leaving a review or discarding packaging.

# TwinMOS Egypt

Egypt is a major North African market for TwinMOS, supported by a robust network of authorized distributors serving Cairo, Alexandria, and cities across the country. As one of the largest economies in Africa and the Middle East, Egypt offers significant opportunities across consumer retail, system integration, and enterprise IT sectors.

> **Regional Features:** EGP pricing references, Arabic RTL language support (Phase 2), multi-distributor network, and Egyptian import regulation compliance.
> **Currency Disclaimer:** Prices displayed in EGP are indicative only. Contact local retailers or authorized distributors for actual pricing.

TwinMOS distributors in Egypt supply memory and storage products to:

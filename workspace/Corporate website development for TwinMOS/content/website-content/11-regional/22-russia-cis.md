## EAC Certification

TwinMOS products carry **EAC (Eurasian Conformity)** certification, confirming compliance with the technical regulations of the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU). This certification covers:

- Electromagnetic compatibility
- Safety of low-voltage equipment
- Restriction of hazardous substances
- Energy efficiency requirements for applicable product categories

In addition to EAC certification, TwinMOS products meet:

- **TR CU 004/2011** — safety of low-voltage equipment
- **TR CU 020/2011** — electromagnetic compatibility
- **TR EAEU 037/2016** — restriction of hazardous substances (RoHS equivalent)
- **GOST R standards** — Russian national standards where applicable
- **EAEU customs union** — unified tariff and regulatory framework

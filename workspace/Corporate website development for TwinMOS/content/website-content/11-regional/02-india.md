## Compliance & Certifications

### BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards)

TwinMOS products destined for the Indian market are planned for **BIS compliance certification** as part of Phase 2 market readiness.

> **BIS certification planned for Phase 2.**

All DRAM and SSD products meet JEDEC standards and are manufactured under certified ISO 9001 quality frameworks. TwinMOS is committed to aligning with Indian regulatory requirements as market operations scale, including:

- **BIS Registration** — planned for Phase 2 (Months 6–9)
- **WPC (Wireless Planning and Coordination)** — where applicable for wireless-enabled products
- **India DPDP Act 2023** — consent management for Indian users' personal data
- **LMPC (Legal Metrology Packaged Commodities)** — labeling compliance where required

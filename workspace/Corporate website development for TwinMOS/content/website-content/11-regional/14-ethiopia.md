# TwinMOS Ethiopia

Ethiopia is an emerging East African market for TwinMOS memory and storage solutions, served by a network of authorized distributors in Addis Ababa. With a population exceeding 120 million and rapid digitalization across government, education, and private sectors, Ethiopia presents long-term growth potential for TwinMOS products.

> **Regional Features:** ETB pricing references, English language support, multi-distributor network in Addis Ababa, ECAE compliance, and East Africa expansion market.

# TwinMOS Nigeria

Nigeria is a key West African market for TwinMOS, supported by authorized distributors serving Lagos and cities across the country. As Africa's largest economy and most populous nation, Nigeria represents a major growth opportunity for memory and storage products across consumer, corporate, and government sectors.

> **Regional Features:** NGN pricing references, English language support, multi-distributor network in Lagos, SON compliance, and West Africa regional expansion hub.

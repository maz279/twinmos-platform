## Compliance

TwinMOS respects the **Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)** of Saudi Arabia. All customer data handling, warranty registration, and partner onboarding processes are aligned with PDPL requirements.

TwinMOS products also comply with:

- **SASO standards** — Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization
- **CE marking** — European conformity baseline
- **RoHS** — restriction of hazardous substances
- **FCC** — electromagnetic compatibility standards
- **Saudi Energy Efficiency requirements** — where applicable

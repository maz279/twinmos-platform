# TwinMOS North America

TwinMOS maintains a selective distribution strategy in North America, focusing on targeted channel partnerships rather than mass-market retail presence.

## Distribution Strategy

North America is served through limited and selective distribution channels. This approach ensures that TwinMOS products reach customers through knowledgeable partners who can provide appropriate technical support and product guidance.

## Support

Warranty and technical support for North America are coordinated through authorized partners with escalation to TwinMOS headquarters.

North American customers benefit from:

- English-language technical support
- Warranty per the official TwinMOS Warranty Policy: Lifetime on memory modules; 5 years on NVMe SSDs, flash cards and USB flash drives; 3 years on SATA SSDs; 1 year on computer accessories
- RMA processing through authorized partner locations
- Product authenticity verification

## Compliance

TwinMOS products for North America comply with:

- **FCC** — Federal Communications Commission certification (Part 15 Class B)
- **UL/cUL** — Underwriters Laboratories safety certifications where applicable
- **RoHS** — restriction of hazardous substances
- **CE** — European conformity baseline (for cross-border compatibility)
- **California Prop 65** — Safe Drinking Water and Toxic Enforcement Act compliance
- **Energy Star** — energy efficiency alignment where applicable
- **Industry Canada** — ICES-003 compliance for Canadian market

## Product Availability

North American customers can access select TwinMOS products including:

- DDR4 and DDR5 memory modules for desktop, laptop, and server
- NVMe and SATA SSDs for consumer and professional use
- Portable storage solutions for mobile professionals

- Gaming-focused VOLTX and Thunder GX series

## Market Segments

TwinMOS targets selective segments in North America:

- **System integrators** - custom PC builders and boutique shops
- **Enterprise procurement** - bulk memory for workstations and servers
- **Gaming & esports** - high-performance DDR5 and NVMe solutions
- **Creative professionals** - video editing, 3D rendering, and content creation

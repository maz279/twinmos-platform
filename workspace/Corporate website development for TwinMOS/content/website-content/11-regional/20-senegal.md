# TwinMOS Senegal

Senegal is a West African market for TwinMOS, with opportunities in Dakar and beyond. As one of the most stable democracies in West Africa and a major Francophone economic hub, Senegal offers strategic access to the West African Economic and Monetary Union (UEMOA) region.

## Market Opportunity

Senegal's technology sector presents opportunities in:

- **Government & public sector** — digital transformation and e-administration initiatives
- **Banking & finance** — Dakar's role as a regional financial center
- **Education** — university and school IT infrastructure upgrades
- **Telecommunications** — expanding 4G/5G driving demand for data center equipment
- **Transport & logistics** — Port of Dakar modernization and trade corridor digitization

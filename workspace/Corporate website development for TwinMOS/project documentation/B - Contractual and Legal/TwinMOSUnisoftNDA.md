## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 "Confidential Information"

"**Confidential Information**" means any and all non-public, proprietary, or sensitive information disclosed by the Disclosing Party (whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means) to the Receiving Party, including but not limited to:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Business Information** | Business plans, strategies, financial data, revenue figures, pricing, budgets, forecasts, market analysis, customer lists, supplier information |
| **Technical Information** | Source code, algorithms, technical architectures, database schemas, API specifications, security configurations, system designs, network diagrams |
| **Product Information** | Product specifications, SKU databases, manufacturing processes, quality control data, roadmaps, unreleased product plans, patent applications |
| **Marketing Information** | Marketing strategies, campaign plans, brand guidelines, customer segmentation, analytics data, conversion metrics |
| **Legal and Compliance** | Regulatory filings, compliance audits, legal opinions, litigation strategies, data protection impact assessments |
| **Personnel Information** | Employee records, compensation data, organizational structures, personnel policies |
| **Third-Party Information** | Information received from third parties under confidentiality obligations |
| **Project-Specific Information** | All information related to the TwinMOS website rebuild project, including but not limited to: BRD, RFP, URD, Technology Stack, Implementation Strategy, content maps, design mockups, user research, competitor analysis |

### 2.2 "Representatives"

"**Representatives**" means the Receiving Party's directors, officers, employees, contractors, consultants, advisors, agents, and subcontractors who have a need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose.

### 2.3 "Disclosure Period"

"**Disclosure Period**" means the period during which Confidential Information is disclosed under this Agreement, beginning on the Effective Date and continuing until the earlier of: (a) the termination of the MSA; or (b) the Parties mutually agree in writing to end the Disclosure Period.

## 3. Obligations of Confidentiality

### 3.1 Duty of Confidentiality

The Receiving Party agrees to:

(a) **Hold in Strict Confidence:** Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not disclose it to any third party except as permitted under Section 4.

(b) **Use Solely for Purpose:** Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and not for any other purpose without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.

(c) **Protect with Reasonable Care:** Protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care that the Receiving Party uses to protect its own confidential information of a like nature, but in no event less than reasonable care.

(d) **Limit Access:** Limit access to Confidential Information to those Representatives who have a strict need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those contained in this Agreement.

(e) **Notify of Breach:** Promptly notify the Disclosing Party upon discovery of any unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of Confidential Information.

(f) **No Reverse Engineering:** Not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive the source code, underlying ideas, algorithms, or trade secrets from any Confidential Information.

### 3.2 Specific Protections for TwinMOS Data

Given the nature of the Project, Unisoft acknowledges that it will have access to the following categories of particularly sensitive information:

| Category | Sensitivity Level | Specific Protections |
|----------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Product SKU database and specifications | High | Encrypted storage; access logging; no external sharing |
| Distributor/retailer contact database | High | GDPR/UAE PDPL compliance; encrypted at rest; access controls |
| Customer inquiry data | High | Data minimization; encrypted transmission; no retention beyond project need |
| Competitor analysis and market intelligence | Medium | Need-to-know access; no commercial use |
| Financial and pricing information | High | Segregated storage; executive approval for access |
| Source code and technical architecture | High | Secure repository; MFA; audit logging |
| Design assets and brand materials | Medium | Watermarking; version control; access tracking |

### 3.3 Security Measures

The Receiving Party shall implement and maintain the following minimum security measures:

(a) **Physical Security:** Secure office premises with controlled access; lockable storage for physical documents

(b) **Digital Security:**
   - Encryption of Confidential Information in transit (TLS 1.3 minimum)
   - Encryption of Confidential Information at rest (AES-256)
   - Multi-factor authentication for all systems containing Confidential Information
   - Regular security patching and vulnerability management
   - Endpoint protection (antivirus, EDR) on all devices accessing Confidential Information

(c) **Access Controls:**
   - Role-based access control (RBAC) limiting access to need-to-know
   - Unique user accounts (no shared credentials)
   - Regular access reviews (quarterly)
   - Immediate revocation of access upon termination of employment/engagement

(d) **Data Handling:**
   - No storage of Confidential Information on personal devices without approval
   - No transmission of Confidential Information via unsecured channels (e.g., personal email, WhatsApp)
   - Secure disposal of Confidential Information when no longer needed

## 4. Permitted Disclosures

### 4.1 Exceptions to Confidentiality

The obligations in Section 3 shall not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

(a) **Publicly Available:** Was already publicly available at the time of disclosure, or became publicly available after disclosure through no breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party.

(b) **Already Known:** Was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, as evidenced by written records.

(c) **Independently Developed:** Was independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, as evidenced by written records.

(d) **Rightfully Received:** Was rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without restriction and without breach of any confidentiality obligation.

### 4.2 Required Disclosures

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by:

(a) Applicable law, regulation, or court order
(b) Governmental authority with competent jurisdiction

Provided that the Receiving Party:

(i) Gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the required disclosure (to the extent legally permitted)
(ii) Cooperates with the Disclosing Party's efforts to obtain a protective order or confidential treatment
(iii) Discloses only the minimum amount of Confidential Information legally required

### 4.3 Professional Advisors

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its legal, financial, and insurance advisors ("**Professional Advisors**") who are bound by professional confidentiality obligations, provided:

(a) The disclosure is limited to what is necessary for the Professional Advisor to perform their services
(b) The Receiving Party informs the Professional Advisor of the confidential nature of the information
(c) The Receiving Party remains liable for any breach by the Professional Advisor

## 5. Return and Destruction of Confidential Information

### 5.1 Return or Destruction

Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, or upon termination or expiry of the MSA (whichever is earlier), the Receiving Party shall:

(a) Promptly return to the Disclosing Party all tangible materials containing Confidential Information
(b) Destroy all electronic copies of Confidential Information in its possession or control
(c) Certify such return and destruction in writing within **fourteen (14) days**

### 5.2 Retained Copies

The Receiving Party may retain:

(a) One archival copy of Confidential Information for legal/compliance purposes, provided it remains subject to this Agreement
(b) Confidential Information stored in automated backup systems, provided it is not readily accessible and remains subject to this Agreement

### 5.3 Continuing Obligations

The obligations of confidentiality shall survive the return or destruction of Confidential Information.

## 8. Term and Survival

### 8.1 Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue until the earlier of:

(a) The termination or expiry of the MSA
(b) Mutual written agreement to terminate
(c) Five (5) years from the Effective Date

### 8.2 Survival

The obligations of confidentiality under this Agreement shall survive termination for a period of **five (5) years** from the date of disclosure of the applicable Confidential Information. For trade secrets, the obligations shall survive for as long as the information remains a trade secret under Applicable Law.

## 2. Definitions and Interpretation

### 2.1 Definitions

Unless otherwise defined herein, capitalized terms shall have the meanings assigned to them in the MSA or Applicable Data Protection Laws. The following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **"Applicable Data Protection Laws"** | All laws and regulations applicable to the processing of Personal Data, including: (a) the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection (PDPL); (b) Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR); (c) the India Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act); (d) the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL); (e) any implementing legislation, regulatory guidance, and codes of practice issued thereunder. |
| **"Data Breach"** | A breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data transmitted, stored, or otherwise processed. |
| **"Data Subject"** | An identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates. |
| **"Personal Data"** | Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, including but not limited to: name, email address, phone number, postal address, IP address, cookie identifiers, device information, location data, and online identifiers. |
| **"Processing"** | Any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, dissemination, restriction, erasure, or destruction. |
| **"Special Categories of Personal Data"** | Personal Data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric data, health data, or data concerning sex life or sexual orientation. |
| **"Standard Contractual Clauses" or "SCCs"** | The standard contractual clauses for the transfer of Personal Data to third countries approved by the European Commission, as may be updated from time to time. |
| **"Supervisory Authority"** | The independent public authority responsible for monitoring the application of Data Protection Laws in a relevant jurisdiction. |

### 2.2 Interpretation

(a) References to "Articles" or "Sections" of GDPR, UAE PDPL, India DPDP Act, or KSA PDPL are for convenience and do not limit the scope of obligations under other Applicable Data Protection Laws.  
(b) This DPA is drafted to satisfy the requirements of multiple jurisdictions. Where provisions conflict, the most protective standard shall apply.  
(c) Terms such as "Controller," "Processor," "Data Subject," "Personal Data," and "Processing" shall have the meanings given in the GDPR, unless the context requires otherwise.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

### 3.1 Controller-Processor Relationship

(a) **TwinMOS as Controller:** TwinMOS is the Data Controller in respect of Personal Data processed in connection with the TwinMOS website and related services. TwinMOS determines the purposes and means of Processing.

(b) **Unisoft as Processor:** Unisoft is the Data Processor in respect of Personal Data that it processes on behalf of TwinMOS in the course of providing the Services. Unisoft processes Personal Data only on documented instructions from TwinMOS.

(c) **Joint Controller Arrangements:** If and to the extent the Parties act as joint controllers for any processing activity, the Parties shall determine their respective responsibilities by mutual agreement in writing.

### 3.2 Processor Obligations

Unisoft shall:

(a) Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from TwinMOS, including with regard to transfers of Personal Data to third countries or international organizations, unless required to do so by Applicable Data Protection Laws

(b) Ensure that persons authorized to process Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality

(c) Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk

(d) Not engage another processor (subprocessor) without prior specific or general written authorization from TwinMOS

(e) Assist TwinMOS in ensuring compliance with obligations relating to Data Subject rights, data breach notification, data protection impact assessments, and prior consultation with Supervisory Authorities

(f) At TwinMOS's choice, delete or return all Personal Data after the end of the provision of services, and delete existing copies unless storage is required by Applicable Data Protection Laws

(g) Make available to TwinMOS all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by TwinMOS or an auditor mandated by TwinMOS

### 3.3 Controller Obligations

TwinMOS shall:

(a) Provide Unisoft with lawful, documented instructions for Processing

(b) Ensure it has a valid legal basis for Processing under Applicable Data Protection Laws

(c) Provide Data Subjects with appropriate privacy notices

(d) Respond to Data Subject requests and complaints, with reasonable assistance from Unisoft

(e) Notify Unisoft of any data protection complaints or regulatory inquiries affecting the Processing

## 5. Data Subject Rights

### 5.1 Assistance with Data Subject Requests

Unisoft shall assist TwinMOS in responding to Data Subject requests under Applicable Data Protection Laws, including:

| Right | GDPR Reference | Unisoft Assistance |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|
| Right to access | Article 15 | Extract and provide relevant data; verify identity |
| Right to rectification | Article 16 | Update records in systems under Unisoft's control |
| Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") | Article 17 | Delete data from all systems and backups |
| Right to restriction of processing | Article 18 | Suspend processing as instructed |
| Right to data portability | Article 20 | Export data in structured, machine-readable format |
| Right to object | Article 21 | Cease processing as instructed |
| Rights related to automated decision-making | Article 22 | Provide information on logic and consequences |

### 5.2 Response Timeframes

Unisoft shall:

(a) Acknowledge receipt of a Data Subject request within **24 hours**
(b) Provide the requested assistance within **five (5) business days**
(c) Notify TwinMOS immediately if a request cannot be fulfilled within the timeframe

### 5.3 Data Deletion Request Form

TwinMOS shall provide a Data Deletion Request form on the website (per BRD Section 16 / URD UR-16.1.5). Unisoft shall ensure that:

(a) Submitted requests are logged and tracked
(b) Deletion is executed within **thirty (30) days** of verified request
(c) Confirmation is sent to the Data Subject
(d) Exceptions (legal retention requirements) are documented

## 8. Data Breach Notification

### 8.1 Notification Obligations

In the event of a Data Breach, Unisoft shall:

(a) Notify TwinMOS without undue delay and in any case within **twenty-four (24) hours** of becoming aware of the breach

(b) Provide the following information in the initial notification:
   - Nature of the breach (categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected)
   - Likely consequences of the breach
   - Measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate potential harm
   - Contact details for more information

(c) Provide additional information as it becomes available

(d) Cooperate with TwinMOS in investigating the breach and preparing notifications to Supervisory Authorities and Data Subjects

### 8.2 TwinMOS Notification Responsibilities

TwinMOS is responsible for:

(a) Assessing whether the breach is reportable to Supervisory Authorities
(b) Notifying the relevant Supervisory Authority within **seventy-two (72) hours** where required
(c) Notifying affected Data Subjects where the breach is likely to result in high risk to their rights and freedoms

### 8.3 Breach Register

Unisoft shall maintain a register of all Data Breaches, including:

(a) Date and time of discovery
(b) Description of the breach
(c) Categories of Personal Data affected
(d) Number of Data Subjects affected (approximate)
(e) Root cause analysis
(f) Remediation actions taken
(g) Lessons learned and preventive measures implemented

## 9. Data Transfers and Residency

### 9.1 Data Residency Requirements

Personal Data shall be stored and processed in accordance with the following residency requirements:

| Data Subject Jurisdiction | Primary Hosting Location | Backup Location | Justification |
|---------------------------|-------------------------|-----------------|---------------|
| EU/EEA | Hetzner Falkenstein (Germany) | Backblaze EU | GDPR data residency |
| UAE / Middle East | Hetzner Falkenstein (Germany) | Backblaze EU | UAE PDPL compliance; EU adequacy |
| India | Hetzner Helsinki (Finland) | Backblaze EU | India DPDP Act; adequate protection |
| KSA / GCC | Hetzner Falkenstein (Germany) | Backblaze EU | KSA PDPL; EU adequacy decision |
| United States | Hetzner Ashburn (US) | Backblaze US | Local processing for US visitors |
| Other regions | Hetzner Falkenstein (Germany) | Backblaze EU | Default EU-hosted |

### 9.2 International Transfers

Where Personal Data is transferred outside the jurisdiction of the Data Subjects:

(a) **GDPR Transfers:** Transfers to countries without an adequacy decision shall be subject to Standard Contractual Clauses (2021/914) or other approved transfer mechanism

(b) **UAE PDPL Transfers:** Transfers outside the UAE shall comply with Article 15 of the UAE PDPL, ensuring adequate protection levels

(c) **India DPDP Act Transfers:** Cross-border transfers shall comply with Section 16 of the India DPDP Act and any subsequent government notifications

(d) **KSA PDPL Transfers:** Transfers outside KSA shall comply with Article 16 of the KSA PDPL

### 9.3 Transfer Safeguards

Unisoft shall implement the following safeguards for international transfers:

(a) Execution of Standard Contractual Clauses with subprocessors where required
(b) Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.3)
(c) Encryption of data at rest (AES-256)
(d) Data minimization (only transfer necessary data)
(e) Regular review of transfer mechanisms for compliance

## 10. Audit and Compliance

### 10.1 Audit Rights

TwinMOS shall have the right to audit Unisoft's compliance with this DPA:

(a) **Annual Audit:** Once per calendar year with thirty (30) days' notice
(b) **Ad-hoc Audit:** In response to a Data Breach, regulatory inquiry, or reasonable suspicion of non-compliance
(c) **Scope:** Review of policies, procedures, access logs, security measures, and data handling practices

### 10.2 Audit Cooperation

Unisoft shall:

(a) Cooperate fully with audits
(b) Provide access to relevant documentation and personnel
(c) Remedy any deficiencies identified within a reasonable timeframe
(d) Bear the cost of the audit unless material non-compliance is found

### 10.3 Compliance Certifications

Unisoft shall provide TwinMOS with:

(a) Annual self-assessment of compliance with this DPA
(b) Evidence of security certifications or audits (if available)
(c) Updated list of subprocessors (quarterly)

## 11. Term and Termination

### 11.1 Term

This DPA shall commence on the Effective Date and continue until the termination or expiry of the MSA, unless earlier terminated in accordance with its terms.

### 11.2 Post-Termination Obligations

Upon termination or expiry:

(a) Unisoft shall cease all Processing of Personal Data
(b) Unisoft shall, at TwinMOS's election, return or securely delete all Personal Data
(c) Unisoft shall provide written certification of deletion within fourteen (14) days
(d) Unisoft may retain Personal Data to the extent required by Applicable Data Protection Laws, subject to continued confidentiality obligations

### 11.3 Survival

Sections 7 (Security Measures), 8 (Data Breach Notification), and 12 (Liability and Indemnification) shall survive termination of this DPA.

## 12. Liability and Indemnification

### 12.1 Liability

Unisoft shall be liable for damages caused by Processing that violates this DPA or Applicable Data Protection Laws, except to the extent that Unisoft is not responsible for the event giving rise to the damage.

### 12.2 Indemnification

Unisoft shall indemnify and hold harmless TwinMOS from and against any claims, damages, fines, penalties, and costs (including reasonable legal fees) arising from:

(a) Unisoft's breach of this DPA
(b) Unisoft's violation of Applicable Data Protection Laws
(c) Any Data Breach caused by Unisoft's negligence or willful misconduct
(d) Unauthorized Processing by Unisoft or its subprocessors

### 12.3 Limitation

The limitations of liability set out in the MSA Section 11 shall apply to this DPA, except that the cap on liability shall not apply to:

(a) Breaches of data protection obligations
(b) Fines imposed by Supervisory Authorities
(c) Willful misconduct or gross negligence

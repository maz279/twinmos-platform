### 1.5 Brand Voice

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Corporate pages | Professional, confident, trustworthy | "TwinMOS Technologies has been at the forefront of memory innovation since 1998." |
| Product pages | Technical, precise, benefit-focused | "VOLTX DDR5 delivers up to 6000MHz with Intel XMP 3.0 support for effortless overclocking." |
| Gaming hub | Energetic, bold, community-driven | "Level up your rig. Light up your game. VOLTX RGB is built for winners." |
| Support | Helpful, patient, clear | "Find the right memory for your system in three simple steps." |
| Marketing campaigns | Aspirational, exciting, action-oriented | "Experience the ultimate. Upgrade to TwinMOS CoreX Pro." |

**Voice Principles:**
1. **Be precise** — Use exact specifications, not vague superlatives
2. **Be human** — Avoid corporate jargon; speak to the user's goal
3. **Be confident** — 27+ years of heritage backs every claim
4. **Be inclusive** — Address global audience (MEA, South Asia, Africa, CIS)

## 2. Logo Guidelines

### 2.1 Logo Variants

| Variant | Usage | Background |
|---------|-------|------------|
| **Primary (Full Color)** | Default for all applications | White or light backgrounds |
| **Monochrome White** | Dark backgrounds, overlays, gaming hub | Dark or colored backgrounds |
| **Monochrome Primary** | Single-color applications, watermarks | Light backgrounds |
| **Compact (Icon Only)** | Favicon, app icons, social avatars, small spaces | Any (adapt color) |

### 2.2 Logo Specifications

| Property | Specification |
|----------|--------------|
| **Primary colors** | Deep Blue (#0A2540) + Electric Blue (#00A3E0) accent |
| **Minimum width** | 120px (digital), 30mm (print) |
| **Clearspace** | Minimum height of the "T" in TwinMOS on all sides |
| **Proportions** | Do not stretch, compress, or distort |
| **File formats** | SVG (primary), PNG (fallback), ICO (favicon) |

### 2.3 Logo Misuse

The TwinMOS logo must NEVER be:
- Stretched or compressed
- Recolored outside approved variants
- Placed on busy or clashing backgrounds without clearance
- Modified or redrawn
- Used with drop shadows or effects that alter its form
- Rotated or tilted

### 2.4 Favicon & App Icons

| Size | Format | Usage |
|------|--------|-------|
| 16x16px | ICO, PNG | Browser favicon |
| 32x32px | PNG | Browser favicon (retina) |
| 180x180px | PNG | Apple touch icon |
| 192x192px | PNG | Android/Chrome icon |
| 512x512px | PNG | PWA icon |

### 3.5 Color Usage Rules

**Corporate Pages:**
- 60% White/Neutral backgrounds
- 30% Deep Blue (#0A2540) for headers, footers, key UI
- 10% Electric Blue (#00A3E0) for accents, CTAs, highlights

**Gaming Pages:**
- 70% Gaming Black (#0D0D0D) backgrounds
- 20% Surface grays for cards and panels
- 10% Neon accents (cyan, magenta, purple) for highlights

## 4. Typography

### 4.1 Brand Typefaces

| Script | Primary | Weights | Usage |
|--------|---------|---------|-------|
| Latin | Inter | 400, 500, 600, 700, 800 | All corporate and gaming content |
| Arabic | Noto Sans Arabic | 400, 500, 700 | Arabic RTL content |
| Devanagari | Noto Sans Devanagari | 400, 500, 700 | Hindi content |
| Chinese (SC) | Noto Sans SC | 400, 500, 700 | Chinese Simplified content |

### 4.2 Type Scale

See [TwinMOSWebsiteDesign_System.md](TwinMOSWebsiteDesign_System.md) §3.2 for complete type scale.

### 4.3 Typography Rules

- **Headings:** Inter 600–800, tight line-height (1.1–1.2), negative letter-spacing for large sizes
- **Body:** Inter 400, comfortable line-height (1.6), neutral letter-spacing
- **Gaming headlines:** Inter 800 (or display font), maximum impact, may use uppercase for short phrases
- **All caps:** Use sparingly; only for overlines, labels, and navigation
- **Underline:** Never underline text that is not a link

## 5. Imagery Style

### 5.1 Product Photography

| Type | Style | Background |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Hero product shots | High-contrast, dramatic lighting, 3/4 angle | Transparent (PNG) or gradient |
| Category thumbnails | Clean, centered, consistent angle | White or light gray |
| Product gallery | Multiple angles, detail shots, scale reference | White or transparent |
| 360° views | Rotating product on axis | Transparent |

### 5.2 Lifestyle Photography

| Context | Style |
|---------|-------|
| Corporate | Professional office/enterprise settings, diverse global teams |
| Gaming | Dynamic PC builds, RGB lighting, immersive gaming setups |
| Regional | Culturally appropriate settings for target markets (MEA, South Asia, Africa) |

### 5.3 Image Treatment

- **Corporate:** Clean, bright, minimal post-processing
- **Gaming:** High contrast, saturated RGB colors, dramatic lighting
- **Consistent color grading** across all images in a section
- **No stock-photo clichés** — authentic, specific to TwinMOS products

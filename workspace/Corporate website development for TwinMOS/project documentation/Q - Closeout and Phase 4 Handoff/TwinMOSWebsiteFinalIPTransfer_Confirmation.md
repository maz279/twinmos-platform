## Purpose

This Final IP Transfer Confirmation serves as the definitive written confirmation that all intellectual property rights in and to the Work Product created during the TwinMOS Corporate Website Redevelopment Project (Phases 1, 2, and 3) have been fully and irrevocably assigned to **TwinMOS Technologies Middle East FZE** ("Assignee"), as provided for under the Intellectual Property Ownership Transfer Agreement (TWN-LEGAL-IP-2026-001) executed at project commencement.

This document:
1. Confirms the **assignment is complete** — all Foreground IP has vested in TwinMOS
2. Enumerates the **full scope of assigned IP** including all deliverables across all three phases
3. Documents **third-party components** with their respective licenses
4. Records **Unisoft's Background IP** (retained by Unisoft) and the perpetual licence granted to TwinMOS
5. Provides a **final IP register** for TwinMOS Legal to maintain

| Unisoft CI/CD Templates | Generic GitHub Actions workflow templates (lint, test, deploy) not specific to TwinMOS | Unisoft pre-existing templates |
| Unisoft Knowledge | Unisoft engineers' professional knowledge, skills, and methodologies | Non-assignable professional know-how |

### 3.2 Perpetual Licence of Background IP to TwinMOS

Per TWN-LEGAL-IP-2026-001 §6.3, Unisoft grants to TwinMOS a **non-exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide, perpetual licence** to use any Background IP that is incorporated into or necessary for the use of the Work Product. This licence:

- Allows TwinMOS to operate, modify, and distribute the complete website platform
- Does not require any ongoing payment to Unisoft
- Survives termination of the MSA or any SOW
- Does not grant TwinMOS the right to sub-license Unisoft's Background IP to third parties independently of the Work Product

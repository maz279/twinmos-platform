# Out-of-Warranty & Paid Repair Options

If your TwinMOS product is no longer covered under warranty, you may still have options for repair, replacement, or data recovery. This page outlines the available paid services.

> **First, check your status:** Use the [Warranty Lookup](/support/warranty-lookup/) tool to confirm your product is out of warranty before exploring paid options.

## Is My Product Out of Warranty?

A product is considered out of warranty when:
- The warranty period has expired (1 year for portable SSDs, 3 years for SATA SSDs and Gen4/Gen3 NVMe SSDs, 5 years for CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSDs, or if the limited lifetime DRAM warranty terms are not met)
- The defect is caused by physical damage, liquid damage, or misuse
- The product was purchased from an unauthorized seller
- The SSD has exceeded its TBW (Terabytes Written) endurance limit

## Paid Repair Assessment

TwinMOS offers paid repair assessments for certain products. To request an assessment:

1. Contact support@twinmos.com with the subject line "Paid Repair Request."
2. Provide the product model, serial number, and a detailed description of the issue.
3. Our support team will evaluate whether a paid repair is feasible and provide a cost estimate.
4. If you approve the estimate, you will receive instructions for shipping the product.

**Important notes:**
- Assessment fees may apply and are non-refundable
- Not all products are eligible for paid repair
- Memory modules (DRAM) are generally not economically repairable
- Repair turnaround: 7–14 business days after product receipt
- You will be contacted before any work begins

## Data Recovery

TwinMOS does not provide in-house data recovery services. If your SSD or portable drive has failed and contains important data:

**Immediate actions:**
- Stop using the device immediately to prevent further damage
- Do not attempt to reformat, repartition, or run repair utilities
- Label the device clearly and store it in anti-static packaging

**Recommended data recovery specialists:**
- Contact support@twinmos.com for a list of recommended third-party providers in your region
- Always verify the provider's credentials and data security practices
- Data recovery costs are the customer's responsibility

**Important:** TwinMOS is not liable for data loss, even under warranty claims. Regular backups are essential.

## Recycling & Disposal

Out-of-warranty products that cannot be repaired should be disposed of responsibly.

**Why recycle electronics?**
- Prevents hazardous materials from entering landfills
- Recovers valuable metals and components
- Reduces environmental impact

**How to recycle:**
- Use local e-waste recycling programs or authorized collection points
- Many retailers offer take-back programs for electronic devices
- Do not dispose of electronic products in general household waste

**Trade-In Program (Select Regions):**
- Trade your old TwinMOS product for credit toward a new purchase
- Credit amount depends on product type and condition
- Contact support@twinmos.com for trade-in eligibility

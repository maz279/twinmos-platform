## Article Structure

### 1. Title
Use a clear, action-oriented title. Example: "How to Enable XMP in BIOS"

### 2. Introduction (1–2 paragraphs)
Explain what the article covers and why it matters. Mention who the article is for.

### 3. Prerequisites
List what the reader needs before starting:
- Required tools or software
- Necessary hardware
- Prior knowledge or permissions

### 4. Step-by-Step Instructions
Use numbered lists for sequential steps. Include screenshots or diagrams where helpful.

1. Step one
2. Step two
3. Step three

### 5. Troubleshooting
Address common issues that may arise during the process.

### 6. Related Articles
Link to other relevant KB articles and support pages.

### 7. Revision History
Track significant changes to the article.

| Date | Author | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | Name | Initial draft |

## Writing Guidelines

- Use simple, direct language.
- Define acronyms on first use.
- Be accurate — do not speculate or hallucinate facts.
- Include warnings or cautions for risky steps.
- Test all instructions before publishing.

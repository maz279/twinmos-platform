# Anti-Counterfeit Policy

TwinMOS Technologies Limited is committed to protecting customers from counterfeit products. This policy outlines our stance, how to identify fakes, and the actions we take.

## Our Stance

Counterfeit products harm consumers, damage the TwinMOS brand, and undermine trust in genuine technology. TwinMOS does not authorize the sale of our products through unauthorized channels, gray-market importers, or counterfeit manufacturers.

## How to Identify Counterfeit Products

### Packaging
- Poor print quality, blurry logos, or misspelled text
- Missing or incorrect barcodes and serial numbers
- Absence of TwinMOS holographic seals or security labels

### Product
- Incorrect or missing serial number labels
- Substandard build quality or materials
- Performance significantly below advertised specifications
- Firmware that does not match official releases

### Seller
- Prices far below market value
- No authorized distributor relationship
- No valid proof of purchase or warranty documentation

## Verification

Always verify your product using our [Serial Number Check](/support/serial-number-check/) tool before use.

## Warranty Implications

Counterfeit products are **not** covered under the TwinMOS warranty. Claims submitted for counterfeit products will be rejected. TwinMOS reserves the right to retain counterfeit products submitted for RMA and cooperate with law enforcement.

## Reporting Counterfeits

If you believe you have purchased a counterfeit TwinMOS product, please report it:

1. Email support@twinmos.com with the subject line "Counterfeit Report."
2. Include:
   - Clear photos of the product and packaging
   - The serial number (if present)
   - Seller name and website
   - Proof of purchase

## Actions We Take

TwinMOS actively investigates reports of counterfeit products and takes appropriate action, including:
- Working with online marketplaces to remove counterfeit listings
- Pursuing legal action against manufacturers and distributors of fake goods
- Educating consumers through our support channels and partners

## Protect Yourself

- Buy only from authorized TwinMOS retailers and distributors.
- Verify serial numbers at the time of purchase.
- Be cautious of deals that seem too good to be true.
- Report suspicious products to TwinMOS and the selling platform.

## Authorized vs. Unauthorized Sellers

| Authorized Seller | Unauthorized Seller |
|---|---|
| Official TwinMOS distributor | Unknown online marketplace seller |
| Listed on TwinMOS website | Significantly below market price |
| Provides valid receipt/invoice | No warranty documentation |
| Product serial number verifies | Serial number check fails or missing |
| Full warranty support | No warranty eligibility |

## Common Counterfeit Indicators

### Packaging Red Flags
- Misspelled product names or branding
- Blurry or low-resolution logos
- Missing holographic security seals
- Incorrect or missing regulatory markings (FCC, CE, RoHS)
- Poor-quality printing or color mismatch

### Product Red Flags
- Serial number sticker looks tampered or re-applied
- PCB color or layout differs from official images
- Heat spreader design inconsistencies
- Performance significantly below specifications
- Firmware version not recognized by official tools

## What TwinMOS Does About Counterfeits

### Immediate Actions
- Investigate all reports within 5 business days
- Coordinate with online marketplaces to remove listings
- Trace supply chains to identify sources

### Long-Term Measures
- Legal action against counterfeit manufacturers
- Enhanced packaging security features
- Regular market monitoring and enforcement
- Consumer education campaigns

## Legal Notice

TwinMOS reserves the right to:
- Retain counterfeit products submitted for RMA
- Report counterfeit sellers to relevant authorities
- Pursue legal remedies against counterfeit manufacturers and distributors
- Refuse warranty service for counterfeit products without exception

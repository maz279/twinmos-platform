# Corporate Entities & Legal Structure

## Page Purpose
The Corporate Entities page provides transparent disclosure of TwinMOS's legal structure for distributors, enterprise customers, investors, and regulatory professionals. It addresses the forensic audit finding that corporate structure information was incomplete and confusing on the current website.

**Key Messages**:
- Clear legal entity structure across jurisdictions
- Dubai FZE as primary international headquarters
- Regional entities for localized operations
- Transparent invoicing and contracting guidance

---

## Legal Structure Overview

TwinMOS Technologies operates through a network of corporate entities designed to optimize regional market access, regulatory compliance, and operational efficiency. The following disclosure outlines our primary legal entities and their respective roles.

**Entity Relationship Diagram:**
```
TwinMOS Technologies Inc. (Taiwan) — Parent / R&D Entity
│
├── TwinMOS Technologies Middle East FZE (Dubai, DAFZA)
│   └── Primary International HQ — Middle East, Africa, CIS
│
├── TwinMOS Technologies ME LLC (Dubai)
│   └── Regional Commercial Operations
│
└── TwinMOS Technologies (India) Pvt. Ltd. (New Delhi)
    └── South Asian Market Operations

Bangladesh Market: Served via Authorized Distributors
```

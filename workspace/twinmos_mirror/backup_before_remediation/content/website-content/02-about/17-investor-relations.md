## Investment Inquiries

### Who Should Inquire
TwinMOS welcomes inquiries from:
- **Institutional investors** seeking exposure to memory/storage markets
- **Private equity firms** evaluating growth opportunities
- **Strategic partners** interested in technology collaboration
- **Regional distributors** seeking equity partnerships

### Inquiry Process
1. **Submit Initial Inquiry**: Email investors@twinmos.com with:
   - Organization name and type
   - Nature of interest (equity, partnership, debt, other)
   - Investment thesis and proposed structure
   - Proposed timeline
   - Contact details

2. **Initial Review**: TwinMOS management evaluates fit and strategic alignment

3. **NDA Execution**: Mutual non-disclosure agreement for detailed discussions

4. **Due Diligence**: Information sharing and facility visits (where appropriate)

5. **Term Discussion**: Negotiation of investment terms and structure

## Risk Factors

Prospective investors should carefully consider the following risks:

### Market Risks
- **Cyclical Pricing**: Memory and storage pricing subject to significant cyclical fluctuations driven by supply-demand imbalances
- **Competition**: Intense competition from established global brands with greater resources
- **Technology Disruption**: Rapid technology evolution may obsolete current product lines

### Operational Risks
- **Supply Chain**: Dependence on third-party suppliers for DRAM ICs, NAND flash, and controllers
- **Geopolitical**: Trade tensions, tariffs, and sanctions may affect supply chains and market access
- **Manufacturing**: Quality issues or production disruptions could impact reputation and revenue

### Regulatory Risks
- **Compliance Costs**: Changes in EU, UK, USA, or regional regulations may increase compliance costs
- **Environmental**: Evolving environmental regulations may require product or process modifications
- **Data Privacy**: Increasing data protection requirements across operating regions

### Financial Risks
- **Currency Fluctuation**: Operations in multiple currencies expose to exchange rate risk
- **Credit Risk**: Distributor and customer payment defaults
- **Liquidity**: Working capital requirements for inventory and receivables

**Disclaimer**: This is not an exhaustive list of risks. Prospective investors should conduct independent due diligence.

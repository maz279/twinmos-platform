## CE Marking — European Conformity

### Applicable Directives
TwinMOS products bearing the **CE** mark comply with applicable European Union directives:

| Directive | Scope | TwinMOS Applicability |
|-----------|-------|----------------------|
| **EMC Directive 2014/30/EU** | Electromagnetic compatibility | All electronic products |
| **LVD Directive 2014/35/EU** | Low voltage safety | Products with internal power supplies |
| **RED Directive 2014/53/EU** | Radio equipment | Wireless-enabled products only |
| **RoHS Directive 2011/65/EU** | Hazardous substance restriction | All electrical/electronic products |

### What CE Marking Means
- Products meet EU safety, health, and environmental protection requirements
- Free movement within the European Economic Area (EEA)
- Compliance verified through internal production control (Module A) or EU-type examination (Module B)

### Declaration of Conformity
[Request CE DoC](/contact) — Available to distributors and enterprise customers upon request

## UKCA — UK Conformity Assessed

### Background
Following the United Kingdom's exit from the European Union, the **UKCA** (UK Conformity Assessed) marking replaced CE marking for products placed on the Great Britain market (England, Scotland, Wales).

### UKCA Applicability
| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Regulations** | UK-specific versions of EU directives |
| **Recognized Standards** | BS EN standards (identical to EU harmonized standards) |
| **Northern Ireland** | CE marking still required (under Windsor Framework) |
| **TwinMOS Status** | UKCA marking applied to products destined for GB market |

### Technical Documentation
UKCA technical files maintained at TwinMOS headquarters, available for market surveillance authority review.

## FCC — Federal Communications Commission (USA)

### Certification Class
TwinMOS products are tested and certified to meet **FCC Part 15** standards for electromagnetic interference:

| Class | Description | TwinMOS Products |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Class B** | Residential/consumer environment | DRAM modules, SSDs, portable storage, USB solutions |

### Testing Scope
- Radiated emissions (30 MHz – 1 GHz)
- Conducted emissions (150 kHz – 30 MHz)
- Ensures products do not cause harmful interference to radio communications

### FCC Identifier
Available upon request for specific product models.

## JEDEC Standards Compliance

### DRAM Standards
TwinMOS DRAM modules are designed and tested in accordance with **JEDEC** (Joint Electron Device Engineering Council) standards:

| Standard | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| JESD79-5 | DDR5 SDRAM Specification |
| JESD79-4 | DDR4 SDRAM Specification |
| JESD79-3 | DDR3 SDRAM Specification |
| JESD21-C | Configurations for Solid State Memories |
| JESD401 | SPD Content for DDR5 Modules |

### What JEDEC Compliance Ensures
- **Pinout Compatibility**: Standardized module pin assignments
- **Electrical Characteristics**: Verified signal integrity and timing
- **Timing Parameters**: Standardized latency and frequency specifications
- **Thermal Behavior**: Defined operating temperature ranges
- **Motherboard Compatibility**: Seamless integration with JEDEC-compliant systems

## 1. Proven Heritage

### 27 Years of Continuous Innovation

Founded in **1998 in Taipei, Taiwan** by William Chen, TwinMOS has grown from a fledgling memory module brand into a global operation serving **93+ countries**. Our Middle East headquarters, established in **Dubai DAFZA in 2001**, has been the regional anchor for international expansion for more than two decades.

**What This Means for You:**
- **Stability**: We've supported customers through SDRAM, DDR1, DDR2, DDR3, DDR4, and now DDR5

## 3. Uncompromising Quality

### ISO 9001:2015 Certified Quality Management

Our quality management system governs every stage of production:

**5-Stage Testing Protocol:**
1. **Incoming Material Inspection** — All components verified before production
2. **In-Process Quality Control** — AOI and X-ray inspection during assembly
3. **Burn-In Testing** — Extended stress testing at elevated temperatures
4. **Final Functional Testing** — Every product tested before shipment
5. **Packaging & Shipment Audit** — Integrity and accuracy verification

## 4. Warranty You Can Trust

### Industry-Competitive Warranty Terms

Our warranty terms reflect our confidence in manufacturing consistency:

| Product Category | Warranty Term | What It Covers |
|-----------------|---------------|----------------|
| **DRAM Memory Modules** | Limited Lifetime | Manufacturing defects, component failure |
| **NVMe Gen5 SSDs (CoreX Pro)** | 5 Years | Defects, NAND wear within TBW |
| **NVMe Gen4/Gen3 SSDs** | 3 Years | Manufacturing defects, controller failure |
| **SATA SSDs** | 3 Years | Manufacturing defects |
| **Portable Storage** | 3 Years | Manufacturing defects, interface failure |

**What "Limited Lifetime" Means:**
- Covers the original purchaser for the life of the product
- Subject to normal use conditions
- Proof of purchase required
- Non-transferable (except where required by law)

**Warranty Services:**
- Online registration and RMA submission
- Regional warranty service centers
- 24-hour acknowledgment, 72-hour initial response

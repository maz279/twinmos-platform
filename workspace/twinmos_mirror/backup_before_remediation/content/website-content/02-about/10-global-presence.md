# Global Presence

## Page Purpose
The Global Presence page demonstrates TwinMOS's international reach and regional capabilities. It targets potential distributors evaluating market coverage, enterprise buyers assessing local support availability, and press researching the company's geographic footprint. This page addresses the forensic audit finding that regional office details were missing from the current website.

- Regional headquarters in Dubai with localized support

### South Asia
**Countries**: India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal

**Market Characteristics**:
- Price-sensitive but quality-conscious consumers
- Rapidly growing gaming and content creation segments
- Strong system builder and SI channel
- Government and education procurement opportunities

**India-Specific**: BIS certification for regulated products; GST-compliant invoicing

---

### CIS (Commonwealth of Independent States)
**Countries**: Russia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Azerbaijan, Armenia, Kyrgyzstan, Belarus

**Market Characteristics**:
- EAC certification required for market access
- Strong PC gaming culture
- Price-competitive market environment
- Local language support (Russian) beneficial

---

### Europe
**Coverage**: Selective distribution

**Market Characteristics**:
- CE marking enables EU/EEA market access
- UKCA marking for post-Brexit UK market
- Competitive landscape with established local brands
- Focus on value-oriented segments

---

### Southeast Asia (SEA)
**Countries**: Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Philippines

**Market Characteristics**:
- Growing middle class driving PC upgrades
- Strong e-commerce channel development
- Gaming and esports popularity
- Emerging enterprise storage demand

---

### North America
**Coverage**: Limited but growing

**Market Characteristics**:
- FCC certification for US market access
- Highly competitive with dominant local brands
- Focus on niche segments and specialty retailers
- Online channel primary distribution method

# Awards & Recognition

## Page Purpose
The Awards page showcases TwinMOS's industry recognition while maintaining transparency about verification status. It targets press, potential distributors, and customers researching brand credibility. This page directly addresses the forensic audit finding that the current website's awards section is empty and misleading.

**Content Governance**: All awards are clearly labeled with verification status per BR-5.5 requirements. Unverified awards are presented as "internally documented" rather than confirmed facts.

---

## Verification Status Overview

> **Important Notice:**

> | Status Icon | Meaning |

> | ✅ **Verified** | Original certificate or independent source confirmed |
> | ⏳ **Pending** | Internal documentation exists; verification in progress |

## Usage Guidelines

### For Internal Use
- All awards may be referenced in internal presentations
- Include verification status when citing specific awards

### For External Marketing
- **Verified awards only**: May be used in marketing materials without qualification
- **Pending awards**: Use with qualifier "claimed" or "internally documented"
- **Press releases**: Consult media relations team before citing any award

### For Press & Media
- Journalists should contact press@twinmos.com for current verification status
- High-resolution award imagery available only for verified awards
- Press kit updated quarterly with verification progress

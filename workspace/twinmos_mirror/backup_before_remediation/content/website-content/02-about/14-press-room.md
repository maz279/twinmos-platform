## Review Sample Program

### Eligibility
Qualified journalists and reviewers may request product samples for editorial coverage. Eligibility criteria:

- Active publication or channel with established audience
- Previous technology hardware coverage
- Editorial independence (no pay-for-play)
- Commitment to publish within 60 days of receipt

### Request Process
1. **Submit Request**: Email press@twinmos.com with required information
2. **Review**: Press team evaluates request (3–5 business days)
3. **Approval**: Confirmation with shipping details
4. **Shipment**: Product sent with tracking
5. **Follow-up**: Press team checks in post-publication

### Required Information
- Publication/channel name and URL
- Audience size and demographics
- Examples of previous hardware reviews
- Planned review date and format (written, video, etc.)
- Shipping address and contact details
- Preferred product category and specific model interest

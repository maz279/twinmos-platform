## Introduction

TwinMOS Technologies is a globally recognized manufacturer of memory and storage solutions, serving customers in more than 93 countries across six continents. Founded in 1998 in Taipei, Taiwan by William Chen, the company has spent over 27 years building a reputation for quality, innovation, and reliability in the competitive semiconductor industry.

Our core business spans four pillars: **DRAM Memory Modules**, **Solid State Drives (SSD)**, **Portable Storage**, and **USB Solutions**. From gaming enthusiasts seeking high-frequency DDR5 RGB memory to enterprises deploying NVMe Gen5 storage, TwinMOS delivers products that meet the demands of diverse markets and applications.

## Our Heritage

TwinMOS was established during a pivotal era in computing — the transition from SDRAM to DDR memory. William Chen's vision was to create a brand that embodied three principles: **Innovation, Perfection, and Quality**. These values continue to guide every aspect of our operations today.

In 2001, TwinMOS expanded its global footprint by establishing **TwinMOS Technologies Middle East FZE** in the **Dubai Airport Free Zone (DAFZA)**. This strategic move positioned the company at the crossroads of Europe, Asia, and Africa, enabling efficient distribution and regional support. The Middle East headquarters remains a cornerstone of our international operations, coordinating distribution across the Middle East, Africa, South Asia, CIS, Europe, Southeast Asia, and North America.

## Product Portfolio at a Glance

### DRAM Memory Modules
From legacy DDR3 to flagship DDR5, TwinMOS offers desktop, laptop, and gaming memory solutions. Key series include:
- **VOLTX DDR5 RGB** — Up to 6000MHz, dynamic RGB lighting
- **TornadoX7 Pro DDR4** — 3200MHz CL16, optimized latency
- **Thunder GX DDR4** — Reliable performance memory
- **Concord DDR3** — Legacy system support

### NVMe & SATA SSDs
Internal storage spanning every performance tier:
- **CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe** — Up to 14,000 MB/s read, PCIe 5.0
- **Xtreme Gen4 NVMe** — High-performance Gen4 storage
- **Alpha Pro Gen3 NVMe** — Mainstream NVMe value
- **Hyper H2 Ultra SATA III** — Budget-friendly SATA upgrade

### Portable Storage
- **ELITE Drive Pro** — USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C portable SSD (500GB–2TB)
- **ProDrive Ultra** — Portable HDD solutions

### USB Solutions
- USB Hubs (4-port, multi-function)
- USB Flash Drives

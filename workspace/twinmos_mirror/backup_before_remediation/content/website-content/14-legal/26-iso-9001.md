# ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

TwinMOS Technologies maintains a **Quality Management System (QMS)** certified to **ISO 9001:2015** (the current version of the ISO 9001 standard), demonstrating our systematic commitment to consistent product quality, customer satisfaction, and continuous improvement.

## What ISO 9001:2015 Means

ISO 9001:2015 is the internationally recognized standard for quality management systems, published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO). Certification under ISO 9001:2015 means that an independent, accredited certification body has audited TwinMOS's quality management processes and verified that they meet the standard's requirements.

ISO 9001:2015 is based on seven quality management principles:

| Principle | Description |
|---|---|
| **Customer Focus** | Consistently meeting customer requirements and striving to exceed customer expectations |
| **Leadership** | Unity of purpose and direction; management creates the conditions for quality achievement |
| **Engagement of People** | Competent, empowered, and engaged people at all levels to deliver value |
| **Process Approach** | Consistent, predictable results through managing activities as interrelated processes |
| **Improvement** | Ongoing focus on improvement of performance |
| **Evidence-Based Decision Making** | Decisions based on analysis and evaluation of data and information |
| **Relationship Management** | Sustained success through managing relationships with interested parties (suppliers, partners) |

## Scope of Certification

The TwinMOS ISO 9001:2015 certification scope covers:

> **Design, development, procurement, manufacturing oversight, quality assurance, and global distribution of DRAM memory modules, NAND flash-based solid-state drives, portable storage devices, and USB flash drives.**

This scope encompasses our operations from product concept and engineering validation through supplier qualification, production monitoring, incoming quality control, outgoing inspection, packaging, and delivery to our global distribution network.

## Key Quality Processes Covered

- **Design & Development Control:** Structured product development with design reviews, validation testing, and JEDEC/industry standard compliance verification
- **Supplier Management:** Supplier qualification, approved vendor lists, incoming quality inspection, and supplier performance monitoring
- **Production Monitoring:** Oversight of contract manufacturing processes, statistical process control, and production audit programs
- **Nonconformance Management:** Documented identification, disposition, and root cause analysis of nonconforming products
- **Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA):** Systematic process for identifying, analyzing, and eliminating root causes of quality issues
- **Customer Feedback & Complaint Management:** Structured RMA and warranty claim analysis to drive product and process improvements
- **Internal Audit Program:** Regular internal audits of QMS processes by trained internal auditors
- **Management Review:** Periodic senior management review of QMS performance, quality objectives, and improvement targets

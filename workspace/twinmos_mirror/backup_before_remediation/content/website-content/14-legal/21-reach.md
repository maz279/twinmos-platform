# REACH Compliance Statement

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

TwinMOS Technologies is committed to compliance with **EU Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006** (REACH — Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals) concerning the registration, evaluation, authorisation, and restriction of chemical substances.

## Regulatory Overview

REACH applies to chemical substances manufactured in or imported into the EU, and to substances present in articles (products) placed on the EU market. TwinMOS products are "articles" under REACH — manufactured objects that during production are given a special shape, surface, or design that determines their function.

## Our Role Under REACH

As a manufacturer and importer of electronic products (articles) that are placed on the EU market, TwinMOS acts as an **article supplier** with the following primary obligations:

1. **SVHC Communication Duty (Article 33):** Communicate substance information to customers and consumers if any article contains a Substance of Very High Concern (SVHC) above 0.1% by weight (w/w)
2. **SCIP Database Notification (Article 9 WEEE Directive as amended):** Submit information on articles containing SVHCs to the ECHA SCIP (Substances of Concern In articles as such or in complex objects / Products) database
3. **Supply Chain Communication:** Pass SVHC information through the supply chain

## Substances of Very High Concern (SVHC)

### ECHA Candidate List Status

The ECHA **SVHC Candidate List** is updated biannually (January and June). As of **April 2026**, the Candidate List contains approximately **253 substances** across 36 updates. We continuously monitor additions to the Candidate List and assess our products against new entries.

**Current TwinMOS Position:**
To the best of our knowledge and based on material declarations received from our component suppliers, TwinMOS products **do not contain SVHCs above the 0.1% w/w threshold** in any homogeneous material or article, except where:
- Specific EU Annex XIV authorization exemptions apply, or
- Supplier declarations are pending for newly added Candidate List substances (reviewed within 90 days of each Candidate List update)

We will update this statement within 90 days of each biannual Candidate List update.

### SCIP Database

In compliance with Article 9 of the EU WEEE Directive (as amended), TwinMOS submits notifications to the ECHA SCIP database for articles containing SVHCs above the 0.1% threshold. Our SCIP submissions reference our product portfolio and are updated when Candidate List changes affect our products.

## Restricted Substances (Annex XVII)

TwinMOS products comply with applicable restrictions listed in REACH Annex XVII. Our substance screening covers all Annex XVII restrictions relevant to electronic components and articles.

## Substance Registration

As an importer of components containing registered substances from outside the EU, TwinMOS relies on the registration status of chemical substances by our upstream suppliers (manufacturers and importers of substances and preparations). We require our suppliers to maintain current registration status for all REACH-relevant substances used in their products.

## Supplier Communication Program

TwinMOS requires material disclosure from all component suppliers to ensure traceability of SVHC substances. Our program includes:
- Collecting **Material Declaration Forms** aligned with IEC 62474 (Material Declaration for Electrotechnical Products and Equipment)
- Requesting updates from suppliers within 60 days of each biannual SVHC Candidate List update
- Reviewing supplier declarations against the current Candidate List
- Maintaining a substance tracking database for our product portfolio

## Safe Use Information

TwinMOS products are manufactured articles and, under normal conditions of intended use, do not release or produce chemical substances in quantities or at rates that would pose risks to human health or the environment. No special safe-use instructions beyond standard handling guidelines for electronic equipment are required for end users.

For professional handlers (recyclers, dismantlers), standard EEE recycling precautions apply (see our [WEEE Compliance](/legal/weee/) page).

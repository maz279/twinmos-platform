# Cookie Preferences

> **Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

Use the controls below to manage which categories of cookies you accept while browsing the TwinMOS website. Your choices apply to this browser and device. If you use multiple browsers or devices, you may need to set your preferences on each.

Your preferences are saved for **12 months** from the date you last updated them. You can update your choices at any time by returning to this page, or by clicking "Manage Cookie Preferences" in the footer of any TwinMOS page.

---

## Necessary Cookies

**Always Active — Cannot Be Disabled**

These cookies are essential for the TwinMOS website to function securely and cannot be turned off. They are set in response to your actions on the site and include:

- Session management (maintaining your login state)
- Security tokens (preventing cross-site request forgery)
- Cart and account state
- Cookie consent recording

These cookies do not track you for advertising purposes and do not require your consent.

---

## Functional Cookies

**You Choose — On / Off**

These cookies enhance your experience by remembering your choices and preferences. If disabled, some features may not work as intended:

- Language and region preferences
- Recently viewed products
- Accessibility mode settings (high contrast, font size)
- Live chat session continuity

**Default: On** — You may turn these off if you prefer a non-personalized browsing experience.

---

## Analytics Cookies

**You Choose — On / Off**

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website so we can improve content, performance, and navigation. All data collected is anonymized or aggregated — it is not used to identify you personally.

Analytics tools in use include: Google Analytics 4, Amplitude, Hotjar.

**Default: On** — You may turn these off. Doing so will not affect your browsing experience but will reduce our ability to improve the site for all users.

---

## Marketing Cookies

**You Choose — On / Off**

These cookies are used to serve you relevant advertisements based on your browsing behavior and interests. They may be set by TwinMOS or our advertising partners (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, X/Twitter).

If you disable marketing cookies:
- You will still see TwinMOS advertisements on other websites, but they will not be tailored to your interests
- Campaign performance measurement on our end will be limited

**Default: Off** — We request your opt-in for marketing cookies.

---

## Global Privacy Control (GPC)

TwinMOS honors the **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** browser signal. If your browser or browser extension is configured to send a GPC signal, we will automatically treat it as equivalent to declining marketing cookies for your session in jurisdictions where the GPC signal is legally recognized (including certain US states and the EU).

If your browser sends a GPC signal, you do not need to manually decline marketing cookies on this page — your preference will be applied automatically.

---

## Your Choices

After adjusting the toggles above, click **"Save My Preferences"** to apply your settings. A confirmation will appear and your choices will be saved for 12 months.

To withdraw consent or change your preferences at any time, return to this page using the "Manage Cookie Preferences" link in the website footer.

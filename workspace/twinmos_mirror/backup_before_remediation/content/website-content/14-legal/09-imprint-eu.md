## EU Responsible Person — General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR)

Under **EU Regulation 2023/988 on General Product Safety** (effective 13 December 2024), which replaced the General Product Safety Directive 2001/95/EC, non-EU manufacturers placing products on the EU market are required to designate an EU Responsible Person established in the European Union.

TwinMOS has designated the following entity as its **EU Responsible Person** under the GPSR for products marketed in the European Economic Area:

> **[EU Responsible Person name and address to be finalized upon appointment]**
> Contact: eu-responsible@twinmos.com

The EU Responsible Person is the contact point for EU market surveillance authorities and serves as the point of reference for product safety communications, recall coordination, and compliance documentation within the EU.

*Note: EU Responsible Person details will be updated upon formal appointment. For urgent product safety queries, contact legal@twinmos.com.*

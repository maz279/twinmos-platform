# WEEE / E-Waste Compliance

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

TwinMOS Technologies complies with the **EU Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment (WEEE) Directive 2012/19/EU**, as amended by **Directive (EU) 2024/884** (published 13 March 2024, transposition deadline for member states: **9 October 2025**), and equivalent national legislation.

## Producer Responsibility

As a producer of electrical and electronic equipment (EEE) placed on the EU market, TwinMOS bears Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) obligations under the WEEE Directive. These include:

- **Financing collection, treatment, and recovery** of WEEE arising from our products
- **Registration** with national WEEE producer registers in EU member states where we place products on the market
- **Reporting** of quantities of EEE placed on the market and WEEE collected
- **Achieving collection and recovery targets** in line with Directive requirements (currently 65% of the average weight of EEE placed on the market in the preceding 3 years, equivalent to approximately 85% of WEEE generated)

TwinMOS fulfills its producer responsibilities either directly or through authorized WEEE compliance schemes in each relevant EU member state.

## Product Labeling — Crossed-Bin Symbol

All applicable TwinMOS products bear the **crossed-out wheeled bin symbol** (🗑 with an X) on the product or packaging, in accordance with Annex IX of the WEEE Directive. This symbol means:

> **This product must NOT be disposed of as unsorted municipal waste. It must be collected and recycled separately through designated collection schemes.**

The symbol may also include a solid bar beneath the bin, indicating that the product was placed on the market after 13 August 2005.

## What the 2024 Amendment Changes

**Directive (EU) 2024/884** (the WEEE amending directive) clarifies:
- Photovoltaic panel waste management financing responsibilities
- Scope of Extended Producer Responsibility for EEE placed on the market after 15 August 2018
- Administrative and reporting obligations under national WEEE registers

As of 2026, the European Commission is conducting a review of the WEEE Directive with a report expected by end of 2026, which may lead to further amendments. TwinMOS monitors this process and will update its compliance program accordingly.

## How to Recycle TwinMOS Products

### European Union
- Return products to **authorized WEEE collection points** (municipal recycling centers, retail take-back programs, or designated drop-off sites)
- Use **retailer take-back schemes** — in many EU countries, retailers are required to accept WEEE when selling new equivalent products (in-store or online take-back)
- Check your local municipality's website for the nearest certified e-waste collection facility

### United Kingdom
- The **UK WEEE Regulations 2013** (as amended) apply equivalent obligations. Return products to approved UK WEEE take-back programs or local authority household waste recycling centres (HWRCs)

### India
- Under the **E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022** (effective April 2023), TwinMOS Technologies (India) Pvt. Ltd. holds Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) authorization. Products should be returned to our authorized collection partners or Producer Responsibility Organizations (PROs)

### United Arab Emirates
- TwinMOS supports the UAE Integrated Waste Management initiative. Return products to authorized e-waste collection points operated by licensed recyclers under EPDA / municipal authority programs

### Other Regions
- Contact local authorities, certified e-waste recyclers, or your retailer for proper disposal instructions
- Do not dispose of electronic products with general household or municipal solid waste

## Battery & Packaging

Batteries installed in or supplied with TwinMOS products (e.g., CMOS batteries in products where present) must be separately recycled in accordance with applicable battery regulations, including the **EU Battery Regulation (EU) 2023/1542**, which establishes comprehensive EPR obligations for batteries and accumulators.

Packaging materials (cardboard, plastic foam, printed materials) should be separated and recycled through local packaging recycling programs.

# Compliance Hub

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

TwinMOS Technologies designs and manufactures products to meet rigorous international regulatory, safety, and quality standards across all our global markets. This hub provides a central point of access to our compliance declarations, certification statements, and conformity documentation.

For certification copies or Declarations of Conformity not available for download here, please contact **legal@twinmos.com**.

---

## Environmental & Chemical Compliance

| Standard | Description | Markets |
|---|---|---|
| [RoHS](/legal/rohs/) | Restriction of Hazardous Substances — EU Directive 2011/65/EU (RoHS 3 as amended by EU 2015/863) | EU, UK, global |
| [REACH](/legal/reach/) | Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals — EU Regulation (EC) 1907/2006; SVHC Candidate List (253 substances, 2026) | EU, UK, global |
| [WEEE](/legal/weee/) | Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment — EU Directive 2012/19/EU as amended by Directive 2024/884 | EU, UK |
| [EPEAT](/legal/epeat/) | Electronic Product Environmental Assessment Tool | Global (primarily North America, Europe) |
| [Conflict Minerals](/legal/conflict-minerals/) | EU Conflict Minerals Regulation 2017/821; OECD Due Diligence Guidance; RMI RMAP | Global |

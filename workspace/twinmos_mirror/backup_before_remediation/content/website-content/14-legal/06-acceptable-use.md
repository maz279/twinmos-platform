# Acceptable Use Policy

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the rules and standards governing your use of TwinMOS Technologies' websites, support portal, download center, partner portal, product registration system, and any other digital services or platforms operated by TwinMOS (collectively, the "Services").

This AUP forms part of our [Terms of Use](/legal/terms-of-use/) and applies to all users of the Services, including visitors, registered customers, distributors, resellers, and business partners. By using the Services, you agree to comply with this AUP. Violation of this AUP may result in immediate suspension or termination of access and may lead to legal action.

## 1. Permitted Use

You may use the Services for lawful, legitimate purposes, including:
- Researching and purchasing TwinMOS products
- Accessing product documentation, datasheets, drivers, and firmware
- Submitting warranty claims and support requests
- Registering products and managing your account
- Communicating with TwinMOS for business or technical purposes
- Participating in authorized partner and reseller programs
- Subscribing to and receiving TwinMOS marketing communications (with consent)

## 2. Prohibited Activities

### 2.1 Unlawful Conduct
You may not use the Services to:
- Violate any applicable local, national, or international law or regulation
- Engage in or facilitate fraud, theft, identity theft, or financial crimes
- Violate any third party's intellectual property, privacy, or other legal rights
- Violate any applicable sanctions, export control, or trade restriction laws (see Section 5)

### 2.2 Security & Infrastructure
You may not:
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to any part of the Services, TwinMOS servers, internal networks, or connected systems
- Introduce, upload, or transmit viruses, worms, ransomware, trojans, spyware, adware, or any other malicious or harmful code
- Interfere with, disrupt, or degrade the performance, integrity, or security of the Services or their underlying infrastructure
- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of TwinMOS systems without our express prior written consent (see our [Security Disclosure Policy](/legal/security-disclosure/))
- Conduct or facilitate denial-of-service (DoS) or distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks
- Bypass or circumvent any security measures, access controls, rate limiters, CAPTCHAs, or content protection mechanisms
- Exploit or attempt to exploit any security vulnerability in the Services

### 2.3 Automated Access & Data Collection
You may not:
- Use bots, crawlers, scrapers, spiders, scripts, or any other automated means to access, collect, index, or extract data from the Services without our prior written consent
- Conduct systematic or high-volume downloading or copying of website content to build competing databases or datasets
- Use the Services or data obtained from them to train, fine-tune, evaluate, or otherwise develop artificial intelligence models, large language models (LLMs), or machine learning systems without our prior explicit written consent
- Submit or use AI-generated content on the Services in a way that misrepresents the content as human-authored

### 2.4 Spam & Unsolicited Communications
You may not:
- Use the Services to send unsolicited commercial communications (spam), bulk email, or chain messages
- Harvest or collect email addresses or other contact information from the Services for the purpose of sending unsolicited communications
- Send communications that are deceptive, misleading, or impersonate TwinMOS or any of its staff, representatives, or partners

### 2.5 Impersonation & Misrepresentation
You may not:
- Impersonate TwinMOS, its employees, officers, authorized representatives, or any other person or entity
- Falsely represent your affiliation with any person, company, or organization
- Use the Services to make false or misleading statements about TwinMOS, its products, or its partners
- Create fake product reviews, testimonials, or ratings

### 2.6 Harassment & Harmful Content
You may not use the Services to:
- Harass, intimidate, threaten, abuse, defame, or stalk any person
- Publish or distribute content that is obscene, pornographic, or sexually exploitative
- Incite violence, hatred, or discrimination based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, nationality, disability, or other protected characteristics
- Publish content that is defamatory, libellous, or unlawfully harmful to any individual or organization

### 2.7 Intellectual Property
You may not:
- Reproduce, distribute, modify, publicly display, or create derivative works from TwinMOS Content without our prior written consent
- Reverse-engineer, decompile, disassemble, or attempt to derive the source code of any TwinMOS software or firmware
- Remove or alter any copyright, trademark, or other proprietary notices embedded in TwinMOS Content
- Use TwinMOS trademarks, logos, or trade names other than as permitted under the [Trademark & IP Usage Policy](/legal/trademark-policy/)

### 2.8 Counterfeit & Unauthorized Products
You may not use the Services to:
- Advertise, sell, or promote counterfeit TwinMOS products
- Misrepresent gray-market, used, or third-party products as genuine TwinMOS products
- Submit fraudulent warranty or RMA claims

## 3. User-Generated Content

If you submit reviews, comments, questions, forum posts, product photos, or other content through the Services:
- You are solely responsible for the accuracy and legality of the content you submit
- You warrant that you own or have the necessary rights to submit the content and that it does not infringe third-party rights
- TwinMOS reserves the right to review, edit, remove, or reject any user-generated content at any time and without notice if we determine it violates this AUP, our Terms of Use, or applicable law
- Submitted content may be displayed publicly and indexed by search engines; do not submit private or confidential information

## 4. Account Security

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account. You must:
- Use a strong, unique password and keep it confidential
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) where available
- Notify TwinMOS immediately at **support@twinmos.com** if you suspect unauthorized access to your account
- Not share your account credentials with any other person

TwinMOS will never ask you for your password via email, phone, or chat.

## 5. Export Controls & Sanctions

Use of the Services is subject to applicable export control and trade sanctions laws. You may not use the Services to:
- Access, download, or transmit technical data, software, or products in violation of applicable export controls (including US EAR, EU dual-use regulations, or UAE export control laws)
- Conduct business with or on behalf of any person, entity, or country subject to applicable economic or trade sanctions

## 6. Enforcement

TwinMOS may, at its sole discretion and without prior notice, take the following actions in response to violations of this AUP:
- Issue a formal warning
- Temporarily suspend access to some or all of the Services
- Permanently terminate your account and access to the Services
- Remove content that violates this AUP
- Refer the matter to law enforcement authorities or take civil legal action
- Pursue any combination of the above

Any suspected criminal violations will be reported to appropriate law enforcement authorities.

## 7. Reporting Abuse

If you observe or suspect a violation of this AUP, please report it promptly:

- **Email:** legal@twinmos.com
- **Subject Line:** "AUP Abuse Report — [Brief Description]"
- **Include:** The URL(s) involved, a description of the suspected violation, and any supporting evidence

For suspected security vulnerabilities, please use our [Security Disclosure](/legal/security-disclosure/) process instead.

All abuse reports will be reviewed and investigated. TwinMOS will maintain confidentiality of the reporting party to the extent practicable.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

TwinMOS may update this AUP from time to time to reflect changes in law, technology, or our operational practices. The "Last Reviewed" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated. Your continued use of the Services after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised AUP.

# CE Marking & Declaration of Conformity

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

TwinMOS products bearing the **CE mark** comply with all applicable EU harmonization legislation required for products placed on the European Economic Area (EEA) market. CE marking demonstrates that a product has been assessed and meets EU safety, health, and environmental protection requirements.

## EU General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR) 2023/988

The **EU General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988** (GPSR) entered into force on **13 December 2024**, replacing the General Product Safety Directive 2001/95/EC. The GPSR introduces enhanced product safety obligations applicable to all consumer products sold in the EU, including online sales.

### EU Responsible Person

Under Article 16 of the GPSR, manufacturers established outside the EU are required to designate an **EU Responsible Person** established in the EU to act as the contact point for market surveillance authorities and consumers.

TwinMOS Technologies Inc. (Taiwan) and TwinMOS Technologies Middle East FZE (UAE) have designated an EU Responsible Person for the EEA market:

> **EU Responsible Person:** [Contact details to be completed upon formal appointment]
> **Contact Email:** eu-responsible@twinmos.com

The EU Responsible Person maintains documentation required by the GPSR, cooperates with EU market surveillance authorities, and serves as the point of contact for safety inquiries, recalls, and corrective actions within the EU.

### Online Sales Obligations (Article 19 GPSR)
For products sold through twinmos.com and authorized online retailers, TwinMOS ensures that the following information is displayed on product listings:
- TwinMOS name, registered trade name/trademark, and postal address
- EU Responsible Person name and contact details
- Product identification (type, batch, serial number range, or product image)
- Applicable warnings, safety information, and instructions as required by the GPSR and relevant sector legislation

## Applicable EU Directives & Regulations

TwinMOS applies the CE mark after completing the appropriate conformity assessment procedures under the following harmonized legislation:

| Directive / Regulation | Scope | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Directive 2014/30/EU** — Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC) | All electronic products that may cause or be susceptible to electromagnetic interference | All TwinMOS computing peripherals and storage devices |
| **Directive 2014/35/EU** — Low Voltage Directive (LVD) | Electrical equipment operating between 50V–1000V AC or 75V–1500V DC | Externally powered products (USB hubs, powered accessories) |
| **Directive 2011/65/EU** (as amended by 2015/863) — RoHS | Restriction of hazardous substances | All EEE products |
| **Regulation (EU) 2023/988** — GPSR | General product safety | All consumer products |

For products incorporating radio frequency functions (where applicable), the **Radio Equipment Directive (RED) 2014/53/EU** is also applied.

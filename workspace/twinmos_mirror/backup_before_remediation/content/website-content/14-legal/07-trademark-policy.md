## 2. Authorization Requirement

Use of TwinMOS trademarks requires a written authorization from TwinMOS. Authorization is granted to:
- **Authorized Distributors & Resellers** — under the terms of a current, signed distribution or reseller agreement
- **Authorized System Integrators** — under the terms of a signed partner agreement
- **Press & Media** — for factual reporting purposes, subject to the guidelines in Section 4
- **Third Parties** — only with prior written approval from TwinMOS (contact press@twinmos.com)

## 3. Permitted Uses for Authorized Partners

Authorized distributors, resellers, and system integrators may use TwinMOS trademarks solely to:
- Identify and accurately describe genuine TwinMOS products in product listings, marketing materials, advertisements, and sales collateral
- Identify themselves as an authorized TwinMOS distributor, reseller, or partner
- Include the TwinMOS logo in partner directories, certification badges, and co-branded materials, provided prior written approval for the specific use is obtained
- Promote genuine TwinMOS products in online and offline channels

All permitted uses are subject to the conditions in Section 5 (Usage Standards) and the current TwinMOS Brand Style Guide (available from press@twinmos.com).

## 4. Press & Editorial Use

Members of the press and media may use TwinMOS trademarks for:
- Accurate, factual reporting on TwinMOS, its products, and its business activities
- Editorial mentions, reviews, and comparisons of TwinMOS products
- Reproduction of official product images provided by TwinMOS's press kit

Press use must not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation beyond the factual relationship described. For press kit assets, product imagery, and media inquiries, contact **press@twinmos.com**.

## 5. Usage Standards & Requirements

All authorized uses of TwinMOS trademarks must comply with the following standards:

### 5.1 Accuracy & Non-Alteration
- Use the TwinMOS mark accurately, exactly as it appears in approved brand assets
- Do not abbreviate, alter, modify, stylize, rotate, distort, add to, or otherwise change any TwinMOS mark
- Do not combine TwinMOS marks with other marks, symbols, words, or imagery without prior written approval
- Use the correct trademark symbol: **®** for registered marks and **™** for unregistered marks where applicable

### 5.2 Spacing & Minimum Size
- Maintain clear space around the TwinMOS logo equal to at least the height of the "T" in "TwinMOS" on all sides
- Do not reproduce the logo smaller than the minimum size specified in the Brand Style Guide
- Do not use the logo in any manner that renders it illegible

### 5.3 Color
- Use only approved color versions of the TwinMOS logo (full color, white, or black) as specified in the Brand Style Guide
- Do not change the colors of the logo or apply unapproved color treatments
- Ensure sufficient contrast between the logo and the background on which it is placed

### 5.4 Not as Part of Company Name or Domain
- Do not use TwinMOS trademarks as part of your own company name, business name, product name, service name, or domain name (e.g., "twinmosshop.com" is not permitted without authorization)
- Do not register social media handles that incorporate TwinMOS trademarks without authorization

### 5.5 No False Implication of Endorsement
- Do not use TwinMOS trademarks in a way that suggests TwinMOS endorses, sponsors, or is affiliated with your business, products, or services beyond your authorized status
- Do not claim to be TwinMOS or a TwinMOS subsidiary unless you are

## 6. Prohibited Uses

You may not use TwinMOS trademarks to:
- Identify, label, promote, or sell counterfeit, modified, or non-genuine products
- Create confusion among consumers about the origin or authenticity of products
- Make disparaging, derogatory, or defamatory statements about TwinMOS or its products
- Register domain names, social media handles, usernames, or app names that include TwinMOS marks without prior written authorization
- Use TwinMOS marks in comparative advertising in a manner that is deceptive or likely to mislead consumers
- Reproduce, copy, modify, or create derivative works based on TwinMOS firmware, software, circuit layouts, or technical documentation
- Use TwinMOS marks in connection with products or services that do not meet TwinMOS quality standards

## 8. Quality Control

TwinMOS reserves the right to:
- Inspect and approve any use of its trademarks in materials that have not been previously approved
- Request changes to non-compliant uses of TwinMOS trademarks
- Revoke authorization to use TwinMOS trademarks if quality standards are not maintained or if the authorized partner violates this Policy or the underlying agreement

Failure to comply with quality standards constitutes grounds for immediate revocation of trademark usage rights.

## 9. Termination of Rights

Trademark usage rights are automatically revoked upon:
- Termination or expiration of the applicable distribution, reseller, or partner agreement
- Written notice from TwinMOS revoking usage rights
- Breach of this Policy or the underlying agreement

Upon revocation, you must immediately cease all uses of TwinMOS trademarks, remove all TwinMOS marks from your materials (physical and digital), and certify in writing that you have done so within thirty (30) days of revocation.

## 10. Reporting Unauthorized Use

If you observe unauthorized or infringing use of TwinMOS trademarks, please report it:

- **Email:** legal@twinmos.com
- **Subject:** "Trademark Infringement Report — [Description]"
- **Include:** Description of the infringing use, URL or location, and any supporting evidence (screenshots, photographs)

TwinMOS takes trademark infringement seriously and actively pursues civil and criminal remedies against infringers in cooperation with local authorities. For counterfeit product reports, see our [Anti-Counterfeit Policy](/legal/counterfeit-policy/).

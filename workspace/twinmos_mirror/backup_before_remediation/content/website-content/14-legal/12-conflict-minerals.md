# Conflict Minerals Policy

> **Version 2.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

TwinMOS Technologies is committed to the responsible sourcing of minerals and to ensuring that our products do not contribute to human rights abuses, armed conflict, or environmental destruction through the use of minerals sourced from conflict-affected or high-risk areas (CAHRAs).

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to **3TG minerals** — tin (Sn), tantalum (Ta), tungsten (W), and gold (Au) — and their derivative metals and compounds used in the manufacture of TwinMOS products. These minerals are critical components in:
- NAND flash and DRAM memory chips (tantalum capacitors, gold bonding wires)
- PCB and substrate manufacturing (tin solder, gold plating)
- SSD controller ICs and passive components (tantalum, tin, tungsten contacts)

## 2. Regulatory Framework

TwinMOS's conflict minerals due diligence program is designed to comply with or align to the following regulatory and voluntary frameworks:

| Framework | Jurisdiction | Applicability |
|---|---|---|
| **EU Conflict Minerals Regulation (2017/821)** | European Union | Applies to EU importers of 3TG minerals and ores above specified thresholds |
| **OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Supply Chains of Minerals from CAHRAs** | International | Voluntary standard followed by TwinMOS globally |
| **SEC Rule 13p-1 (Section 1502 of Dodd-Frank Act)** | United States | Reporting obligations for US-listed companies; TwinMOS monitors for customer compliance requirements |
| **Responsible Minerals Initiative (RMI) / RMAP** | Industry | TwinMOS favors suppliers sourcing from RMAP-certified smelters and refiners |

The **Responsible Minerals Assurance Process (RMAP)**, operated by the Responsible Minerals Initiative (RMI), was officially recognized by the European Commission in October 2025 as a compliant supply chain due diligence scheme under the EU Conflict Minerals Regulation, providing an additional assurance pathway for our upstream supply chain.

## 3. Our Commitment

TwinMOS is committed to:

1. **Avoiding complicity** in serious human rights abuses, armed conflict, or corruption associated with the extraction, trade, or processing of 3TG minerals from conflict-affected and high-risk areas
2. **Preferring conflict-free sources:** Sourcing components from suppliers who source 3TG from smelters and refiners certified under recognized industry assurance programs (RMI RMAP, LBMA Good Delivery, etc.)
3. **Exercising supply chain due diligence** in accordance with the OECD Five-Step Framework and the EU Conflict Minerals Regulation
4. **Transparency and disclosure:** Communicating our due diligence activities to customers, investors, and other stakeholders
5. **Continuous improvement:** Reviewing and strengthening our due diligence program annually

## 4. OECD Five-Step Due Diligence Process

TwinMOS follows the **OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Supply Chains** five-step framework:

### Step 1: Establish Strong Company Management Systems
- Board-level oversight of conflict minerals policy
- Designated compliance owner within procurement/supply chain function
- Supplier communication of policy expectations through the [Vendor Code of Conduct](/legal/vendor-code-of-conduct/)
- Grievance mechanism for reporting concerns about mineral sourcing

### Step 2: Identify & Assess Risks in the Supply Chain
- Annually survey Tier 1 suppliers using the **Conflict Minerals Reporting Template (CMRT)** (standardized template by RMI)
- Map reported smelters and refiners to the RMAP conformance list
- Flag suppliers reporting 3TG from the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC), adjoining countries, or other designated CAHRAs
- Apply risk scoring based on country of origin, smelter/refiner conformance status, and supplier responsiveness

### Step 3: Design & Implement a Strategy to Respond to Identified Risks
- For non-conformant smelters or refiners identified in the supply chain: engage supplier to transition to RMAP-certified sources within a defined period
- For refusals to respond or persistent non-conformance: escalate to procurement management and consider supplier replacement
- For direct evidence of serious human rights abuses: immediately suspend the relevant supply and report to relevant authorities

### Step 4: Carry Out Independent Third-Party Audits
- We rely on RMAP audits of smelters and refiners rather than conducting our own audits of individual smelters
- Third-party audits of our Tier 1 supplier compliance are incorporated into our broader supplier audit program (see [Supply Chain Disclosure](/legal/supply-chain-disclosure/))

### Step 5: Report on Supply Chain Due Diligence
- Annual conflict minerals due diligence report summary published on this page
- CMRT data available to customers and investors upon request
- Disclosure to EU importers as required under EU Conflict Minerals Regulation 2017/821

## 5. Supplier Requirements

All TwinMOS suppliers providing components containing 3TG are required to:
- Complete and return the **CMRT (Conflict Minerals Reporting Template)** annually
- Maintain traceability records identifying smelters and refiners in their own supply chains
- Preferentially source from smelters and refiners certified under RMI RMAP or equivalent recognized programs
- Cooperate with TwinMOS due diligence inquiries and provide supporting documentation upon request
- Flow down equivalent conflict minerals requirements to their own sub-suppliers

Failure to cooperate with CMRT requests or to demonstrate credible progress toward conflict-free sourcing may result in disqualification. Confirmed use of minerals financing armed groups in the DRC or adjoining countries will result in immediate termination of the business relationship.

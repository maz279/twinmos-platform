# Privacy Policy

> **Version 3.0 — Last Reviewed 2026-04-30**

## 1. Introduction & Scope

This policy applies to all personal data processed by TwinMOS in the course of our business activities, including:

- Visitors to our corporate websites and regional portals
- Customers, distributors, resellers, and system integrators
- End-users who register products or request warranty support

- Individuals who subscribe to marketing communications or participate in surveys and events

By using our services or providing your personal data to us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our services or provide your personal data.

## 4. Information We Collect

We collect and process the following categories of personal data:

### 4.1 Identity & Contact Information
- Full name, job title, and department
- Business email address, phone number, and postal address
- Company name, registration number, and industry sector

### 4.2 Account & Registration Data
- Usernames, passwords (stored as cryptographic hashes), and account preferences
- Product registration details (serial numbers, purchase dates, retailer information)
- Support ticket history and RMA records

### 4.3 Transaction & Financial Data
- Purchase history, order details, and invoice information
- Payment method details (processed via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways; we do not store full card numbers)
- Billing and shipping addresses

### 4.4 Technical & Usage Data
- IP address, browser type and version, operating system
- Device identifiers, geolocation data (country/region level), and time zone
- Website browsing behavior, page views, clickstream data, and referral sources
- Cookie and tracking technology data (see our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy/))

### 4.5 Marketing & Communications Data
- Marketing preferences and subscription status
- Engagement metrics (email opens, link clicks, event attendance)
- Survey responses and feedback submissions

### 4.6 Employment & Applicant Data
- CVs, resumes, cover letters, and portfolio materials
- Employment history, educational background, and professional certifications
- Interview notes and assessment results

We do not intentionally collect **sensitive personal data** (such as health data, biometric data, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, or political opinions) through routine interactions. If such data is shared voluntarily or necessarily (e.g., in accommodation requests), it will be handled with heightened protection measures.

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing (by Region)

We process personal data only where we have a valid legal basis under applicable law:

### 5.1 EU GDPR and UK Data Protection Law

**EU:** Under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679, our legal bases include:
- **Consent** — For marketing communications, non-essential cookies, and certain surveys
- **Contractual Necessity** — To fulfill orders, provide warranty support, and manage accounts
- **Legal Obligation** — To comply with tax, accounting, and regulatory requirements
- **Legitimate Interests** — For fraud prevention, network security, and product improvement, balanced against your rights

**UK:** Following the Royal Assent of the **Data (Use and Access) Act 2025** (DUAA) on 19 June 2025, UK data protection law is being updated in phases through 2026. We apply UK GDPR as amended by the DUAA, including phased implementation of new rights and obligations. Key changes effective from February 2026 and June 2026 are reflected in Section 11 below.

We are required to appoint an **EU Article 27 Representative** for GDPR purposes. Contact: eu-privacy@twinmos.com

### 5.2 UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)

Under Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (UAE PDPL) and its Executive Regulations (2024), we process personal data:
- With your explicit consent for marketing and profiling activities
- To perform contracts to which you are a party
- To comply with legal obligations applicable to TwinMOS in the UAE
- To protect vital interests or public interest as permitted by law

We have appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO) where processing presents high risk to privacy, and our DPO contact details are available at privacy@twinmos.com.

### 5.3 India Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023

Under the DPDP Act 2023 and the **DPDP Rules 2025** (notified 13 November 2025), we process personal data:
- Based on your free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous **consent**
- For legitimate uses as defined under Section 7 of the Act, including voluntary provision of data for specified purposes

The **Data Protection Board of India** is operational as of November 2025. Full operational compliance is required by 13 May 2027. We have appointed a **Grievance Officer** for India (contact: in-privacy@twinmos.com) as required under the Act. Cross-border transfers from India follow the negative-list model under Section 16 of the DPDP Act; no countries are currently prohibited.

### 5.4 KSA Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree M/19, as amended by M/148 of 27 March 2023) and its Implementing Regulations issued by the **Saudi Data & Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA)**, we process personal data:
- With your explicit consent, clearly documented and easily withdrawable
- To perform contractual obligations or pre-contractual measures
- To comply with legal obligations under Saudi law
- To achieve legitimate interests, provided your privacy rights are not prejudiced

Full enforcement of the KSA PDPL has been in effect since 14 September 2024. International transfers require SDAIA-compliant mechanisms including contractual safeguards.

### 5.5 Bangladesh Data Protection

Under Bangladesh's **Personal Data Protection Ordinance** and applicable digital security framework, we process personal data:
- With your informed consent
- For the performance of a contract or at your request prior to entering a contract
- To comply with legal obligations
- For legitimate interests, subject to balancing tests and data minimization principles

### 5.6 Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023

Under the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023** (which repeals and replaces the NDPR 2019), enforced by the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)**, we process personal data:
- With your freely given, specific, and informed consent
- For the performance of a contract or compliance with legal obligations
- To protect your vital interests or the public interest
- For legitimate interests, except where such interests are overridden by your fundamental rights

### 5.7 South Africa POPIA

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), enforced by the **Information Regulator**, we process personal information:
- With your informed consent
- To fulfill contractual obligations or take steps at your request
- To comply with legal obligations under South African law
- To protect legitimate interests, provided your privacy is not infringed upon unreasonably

The Information Regulator has significantly intensified enforcement in 2026, including mandatory e-portal breach notification (effective 1 April 2025) and new health data regulations (March 2026).

## 11. Your Rights (by Jurisdiction)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any right, contact us at **privacy@twinmos.com** or use our [Data Deletion Request](/legal/data-deletion-request/) form.

### 11.1 EU GDPR
- Right to access your personal data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing
- Right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing
- Right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (list at edpb.europa.eu)

### 11.2 UK (DUAA 2025)
Rights substantially mirror EU GDPR rights. Notable 2025–2026 changes under the DUAA:
- Right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection remain in force
- Relaxed restrictions on automated decision-making for non-special-category data (effective February 2026)
- New **right to complain to your employer** if you believe UK data law has been breached regarding your personal data (effective 19 June 2026)
- Right to lodge a complaint with the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** (ico.org.uk)

### 11.3 UAE PDPL
- Right to access and obtain a copy of your personal data
- Right to request correction or updating of your data
- Right to request destruction or anonymization under specific conditions

### 11.4 India DPDP Act 2023
- Right to access information about your personal data
- Right to correction and erasure
- Right to grievance redressal (contact our Grievance Officer at in-privacy@twinmos.com; acknowledgment within 48 hours, resolution within 7 working days)

### 11.5 KSA PDPL

### 11.6 Bangladesh

## 13. Data Security Measures

We implement a comprehensive information security program aligned with ISO 27001 principles, including:

- **Organizational Measures:** Designated data protection responsibilities, staff training, confidentiality agreements, and DPO/Grievance Officer appointments where required
- **Technical Measures:** Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest, firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and regular vulnerability assessments
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access controls (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), and least-privilege principles
- **Physical Security:** Secured data centers and offices with access logging and environmental controls
- **Incident Response:** A documented breach response plan with notification procedures to regulators and affected individuals as required by applicable law

Despite these measures, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and protect your account credentials.

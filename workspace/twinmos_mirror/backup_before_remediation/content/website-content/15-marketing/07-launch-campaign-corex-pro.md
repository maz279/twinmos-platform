## Built for Sustained Performance — Not Just Benchmarks

Most drives achieve peak speeds only in burst conditions. CoreX Pro Gen 5 is designed for sustained throughput:

### Full DRAM Cache
A dedicated DRAM buffer ensures the drive maintains peak read/write speeds across extended sequential transfers. No SLC cache cliff. No throttle during large project saves.

### Graphene Thermal Dissipation
Gen 5 drives run hot — that's physics. CoreX Pro's graphene heatsink keeps operating temperatures in the **65–80°C optimal range**, preventing thermal throttling that cuts performance on uncooled Gen 5 drives. Compatible with most M.2 slot coolers and motherboard heatsinks.

### NVMe 2.0 Protocol
Full NVMe 2.0 compliance enables the complete command set for modern operating systems — including improved power management for laptops and small-form-factor PCs using PCIe Gen 5 expansion cards.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does CoreX Pro Gen 5 need a special heatsink?**
A: It ships with its own graphene heatsink. If your motherboard has a built-in M.2 heatsink, you can use either — remove the drive's heatsink if the motherboard's cover doesn't accommodate both.

**Q: Will it work in my current PCIe Gen 4 system?**
A: Yes. The drive operates at PCIe Gen 4 speeds in a Gen 4 slot — approximately 7,000 MB/s sequential read. You can upgrade your platform later and unlock full Gen 5 performance.

**Q: How does the 5-year warranty work?**
A: TwinMOS offers in-region warranty support. Visit [/support/warranty/](/support/warranty/) to register your product and review the full warranty policy.

**Q: Is there a version with an integrated heatsink for high-airflow cases?**
A: The graphene heatsink included with CoreX Pro Gen 5 is designed for both standard and high-airflow environments. For cases with a dedicated M.2 SSD cooler, the heatsink can be removed.

**Q: What operating systems are supported?**
A: Windows 10 / 11, macOS (with compatible PCIe enclosure), Linux 2.4+. NVMe 2.0 driver support included in Windows 11 natively.

## Why TwinMOS in India?

### The Market Needs It
India's PC and storage hardware market is growing at **6.78% CAGR**, driven by:
- Tier 2 and Tier 3 city expansion — consumers outside metros buying IT hardware in increasing volumes
- Digital India's infrastructure push creating demand for enterprise and SMB storage
- Rapid growth in content creation, gaming, and education segments
- System builders and integrators demanding reliable, competitively priced components

### What's Missing
The premium Western brands — Kingston, Corsair, Samsung — price for premium markets. Chinese budget alternatives compromise on quality assurance. **TwinMOS sits in the optimal position:** Taiwan-manufactured quality, ISO 9001:2015 certified, 100% pre-shipment tested, at price points Indian consumers and businesses can work with.

### What TwinMOS Delivers
- **Performance:** DDR5, PCIe Gen 5, and Gen 4 NVMe — next-generation storage at accessible price points
- **Reliability:** Lifetime warranty on memory modules; 3–5 years on SSDs
- **Local presence:** In-country warranty processing — no international shipping for returns
- **Channel support:** Dedicated trade pricing and margin support for distributors and retailers

## Why Indian Buyers Choose TwinMOS

### 1. BIS-Registered Products — Fully Compliant
All TwinMOS products sold in India are registered with the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) under the mandatory BIS CRS scheme where applicable. No grey imports, no compliance risk.

### 2. Local Warranty — No Hassle Returns
Indian consumers and businesses should not bear the cost and delay of international warranty returns. TwinMOS India handles warranty claims in-country through our distribution network. Typical turnaround: 5–10 business days.

### 3. Competitive Pricing for a Price-Sensitive Market
India's IT hardware market is highly price-sensitive, particularly as component costs rose **10–20% YoY** in 2025–2026. TwinMOS products are priced to compete effectively:
- DDR4 SO-DIMM options for legacy laptop upgrades at accessible price points
- PCIe Gen 3 NVMe for budget-conscious first-time SSD buyers
- DDR5 and Gen 5 at pricing below premium Western brands

### 4. Full Portfolio for All Segments
From a INR 800 DDR3 4GB for an older system to a full DDR5-6000 + Gen 5 gaming build — TwinMOS covers every buyer in India's diverse market.

### 5. Trusted by Partners Across 93+ Countries
TwinMOS has operated internationally since 1998, with 27+ years of manufacturing and distribution experience. Indian buyers join a global network of customers who trust TwinMOS for reliability, quality, and support.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are TwinMOS products genuine and BIS compliant in India?**
A: Yes. Products sold through authorised TwinMOS India channels are BIS-registered where required and sourced through our official distribution network. To verify authenticity, use the serial number check at [/support/serial-check/](/support/serial-check/).

**Q: How does warranty work if I need to return a product?**
A: TwinMOS India processes warranty claims locally. Contact the retailer where you purchased, or [submit a warranty claim](/support/warranty/). You should not need to ship products internationally.

**Q: Are prices the same across all retailers?**
A: Retailers set their own retail prices within our suggested RRP. For bulk or B2B pricing, contact our India sales team directly.

**Q: Does TwinMOS support legacy DDR3 systems?**
A: Yes. TwinMOS stocks DDR3 1333MHz and 1600MHz in 4GB and 8GB for older desktops and laptops — relevant for India's significant installed base of legacy systems.

**Q: How can I verify a TwinMOS India reseller is authorised?**
A: All authorised TwinMOS India resellers are listed in our [Partner Directory](/partners/). If your retailer is not listed, exercise caution and contact [info@twinmos.com](mailto:info@twinmos.com) to verify.

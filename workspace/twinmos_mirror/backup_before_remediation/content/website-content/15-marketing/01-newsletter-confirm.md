# One Step Away — Check Your Inbox

Thank you for signing up for **TwinMOS Insider**! We've sent a confirmation email to the address you provided.

**Please click the verification link in that email to activate your subscription.** This two-step process ensures only you can subscribe to our list — and keeps our communications relevant and free from spam.

---

## Why Confirm?

Double opt-in protects you in three ways:

1. **Prevents unauthorised signups** — if someone else entered your email, clicking nothing means nothing happens.
2. **Ensures better email quality** — our list stays clean, so every send reaches real, engaged subscribers.
3. **GDPR compliance** — confirming your email gives us verified consent under European data protection law, wherever you are in the world.

## Didn't Receive the Email?

Here are the most common reasons and quick fixes:

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Landed in spam or junk | Check your spam folder and mark us as "Not Spam" or "Safe Sender" |
| Wrong email entered | [Sign up again](/newsletter/signup/) with the correct address |
| Corporate firewall | Try a personal email address or ask your IT admin to whitelist `@twinmos.com` |
| Delivery delay | Allow up to **10 minutes** — transient delays are normal |
| Gmail Promotions tab | Look in your Promotions or Updates tab, then move us to Primary |

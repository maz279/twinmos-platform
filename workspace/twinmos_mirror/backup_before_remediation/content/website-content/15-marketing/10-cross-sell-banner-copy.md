# Cross-Sell & Upsell Banner Copy Bank

This document contains approved, tested copy snippets for cross-sell and upsell placements across the TwinMOS website. Each section includes multiple A/B variants, placement guidance, and contextual notes.

---

## Performance Benchmarks (Reference)

Effective cross-sell and upsell strategies account for **10–30% of total ecommerce revenue**. Amazon generates 35% of its revenue through personalised recommendations. Use these baselines when evaluating banner performance:

| Placement | Expected Conversion Lift |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Product page cross-sell | 8–15% AOV increase |
| Cart page cross-sell | 10–20% AOV increase |
| Checkout upsell (order bump) | 5–12% take rate |
| Post-purchase email cross-sell | 5–15% conversion |
| Homepage featured bundle | Awareness, not direct conversion |

---

## Placement Philosophy

- **Product pages:** Recommend complementary products at ≤50% of the primary product price. Do not recommend direct alternatives (this is a product comparison, not a cross-sell).
- **Cart:** Recommend simple add-ons at ≤10% of cart total where possible. Reduce friction — "add to cart" with one click, no navigation away.
- **Checkout:** Minimal friction only. Single-click add-on. Do not introduce a new product category at checkout.
- **Post-purchase email:** Best moment for cross-category recommendations. Customer is committed and happy — highest receptivity.
- **Mobile:** Reduce to headline + CTA only. Remove secondary copy on screens under 390px wide.

## Usage Guidelines & Quality Standards

### Copy Rules
- Headlines: **60 characters maximum** for cross-sell banners (mobile truncates beyond this)
- Body copy: **80–120 characters** for product page placements; remove body entirely for checkout
- CTAs: Action-first verbs — "Shop", "Add", "Upgrade", "See", "Claim". Avoid passive ("View more", "Click here")
- Always match banner imagery to the **target product category**, not the page product
- For urgency copy, only use genuine scarcity/time claims — do not fabricate urgency

### A/B Testing Cadence
- Run each variant for **minimum 2 weeks** before declaring a winner
- Change only **one element** per test (headline vs. body vs. CTA vs. image)
- Minimum sample size: 500 impressions per variant before drawing conclusions
- Document winners in the campaign performance tracker

### Regional Adaptation
- UAE / GCC: Price references in AED
- India: Price references in INR; consider local festival periods (Diwali, Eid) for seasonal copy swaps
- Bangladesh: BDT price references; use "Taka" not "BDT" in consumer-facing copy

### Prohibited Copy Patterns
- Do not use competitor brand names in cross-sell copy (legal risk)
- Do not make unverified speed claims (use only approved datasheet figures)
- Do not use countdown timers in cross-sell banners unless the promotion has a confirmed end date
- Do not show "average savings" without verified data from actual pricing analysis

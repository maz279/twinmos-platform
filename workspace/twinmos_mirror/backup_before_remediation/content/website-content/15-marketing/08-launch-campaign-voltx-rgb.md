## Why 6000 MT/s Is the Sweet Spot for DDR5 in 2026

Not all DDR5 speeds are equal in real-world impact. Here is why 6000 MT/s is the optimal target:

- **Intel Core Ultra platforms:** 6000 MT/s hits the DRAM Gear 1 boundary on Z790 and Z890 platforms — maximising bandwidth at the lowest latency penalty.
- **AMD Ryzen 9000 (Zen 5) platforms:** AMD's EXPO profiles are designed to run stably at 6000 MT/s with fabric ratio optimisation, delivering the best fclk alignment.
- **Real-world gaming:** Benchmarks consistently show DDR5-6000 provides the best FPS improvement per dollar in CPU-sensitive games — particularly at 1080p and 1440p with high-end GPUs.
- **Beyond 6000:** DDR5-6400 and above offer diminishing gaming returns and often require manual timing tuning. DDR5-6000 at CL36 runs stable out of the box on XMP/EXPO with the right platform.

VOLTX RGB 6000 CL36 hits this sweet spot — maximum platform compatibility, excellent timing, and zero manual configuration required.

## Platform Setup Guide

### Step 1: Install the Modules
Seat VOLTX RGB DDR5 modules in your motherboard's recommended dual-channel slots (typically A2 and B2 for Intel; consult your board manual).

### Step 2: Enable XMP 3.0 or EXPO
On first boot, enter BIOS/UEFI (Del or F2 at POST). Navigate to the memory or OC section. Select **XMP 3.0 Profile 1** (Intel) or **EXPO Profile 1** (AMD). This enables DDR5-6000 automatically — no manual timing entry.

### Step 3: Configure RGB Sync
Install your motherboard's RGB software (Armoury Crate for ASUS, MSI Center for MSI, RGB Fusion for Gigabyte, or Polychrome for ASRock). VOLTX RGB will appear automatically in the peripheral list. Select your lighting mode and colour, and synchronise with other connected ARGB devices.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do VOLTX RGB modules require a separate software installation?**
A: No separate TwinMOS software is needed. RGB control is handled entirely by your motherboard's native RGB application (Aura Sync, Mystic Light, RGB Fusion, or Polychrome). If your motherboard is not listed, use OpenRGB — an open-source alternative that supports a wide range of hardware.

**Q: Can I run four VOLTX RGB sticks in a quad-channel configuration?**
A: DDR5 on consumer Intel Z-series and AMD X-series motherboards operates in dual-channel mode regardless of module count. Four sticks (2×16GB + 2×16GB = 64GB) will operate in dual-channel. For true quad-channel, TwinMOS recommends appropriate server DIMM configurations.

**Q: Will XMP 3.0 void my warranty?**
A: No. Intel XMP 3.0 and AMD EXPO are industry-standard memory overclock profiles validated by TwinMOS. Enabling XMP/EXPO does not affect your warranty.

**Q: Are 8GB VOLTX RGB modules available?**
A: VOLTX RGB is available in 16GB and 32GB only. For 8GB non-RGB modules, see the VOLTX DDR5 UDIMM standard range.

**Q: My RGB isn't syncing with my motherboard. What should I do?**
A: Check that your RGB software is up to date. Ensure the module is fully seated. See our [RGB troubleshooting guide](/support/kb/rgb-pairing-guide/) for step-by-step diagnostics. If the issue persists, [contact support](/contact/support/).

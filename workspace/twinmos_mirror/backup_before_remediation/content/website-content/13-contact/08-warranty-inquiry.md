# Warranty Inquiry

Have questions about your product warranty or need to start a claim? Our warranty team is here to help you verify coverage, process returns, and get a replacement or refund as quickly as possible.

## Warranty Coverage Overview

TwinMOS products carry limited warranties against defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. Coverage varies by product category:

| Product Category | Standard Warranty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| DDR4 / DDR5 Memory Modules | Lifetime limited warranty | Covers manufacturing defects |
| PCIe Gen 3/4/5 NVMe SSDs | 3–5 years | Varies by model; check product page |
| SATA III SSDs | 3 years | Standard across all SATA models |
| Portable SSDs | 3 years | Includes external enclosures |
| USB Flash Drives | 2–5 years | Varies by series |
| MicroSD Cards | 2–5 years | Varies by series |

Coverage periods and terms may vary by region. For full details, exclusions, and regional variations, please review our [Warranty Policy](/support/warranty-policy/).

## Before Submitting a Claim

1. **Register your product** at [/support/warranty-registration/](/support/warranty-registration/) for faster processing
2. **Check warranty status** using our [Warranty Lookup](/support/warranty-lookup/) tool
3. **Try basic troubleshooting** — visit our [Support Center](/support/) for common fixes
4. **Gather your documentation** — proof of purchase is required for all claims

## RMA Process

1. **Submit Claim** — Complete the form with all required details and documentation
2. **Verification** — Our team verifies warranty coverage within **1–2 business days**
3. **RMA Authorization** — Approved claims receive an RMA number and return instructions
4. **Ship Product** — Send the defective product to the designated RMA center (shipping instructions provided)
5. **Inspection** — Product is inspected and tested upon receipt (2–3 business days)
6. **Resolution** — Replacement shipped or refund processed within **3–5 business days** of inspection

> **Important:** Do not return products without an approved RMA number. Unauthorized returns may be refused or delayed.

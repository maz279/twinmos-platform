# OEM / ODM Inquiry

TwinMOS offers comprehensive OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) and ODM (Original Design Manufacturer) solutions for memory modules, SSDs, flash storage, and enterprise hardware. Leverage our 27+ years of manufacturing expertise, Taiwan-based R&D, and global supply chain partnerships to bring your branded storage products to market.

## Our Manufacturing Capabilities

- **Custom PCB Design** — Tailored circuit layouts for specific form factors and performance requirements
- **Firmware Tuning & Customization** — Custom firmware with your branding, serial numbering, and feature sets
- **Branded Packaging & Labeling** — Retail-ready packaging design, holographic seals, and anti-counterfeiting measures
- **Volume Manufacturing** — Scalable production from pilot runs to high-volume orders with consistent quality
- **JEDEC-Compliant Testing** — Full compliance with JEDEC standards plus custom test protocols
- **Thermal & Power Optimization** — Custom heatsink designs, thermal profiling, and power management tuning
- **Global Certifications** — FCC, CE, UKCA, EAC, ROHS, REACH, BIS compliance support

## Engagement Process

1. **Initial Inquiry** — Submit the form with project details
2. **Feasibility Assessment** — Engineering team evaluates technical requirements (3–5 business days)
3. **Proposal & NDA** — Detailed proposal with pricing, timeline, and NDA execution
4. **Prototype Development** — Sample units for validation and testing
5. **Pilot Production** — Small-batch run for quality verification
6. **Mass Production** — Full-scale manufacturing with ongoing quality monitoring
7. **After-Sales Support** — Warranty backing and technical support for your customers

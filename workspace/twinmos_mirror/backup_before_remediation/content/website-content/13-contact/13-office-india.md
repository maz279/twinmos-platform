## BIS Compliance

TwinMOS is committed to full BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) compliance for all products sold in India:

- **BIS Registration** — All applicable product categories registered
- **IS Standards** — Compliance with relevant Indian Standards for electronics
- **Labeling Requirements** — Proper BIS marking and documentation on product packaging
- **Local Testing** — Products tested at BIS-recognized laboratories

For BIS-specific questions or certification documentation, email [legal@twinmos.com](mailto:legal@twinmos.com) with "BIS Compliance" in the subject line.

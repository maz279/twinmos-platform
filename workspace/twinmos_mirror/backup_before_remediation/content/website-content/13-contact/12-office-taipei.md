## Role & Responsibilities

TwinMOS Technologies Inc. (Taiwan) is the original founding entity, established in 1998 by William Chen. The Taipei office oversees:

- **Global Research & Development** — DRAM and SSD technology innovation, firmware development, and performance tuning
- **Product Strategy & Roadmaps** — Technology roadmaps for DDR5, PCIe Gen 5.0, and next-generation storage
- **Intellectual Property Management** — Patents, trademarks, and technology licensing
- **Semiconductor Partnerships** — Strategic relationships with NAND flash suppliers (Micron, SK Hynix) and controller vendors (SMI, Phison)
- **Group Governance** — Corporate oversight for all TwinMOS entities worldwide
- **Manufacturing Coordination** — Quality control and production planning with Taiwan and China facilities
- **COMPUTEX Participation** — Annual showcase at COMPUTEX Taipei (Nangang Exhibition Center)

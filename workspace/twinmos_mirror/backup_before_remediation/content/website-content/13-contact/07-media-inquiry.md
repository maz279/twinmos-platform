# Media & Press Inquiry

Welcome to the TwinMOS press center. We work with technology journalists, reviewers, analysts, and content creators worldwide to share accurate, timely information about our products, technologies, and company developments.

## What We Offer

- **Product Review Samples** — Qualified publications and reviewers can request samples of our latest DDR5 memory, PCIe Gen 5.0 SSDs, and portable storage devices
- **Executive Interviews** — Access to our leadership team including Chairman Mohd Mazharul Islam and regional managers for expert commentary on industry trends
- **High-Resolution Media Assets** — Product photography, logos, renders, and lifestyle imagery available via our [Media Kit](/media-kit/)
- **Press Releases & Launch Announcements** — Early access to embargoed news and product launch materials
- **Technology Background Briefings** — Deep-dive sessions on DDR5, PCIe Gen 5.0, NAND flash technology, and thermal management
- **Event Access** — Press passes and briefing opportunities at COMPUTEX, Gitex, and other trade shows
- **B-Roll & Video Assets** — Product demo footage and corporate video content

## Eligibility

Review samples and embargoed materials are available to:

- Established technology publications (online or print)
- YouTube reviewers and tech influencers with demonstrable audience
- Industry analysts and research firms
- Broadcast and podcast creators covering PC hardware
- Academic researchers studying memory/storage technology

*First-time requesters may be asked to provide audience metrics or recent work samples.*

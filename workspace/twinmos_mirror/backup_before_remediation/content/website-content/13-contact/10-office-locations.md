# Global Office Locations

TwinMOS operates through regional offices and authorized distribution partners across the Middle East, Africa, Asia, and Europe. With headquarters in Dubai and corporate R&D in Taiwan, we provide localized support and sales coverage in over 93 countries.

## Regional Hubs

| Location | Role | Primary Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| [Dubai, UAE](/contact/offices/dubai/) | Middle East & Africa Headquarters | MEA, MENA, Africa, CIS, South Asia |
| [Taipei, Taiwan](/contact/offices/taipei/) | Corporate R&D & Group Governance | Global R&D, IP, technology strategy |
| [New Delhi, India](/contact/offices/india/) | South Asia Sales & Compliance | India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Nepal |
| [Dhaka, Bangladesh](/contact/offices/bangladesh/) | Authorized Distribution Partner | Bangladesh market |

## Contact Guidelines

| Inquiry Type | Best Contact Method |
|---|---|
| **Sales & Partnerships** | Contact the regional office nearest to your market, or use the [Sales Inquiry](/contact/sales/) form |
| **Technical Support** | Submit a [support ticket](/contact/support/) for fastest resolution |
| **Press & Media** | Email [press@twinmos.com](mailto:press@twinmos.com) or contact Dubai HQ |
| **Legal & Compliance** | Email [legal@twinmos.com](mailto:legal@twinmos.com) for all legal matters |
| **Warranty Claims** | Use the [Warranty Inquiry](/contact/warranty/) form or contact your regional office |
| **General Inquiries** | Dubai headquarters remains the primary point of contact for all general questions |

- **Africa:** South Africa, Nigeria, Kenya, Egypt, Morocco, Algeria, Angola, Ghana
- **South Asia:** Bangladesh, India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Nepal
- **Southeast Asia:** Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, Philippines
- **Europe:** Germany, Netherlands, UK, France, Italy, Spain, Poland, CIS countries
- **East Asia:** Hong Kong, Japan, South Korea

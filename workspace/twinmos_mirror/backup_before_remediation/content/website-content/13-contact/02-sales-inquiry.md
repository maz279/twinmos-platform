# Sales Inquiry

Interested in TwinMOS memory modules, SSDs, portable storage, or enterprise solutions? Complete this form to connect with our regional sales team. We serve customers across the Middle East, Africa, South Asia, Europe, and Southeast Asia.

## How We Can Help

- **Product specifications and datasheets** — Detailed technical documentation for DRAM, SSD, and flash storage lines
- **Pricing for all volume tiers** — Consumer, commercial, SMB, and enterprise pricing structures
- **Regional availability and lead times** — Stock status and shipping estimates for your market
- **Distribution and reseller partnerships** — Margin structures, MOQs, and territory exclusivity
- **OEM/white-label opportunities** — Custom branding and configuration for volume buyers
- **Government and education pricing** — Special programs for public-sector and institutional buyers

## What Happens Next

1. **Acknowledgment** — Automated confirmation within minutes
2. **Routing** — Assigned to the regional sales manager for your territory
3. **Response** — Personalized reply within **1–2 business days** with pricing or next steps
4. **Follow-up** — Dedicated sales contact for ongoing discussions

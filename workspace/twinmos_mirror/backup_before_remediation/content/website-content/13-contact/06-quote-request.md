# Request a Quote

Need volume pricing for enterprise, government, educational, or large reseller projects? Submit your requirements and our sales team will prepare a tailored, competitive quotation. TwinMOS serves qualified B2B customers across the Middle East, Africa, South Asia, Europe, and Southeast Asia.

## Who We Serve

- **System Integrators & OEMs** — Build TwinMOS memory and storage into your systems
- **Government & Public-Sector Buyers** — Compliant products with formal quotation and tender support
- **Corporate IT Procurement Teams** — Volume pricing for desktop, laptop, and server upgrades
- **Educational Institutions** — Special academic pricing for labs, classrooms, and research facilities
- **Large-Volume Resellers** — Competitive tiered pricing for high-turnover partners
- **Data Center & Cloud Providers** — Enterprise-grade SSD and ECC memory quotations

## Information Needed

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Full Name | Yes | Primary contact for the quotation |
| Email Address | Yes | For quote delivery |
| Phone Number | Yes | For clarifications |
| Company / Organization | Yes | Legal name for quotation |
| Company Website | No | Helps validate the business |
| Country / Region | Yes | For regional pricing and shipping |
| City / Delivery Location | Yes | For freight calculation |
| Project Type | Yes | Corporate, Government, Education, Reseller, OEM, Data Center |
| SKU / Product Names | Yes | Specific TwinMOS models or general requirements |
| Quantities | Yes | Units per SKU or total volume |
| Target Delivery Timeline | Yes | When products are needed |
| Preferred Incoterms | No | EXW, FOB, CIF, DDP, etc. |
| Payment Terms | No | Net 30, Net 60, LC, advance payment |
| Budget Range | No | Helps us recommend the right tier |
| Special Requirements | No | Custom packaging, labeling, firmware, etc. |
| Message | No | Additional project context |

## Quote Turnaround

| Order Type | Typical Response |
|---|---|
| Standard volume (under 1,000 units) | 1–2 business days |
| Large enterprise (1,000–10,000 units) | 2–3 business days |
| Government / tender (formal process) | 3–5 business days |
| Custom/OEM configuration | 5–7 business days |

All quotations are valid for 30 days unless otherwise specified. Volume discounts are tiered based on quantity and commitment level.

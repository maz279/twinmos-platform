# Become a Distributor

Join the TwinMOS global distribution network and partner with a 27+ year heritage brand in memory and storage solutions. We are actively seeking experienced distribution partners in key markets across the Middle East, Africa, South Asia, Southeast Asia, Europe, and the CIS region.

## Why Partner With TwinMOS

- **Established brand recognition** — Trusted since 1998 across 93+ countries
- **Competitive margin structures** — Attractive pricing for authorized partners
- **Full product portfolio** — DDR4/DDR5 DRAM, PCIe Gen 3/4/5 SSDs, SATA SSDs, portable storage, USB solutions
- **Marketing support** — Co-marketing funds, promotional materials, and product training
- **Technical support** — Direct access to our engineering team for pre- and post-sales support
- **Territory protection** — Exclusive or semi-exclusive arrangements for committed partners
- **Gaming brand growth** — VOLTX RGB gaming line with strong enthusiast appeal

## What We Look For

- Established IT hardware distribution channels with proven reach
- Experience with memory, storage, or consumer electronics brands
- Local technical support and after-sales service capabilities
- Strong retail, e-commerce, or B2B customer relationships
- Financial stability and warehousing/logistics infrastructure
- Commitment to brand building and local marketing investment

## Application Process

1. **Submit Application** — Complete the form above with as much detail as possible
2. **Initial Review** — Our partnership team reviews within **5 business days**
3. **Due Diligence** — We may request a video call, references, or financial documents
4. **Partnership Agreement** — Approved partners receive a formal distribution agreement
5. **Onboarding** — Product training, portal access, and marketing kit delivery
6. **Go Live** — Place your first order and begin selling TwinMOS products

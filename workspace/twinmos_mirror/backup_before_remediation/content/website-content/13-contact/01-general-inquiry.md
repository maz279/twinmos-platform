# General Inquiry

Have a question that doesn't fit into a specific category? Whether you're curious about TwinMOS history, product availability in your region, career opportunities, or anything else, our team is here to help. Use the form below and we will route your message to the right department.

## Common Topics We Handle

- Company background, history, and corporate information
- Product availability and regional distribution
- General partnership or collaboration questions
- Career and internship inquiries
- Event and exhibition participation
- Sustainability and CSR initiatives
- Investor and corporate governance questions

## Form Fields

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Full Name | Yes | First and last name |
| Email Address | Yes | For response delivery |
| Phone Number | No | Include country code |
| Country / Region | Yes | Helps us route to the right team |
| Subject | Yes | Brief summary of your inquiry |
| Message | Yes | Detailed description (min. 20 characters) |
| Preferred Contact Method | No | Email or phone |

## What to Include for Faster Response

- A clear, specific subject line summarizing your question
- Relevant product names or model numbers (if applicable)
- Your country or region for localized routing
- Any deadlines or time-sensitive considerations

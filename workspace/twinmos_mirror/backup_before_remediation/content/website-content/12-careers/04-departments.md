# Departments at TwinMOS

**Find Your Place in the Future of Technology**

TwinMOS offers diverse career paths across six core functions. Each department plays a vital role in our mission to deliver reliable, high-performance memory and storage products to customers in 93+ countries. Whether you are a technical specialist, a creative strategist, or an operational expert, there is a place for you at TwinMOS.

---

## Engineering

**Building the Technology of Tomorrow**

The Engineering team is the creative engine behind every TwinMOS product. From concept to completion, our engineers design, develop, and validate the memory and storage solutions that power millions of devices worldwide.

### What We Do
- Design next-generation DDR5 DRAM modules and PCIe Gen 5 NVMe SSDs
- Develop firmware for SSD controllers and memory management
- Engineer thermal solutions including graphene heatsinks and aluminum heatspreaders
- Validate hardware compatibility across desktop, laptop, server, and gaming platforms
- Optimize power management and signal integrity for peak performance

### Career Paths
- Hardware Design Engineer (DRAM / SSD)
- Firmware Engineer
- PCB Layout Engineer
- Thermal Design Engineer
- Signal Integrity Engineer
- Validation & Compatibility Engineer
- R&D Scientist

### Key Skills
- Electrical engineering, computer engineering, or related technical degree
- Experience with CAD tools, simulation software, and lab equipment
- Knowledge of DDR architecture, NAND flash, PCIe interfaces
- Problem-solving mindset and attention to detail

## Sales

**Connecting Technology with the World**

The Sales team is the bridge between TwinMOS innovations and the global market. We build lasting relationships with distributors, system builders, enterprise clients, and channel partners across 93+ countries.

### What We Do
- Manage distributor relationships and channel partnerships worldwide
- Develop and execute regional sales strategies to meet revenue targets
- Identify and cultivate new business opportunities in emerging markets
- Support enterprise accounts with customized storage solutions
- Represent TwinMOS at trade shows including COMPUTEX, GITEX, and regional exhibitions

### Career Paths
- Regional Sales Manager
- Key Account Manager
- Business Development Representative
- Channel Sales Specialist
- Enterprise Sales Executive
- Sales Operations Analyst

### Key Skills
- Business, marketing, or engineering background
- Strong communication and negotiation abilities
- Relationship-building expertise
- Understanding of technology products and market dynamics
- Willingness to travel internationally

## Marketing

The Marketing team crafts the narrative that connects TwinMOS with tech enthusiasts, gamers, professionals, and businesses worldwide. We build brand awareness, drive product demand, and create compelling content across digital and physical channels.

- Plan and execute product launches for new DRAM and SSD lines
- Manage digital marketing campaigns across social media, search, and display
- Create product datasheets, videos, blog content, and technical documentation
- Support regional marketing initiatives tailored to local markets
- Organize event presence at COMPUTEX, gaming conventions, and industry expos
- Manage the TwinMOS brand identity and visual standards

### Career Paths
- Product Marketing Manager
- Digital Marketing Specialist
- Content Creator / Copywriter
- Social Media Manager
- Graphic Designer
- Event Marketing Coordinator
- Brand Manager

### Key Skills
- Marketing, communications, or design background
- Creativity and storytelling ability
- Proficiency with marketing tools and analytics platforms
- Understanding of technology products and target audiences
- Multilingual capabilities valued for global campaigns

## Operations

**Keeping the World Supplied**

The Operations team ensures that TwinMOS products move seamlessly from manufacturing facilities to customers worldwide. We manage the complex global supply chain that keeps inventory flowing and orders fulfilled.

### What We Do
- Oversee end-to-end supply chain and logistics operations
- Manage inventory planning and demand forecasting
- Coordinate with manufacturing partners in Taiwan and China
- Handle procurement of NAND flash, controllers, and components
- Ensure import/export compliance across multiple jurisdictions
- Optimize warehouse operations and distribution networks

### Career Paths
- Supply Chain Manager
- Procurement Specialist
- Logistics Coordinator
- Inventory Planner
- Import/Export Compliance Officer
- Warehouse Operations Manager
- Operations Analyst

### Key Skills
- Supply chain management, business, or engineering background
- Analytical and problem-solving capabilities
- Familiarity with ERP systems and inventory management tools
- Understanding of international trade regulations
- Attention to detail and process optimization mindset

## Quality Assurance

**Excellence in Every Product**

The Quality Assurance team is the guardian of TwinMOS's reputation. Every product that bears the TwinMOS name has passed through rigorous testing and validation — a standard we have maintained since 1998.

### What We Do
- Perform incoming quality control on raw materials and components
- Conduct in-process testing during manufacturing
- Execute burn-in validation and stress testing on finished products
- Perform final inspection before product shipment
- Investigate and analyze product failures to drive continuous improvement
- Maintain compliance with ISO, CE, FCC, RoHS, and REACH standards

### Career Paths
- Quality Assurance Engineer
- Lab Test Technician
- Failure Analysis Engineer
- Compliance Specialist
- Quality Control Inspector
- Reliability Engineer

### Key Skills
- Engineering or technical degree preferred
- Experience with testing equipment and methodologies
- Understanding of quality management systems (ISO 9001)
- Analytical thinking and methodical approach
- Commitment to precision and excellence

## Customer Service

**Supporting Our Global Community**

The Customer Service team is the voice of TwinMOS to end-users, distributors, and partners. We provide expert technical assistance, manage warranty programs, and ensure every customer interaction reflects our commitment to quality.

### What We Do
- Provide technical support for memory and SSD products
- Administer warranty policies and process RMA requests
- Assist distributors and partners with product inquiries
- Maintain knowledge bases and support documentation
- Gather customer feedback to inform product improvements
- Coordinate with engineering on escalated technical issues

### Career Paths
- Technical Support Specialist
- Warranty Administrator
- Customer Success Manager
- Support Team Lead
- Knowledge Base Manager
- Partner Support Coordinator

### Key Skills
- Strong technical aptitude and troubleshooting skills
- Excellent written and verbal communication
- Patience and empathy in customer interactions
- Familiarity with PC hardware, operating systems, and storage technologies
- Multilingual support capabilities valued

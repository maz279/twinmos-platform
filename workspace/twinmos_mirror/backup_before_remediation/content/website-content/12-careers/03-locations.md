# TwinMOS Office Locations

**A Global Network, A Local Presence**

TwinMOS maintains offices and operational presence across key markets in the Middle East, Asia, Europe, and North America. Each location plays a vital role in our mission to deliver cutting-edge memory and storage solutions worldwide.

| **Phone** | +886 970-368-077 |
| **Email** | sales@twinmos.com |
| **Functions** | Research & development, product design, manufacturing coordination, quality engineering |

The Taipei office is the heartbeat of TwinMOS innovation. Founded in 1998 by William Chen, this is the original home of TwinMOS and the center of our R&D efforts. Our engineers here design next-generation DDR5 memory modules, PCIe Gen 5 NVMe SSDs, and advanced storage solutions. Close proximity to Taiwan's semiconductor ecosystem gives our team access to world-class manufacturing partners and cutting-edge component suppliers.

**Career Opportunities in Taipei:** Hardware engineering, firmware development, PCB design, thermal engineering, product management, quality assurance, R&D leadership.

# Careers at TwinMOS

**Build the Future of Memory and Storage Technology**

TwinMOS is a global leader in memory and storage solutions, powering the next generation of computing across 93+ countries. For over 27 years, our products — from cutting-edge DDR5 DRAM and PCIe Gen 5 NVMe SSDs to portable storage solutions — have fueled gaming rigs, enterprise servers, creative workstations, and everyday devices worldwide.

When you join TwinMOS, you become part of a team that lives by the motto: **"Innovation, Perfection, and Quality."** Whether you are an experienced professional, a recent graduate, or a student seeking an internship, TwinMOS offers a dynamic environment where your ideas drive real impact.

## What We Value

Our culture is built on four core values that guide every decision we make:

- **Integrity** — We do business honestly and transparently with partners, customers, and each other
- **Quality** — 100% pre-delivery testing is not just for products; it reflects our standards in everything we do
- **Innovation** — From DDR1 to DDR5 and PCIe Gen 5, we continuously push technological boundaries
- **Collaboration** — Cross-functional teamwork across regions, disciplines, and time zones drives our success

# Internships & Graduate Programs at TwinMOS

**Launch Your Career at the Forefront of Memory and Storage Technology**

TwinMOS offers structured internship and graduate programs for students and recent graduates who want to build meaningful careers in the memory and storage technology industry. Whether you dream of designing next-generation DDR5 modules, crafting global marketing campaigns, or optimizing international supply chains, TwinMOS provides the platform to turn your ambitions into reality.

Our programs are designed to give you real-world experience, professional mentorship, and exposure to the full spectrum of operations at a global technology company.

---

## Who We Look For

We welcome ambitious candidates who are passionate about technology and eager to learn. Ideal candidates are studying or have recently graduated in:

- **Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related technical fields** — For hardware design, firmware, testing, and R&D roles
- **Business Administration, Marketing, or Supply Chain Management** — For sales, marketing, operations, and business development roles
- **Information Technology and Computer Science** — For software, firmware, technical support, and systems roles
- **Any discipline with demonstrated interest in hardware technology** — We value curiosity and passion as much as formal qualifications

### What Makes a Strong Candidate
- Strong academic performance (minimum GPA requirements may vary by program)
- Demonstrated interest in technology, hardware, or semiconductors
- Excellent communication and teamwork skills
- Proactive attitude and willingness to take initiative
- Fluency in English; additional languages are a plus for regional roles

---

## Program Structure

### Internship Program

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Duration** | 8–16 weeks (flexible based on academic calendar) |
| **Locations** | Dubai HQ, Taipei R&D Center, New Delhi office, Cologne office, San Jose office |
| **Schedule** | Full-time preferred; part-time arrangements possible for local students |
| **Start Dates** | Rolling admissions with primary intakes in January, May, and September |

Internships are project-based, meaning you will work on real assignments that contribute to actual business outcomes — not busywork. You will be assigned a mentor, participate in team meetings, and present your work to department leadership at the end of your program.

### Graduate Program

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Duration** | 12–24 months (varies by function and location) |
| **Structure** | Rotational assignments across multiple departments or deep specialization in one area |
| **Mentorship** | Dedicated mentor plus access to senior leadership |
| **Outcome** | Pathway to full-time employment upon successful completion |

Our graduate program is designed to fast-track high-potential talent into permanent roles. Participants gain broad exposure to the business while developing deep expertise in their chosen field.

### Hands-On Technical Experience
- Work with real DRAM, SSD, and storage technology products
- Participate in product testing, validation, and quality assurance processes
- Gain exposure to industry-standard tools and methodologies

### Business and Operational Exposure
- Understand how global supply chains function in the semiconductor industry

### Professional Development
- One-on-one mentorship from experienced professionals

### Career Advancement
- **Potential conversion to full-time roles** based on performance and business needs

## Available Internship Tracks

### Engineering Track
- Assist in hardware design and validation testing
- Support firmware development and debugging
- Participate in thermal and power management analysis
- Learn PCB layout and signal integrity fundamentals

### Marketing Track
- Assist in developing marketing strategies and campaigns
- Conduct market research and competitive analysis
- Create content for social media, blogs, and product pages
- Support event planning and trade show coordination

### Sales & Business Development Track
- Support regional sales teams with lead generation and market research
- Assist in preparing sales presentations and proposals
- Learn about distributor management and channel partnerships
- Contribute to market expansion planning

### Operations & Supply Chain Track
- Assist in inventory planning and demand forecasting
- Support procurement and vendor management activities
- Learn about logistics coordination and import/export compliance
- Participate in process improvement initiatives

### Customer Support Track
- Provide support for customer inquiries and technical issues
- Assist in maintaining knowledge bases and support documentation
- Learn about warranty administration and RMA processes
- Participate in product training sessions

## How to Apply

1. **Prepare your documents:**
   - Current resume or CV
   - Cover letter explaining your interest in TwinMOS and your preferred track
   - Academic transcripts (unofficial transcripts accepted)
   - Portfolio or work samples (for technical and creative roles)

2. **Submit your application:**
   - Use our [Job Application Form](/careers/apply/)
   - Select "Internship" or "Graduate Program" as the position type
   - Specify your preferred department and location

3. **Application timeline:**
   - Applications reviewed on a rolling basis
   - Shortlisted candidates contacted within 2–3 weeks
   - Interview process includes HR screening and department interview
   - Offers extended 4–6 weeks before program start date

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are internships paid?**
Yes, all TwinMOS internships offer competitive stipends aligned with local market standards. Specific compensation details are shared during the offer process.

**Do you provide accommodation or relocation support?**
Relocation assistance may be available for international candidates depending on the program and location. Local candidates are generally expected to arrange their own accommodation.

**Can I apply if I am not currently enrolled in university?**
Yes, recent graduates (within 12 months of graduation) are eligible for both internship and graduate program opportunities.

**Is there a possibility of full-time employment after the internship?**
Absolutely. Many of our full-time team members began their TwinMOS journey as interns. Conversion to full-time roles is based on performance, business needs, and mutual interest.

# TwinMOS Ghana

Ghana is a growing West African market for TwinMOS, supported by authorized distributors in Accra and regional coverage. As one of the most stable and business-friendly economies in West Africa, Ghana serves as a strategic entry point for TwinMOS expansion in the region.

> **Regional Features:** GHS pricing references, English language support, multi-distributor network in Accra, GSA compliance, and West Africa regional expansion hub.
> **Currency Disclaimer:** Prices displayed in GHS are indicative only. Contact local retailers or authorized distributors for actual pricing.

## Channel Support

Distributors provide local inventory, retailer onboarding, and warranty administration for the Ghanaian market. Fairgreen and CompuGhana offer nationwide coverage with established logistics networks.

## Support

Technical support and RMA services are coordinated through the authorized distributor network with escalation to TwinMOS Dubai HQ. Ghanaian customers benefit from:

- **Local English-language support**
- **Standard limited lifetime warranty (DRAM), 5-year warranty on CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSDs, and 3-year warranty on Gen4/Gen3 NVMe and SATA SSDs**
- Product authenticity verification
- Authorized service center coordination
- RMA processing through distributor network

## Compliance

TwinMOS products comply with:

- **CE marking** — European conformity baseline
- **RoHS compliance** — restriction of hazardous substances
- **FCC certification** — electromagnetic compatibility
- **GSA (Ghana Standards Authority)** — where applicable for imported electronics
- **Ghanaian import regulations** — customs and consumer protection alignment

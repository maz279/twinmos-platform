# TwinMOS Southeast Asia

Southeast Asia is a strategic growth region for TwinMOS, served by established distributors in Singapore and Malaysia with expanding coverage across ASEAN markets. The region's rapid digitalization, gaming culture, and manufacturing base create strong demand for memory and storage solutions.

## Support

Warranty and technical support are administered through authorized distributors with escalation to TwinMOS headquarters. Regional support includes:

- English and local language technical assistance
- Standard limited lifetime warranty (DRAM), 5-year warranty on CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSDs, and 3-year warranty on Gen4/Gen3 NVMe and SATA SSDs
- Regional RMA processing through Singapore and Kuala Lumpur hubs
- Product training for reseller partners

## Compliance

TwinMOS products meet ASEAN regulatory requirements and international standards:

- **CE marking** — EU conformity baseline for electronics
- **RoHS compliance** — restriction of hazardous substances
- **FCC** — electromagnetic compatibility certification
- **IMDA** — Infocomm Media Development Authority (Singapore) alignment
- **MCMC** — Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission alignment
- **NBTC** — National Broadcasting and Telecommunications Commission (Thailand) alignment
- **SDPPI** — Direktorat Jenderal Sumber Daya dan Perangkat Pos dan Informatika (Indonesia) alignment
- **ASEAN harmonized standards** — regional technical regulation alignment

## Market Focus

TwinMOS addresses multiple Bangladeshi market segments:

- **Consumer & gaming** — DDR5 and DDR4 upgrades for enthusiasts and esports venues
- **System integrator builds** — reliable memory for custom PC assembly shops
- **Enterprise & SMB** — DDR4 SO-DIMM, SATA SSD, and bulk storage for offices
- **Education & government** — institutional procurement programs and tenders
- **Budget-conscious buyers** — DDR3 and entry-level DDR4 for affordable upgrades

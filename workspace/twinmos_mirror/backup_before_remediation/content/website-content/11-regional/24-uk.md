# TwinMOS United Kingdom

The United Kingdom is a key European market for TwinMOS, with products certified under UKCA (UK Conformity Assessed) standards for Great Britain. Post-Brexit regulatory requirements are fully addressed to ensure seamless market access for TwinMOS memory and storage products.

## Compliance

TwinMOS products for the UK market carry comprehensive certifications:

- **UKCA marking** — required for goods placed on the market in Great Britain
- **CE compatibility** — where dual-marking is applicable under Northern Ireland Protocol
- **RoHS compliance** — restriction of hazardous substances
- **REACH compliance** — UK REACH chemicals regulation adherence
- **WEEE compliance** — electronic waste recycling requirements
- **UK GDPR** — UK General Data Protection Regulation compliance
- **Data Protection Act 2018** — UK data protection legislation
- **BSI standards** — British Standards Institution alignment

# TwinMOS Europe

TwinMOS serves the European market through authorized distributors in Germany and the Netherlands, with products certified for EU compliance including CE marking, UKCA, and REACH. Europe represents a key market for TwinMOS across consumer, gaming, SMB, and enterprise segments.

## Authorized Distributors

### Mediacom
- **Market:** Germany
- **Role:** Authorized distributor for TwinMOS products in the DACH region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland)

### Zynex
- **Market:** Netherlands
- **Role:** Authorized distributor for TwinMOS products in the Benelux region (Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg)

> **Note:** Specific distributor addresses and phone numbers in Europe are not verified for public release. Contact TwinMOS for authorized distributor information in Europe.

## Compliance & Certifications

TwinMOS products for the European market meet comprehensive regulatory requirements:

- **CE marking** — Declaration of Conformity for EU market access
- **UKCA** — UK Conformity Assessed for Great Britain
- **REACH** — Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals
- **RoHS** — Restriction of Hazardous Substances Directive 2011/65/EU
- **GDPR** — General Data Protection Regulation (EU 2016/679) compliance
- **WEEE** — Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment directive compliance
- **Ecodesign** — Energy-related products directive alignment
- **German BSI** — Federal Office for Information Security baseline
- **Dutch NEN** — Netherlands standardization institute alignment

## Support

Warranty and technical support are administered through authorized distributors with escalation to TwinMOS headquarters. European customers benefit from:

- Local language support (German, Dutch, French, English)
- Standard limited lifetime warranty (DRAM), 5-year warranty on CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSDs, and 3-year warranty on Gen4/Gen3 NVMe and SATA SSDs
- RMA processing through regional service centers
- Product compliance documentation and Declarations of Conformity

# TwinMOS Morocco

Morocco is a key North African market for TwinMOS, with distribution coverage spanning Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, and other major cities through a network of authorized partners. Morocco's growing IT sector and strategic position as a gateway to Francophone Africa make it a priority market for TwinMOS expansion.

> **Regional Features:** MAD pricing references, French and Arabic language support (Phase 2/3), multi-distributor network, and Francophone Africa gateway positioning.
> **Currency Disclaimer:** Prices displayed in MAD are indicative only. Contact local retailers or authorized distributors for actual pricing.

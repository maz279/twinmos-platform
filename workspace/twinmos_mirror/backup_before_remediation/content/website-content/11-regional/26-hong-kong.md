## Support

Warranty claims and technical support are coordinated through Asiapac with direct escalation to TwinMOS headquarters. Hong Kong customers benefit from:

- English and Cantonese technical support
- Standard limited lifetime warranty (DRAM), 5-year warranty on CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSDs, and 3-year warranty on Gen4/Gen3 NVMe and SATA SSDs
- Efficient RMA processing through local service centers
- Product authenticity verification

# TwinMOS Botswana & Lesotho

TwinMOS serves the Southern African markets of Botswana and Lesotho through a combined regional approach, leveraging distributor partnerships that span the broader Southern Africa region. Both markets offer stable business environments with growing demand for IT infrastructure.

## Regional Coverage

Botswana and Lesotho are supported as part of TwinMOS's Southern Africa expansion, with logistics and distribution coordinated through regional hub partners based in South Africa and Namibia.

> **Seeking Distribution Partners:** TwinMOS is actively seeking verified, authorized distributors with local presence in Botswana and Lesotho. For current authorized distributor information, please contact TwinMOS regional sales.

## Market Opportunity

### Botswana
- **Mining & resources** — IT infrastructure for extraction and logistics companies
- **Government & public sector** — e-government and digital service delivery
- **Financial services** — Gaborone's banking and insurance sector
- **Education** — university and technical college IT upgrades

### Lesotho
- **Textile & manufacturing** — factory automation and ERP systems
- **Government** — public sector digitization projects
- **Agriculture** — technology for irrigation and supply chain management
- **Cross-border trade** — logistics technology for South Africa trade corridor

# TwinMOS Rwanda

Rwanda is an East African market for TwinMOS memory and storage solutions. Known as the "Singapore of Africa" for its business-friendly policies and rapid digital transformation, Rwanda presents emerging opportunities for IT distribution across Kigali and secondary cities.

## Market Opportunity

Rwanda's technology sector is expanding rapidly, driven by:

- **Government digitization** — Smart Rwanda initiatives and e-government projects
- **Education sector** — One Laptop Per Child programs and university IT upgrades
- **Financial services** — Kigali's growing fintech and banking sector
- **Tourism & hospitality** — technology infrastructure for hotels and conference centers
- **Entrepreneurship** — Kigali Innovation City and tech startup ecosystem

TwinMOS products comply with international standards and Rwandan regulations:

- **CE marking** — EU conformity baseline for electronics
- **RoHS compliance** — restriction of hazardous substances
- **FCC** — electromagnetic compatibility certification

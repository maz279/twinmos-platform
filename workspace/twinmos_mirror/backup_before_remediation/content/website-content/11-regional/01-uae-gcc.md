# TwinMOS UAE & GCC

The UAE is the headquarters region for TwinMOS Technologies Middle East FZE, serving as the central operations hub for the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) and broader Middle East & Africa markets. Established on 21st September 2001, the Dubai headquarters coordinates global distribution, R&D liaison, quality assurance, and regional partner management.

> **Regional Features:** Arabic language support (Phase 2, RTL layout), AED pricing references, local distributor directory, and UAE-specific warranty services.

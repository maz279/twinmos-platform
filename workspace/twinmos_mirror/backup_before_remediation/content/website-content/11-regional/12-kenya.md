# TwinMOS Kenya

Kenya is an important East African market for TwinMOS, with distribution partners serving Nairobi and extending coverage across the region. As East Africa's leading technology hub and home to a vibrant startup ecosystem, Kenya offers strong demand for reliable memory and storage solutions.

## Support

Warranty and technical support are administered through the authorized distributor network with direct access to TwinMOS regional support teams. Each distributor provides:

- Local technical expertise and RMA coordination
- **Standard limited lifetime warranty (DRAM), 5-year warranty on CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSDs, and 3-year warranty on Gen4/Gen3 NVMe and SATA SSDs**
- English-language technical support
- Product authenticity verification

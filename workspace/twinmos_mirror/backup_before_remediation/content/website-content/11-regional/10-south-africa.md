# TwinMOS South Africa

South Africa is a leading sub-Saharan African market for TwinMOS, served by a strong distributor network covering Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, and beyond. As the most industrialized economy in Africa, South Africa offers a mature IT channel with opportunities across consumer, gaming, enterprise, and government sectors.

> **Regional Features:** ZAR pricing references, English language support, multi-distributor nationwide coverage, SANS/ICASA compliance, and Southern Africa regional hub status.

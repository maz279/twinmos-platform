# TwinMOS India

TwinMOS maintains a dedicated presence in India through **TwinMOS Technologies (India) Pvt. Ltd.**, based in New Delhi. The Indian market is a strategic priority (P0) for memory and storage expansion across consumer, system integrator (SI), and enterprise channels. With a population exceeding 1.4 billion and a rapidly growing digital economy, India represents one of the most significant opportunities for TwinMOS in the South Asia region.

## Compliance & Certifications

### BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards)

TwinMOS products destined for the Indian market are planned for **BIS compliance certification** as part of Phase 2 market readiness.

> **BIS certification planned for Phase 2.**

All DRAM and SSD products meet JEDEC standards and are manufactured under ISO 9001:2015 partner frameworks. TwinMOS is committed to aligning with Indian regulatory requirements as market operations scale, including:

- **BIS Registration** — planned for Phase 2 (Months 6–9)
- **WPC (Wireless Planning and Coordination)** — where applicable for wireless-enabled products
- **India DPDP Act 2023** — consent management for Indian users' personal data
- **LMPC (Legal Metrology Packaged Commodities)** — labeling compliance where required

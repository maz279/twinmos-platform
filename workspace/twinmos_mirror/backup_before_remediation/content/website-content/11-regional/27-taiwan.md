# TwinMOS Taiwan

Taiwan is the founding home of TwinMOS Technologies Inc., established in 1998. The Taipei office remains central to our R&D, manufacturing partnerships, and product innovation.

## Company Heritage

TwinMOS Technologies Inc. was founded in Taipei, Taiwan in 1998 by William Chen. Today, the Taiwan entity continues to drive product research and development, manufacturing partner coordination, and global supply chain management.

## Authorized Distributor

**Airwave** is the authorized TwinMOS distributor for Taiwan, serving the local consumer, SI, and enterprise markets.

## Manufacturing and Quality

Taiwan-based manufacturing partner facilities, combined with 100% pre-delivery testing, ensure that TwinMOS products meet the highest quality standards before reaching global markets.

## Manufacturing and Quality

Taiwan-based manufacturing partner facilities, combined with 100 percent pre-delivery testing, ensure that TwinMOS products meet the highest quality standards before reaching global markets. Key quality processes include:

- **Component sourcing** - direct relationships with Tier 1 DRAM and NAND suppliers
- **PCB assembly** - automated SMT lines with AOI inspection
- **Burn-in testing** - 100 percent stress testing before packaging
- **Final QA** - functional verification and compatibility testing
- **Traceability** - batch tracking from wafer to finished product

## Support

Warranty and technical support for Taiwan are coordinated through Airwave with direct escalation to TwinMOS headquarters. Taiwan customers benefit from:

- Local Mandarin and English technical support
- Standard limited lifetime warranty (DRAM), 5-year warranty on CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSDs, and 3-year warranty on Gen4/Gen3 NVMe and SATA SSDs
- Efficient RMA processing through local service centers
- Direct access to factory-level technical expertise

## Research and Development

The Taiwan R&D team focuses on:

- Next-generation memory technologies (DDR6 roadmap)
- High-speed PCIe storage controllers
- Thermal optimization for compact devices
- Power efficiency improvements for mobile and data center applications

## Compliance

TwinMOS products manufactured and distributed in Taiwan meet:

- **BSMI** — Bureau of Standards, Metrology and Inspection certification
- **CE marking** — EU conformity for export markets
- **RoHS compliance** — restriction of hazardous substances
- **FCC** — electromagnetic compatibility for North American export
- **JEDEC standards** — memory industry standard compliance
- **ISO 9001** — quality management system certification

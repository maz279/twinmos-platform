### VOLTX DDR5 RGB Achieves Full RGB Sync Certification

**Date:** March 2025  
**Category:** Product Launches

VOLTX DDR5 RGB now officially supports all four major motherboard RGB ecosystems out of the box — no adapters, no third-party utilities required.

**Certified Platforms:**
- **ASUS Aura Sync** (Armoury Crate) — Full addressable RGB control
- **Gigabyte RGB Fusion** (GCC) — Multi-zone per-LED customization
- **MSI Mystic Light** (MSI Center) — Dynamic profiles and game-sync
- **ASRock Polychrome Sync** — Custom patterns and color palettes

**What This Means for Builders:**
Plug in your VOLTX DDR5 RGB modules, install your motherboard's RGB software, and your memory is automatically detected and ready to sync. One ecosystem. Total control.

[QVL verification](/support/compatibility-finder/) is available for all major motherboard models to confirm both memory stability and RGB functionality.

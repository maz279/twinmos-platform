## Build Tips & Best Practices

### Memory Placement
Always install VOLTX DDR5 modules in the slots recommended by your motherboard manual — typically A2 and B2 (slots 2 and 4 from the CPU) for dual-channel operation. This ensures optimal signal integrity and maximum bandwidth.

### RGB Sync Strategy
Plan your RGB ecosystem before you build. VOLTX DDR5 RGB syncs with ASUS Aura Sync, Gigabyte RGB Fusion, MSI Mystic Light, and ASRock Polychrome — but mixing ecosystems (e.g., ASUS motherboard with MSI GPU) can create sync gaps. Stick to one platform when possible.

### Storage Hierarchy
Use a tiered storage approach for the best gaming experience:
- **CoreX Pro Gen5 (1–2TB):** OS, competitive games, frequently played titles
- **Xtreme Gen4 (2TB):** Large game library, open-world RPGs
- **Alpha Pro Gen3 (512GB):** Secondary storage, older titles, emulators
- **ELITE Drive Pro (1TB portable):** LAN parties, console expansion, game backups

### Thermal Management
DDR5 at 6000MHz generates more heat than DDR4. Ensure your case has:
- Front intake fans feeding cool air across the motherboard
- Top/rear exhaust fans creating positive pressure
- At least 2–3 inches of clearance above the DIMM slots for airflow

### Cable Management for Showcase Builds
Good cable management is not just aesthetics — it improves airflow and makes future upgrades easier. Route cables behind the motherboard tray, use velcro ties, and leave the area around the memory and GPU clean for maximum visual impact.

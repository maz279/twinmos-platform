# OEM / ODM Program

TwinMOS offers comprehensive OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) and ODM (Original Design Manufacturer) services for businesses seeking custom-branded or specification-tailored memory and storage solutions. Leveraging 27+ years of manufacturing expertise, Taiwan-based R&D, and a robust global supply chain, we partner with leading brands to deliver products that meet exacting requirements.

## Why Partner with TwinMOS for OEM/ODM?

- **Proven manufacturing heritage** — since 1998, with Taiwan-based production facilities and regional partner facilities
- **Full-spectrum capabilities** — from standard DRAM modules to cutting-edge PCIe Gen 5.0 SSDs
- **Flexible engagement models** — OEM for branded integration, ODM for fully custom designs
- **Long-term supply security** — multi-year agreements with committed capacity allocation
- **Quality assurance** — 100% pre-delivery testing, ISO 9001:2015 framework, and multi-stage QC
- **Competitive economics** — direct manufacturing access delivering strong value at volume

## OEM Partnership

Integrate TwinMOS products into your systems under your own brand. Our OEM program is ideal for PC manufacturers, system builders, and device makers who want reliable, proven memory and storage with their own branding.

### OEM Services Include:
- **Custom labeling and packaging** — your brand, your design, our quality
- **Firmware customization** — tailored product identifiers, serial numbers, and feature sets
- **Bundle integration** — pre-installed components in your systems with validated compatibility
- **Volume pricing** — tiered pricing structures scaled to your production volumes
- **Long-term supply agreements** — 12- to 36-month contracts with price stability and capacity reservation
- **White-label datasheets** — technical documentation branded for your sales channels

## ODM Partnership

Collaborate with TwinMOS engineering to develop fully custom memory and storage products designed from the ground up for your specific application.

### ODM Capabilities:
- **Custom form factors** — non-standard PCB layouts, specialized connectors, unique physical dimensions
- **Specialized performance tuning** — optimized firmware for specific workloads (AI/ML, video editing, database, gaming)
- **Unique industrial or enterprise specifications** — extended temperature ranges, enhanced shock/vibration resistance, conformal coating
- **End-to-end design and manufacturing** — from concept and electrical design to validation, certification, and mass production
- **Custom thermal solutions** — graphene heatsheets, aluminum heatsinks, or passive/active cooling integration
- **Security features** — hardware encryption, secure erase, write-protect switches, tamper-evident designs

### ODM Development Process:

| Phase | Activities | Timeline |
|-------|------------|----------|
| **1. Requirements** | Joint workshops to define specs, performance targets, and compliance needs | 2–4 weeks |
| **2. Feasibility** | Engineering assessment, BOM estimation, and prototype planning | 2–3 weeks |
| **3. Design** | Schematic, PCB layout, firmware development, and mechanical design | 4–8 weeks |
| **4. Prototype** | EVT (Engineering Validation Test) samples and initial testing | 3–4 weeks |
| **5. Validation** | DVT (Design Validation Test), compatibility testing, and certification prep | 4–6 weeks |
| **6. Pilot Run** | PVT (Production Validation Test) and ramp readiness review | 2–3 weeks |
| **7. Mass Production** — | Sustained manufacturing with ongoing quality monitoring | Ongoing |

*Typical total ODM cycle: 4–6 months from requirements to mass production.*

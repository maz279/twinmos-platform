# TwinMOS India

TwinMOS is committed to the Indian market — one of the world's fastest-growing technology economies. Through our India subsidiary and expanding distribution network, we deliver high-performance memory and storage solutions to consumers, businesses, and system builders across the country.

## About TwinMOS Technologies (India) Pvt. Ltd.

**TwinMOS Technologies (India) Pvt. Ltd.** is headquartered in New Delhi and serves as the operational hub for TwinMOS in the Indian subcontinent. The India team manages national distribution, regulatory compliance, partner relationships, and local marketing initiatives.

### India Operations
- **Headquarters:** New Delhi, India
- **Leadership:** Mohammad Zahirul Islam (Director), Mohammed Abdul Mannan (Director)
- **Focus Areas:** Consumer retail, SMB, enterprise, government, and education segments
- **Regulatory Compliance:** BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) certification for applicable products

## Distribution Network

### Current Distribution Structure
TwinMOS products are available in India through a combination of direct subsidiary operations and authorized distribution partnerships.

#### TwinMOS Technologies (India) Pvt. Ltd.
- **Role:** National distribution and channel management
- **Coverage:** Pan-India presence with focus on North, West, and South India
- **Channels:** E-commerce platforms, retail chains, system integrators, and direct corporate sales

#### Supertron Electronics Partnership (Conditional)
TwinMOS has entered into a conditional distribution partnership with **Supertron Electronics Private Limited** to expand product availability across India.

- **Status:** Conditional / Under development — pending final agreement execution
- **Coverage:** Proposed national distribution network leveraging Supertron's extensive IT distribution infrastructure
- **Focus Segments:** Consumer, SMB, and enterprise
- **Expected Benefits:** Broader retail reach, enhanced logistics, and deeper market penetration

*This partnership is subject to completion of contractual formalities. Updates will be published upon confirmation.*

## Regulatory Compliance

### BIS Certification
TwinMOS is actively pursuing BIS certification for products sold in the Indian market to ensure compliance with local quality and safety standards.

- **Certified products:** Selected DDR4 and SSD SKUs (check current status with India team)
- **In-progress:** DDR5 memory modules and additional SSD models
- **Labeling:** BIS-marked products carry the ISI mark as required

### Import & Customs
- TwinMOS products are imported under valid IEC (Import Export Code)
- Proper HS code classification for memory and storage devices
- Compliance with Indian customs regulations and duty structures

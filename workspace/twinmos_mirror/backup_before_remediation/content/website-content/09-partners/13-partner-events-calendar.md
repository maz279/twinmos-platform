### Event Types

**Distributor Summits**
Annual or bi-annual gatherings for authorized distributors to network, share best practices, and align on strategic priorities. Summits typically include:
- Executive keynote and business review
- Product roadmap presentations
- Sales and marketing workshops
- Awards and recognition ceremony
- Networking dinners and cultural activities

**Training Webinars**
Monthly online sessions covering sales techniques, technical topics, and product updates. All webinars are recorded and archived for on-demand viewing.

**Product Launch Briefings**
Exclusive pre-launch briefings for partners to prepare go-to-market strategies before public announcement. Briefings include embargoed specifications, competitive positioning, and launch toolkit distribution.

**Trade Show Appearances**
TwinMOS participates in major global technology exhibitions. Partners are invited to join us at:
- **COMPUTEX Taipei** — annual showcase of latest products and innovations
- **GITEX Global** — Middle East's largest technology exhibition (planned Phase 2)
- **CES** — Consumer Electronics Show, Las Vegas (planned Phase 2)
- **Regional trade shows** — local exhibitions supported by distributor partners

## How to Register

Partner events are **invite-only** for authorized distributors, resellers, and system builders.

### Registration Process:
1. **Check the Partner Portal** — event invitations and registration links are posted in the Events Calendar section
2. **Email invitation** — registered partners receive email invitations 2–4 weeks before each event
3. **Confirm attendance** — RSVP through the portal or by replying to the invitation email
4. **Receive event details** — agenda, venue information, and preparation materials sent one week prior

*Space at in-person events is limited. Registration is confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis.*

## Host a Local Partner Event

TwinMOS encourages distributors to host local partner events, reseller training days, and product demonstrations. Marketing Development Fund (MDF) support may be available for eligible activities.

### Eligible Local Events:
- Reseller training days and certification workshops
- Local technology exhibitions and consumer electronics shows
- Product launch events and press conferences
- Roadshows visiting multiple cities within your territory
- Gaming tournaments and esports sponsorships featuring TwinMOS products

### MDF Support for Events:
- Up to 50% co-funding for eligible event costs (venue, catering, materials)
- TwinMOS speaker or representative attendance (subject to availability)
- Promotional product samples and demo units
- Co-branded marketing materials and giveaways

### How to Request Support:
1. Submit an event proposal through the Partner Portal at least 6 weeks before the event date
2. Include event objectives, target audience, expected attendance, and budget breakdown
3. TwinMOS reviews and approves within 10 business days
4. Execute the event and submit post-event report with photos and attendance records
5. Receive reimbursement per agreed terms

[Learn About MDF Program](/partners/mdf-program-reserved)

## Event Policies

- **Attendance eligibility** — events are restricted to authorized partners in good standing
- **Substitution policy** — registered attendees may be substituted with colleague notification 48 hours in advance
- **Cancellation** — please cancel at least 7 days before the event to allow waitlisted partners to attend
- **Conduct** — partners are expected to represent TwinMOS professionally at all events
- **Photography** — event photos may be used in TwinMOS marketing materials; notify staff if you prefer not to be photographed

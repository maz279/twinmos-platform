# TwinMOS Bangladesh

Bangladesh is one of TwinMOS's most important markets, with a strong partner ecosystem that has delivered quality memory and storage solutions to consumers, businesses, and gamers for over 27 years. Our authorized distributors and retail partners ensure genuine TwinMOS products are available nationwide — from Dhaka to Chittagong, Sylhet to Rajshahi.

## Authorized Distributors

### Smart Technologies (BD) Ltd.
- **Address:** 156 Mirpur Road, Dhaka-1205
- **Role:** Authorized National Distributor
- **Partner Since:** 1998
- **Specialization:** Full portfolio distribution — DRAM, SSDs, portable storage, flash products
- **Channels:** Retail, corporate, government, and e-commerce
- **Website:** [smartbd.com](https://smartbd.com)

Smart Technologies (BD) Ltd. is one of Bangladesh's largest ICT distributors, representing 100+ global technology brands. As TwinMOS's longest-standing distribution partner, Smart Technologies has played a pivotal role in establishing TwinMOS as a leading memory brand in Bangladesh.

### Tech Valley Distribution
- **Address:** Dhaka, Bangladesh
- **Role:** Authorized TwinMOS Distributor
- **Partner Since:** 2015
- **Specialization:** Gaming and enthusiast segment focus
- **Channels:** Retail, system builders, gaming cafes

Tech Valley Distribution has been instrumental in growing TwinMOS's presence in Bangladesh's gaming and PC enthusiast community, with particular strength in RGB memory and high-performance SSD distribution.

## Authorized Retailers & Resellers

### Ryans IT Limited
- **Role:** Authorized Retailer & Reseller
- **Channels:** Physical stores and e-commerce (ryans.com)
- **Specialization:** Consumer electronics, PC components, and accessories
- **Coverage:** Nationwide with flagship stores in major cities

Ryans IT is one of Bangladesh's most recognized technology retailers, offering a wide range of TwinMOS products including DDR4/DDR5 memory, SATA and NVMe SSDs, USB flash drives, and memory cards.

### Star Tech
- **Role:** Authorized Retailer
- **Channels:** Physical stores and online store
- **Specialization:** PC components, laptops, and accessories
- **Coverage:** Multiple locations across Bangladesh

Star Tech provides consumers and system builders with access to genuine TwinMOS components, competitive pricing, and knowledgeable sales support.

### EERNA
- **Role:** Authorized Partner & System Integrator
- **Channels:** B2B sales and custom PC builds
- **Specialization:** Enterprise solutions and system integration
- **Coverage:** Corporate and government sectors

EERNA serves Bangladesh's business and government sectors with TwinMOS memory and storage solutions integrated into custom workstations and server configurations.

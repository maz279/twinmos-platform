# Why Partner with TwinMOS

TwinMOS is a globally recognized brand in memory and storage solutions, trusted by consumers, businesses, and system builders across 93+ countries for over 27 years. Partnering with us means aligning with a heritage of quality, continuous innovation, and competitive price-performance — while building a profitable, sustainable business in your region.

## Financial Benefits

### Competitive Margins
TwinMOS offers attractive pricing structures designed to ensure healthy distributor margins while remaining competitive in the market. Our tiered pricing model rewards volume commitment and performance.

- **Standard distributor margin:** Competitive baseline on all product categories
- **Volume rebate tiers:** Quarterly rebates scaling with purchase volume
- **New product launch incentives:** Enhanced margins during introductory periods
- **Promotional pricing access:** Special campaigns for seasonal demand (back-to-school, holiday, gaming events)

### Marketing Development Funds (MDF)
TwinMOS co-invests in partner-led marketing activities that drive mutual growth. Eligible activities include:

- Local trade shows and consumer electronics exhibitions
- Digital marketing campaigns (search, social, display)
- Co-branded print and outdoor advertising
- In-store displays and point-of-sale materials
- Partner-hosted seminars and product demonstrations
- Showroom and demo unit setups

*MDF allocations are negotiated annually based on territory size, sales targets, and marketing plan quality.*

### Payment & Commercial Terms
- **Flexible payment terms** — Net 30/60/90 based on credit assessment and partnership tenure
- **Letter of Credit (L/C) support** for large international orders
- **Consignment inventory programs** available for strategic partners
- **Price protection** — protection against announced price decreases on held inventory

## Operational Support

### Dedicated Account Management
Every authorized distributor is assigned a dedicated TwinMOS account manager who serves as your single point of contact for:

- Order management and logistics coordination
- Marketing campaign planning and execution
- Technical escalations and product inquiries
- Quarterly business reviews and strategic planning
- New product introductions and go-to-market support

### Reliable Logistics & Fulfillment
Our Dubai headquarters in the Dubai Airport Free Zone (DAFZA) serves as a global logistics hub, enabling efficient distribution to Middle East, Africa, South Asia, CIS, and beyond.

- **Multi-channel fulfillment** — sea freight, air cargo, and express courier options
- **Regional stocking hubs** — strategic inventory positioning to reduce lead times
- **Order tracking** — real-time shipment visibility through the Partner Portal
- **Flexible minimum order quantities** — scaled to match your market size and turnover

### Inventory Programs
- **Safety stock agreements** — reserved inventory for high-demand SKUs
- **Forecasting collaboration** — joint demand planning to optimize stock levels
- **Product lifecycle management** — advance notice of end-of-life transitions and successor recommendations

## Marketing & Brand Support

### Co-Branded Marketing Assets
Access a comprehensive library of ready-to-use marketing materials through the Partner Portal:

- **Product photography** — high-resolution hero shots, lifestyle images, and 360° product views
- **Digital banners** — web and social media ad templates in common sizes
- **Print materials** — brochures, line cards, datasheets, and comparison guides
- **Video content** — product unboxing, installation guides, and benchmark highlights
- **Brand guidelines** — logo usage rules, color palettes, typography, and co-branding templates

### Product Launch Support
TwinMOS coordinates go-to-market campaigns for every major product launch:

- Pre-launch briefing kits with embargoed specifications
- Launch day press releases and social media toolkits
- Coordinated retailer and e-commerce listing support
- Launch event support (virtual or in-person)

### Event Sponsorship & Participation
- **Trade show support** — booth design assistance, demo units, and staffing for major events (COMPUTEX, GITEX, CES)
- **Partner summits** — annual distributor gatherings for networking, training, and strategic alignment
- **Local event co-funding** — MDF support for partner-hosted events and roadshows

## Technical & Training Support

### Product Training Programs
Equip your sales and technical teams with the knowledge to succeed:

- **TwinMOS Certified Sales Professional** — product positioning, competitive differentiation, and objection handling
- **TwinMOS Certified Technical Specialist** — memory technology fundamentals, SSD interfaces, compatibility, and troubleshooting
- **Webinar series** — monthly technical deep dives on new products and market trends
- **Self-paced e-learning** — on-demand modules accessible through the Partner Portal

### Pre-Sales Engineering Support
For complex enterprise and government deals, TwinMOS provides:

- **Technical specification reviews** — ensuring proposed solutions meet customer requirements
- **Compatibility validation** — testing and certification for specific platform configurations
- **Custom quotation support** — large-volume and specialized SKU pricing
- **Proof-of-concept assistance** — evaluation units and benchmarking support

### Warranty & Service Administration
- **Streamlined RMA processes** — online claim submission with 48-hour acknowledgment
- **Advance replacement program** — available for Certified and Elite partners
- **Spare parts availability** — committed inventory for warranty fulfillment
- **Technical documentation** — comprehensive product manuals, datasheets, and FAQ resources

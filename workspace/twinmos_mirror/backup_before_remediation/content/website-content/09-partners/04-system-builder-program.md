# System Builder Program

The TwinMOS System Builder Program is designed for certified PC builders, system integrators (SIs), and custom workstation assemblers who demand reliable, high-performance memory and storage for their builds. As a program member, you gain access to preferred pricing, technical resources, and marketing support that help you deliver exceptional systems to your customers.

## Why Join the System Builder Program?

### Financial Advantages
- **Preferred component pricing** — discounted rates on DDR4, DDR5, SSDs, and portable storage
- **Volume rebate tiers** — quarterly rebates based on cumulative purchase volume
- **Marketing co-op funds** — reimbursement for eligible promotional activities featuring TwinMOS products
- **Bundle discounts** — special pricing on matched memory and storage configurations

### Technical & Product Benefits
- **Extended warranty options** — offer your end customers up to 5-year warranty coverage on select components
- **Early access to new products** — preview and validate upcoming DDR5 modules, PCIe Gen 5.0 SSDs, and gaming memory before public launch
- **Technical validation support** — compatibility testing assistance for new platforms and motherboard models
- **Engineering consultation** — direct access to TwinMOS technical team for complex build requirements
- **Sample program** — evaluation units for qualification and demonstration purposes

### Marketing & Sales Support
- **Co-branded marketing assets** — "Powered by TwinMOS" badges, build gallery submissions, and social media kits
- **System builder directory listing** — featured placement on our "Where to Buy" system builder page
- **Case study opportunities** — showcase your builds in TwinMOS marketing and at trade shows
- **Partner events** — invitations to exclusive product briefings and builder summits

## Program Tiers

| Tier | Qualification | Benefits |
|------|---------------|----------|
| **Registered Builder** | Complete application and purchase $5,000+ annually | Preferred pricing, portal access, standard warranty |
| **Certified Builder** | Purchase $25,000+ annually + 2 certified technicians | All Registered benefits + extended warranty, co-op funds, early access |
| **Elite Builder** | Purchase $100,000+ annually + 5+ certified technicians | All Certified benefits + dedicated partner manager, custom SKUs, event sponsorship |

## Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for the TwinMOS System Builder Program, applicants must meet the following criteria:

- **Registered business** — valid business registration as a system integrator, PC builder, or IT solutions provider
- **Minimum annual volume commitment** — $5,000+ in TwinMOS component purchases (Registered tier)
- **Use of genuine TwinMOS components** — commitment to using authentic TwinMOS products in advertised builds
- **Brand guideline compliance** — agreement to use TwinMOS logos and product imagery in accordance with our brand standards
- **Technical capability** — at least one technician capable of installing and troubleshooting memory and storage components
- **Customer support commitment** — willingness to provide first-level support to end customers

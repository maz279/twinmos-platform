# Partner Price Lists

Current pricing for all authorized TwinMOS partners is maintained in the Partner Portal. Price lists are updated quarterly and supplemented with promotional pricing for campaigns, product launches, and seasonal opportunities.

## Access Price Lists

Authorized partners can access pricing through the Partner Portal. If you do not have portal access, please complete the partner onboarding process first.

[Log In to Partner Portal](/partners/partner-portal-login)

## Price List Categories

TwinMOS maintains separate price lists tailored to each partner type, ensuring appropriate margins and commercial terms for every channel.

### Distributor Price List
- **Who:** Authorized TwinMOS distributors with signed distribution agreements
- **Pricing:** Volume-based distributor buy prices with tiered rebate structures
- **Terms:** Net 30/60/90 based on credit assessment
- **Includes:** Full product portfolio — DRAM, SSDs, portable storage, flash, accessories
- **Extras:** Access to promotional pricing, launch incentives, and MDF-eligible campaigns

### Reseller Price List
- **Who:** Authorized resellers purchasing through an authorized distributor
- **Pricing:** Reseller margins established by the distributor, with TwinMOS MAP guidance
- **Terms:** As agreed with your distributor
- **Includes:** Consumer-focused SKUs with strong retail demand
- **Extras:** Seasonal promotional pricing and bundle offers

### System Builder Price List
- **Who:** Certified TwinMOS System Builder Program members
- **Pricing:** Preferred component pricing below standard distributor rates
- **Terms:** Net 30 with volume rebate tiers
- **Includes:** DRAM, SSDs, and portable storage optimized for system integration
- **Extras:** Bundle discounts on matched memory + storage configurations

### OEM/ODM Pricing
- **Who:** OEM and ODM partners with active manufacturing agreements
- **Pricing:** Custom quotes based on volume, specifications, and contract duration
- **Terms:** Negotiated per agreement — typically 12- to 36-month contracts
- **Includes:** Custom SKUs, private-label products, and specification-tailored solutions
- **Extras:** Dedicated project pricing, NRE (Non-Recurring Engineering) arrangements, and capacity reservation

## Price List Update Schedule

| Update Type | Frequency | Notification |
|-------------|-----------|--------------|
| **Quarterly Standard Update** | January, April, July, October | 14 days advance notice via email |
| **Promotional Pricing** | Monthly or campaign-based | 7 days advance notice via portal and email |
| **New Product Launch Pricing** | Per launch | Coordinated with product announcement |
| **Emergency Price Adjustment** | As needed | 48-hour notice for significant market-driven changes |

### How Updates Work
1. **Advance notification** — email alert with summary of changes
2. **Portal publication** — new price list posted on the effective date
3. **Price protection** — orders placed before the effective date are honored at previous pricing
4. **Historical archive** — past price lists retained for 24 months for reference

## Pricing Policies

### Confidentiality
- All price lists are **strictly confidential** and for authorized partner use only
- Price lists may not be shared with unauthorized third parties, including non-partner resellers
- Breach of confidentiality may result in suspension of portal access and partnership review

### Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) Policy
- Partners must adhere to MAP policies where specified for individual SKUs
- **MAP violations** include advertising prices below the stated minimum in any public channel (website, marketplace, print, social media)
- First violation: written warning and 30-day corrective period
- Second violation: suspension of promotional pricing access for 90 days
- Third violation: partnership termination review

### Regional Pricing
- **Regional variations** apply based on local taxes, import duties, logistics costs, and competitive dynamics
- Partners must sell within their authorized territory at the pricing applicable to that region
- Cross-border selling at lower regional prices is prohibited and subject to partnership review

### Price Protection
- TwinMOS offers **price protection** on held inventory when announced price decreases exceed 10%
- Claims must be submitted within 14 days of the price change effective date
- Credit is applied to the partner's account for future purchases
- Price protection does not apply to promotional or closeout pricing

## Special Pricing Programs

### Volume Rebate Tiers
| Annual Purchase Volume | Rebate Rate |
|------------------------|-------------|
| $100,000 – $249,999 | 2% |
| $250,000 – $499,999 | 3% |
| $500,000 – $999,999 | 4% |
| $1,000,000+ | 5% |

*Rebates are calculated quarterly and applied as account credits toward future orders.*

### New Product Launch Incentives
- Enhanced margins (typically +3–5%) for the first 60 days after launch
- Co-marketing support for launch campaigns
- Priority allocation for high-demand SKUs

### Seasonal Promotions
- **Back to School** (July–September): Student-focused bundles and discounts
- **Holiday Season** (November–December): Gift-oriented promotions and retail support
- **Gaming Season** (Year-round): Esports and gaming-themed campaigns

## Custom Quotes

For large-volume orders, government tenders, or specialized requirements not covered by standard price lists, contact your account manager for a custom quotation.

### Custom Quote Requests Should Include:
- Product SKUs and quantities
- Delivery timeline and destination
- Special requirements (custom labeling, packaging, specifications)
- Competitive pricing context (if applicable)
- Contract duration (for multi-year agreements)

*Custom quotes are typically returned within 2–3 business days.*

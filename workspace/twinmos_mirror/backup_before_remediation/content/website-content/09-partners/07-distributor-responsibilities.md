# Distributor Responsibilities

Authorized TwinMOS distributors are essential ambassadors of our brand. Upholding the responsibilities outlined below ensures mutual success, protects brand integrity, and delivers consistent value to end customers across your territory.

These responsibilities are incorporated into every TwinMOS distribution agreement and are reviewed quarterly during business performance reviews.

## Sales & Marketing Responsibilities

### Sales Targets & Growth
- **Meet mutually agreed annual sales targets** — minimum purchase commitments defined in your distribution agreement, typically reviewed quarterly
- **Drive year-over-year growth** — actively expand market share and deepen penetration in existing accounts
- **Broad product portfolio adoption** — promote the full TwinMOS range (DRAM, SSDs, portable storage, flash) rather than focusing on a single category
- **New customer acquisition** — continuously expand your reseller and dealer network

### Marketing & Promotion
- **Actively promote TwinMOS products** in your assigned territory through advertising, digital marketing, and in-store displays
- **Participate in product launch campaigns** — execute go-to-market activities within 30 days of new product availability
- **Maintain adequate inventory levels** — stock sufficient units to meet market demand and avoid stockouts on core SKUs
- **Utilize Marketing Development Funds (MDF)** effectively — submit quarterly marketing plans and post-campaign reports
- **Attend key trade shows** — represent TwinMOS at major regional technology exhibitions (minimum one per year)

### Pricing & Market Positioning
- **Maintain recommended resale pricing** — adhere to Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) policies where applicable
- **Avoid destructive price competition** — do not engage in predatory pricing that undermines brand value
- **Report pricing violations** — notify TwinMOS of unauthorized discounting by competitors or gray-market sellers

## Brand & Compliance Responsibilities

### Brand Asset Usage
- **Use TwinMOS brand assets in accordance with guidelines** — logos, product images, and trademarks must follow our Brand Guidelines document
- **Maintain brand consistency** — ensure all marketing materials, packaging, and communications reflect TwinMOS brand standards
- **Submit marketing materials for approval** — all co-branded campaigns using the TwinMOS logo require pre-approval from our marketing team

### Product Integrity
- **Sell only genuine TwinMOS products** — sourced exclusively through authorized channels
- **Do not sell counterfeit, refurbished, or gray-market products** — maintain full traceability and authenticity
- **Report suspected counterfeit products** — immediately notify TwinMOS of any counterfeit or suspicious products discovered in your market
- **Maintain brand reputation and customer trust** — uphold the highest standards of business ethics

### Legal & Regulatory Compliance
- **Comply with local laws** — adhere to all applicable import, consumer protection, and data privacy regulations
- **Maintain required certifications** — ensure products carry necessary local compliance marks (e.g., BIS in India, EAC in CIS)
- **Proper product labeling** — ensure local language labeling, warranty cards, and safety information are included

## Customer Support Responsibilities

### Technical Support
- **Provide first-level technical support** to end users, resellers, and dealers in your territory
- **Maintain trained technical staff** — at least one TwinMOS Certified Technical Specialist on your team
- **Escalate complex issues** — route advanced technical problems to TwinMOS engineering within 24 hours

### Warranty & Returns
- **Handle warranty claims and RMAs** per TwinMOS policy and service level agreements
- **Process returns promptly** — acknowledge warranty claims within 48 hours and resolve within 14 days
- **Maintain spare parts inventory** — stock critical replacement units for advance replacement programs (where applicable)

### Customer Relationship Management
- **Maintain accurate customer and sales records** — CRM data, purchase histories, and contact information
- **Collect customer feedback** — gather and relay product feedback, feature requests, and satisfaction scores to TwinMOS
- **Support end-user registration** — encourage and facilitate product warranty registration

## Reporting Responsibilities

### Sales Reporting
- **Submit monthly sales reports** — unit volumes, revenue, and SKU-level breakdown by channel
- **Provide quarterly business reviews** — comprehensive performance assessment including market trends and competitive activity
- **Forecast demand** — submit rolling 90-day demand forecasts to support production planning

### Market Intelligence
- **Provide market feedback and competitive intelligence** — pricing, new product launches, and competitive tactics observed in your territory
- **Notify TwinMOS of significant market changes** — regulatory shifts, economic developments, or channel disruptions
- **Report counterfeit or unauthorized distribution** — immediate notification of gray-market or counterfeit activity

### Inventory Reporting
- **Submit monthly inventory reports** — stock levels, aging analysis, and slow-moving SKU identification
- **Coordinate returns** — manage end-of-life returns and defective product rotations per agreed schedules

## Territory & Exclusivity Responsibilities

### Territory Management
- **Respect territorial boundaries** per the distribution agreement — do not actively solicit business outside your authorized region
- **Do not sell outside the authorized region** without prior written approval from TwinMOS
- **Protect exclusive rights** — if granted exclusivity, actively defend your territory against unauthorized sellers

### Channel Management
- **Maintain authorized reseller network** — ensure all resellers purchasing from you are legitimate businesses
- **Enforce reseller agreements** — communicate and monitor TwinMOS reseller policies within your network
- **Prevent cross-border selling** — implement controls to prevent your resellers from exporting to other distributors' territories

## Training & Development Responsibilities

### Staff Certification
- **Maintain certified personnel** — ensure sales and technical staff complete required TwinMOS certifications
- **Annual recertification** — renew certifications before expiration to maintain partner status
- **New hire training** — onboard new employees with TwinMOS product knowledge within 30 days of joining

### Knowledge Sharing
- **Conduct dealer training** — organize periodic training sessions for your reseller network
- **Distribute product updates** — share new product announcements, technical bulletins, and marketing materials with resellers
- **Represent TwinMOS at industry events** — participate in local technology forums and user groups

## Performance Standards

### Minimum Performance Expectations
| Metric | Frequency | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Sales vs. target | Quarterly | ≥ 80% of agreed target |
| Inventory turns | Quarterly | ≥ 4x annually |
| Warranty claim resolution | Monthly | ≤ 14 days average |
| Training compliance | Annually | 100% of required staff certified |
| Marketing plan submission | Quarterly | On-time, complete |
| Sales report submission | Monthly | On-time, accurate |

### Consequences of Non-Compliance
Failure to meet these responsibilities may result in:

1. **Formal performance review** — written notification with 60-day improvement plan
2. **Reduction in benefits** — temporary suspension of MDF, co-op funds, or early product access
3. **Territory adjustment** — reduction of exclusive territory or addition of complementary distributors
4. **Agreement termination** — formal termination of the distribution agreement with 90-day wind-down period

*TwinMOS is committed to supporting our distributors in meeting these responsibilities. Your account manager is available to provide guidance, resources, and assistance whenever needed.*

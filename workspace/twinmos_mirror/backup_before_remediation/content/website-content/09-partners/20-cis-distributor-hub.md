# CIS Distributor Hub

The Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) and Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU) represent an important growth market for TwinMOS. With EAC certification in place, our memory and storage products are fully compliant for legal import and sale across Russia, Kazakhstan, Belarus, Armenia, Kyrgyzstan, and other member states. TwinMOS is actively building distribution partnerships to serve this region's growing demand for quality PC components.

## EAC Certification

TwinMOS memory and storage products meet **Eurasian Conformity (EAC)** standards, the mandatory certification required for products sold within the EAEU customs union.

### Certification Details
- **Certification body:** EAC-approved testing laboratory
- **Technical regulations:** TR CU 020/2011 (Electromagnetic Compatibility) and TR CU 004/2011 (Low Voltage Equipment)
- **Valid for:** All EAEU member states — Russia, Kazakhstan, Belarus, Armenia, Kyrgyzstan
- **Product scope:** DDR4/DDR5 memory modules, SATA SSDs, NVMe SSDs, USB flash drives, memory cards
- **Labeling:** EAC mark required on product packaging and documentation

### Benefits of EAC Certification
- **Legal market access** — products can be imported and sold without customs delays
- **Consumer trust** — EAC mark signals compliance with regional safety standards
- **Competitive advantage** — non-certified products face import restrictions
- **Unified standard** — single certification valid across all EAEU countries

# Africa Distributor Hub

Africa represents one of TwinMOS's most significant growth opportunities. With rapid economic development, increasing technology adoption, and a young, digitally engaged population, the continent offers immense potential for memory and storage solutions. TwinMOS is actively building a robust distribution network across Africa to serve consumers, businesses, and government institutions.

## Why Africa?

TwinMOS's strategic focus on Africa is driven by several key factors:

- **Rapid digitization** — governments and enterprises across Africa are investing heavily in IT infrastructure
- **Growing PC and gaming markets** — increasing demand for components, upgrades, and custom builds
- **Price-sensitive consumers** — TwinMOS's competitive price-performance positioning aligns perfectly with African market needs
- **Established MEA hub** — our Dubai headquarters provides efficient logistics connectivity to African markets
- **Under-served regions** — many African markets lack strong memory and storage brand presence, creating first-mover opportunities

## Distributor Selection Criteria

TwinMOS applies rigorous criteria when selecting distribution partners in Africa:

### Essential Requirements
- **Business registration** — valid trade license and tax registration
- **Financial stability** — audited financials or bank references
- **Import experience** — proven track record importing IT products
- **Sales network** — established dealer/retailer relationships
- **Warehousing** — secure storage facilities in major cities
- **Technical capability** — staff capable of basic product support

### Preferred Qualifications
- **Existing brand portfolio** — distribution of complementary IT brands
- **Enterprise/government relationships** — experience with B2B and public sector sales
- **Multi-city presence** — coverage of major urban centers
- **E-commerce capability** — online sales and digital marketing experience
- **After-sales service** — warranty handling and customer support infrastructure

### Evaluation Process
1. **Initial screening** — application review and background verification
2. **Financial due diligence** — credit checks and reference verification
3. **Market assessment** — evaluation of territory potential and competitive landscape
4. **Site visit** — physical or virtual assessment of facilities and operations
5. **Agreement negotiation** — terms, targets, and territory definition
6. **Onboarding and training** — product knowledge and systems setup

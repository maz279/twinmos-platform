# Introducing CoreX Pro Gen5: The New Speed Standard

Today, TwinMOS is proud to announce the CoreX Pro Gen5 — our flagship NVMe SSD that pushes the boundaries of what consumer storage can achieve. With sequential read speeds reaching 14,000 MB/s, the CoreX Pro Gen5 isn't just faster than its predecessor; it's faster than most users thought possible just a few years ago.

## Breaking the Speed Barrier

The CoreX Pro Gen5 leverages the full bandwidth of PCIe Gen5 x4, delivering up to 14,000 MB/s sequential read and 12,000 MB/s sequential write speeds. To put that in perspective:

- A 100GB 4K video project copies in under 8 seconds
- A modern 150GB game installs before you finish reading this sentence
- Windows boots from cold in under 10 seconds

These aren't laboratory fantasies — they're real-world capabilities that content creators, enthusiasts, and power users can experience today.

## What's Under the Hood

### Advanced Controller

The CoreX Pro Gen5 is built around a cutting-edge 12nm controller designed specifically for Gen5 throughput. It manages up to 2 million IOPS in random read operations, ensuring snappy responsiveness even under heavy multitasking loads.

### Premium 3D TLC NAND

We selected the latest generation of 3D TLC NAND flash, manufactured on a 176-layer process. This provides:

- Higher density for greater capacity in the standard M.2 2280 form factor
- Improved endurance with higher TBW ratings compared to earlier NAND generations
- Better power efficiency vs. previous NAND generations
- SLC caching for consistent burst write performance

### Dedicated DRAM Cache

A full-size DRAM chip stores the Flash Translation Layer mapping table for instant address translation. Unlike DRAM-less designs that struggle with sustained random I/O, the CoreX Pro maintains consistent performance whether you're copying thousands of small files or streaming massive video assets.

### Graphene Thermal Solution

Gen5 speeds generate significant heat. The CoreX Pro features an advanced graphene heatsink that dissipates thermal energy more efficiently than traditional aluminum solutions — graphene thermal conductivity can reach 5,300 W/mK vs. aluminum's 237 W/mK — while maintaining an ultra-slim profile compatible with most system configurations. This ensures sustained performance without throttling, even in compact builds with limited airflow.

## Designed for What's Next

The CoreX Pro Gen5 isn't just about today's workloads. It's built for the future:

### DirectStorage Ready

Microsoft's DirectStorage API (current version 1.4) allows GPUs to load game assets directly from NVMe SSDs, bypassing CPU decompression bottlenecks. The CoreX Pro Gen5 fully supports DirectStorage, with bandwidth headroom that ensures the drive never becomes the limiting factor as game developers adopt this technology. As more titles implement DirectStorage, Gen5's massive bandwidth becomes increasingly valuable.

### Professional Workloads

Video editors working with 8K RAW footage, 3D artists manipulating massive texture libraries, and data scientists processing multi-gigabyte datasets all benefit from Gen5 throughput. The CoreX Pro reduces waiting time and keeps creative momentum flowing.

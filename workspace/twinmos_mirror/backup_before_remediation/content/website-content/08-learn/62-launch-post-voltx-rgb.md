# VOLTX DDR5 RGB: Where Performance Meets Light

A gaming PC is more than a tool — it's a statement. The case you choose, the cables you route, and the lighting you coordinate all reflect your personality and passion. Today, TwinMOS is excited to unveil the VOLTX DDR5 RGB, a memory module that doesn't compromise. It delivers cutting-edge DDR5 performance with lighting that integrates beautifully into your build's aesthetic vision.

## Performance First

Beneath the illuminated heatsink, the VOLTX DDR5 RGB is pure performance:

### Speed Options

- **DDR5-5600**: The sweet spot for value and performance across all platforms
- **DDR5-6000**: Enthusiast-grade speed — the confirmed optimal frequency for AMD Ryzen 7000's 1:1 Infinity Fabric ratio, and excellent on Intel and Ryzen 9000 platforms

Both speeds are available in 16GB and 32GB module configurations, supporting dual-channel and quad-channel setups up to 128GB.

### Tight Timings

Speed without stability is meaningless. The VOLTX DDR5 RGB is engineered with optimized primary timings:

- DDR5-5600: CL28-34-34-68
- DDR5-6000: CL30-36-36-96

These timings are validated across major motherboard platforms to ensure reliable XMP 3.0 and AMD EXPO operation out of the box.

### DDR5 Architecture Advantages

Like all VOLTX modules, the RGB variant includes:

- **On-die ECC**: Standard error correction integrated within every DDR5 chip, correcting single-bit errors before data reaches the memory controller
- **PMIC**: On-module power management IC for stable, efficient voltage delivery independent of motherboard VRM quality
- **Dual 32-bit subchannels**: DDR5's fundamental improvement — each DIMM provides two independent 32-bit channels, improving access efficiency and effective bandwidth utilization

## Platform Compatibility

The VOLTX DDR5 RGB is validated for:

- **AMD Ryzen 7000 series** (AM5): DDR5-6000 achieves the optimal 1:1 Infinity Fabric ratio
- **AMD Ryzen 9000 series** (AM5): Full compatibility; DDR5-6400 achieves 1:1 on Zen 5
- **Intel 12th/13th/14th Gen** (LGA 1700): Full DDR5 support at rated speeds with XMP 3.0
- **Intel Core Ultra 200S** (LGA 1851): Full DDR5 support; Core Ultra 200S Plus supports DDR5-7200 natively with CUDIMM

## Lighting That Integrates

The VOLTX DDR5 RGB features a diffused light bar that produces uniform, vibrant illumination without visible LED hotspots. But beautiful hardware is only half the equation — integration matters.

### Broad Ecosystem Support

The VOLTX RGB is certified compatible with:

- **ASUS Aura Sync**
- **MSI Mystic Light**
- **Gigabyte RGB Fusion**
- **ASRock Polychrome Sync**

This means your memory lighting synchronizes perfectly with your motherboard, GPU, fans, and peripherals — regardless of brand.

### Effects and Control

Through your motherboard's RGB software, you can apply:

- Static colors for professional builds
- Breathing and pulsing for relaxed ambiance
- Rainbow waves for dynamic showcases
- Music-reactive modes for entertainment setups
- Temperature-based effects for functional monitoring
- Custom patterns with per-LED control on ARGB systems

### Designed for Showcase Builds

The VOLTX RGB module height is optimized for compatibility with large CPU air coolers while still providing enough heatsink mass for thermal management. The diffused light bar delivers consistent brightness from any viewing angle.

## Built for Overclockers

For enthusiasts who want to push beyond XMP profiles, the VOLTX DDR5 RGB provides:

- High-quality memory ICs with proven overclocking headroom
- Robust PCB design with optimized trace routing
- PMIC support for granular voltage adjustments
- On-die ECC that remains active even when overclocked

Our internal testing has achieved DDR5-6800+ on select platforms with manual tuning.

## Stability You Can Trust

Every VOLTX DDR5 RGB module undergoes:

- 100% burn-in testing at rated speed and timings
- Compatibility validation on 20+ motherboard models
- Thermal cycling from -10°C to 85°C
- 72-hour MemTest86 validation

This is why we back every module with a limited lifetime warranty. If it doesn't meet our standards, it doesn't ship.

## Compatibility Badge Guide

| Badge | Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **QVL Listed** | Green | TwinMOS physically installed and stress-tested this exact SKU on your exact motherboard at rated speeds. Highest confidence level. |
| **Compatible** | Blue | Product meets all documented platform specifications. Will work in the vast majority of configurations. Not individually tested on your board. |
| **Likely Compatible** | Yellow | Based on platform generation match. Recommend verifying specifications against your system's documentation before purchase. |
| **Not Compatible** | Red | This product does not meet your system's requirements. Not shown in default results view. |

All QVL-listed results carry a TwinMOS **compatibility guarantee** — if the listed product does not operate stably in the tested configuration, contact TwinMOS Support for a replacement or refund.

## Recommended Configurations

The finder highlights optimal upgrade configurations based on your system:

### Maximum Performance (Desktop)
Install the highest-capacity DDR5 kit at the board's maximum XMP/EXPO-validated speed, plus a Gen 4 or Gen 5 NVMe SSD as primary storage. For systems with multiple M.2 slots, add a secondary Gen 3 or Gen 4 NVMe for expanded storage.

### Best Value Upgrade (Desktop or Laptop)
If your current RAM is 8 GB, upgrade to 16 GB matched pair (the single most impactful upgrade for most users). If you have a spinning HDD, adding any TwinMOS SSD — even SATA — reduces boot time from ~60 seconds to ~10 seconds.

### Dual-Channel Note
On all desktop and laptop platforms, install memory in matched pairs (2×16 GB rather than 1×32 GB) to enable dual-channel mode. This doubles available memory bandwidth and measurably improves performance for games with integrated graphics and memory-intensive workloads.

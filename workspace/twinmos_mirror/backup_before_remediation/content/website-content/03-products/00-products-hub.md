# TwinMOS Products

Welcome to the TwinMOS product catalog. For over two decades, TwinMOS has designed and manufactured high-performance memory and storage solutions trusted by gamers, content creators, IT professionals, and everyday users worldwide. Our portfolio spans cutting-edge DDR5 desktop and laptop memory, high-speed NVMe SSDs, reliable SATA storage, portable drives, and essential PC accessories.

## Memory Solutions

TwinMOS memory modules are engineered for stability, speed, and compatibility. Our DDR5 lineup delivers next-generation performance with speeds up to 6000MHz, while our DDR4 series continues to power millions of desktops and laptops globally.

- **DDR5 Desktop**: VOLTX DDR5 U-DIMM and RGB series for enthusiasts and gamers
- **DDR5 Laptop**: VOLTX DDR5 SO-DIMM with advanced thermal management
- **DDR4 Desktop**: TornadoX7 Pro, TornadoX7, Thunder GX, and Concord RGB families
- **DDR4 Laptop**: SO-DIMM modules for upgrades and replacements
- **DDR3 Legacy**: Reliable memory for older platforms and systems

## Solid-State Drives (SSDs)

From the blistering speeds of PCIe Gen 5.0 to cost-effective SATA III options, TwinMOS SSDs cover every use case and budget.

- **PCIe Gen 5.0 NVMe**: CoreX Pro M.2 with read speeds up to 14,000 MB/s
- **PCIe Gen 4.0 NVMe**: Xtreme, CoreX, and Xtreme Pro M.2 series
- **PCIe Gen 3.0 NVMe**: Alpha Pro and TW300 M.2 for mainstream performance
- **SATA III**: Hyper H2 Ultra 2.5" and M.2 2280 SATA for legacy compatibility

## Portable Storage

Take your data anywhere with TwinMOS portable storage solutions. Our compact SSDs and high-capacity HDDs are built for mobility and durability.

- **ELITE Drive Pro**: USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C portable SSD
- **ProDrive Ultra**: USB 3.0 portable external HDD up to 4TB

## USB Flash Drives

The Mobile Disk X3 series offers compact, reliable USB 3.2 flash storage for everyday file transfers and backups.

## Accessories

Complete your setup with TwinMOS accessories, including our uniquely designed USB Hub 4-Port.

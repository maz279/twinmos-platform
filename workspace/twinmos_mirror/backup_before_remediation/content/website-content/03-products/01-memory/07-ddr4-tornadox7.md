## Platform Compatibility

TornadoX7 modules comply with JEDEC DDR4-3200 specifications, ensuring plug-and-play compatibility with:

### Intel Platforms
- 100-series, 200-series, 300-series, 400-series, 500-series, 600-series DDR4 motherboards
- Core i3/i5/i7/i9 6th Gen through 12th Gen

### AMD Platforms
- 300-series, 400-series, 500-series AM4 motherboards
- Ryzen 1000 through Ryzen 5000 series
- All major OEM desktop platforms supporting DDR4-3200

**No manual BIOS configuration is required**; the module automatically runs at its rated JEDEC speed (3200MHz) on compatible platforms. For platforms that default to lower speeds, enabling XMP 2.0 ensures 3200MHz operation.

## Installation Guide

Installing TornadoX7 DDR4 is straightforward:

1. Power off the computer and unplug the power cord
2. Open the case and locate the DIMM slots
3. Align the notch on the module with the slot's keying
4. Insert at a 45-degree angle and press firmly until the retention clips lock
5. Power on and verify the installed capacity in BIOS or operating system

**Optimal Slot Configuration**: For dual-channel performance, install modules in slots A2 and B2 (or as specified in your motherboard manual).

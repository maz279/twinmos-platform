## Why DDR4 in 2026?

While DDR5 garners headlines, DDR4 remains a practical and cost-effective choice for millions of users:

### Existing Platform Upgrades
Intel 10th/11th Gen and AMD AM4 systems have years of useful life remaining. Upgrading from DDR4-2400 or DDR4-2666 to DDR4-3200 provides a meaningful performance boost without requiring a full platform replacement.

### Budget-Conscious Builds
In 2026, DDR4 motherboards and memory remain significantly more affordable than DDR5 equivalents. For users building basic office PCs, home computers, or entry-level gaming systems, DDR4 offers excellent value.

### Proven Reliability
After nearly a decade of refinement, DDR4 is a proven, stable technology with broad compatibility. BIOS support is mature, and compatibility issues are rare compared to the early days of DDR5.

### Global Availability
DDR4 modules are widely stocked by distributors and retailers globally. Lead times are shorter, and supply is more predictable than newer technologies.

### Industrial & Embedded Applications
Many industrial controllers, point-of-sale systems, and embedded platforms still rely on DDR4. TwinMOS DDR4 modules meet the long-term availability and reliability requirements of these applications.

## DDR4 vs DDR5: When to Choose DDR4

| Scenario | DDR4 | DDR5 | Recommendation |
|----------|------|------|----------------|
| **Intel 10th/11th Gen** | Native | Not supported | DDR4 |
| **AMD AM4** | Native | Not supported | DDR4 |
| **Intel 12th+ / AMD AM5** | Supported | Native | DDR5 for new builds |
| **Budget under $800** | Lower cost | Higher cost | DDR4 |
| **Upgrade existing PC** | Keep platform | Requires new mobo+CPU | DDR4 |
| **Industrial/embedded** | Mature ecosystem | Limited support | DDR4 |

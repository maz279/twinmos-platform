## Why DDR4 in 2026?

While DDR5 garners headlines, DDR4 remains a practical and cost-effective choice for:

### Existing Platform Upgrades
Intel 10th/11th Gen and AMD AM4 systems have years of useful life remaining. Upgrading memory is one of the most cost-effective ways to extend performance on these platforms.

### Budget-Conscious Builds
DDR4 motherboards and memory remain more affordable than DDR5 equivalents. For builds under $800, DDR4 frees budget for a better GPU or larger SSD.

### Proven Reliability
After nearly a decade of refinement, DDR4 is a proven, stable technology with broad compatibility. BIOS support is mature, and compatibility issues are rare.

### Global Availability
DDR4 modules are widely stocked by distributors and retailers globally, with shorter lead times than newer technologies.

### Industrial & Embedded
Many industrial controllers, POS systems, and embedded platforms rely on DDR4. TwinMOS provides long-term availability for these applications.

## DDR4 vs DDR5: Decision Guide

| Factor | DDR4 | DDR5 | Winner |
|--------|------|------|--------|
| **Platform cost** | Lower | Higher | DDR4 |
| **Memory bandwidth** | ~51 GB/s | ~89–96 GB/s | DDR5 |
| **Latency (absolute)** | ~10ns (CL16) | ~10–11ns (CL36) | Tie |
| **Power efficiency** | 1.2V | 1.1V | DDR5 |
| **Maturity** | 10+ years | 3+ years | DDR4 |
| **Upgrade path** | End of life | Active development | DDR5 |

**Verdict**: Choose DDR4 for existing platforms and budget builds. Choose DDR5 for new high-performance builds.

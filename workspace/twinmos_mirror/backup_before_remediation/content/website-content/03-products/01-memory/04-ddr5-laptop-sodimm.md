## Key Features

### High-Speed Mobile Performance
VOLTX DDR5 SO-DIMM delivers significantly more bandwidth than DDR4-3200 laptop memory. At 5600MHz, data transfer rates reach up to 44,800 MB/s — translating to faster application loading, smoother multitasking, and improved responsiveness in memory-intensive tasks.

Compared to DDR4-3200 (~51 GB/s dual-channel), DDR5-5600 delivers approximately ~89 GB/s in dual-channel configuration — a 75% bandwidth improvement that directly impacts game asset streaming, video editing, and large dataset processing.

### Plug-and-Play Compatibility
Fully compliant with JEDEC DDR5 standards, VOLTX SO-DIMM requires no BIOS tweaks or XMP profiles. The system automatically configures optimal settings upon installation. This makes it ideal for users who prefer hassle-free upgrades, corporate IT departments, and educational institutions.

### On-Die ECC
DDR5 on-die ECC improves data reliability by correcting single-bit errors within the memory device itself. For mobile professionals handling critical documents, codebases, or creative projects, this added layer of protection is invaluable.

### Energy Efficient
Operating at just 1.1V (compared to DDR4's 1.2V), VOLTX DDR5 SO-DIMM reduces power consumption compared to DDR4, extending battery life in laptops and ultrabooks while generating less heat. The on-module PMIC enables more precise voltage regulation than DDR4's motherboard-based power delivery.

### Wide Compatibility
VOLTX DDR5 SO-DIMM modules are validated for compatibility with Intel Core 12th/13th/14th Gen mobile platforms and AMD Ryzen 6000/7000/8000 series laptops. The 262-pin interface is the standard for all DDR5 mobile platforms.

## Installation Guide

1. Power off the laptop completely and disconnect the charger
2. Remove the bottom panel (refer to your laptop manual for screw locations)
3. Locate the SO-DIMM slot(s) on the motherboard
4. Release the retention clips and remove any existing module if necessary
5. Align the notch on the VOLTX module with the slot key at approximately 30 degrees
6. Press down until the module clicks into place
7. Reattach the bottom panel and power on

**Note**: DDR5 SO-DIMM uses a 262-pin interface — do not attempt to install in DDR4 (260-pin) slots.

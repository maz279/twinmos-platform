# TwinMOS Product Catalog — H1 2026

Download the latest TwinMOS Product Catalog and explore our complete lineup of memory modules, solid-state drives, portable storage solutions, USB flash drives, and PC accessories. The H1 2026 edition features our newest DDR5 memory series, next-generation PCIe Gen 5.0 NVMe SSDs, and expanded portable storage options.

## What's Inside the Catalog

The H1 2026 product catalog provides detailed specifications, feature highlights, and ordering information for every TwinMOS product family. Whether you are a distributor, system integrator, retailer, or end user, this catalog is your definitive reference for TwinMOS products.

### Memory Section

- **DDR5 Desktop**: VOLTX DDR5 U-DIMM and VOLTX RGB DDR5 U-DIMM with speeds up to 6000MHz
- **DDR5 Laptop**: VOLTX DDR5 SO-DIMM with MTCD thermal technology
- **DDR4 Desktop**: TornadoX7 Pro, TornadoX7, Thunder GX, and Concord CL16 RGB families
- **DDR4 Laptop**: Standard SO-DIMM modules at 2666MHz and 3200MHz
- **DDR3 Legacy**: 1333MHz and 1600MHz modules for older systems

### SSD Section

- **PCIe Gen 5.0**: CoreX Pro M.2 with up to 14,000 MB/s sequential read
- **PCIe Gen 4.0**: Xtreme M.2, CoreX M.2, and Xtreme Pro M.2 families
- **PCIe Gen 3.0**: Alpha Pro M.2 and TW300 M.2 for mainstream users
- **SATA III**: Hyper H2 Ultra 2.5" and M.2 2280 SATA drives

### Portable Storage & Accessories

- **Portable SSD**: ELITE Drive Pro USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C
- **Portable HDD**: ProDrive Ultra USB 3.0 up to 4TB
- **USB Flash**: Mobile Disk X3 USB 3.2 series
- **Accessories**: USB Hub 4-Port with S-shape design

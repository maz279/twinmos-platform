### Product Overview

The TwinMOS ProDrive Ultra is a portable 2.5-inch USB 3.0 hard disk drive engineered for users who require large-capacity portable storage at an economical price point. Housed in a slim, lightweight ABS enclosure, the ProDrive Ultra integrates a 5400 RPM 2.5-inch HDD with a USB 3.0 SuperSpeed bridge, enabling sequential read speeds of up to 130 MB/s and write speeds of up to 120 MB/s — close to the practical ceiling of 5400 RPM rotational media.

Available in capacities of 1 TB, 2 TB, and 4 TB, the ProDrive Ultra provides an expansive and cost-effective solution for media libraries, document archives, and system backups. Bus-powered via a standard USB Type-A cable with no external power adapter required, the drive is immediately usable when connected to any USB port on a laptop, desktop, smart TV, or gaming console. Backward compatibility with USB 2.0 ensures legacy device support, and the drive ships pre-formatted in exFAT for cross-platform compatibility out of the box.

### Key Features

1. **Massive Capacity at Portable Scale** — Up to 4 TB of storage in a 2.5-inch bus-powered form factor — no external power adapter needed — making the ProDrive Ultra ideal for media professionals, travellers, and IT backup workflows.
2. **USB 3.0 SuperSpeed Interface** — Delivers up to 130 MB/s real-world sequential performance, a significant step above USB 2.0's ~35 MB/s practical ceiling, reducing large-file transfer times dramatically.
3. **No External Power Required** — Single USB cable carries both data and power, enabling immediate usability in any environment with a standard USB port.
4. **exFAT Pre-Formatted** — Ships ready to use on Windows, macOS, Linux, PlayStation 4/5, smart TVs, and Android devices without reformatting.
5. **Compact ABS Enclosure** — Lightweight and durable housing protects the internal HDD from everyday handling, while the slim profile fits easily in a bag or pocket.
6. **3-Year Warranty** — An industry-leading three-year warranty on a portable HDD product reflects TwinMOS's commitment to long-term product reliability.
7. **PlayStation 4/5 Certified Compatibility** — Validated for use as extended storage on PlayStation 4 and PlayStation 5 (media storage mode) for console gaming libraries.

### Disclaimer

All specifications are subject to change without notice. Performance figures are maximum values measured under controlled conditions; real-world performance varies with host system, USB controller, file size, and file system. This product contains rotating magnetic media which is inherently susceptible to mechanical shock; avoid dropping or striking the drive during operation. TwinMOS Technologies is not responsible for loss of stored data; users must maintain independent backups at all times. Formatted capacity is less than nominal capacity due to file-system reservation. The exFAT pre-format supports files larger than 4 GB; re-formatting to FAT32 will impose a 4 GB per-file size limit.

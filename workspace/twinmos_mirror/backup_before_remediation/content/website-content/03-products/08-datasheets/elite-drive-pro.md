### Product Overview

The TwinMOS ELITE Drive Pro is a premium portable solid-state drive designed for professionals, creatives, and power users who demand fast, reliable, and compact external storage. Leveraging a USB 3.2 Gen 2 (10 Gbps) interface, the ELITE Drive Pro delivers sequential read speeds of up to 450 MB/s and sequential write speeds of up to 410 MB/s — more than three times faster than portable HDD solutions — in a lightweight, pocket-sized enclosure with no moving parts.

Bus-powered via USB Type-C, the ELITE Drive Pro requires no external power adapter, making it ideal for field work, travel, and on-site data transfers. The drive is available in capacities of 240 GB, 480 GB, 512 GB, and 1 TB, and in multiple colour variants including Dark Grey, Gold, Rose Gold, and Black to match professional and consumer aesthetics. Full backward compatibility with USB 3.1 Gen 1, USB 3.0, and USB 2.0 hosts (via included USB-A adapter) ensures universal connectivity across legacy and modern platforms.

### Key Features

1. **USB 3.2 Gen 2 (10 Gbps) Interface** — Up to 10 Gbps theoretical bandwidth delivers real-world sustained transfers well above 400 MB/s, enabling rapid 4K video transfer, large dataset movement, and fast OS booting from external media.
2. **No Moving Parts — Solid State Reliability** — Flash-based storage eliminates mechanical failure modes, making the ELITE Drive Pro resilient against drops and vibration in field and travel environments.
3. **Bus-Powered — No External Adapter** — Draws power directly from the USB host port; compatible with laptops, tablets, smartphones (USB OTG), and iPadOS devices without any additional cable or charger.
4. **Universal USB-C and USB-A Connectivity** — The included USB-C to USB-A adapter ensures full compatibility with both modern Type-C ports and legacy Type-A ports on older computers.
5. **Cross-Platform exFAT Pre-Format** — Shipped pre-formatted in exFAT for immediate plug-and-play use across Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iPadOS without reformatting.
6. **Multiple Colour Variants** — Dark Grey, Gold, Rose Gold, and Black finish options available for retail differentiation and corporate gifting applications.
7. **Compact and Lightweight** — Pocket-sized enclosure at approximately 210 g makes the ELITE Drive Pro the ideal companion for mobile creative professionals and business travellers.

# Product Datasheets & Technical Documentation

TwinMOS Technologies provides official, revision-controlled datasheets for every product in our current lineup. These documents are authored and maintained by the TwinMOS engineering and product management teams to serve the precise needs of design engineers, hardware integrators, procurement specialists, regional distributors, and OEM/ODM partners.

Each datasheet follows a standardised structure covering general specifications, electrical characteristics, environmental ratings, physical dimensions, interface and pin configuration, ordering information, regulatory compliance, and warranty terms. Documents are published in PDF format and optimised for both on-screen review and print. All datasheets carry a document revision number and release date; when a specification changes — whether a timing parameter, a form factor tolerance, or a certified compliance mark — a new revision is issued and archived here.

If you are evaluating a TwinMOS product for qualification, budgetary comparison, or end-customer documentation, start with the datasheet and follow up with our technical support team at support@twinmos.com for any parameter not covered.

## Memory Datasheets

TwinMOS memory modules span DDR5 and DDR4 in U-DIMM and SO-DIMM form factors, covering everything from consumer gaming kits to business-class workstation upgrades. DDR5 modules follow JEDEC JESD79-5 and operate at 1.1 V, with on-die ECC and improved power management versus DDR4. DDR4 modules comply with JEDEC JESD79-4 and span a wide speed range from 2666 MT/s to 3600 MT/s. The legacy DDR3 datasheet is provided for maintenance and spare-parts programmes on existing deployed systems.

## SSD Datasheets

## Document Version Control

TwinMOS follows a structured document control process aligned with our ISO 9001:2015 quality management system.

- **Version 1.0** — Initial public release of a new product datasheet, issued at or shortly before market launch.
- **Minor revisions (e.g. 1.1, 1.2)** — Corrections, clarification of existing parameters, addition of new capacity or speed-grade SKUs, or updated certification references. No substantive change to core specifications.
- **Major revisions (e.g. 2.0)** — Significant specification changes: new NAND generation, revised performance ratings, updated electrical parameters, new form factor, or change in regulatory status.

When a datasheet is updated, the previous revision is archived and available upon request. The table above always reflects the current (latest) published revision for each product.

If you are working to a specific revision for a qualification or approval process and need to confirm its validity, contact support@twinmos.com quoting the document number and revision shown on your copy.

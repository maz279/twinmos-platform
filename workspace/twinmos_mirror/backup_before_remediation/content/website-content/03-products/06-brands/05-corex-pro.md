# CoreX Pro

CoreX Pro is TwinMOS's flagship SSD brand — the absolute pinnacle of what consumer storage can achieve. Built on the PCIe Gen 5.0 x4 platform with NVMe 2.0 protocol compliance, CoreX Pro drives deliver sequential read speeds up to 14,000 MB/s, roughly doubling the throughput of even the fastest PCIe Gen 4 drives. For professionals whose storage is directly on the critical path of their work — 8K video editors, 3D artists, VFX professionals, large-scale developers — CoreX Pro is the tier where storage stops being a bottleneck and starts being an asset.

## Brand Philosophy

The CoreX Pro name is built from two concepts: "Core" — the central, essential element of a computing experience, and "Pro" — the professional standard of performance, reliability, and capability. These drives are not simply the fastest TwinMOS has to offer; they are tools designed to serve professionals whose time has real value and whose workflows demand storage that keeps pace with their creativity and productivity.

CoreX Pro represents TwinMOS's commitment to manufacturing excellence applied to the most demanding storage environment available on the consumer market.

## PCIe Gen 5: Where the Numbers Become Real

PCIe Gen 5.0 x4 provides a theoretical bandwidth ceiling of 16 GB/s — double the 8 GB/s available to PCIe Gen 4 x4 drives. The CoreX Pro reaches sequential read speeds up to 14,000 MB/s in real-world sustained transfer conditions.

For the right workloads, this is not a synthetic benchmark number — it translates to tangible, measurable time savings:

| Workload | PCIe Gen 4 (7.5 GB/s) | CoreX Pro Gen 5 (14 GB/s) | Time Saved |
|----------|----------------------|--------------------------|------------|
| Copy 100GB project archive | ~13 seconds | ~7 seconds | ~6 seconds |
| Import 1TB of 4K RAW footage | ~133 seconds | ~71 seconds | ~1 minute |
| Load 500GB 3D scene assets | ~67 seconds | ~36 seconds | ~31 seconds |
| Full 1TB backup | ~133 seconds | ~71 seconds | ~1 minute |

*Figures represent sequential read/write scenarios. Actual results vary by controller, NAND, and system configuration.*

**Where Gen 5 makes the most difference:**
- Sustained sequential reads and writes of very large files (video RAW, archive extraction, large model loading)
- Workflows where storage operations are on the critical path — waiting for assets to load, exports to complete, or projects to save

**Where the Gen 5 advantage is minimal:**
- Gaming: Modern games do not saturate PCIe Gen 4 bandwidth. Load time difference vs Gen 4 is negligible to zero.
- General productivity: Web browsing, office applications, and everyday computing do not require Gen 5 speeds.

CoreX Pro is the right choice for users whose workflows genuinely benefit from sustained multi-gigabyte-per-second throughput. For everyone else, the [Xtreme Gen 4](/products/brands/xtreme) provides excellent performance at a lower price point.

## Key Technologies

### PCIe Gen 5.0 x4 with NVMe 2.0
PCIe Gen 5.0 x4 provides up to 16 GB/s of interface bandwidth — double the 8 GB/s ceiling of PCIe Gen 4 x4. CoreX Pro operates at up to 14,000 MB/s sequential read, utilizing approximately 87.5% of the available Gen 5 bandwidth in sustained transfer scenarios.

NVMe 2.0 (the protocol layer) introduces improvements over NVMe 1.4 relevant to sustained heavy workloads: enhanced queue management, improved command priority handling, and refined power state management for more efficient sustained operations.

### DRAM Cache
A dedicated DRAM buffer stores the drive's L2P (Logical-to-Physical) mapping table — the index that tells the SSD controller exactly where on the NAND each piece of data lives. When this table resides in fast DRAM rather than on the slower NAND flash itself, random read and write performance remains high and consistent even under heavy, sustained workloads with large working sets.

DRAM cache is especially important for professional applications: video editing timelines that make frequent random seeks, 3D rendering jobs that scatter-access large texture libraries, and database operations that issue high-IOPS random read workloads. All CoreX Pro capacities (1TB, 2TB, 4TB) include DRAM cache.

### Graphene Heatspreader
PCIe Gen 5 SSDs draw significantly more power than Gen 4 drives under load — typically 10–14W during sustained sequential operations. Without adequate cooling, Gen 5 drives begin thermal throttling at 75–80°C, progressively reducing performance to protect the hardware.

The CoreX Pro's integrated graphene heatspreader addresses this directly. Graphene composites offer exceptional thermal conductivity in the lateral plane, spreading heat across the drive's surface area and reducing hotspot temperatures. The slim-profile design fits under motherboard M.2 heatsinks without clearance issues and maintains compatibility with compact and laptop-style form factors.

For sustained heavy workloads (extended video transcoding, large archive operations, continuous database writes), using the motherboard's M.2 heatsink in conjunction with the CoreX Pro's integrated heatspreader provides optimal sustained performance. Case airflow also contributes to sustained Gen 5 performance under heavy loads.

### Premium TLC NAND
Micron and SK Hynix 3D TLC NAND provides the performance and endurance foundation for CoreX Pro drives. Advanced 3D stacking technology increases density per die while maintaining high program/erase cycle ratings. Multiple layers of error correction — including hardware LDPC ECC — protect data integrity across the drive's lifespan.

SLC write caching accelerates burst write operations for everyday tasks, while direct-to-TLC writing sustains performance for large sequential workloads after the SLC cache is filled.

## Platform Requirements

CoreX Pro M.2 Gen 5 requires a motherboard with a PCIe Gen 5.0 x4 M.2 slot:

| Platform | PCIe Gen 5 M.2 Support |
|----------|------------------------|
| Intel 12th Gen (Z690) | Limited — some boards have one Gen 5 M.2 slot |
| Intel 13th Gen (Z790) | Yes — primary M.2 slot is Gen 5.0 on most Z790 boards |
| Intel 14th Gen (Z790 refresh) | Yes — primary M.2 slot is Gen 5.0 |
| Intel 15th Gen (Z890/B860, Arrow Lake) | Yes — Gen 5 M.2 standard |
| AMD Ryzen 7000 (X670E, X670) | Yes — at least one Gen 5 M.2 slot |
| AMD Ryzen 9000 (X870E, X870) | Yes — Gen 5 M.2 standard |

**Note:** B-series AMD and Intel boards (B650, B760) may have only PCIe Gen 4 M.2 slots. Verify your motherboard's M.2 slot specifications before purchasing a Gen 5 drive.

Drives installed in PCIe Gen 4 M.2 slots will operate at PCIe Gen 4 speeds (~7 GB/s) — the drive is backward compatible but will not reach Gen 5 performance.

## Installation

1. Verify your motherboard has a PCIe Gen 5.0 x4 M.2 slot (consult motherboard manual)
2. Remove the M.2 slot's existing heatsink if present
3. Insert the CoreX Pro M.2 at approximately 30 degrees and seat it in the M.2 connector
4. Secure with the mounting screw
5. Replace the motherboard M.2 heatsink for optimal thermal performance
6. Boot into BIOS and verify the drive is detected and the link speed shows PCIe Gen 5.0 x4
7. Install the OS or initialize the drive via Windows Disk Management / macOS Disk Utility

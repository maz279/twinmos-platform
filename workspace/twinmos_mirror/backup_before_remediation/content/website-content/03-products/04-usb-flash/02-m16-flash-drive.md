## Key Features

### Precision Zinc-Alloy Metal Body

Where most flash drives use polycarbonate plastic, the M16 is machined from zinc alloy — a premium material used in professional tools and high-end electronics for its combination of rigidity, resistance to flex and impact, and refined appearance. The result is a flash drive that feels substantial in the hand, resists the dents and cracks that afflict plastic drives over time, and maintains its appearance after years of daily use.

The sleek metallic finish is smooth and fingerprint-resistant, ensuring the M16 looks as professional in a client meeting as it does in a laptop bag.

### Capless Design — Nothing to Lose

Traditional flash drives require a protective cap over the USB connector. Caps are the single most commonly lost flash drive component — removed once, set down, and forgotten. The M16 eliminates this problem entirely with a capless architecture that permanently exposes the connector without sacrificing protection. The connector retracts or is guarded by the drive's compact body profile, keeping it clean and ready in seconds.

### Integrated Strap Mount — Always Secured

A built-in strap mount allows the M16 to be attached to a keyring, bag loop, lanyard, or zip pull. Unlike clip-on accessories that can detach, the strap point is machined into the drive body — it is as permanent as the drive itself. Keep the M16 on your keys, your bag, or your ID lanyard, and it is always with you.

### 6 Grams, 39 × 12.2 × 4.5 mm — Genuinely Pocketable

At 6 grams and smaller than a typical door key, the M16 is one of the lightest metal-body flash drives available. It can sit in a shirt pocket, a wallet slot, or a glasses case without adding noticeable bulk or weight. This is the drive for users who want maximum storage to accompany minimum mass.

### Up to 120 MB/s Sequential Read — USB 3.2 Performance

The M16 delivers up to 120 MB/s sequential reads via the USB 3.2 Gen 1 interface — up to 4× faster than USB 2.0 drives in real-world sequential transfers. Copying a 4 GB file takes approximately 33 seconds on the M16 versus over two minutes on a typical USB 2.0 drive. Day-to-day transfers of documents, presentations, and compressed media files are effectively instantaneous.

### 256GB Maximum Capacity — The Widest Range in the TwinMOS USB Lineup

The M16 is the only TwinMOS USB flash drive available in 256GB. At that capacity, the M16 can hold approximately 65,000 high-resolution photos, a full year's worth of personal video in Full HD, or a complete system backup for most standard laptop configurations. For enterprise teams, 256GB is sufficient to store a full content distribution kit — product videos, brochures, catalogues, and sales materials — for an entire product range.

### USB 3.2 Gen 1 — Broadly Compatible

USB 3.2 Gen 1 (5 Gbps) is the current mainstream USB performance standard, backward compatible with USB 3.0 and USB 2.0 ports. The M16 goes one step further with USB 1.1 backward compatibility as well, covering essentially every USB port manufactured since 1996. On slower ports, the drive operates at the host's maximum supported speed; full performance is achieved on USB 3.0 and USB 3.2 Gen 1 ports.

### Power-Saving Mode — Efficient Operation

The M16 includes hardware-level power-saving functionality, reducing power draw when the drive is idle or connected but not actively transferring data. This is particularly relevant for laptop users where every milliwatt of unnecessary draw affects battery runtime.

### Bus-Powered — Zero External Dependencies

The M16 draws all power from the USB bus itself. No power adapter, no charging cable, no battery to maintain. Connect to any USB port and the drive operates immediately.

### Hot Swap — Plug and Go

Hot swap support means the M16 can be connected and disconnected while the host computer is running, without requiring a system restart. Standard operating system "Safely Remove Hardware" procedures should always be followed before physical disconnection to prevent data corruption.

### Plug-and-Play — No Configuration Required

Recognised automatically by Windows 10/11, macOS 10.14 Mojave and later, and Linux distributions running Kernel 2.6.31 or newer. No drivers, software installation, or account registration required.

### Default File System Format
- **32GB / 64GB:** FAT32 (4 GB individual file size limit)
- **128GB / 256GB:** exFAT (no individual file size limit)

To copy files larger than 4 GB on the 32GB or 64GB models, reformat as exFAT using Windows Disk Management, macOS Disk Utility, or the Linux `mkfs.exfat` command. This operation erases all existing drive contents — back up data first.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between the M16 and the X3 Ultra?**
The M16 prioritises build quality, capacity, and daily-carry ergonomics: zinc-alloy body, capless design, strap mount, 6-gram weight, and availability up to 256GB. The X3 Ultra prioritises speed: up to 225 MB/s sequential read versus the M16's 120 MB/s. If you move very large files frequently and speed is the primary concern, the X3 Ultra is the better choice. If you want maximum capacity, premium build quality, and the most portable form factor, choose the M16.

**Is the M16 body actually metal?**
Yes. The M16 is built from zinc alloy — a genuine metal construction, not plastic with a metallic coating. Zinc alloy is widely used in professional hardware for its combination of strength, impact resistance, and surface finish quality.

**Does the capless design expose the connector to damage?**
The M16's capless design protects the USB Type-A connector through its compact body profile rather than a removable cap. Keep the drive in a pocket, pouch, or attached to a keyring when not in use. Avoid pressing the connector against abrasive surfaces. Under normal daily-carry conditions, the capless design presents no increased connector wear risk compared to capped drives.

**What does the integrated strap mount accommodate?**
The strap mount is sized for standard keyring loops, lanyard clips, and bag attachment cords. Most commercially available keyring components and lanyard clips fit without modification.

**Can I copy files larger than 4 GB to the 256GB M16?**
Yes. The 128GB and 256GB M16 models ship formatted as exFAT, which supports individual files of any size up to the drive's total capacity. The 32GB and 64GB models ship as FAT32 (4 GB per-file limit); reformat as exFAT to remove this restriction.

**Does the M16 work with USB 1.1 ports?**
Yes. The M16 is backward compatible with USB 1.1 (USB 1.0), USB 2.0, and USB 3.0 ports in addition to USB 3.2 Gen 1. Transfer speeds will be limited by the host port's generation.

**What is the power-saving mode?**
The M16's firmware includes a low-power idle state that reduces bus power consumption when the drive is connected but not actively transferring data. This helps minimise the power draw on laptop USB ports and USB hubs running from limited power budgets.

**Can I use the M16 as a bootable drive?**
Yes. Use standard tools such as Rufus, Balena Etcher, the Windows Media Creation Tool, macOS Disk Utility, or the Linux `dd` command to write bootable images. Note that formatting the drive for bootable use will erase existing contents.

**How should I safely remove the M16?**
Always use "Safely Remove Hardware" on Windows, the Eject function in macOS Finder, or `umount` on Linux before physically disconnecting the drive. Disconnecting while files are open or transfers are pending risks data corruption.

**Is the M16 waterproof?**
The zinc-alloy body provides improved physical durability compared to plastic drives, but the M16 does not carry a formal waterproof or IP rating. Avoid immersion or prolonged exposure to moisture.

**What warranty covers the M16?**
The M16 is covered by the TwinMOS standard product warranty. See the [Warranty Policy](/support/warranty-policy) page for full terms and regional details, or [register your product online](/support/warranty-registration).

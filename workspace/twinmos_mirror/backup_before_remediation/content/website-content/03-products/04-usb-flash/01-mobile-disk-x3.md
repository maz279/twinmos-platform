## Key Features

### 225 MB/s Sequential Read — Class-Leading Speed

The X3 Ultra achieves up to 225 MB/s sequential read speeds — the fastest performance in the TwinMOS USB flash drive range. Translating that into real-world terms: a 4 GB video file that takes nearly three minutes on a USB 2.0 drive copies in under 20 seconds. A 32 GB folder of RAW photos moves in approximately two and a half minutes instead of over twenty.

This speed is delivered through the USB 3.2 Gen 1 interface (5 Gbps bandwidth), using high-quality NAND flash validated to TwinMOS's internal performance standards.

### USB 3.2 Gen 1 — The Modern Standard

USB 3.2 Gen 1 (previously marketed as USB 3.1 Gen 1 and USB 3.0) is the contemporary standard for mainstream USB flash storage, offering up to 5 Gbps of bus bandwidth. Both new USB 3.2-equipped systems and legacy USB 3.0 systems deliver the X3 Ultra's full performance. On USB 2.0 ports, the drive operates at USB 2.0 speeds — full backward compatibility is maintained at all times.

### Dual-Tone Black-Silver Design

The X3 Ultra's black-and-silver two-tone body is designed to stand out in any laptop bag, pencil case, or docking station port cluster. The contrasting colours make the drive easy to locate, while the compact pendrive form factor keeps it unobtrusive on a keyring or in a pocket.

### Plug-and-Play — No Setup Required

Insert the X3 Ultra into any USB Type-A port and your operating system recognises it within seconds. No drivers to download, no software to install, no account registration required. Works natively on Windows 10 and 11, macOS 10.14 Mojave and later, and any Linux distribution running Kernel 2.6.31 or newer.

### Broad Connector Compatibility

The USB Type-A connector is the most widely deployed USB interface in the world — found on desktop computers, laptops, smart TVs, gaming consoles, in-car entertainment systems, projectors, and USB hubs across billions of devices. The X3 Ultra connects to any of them directly, with no adapters required.

### No Moving Parts — Flash Reliability

As a solid-state flash drive, the X3 Ultra contains no spinning disks, read heads, or mechanical actuators. This makes it inherently resistant to the vibration, minor drops, and orientation changes of daily carry — contrasting with hard disk-based portable drives that require careful handling.

### Bus-Powered — No External Power

The X3 Ultra draws all operating power from the USB port itself. No power cable, no wall adapter, no battery. Simply plug in and go.

### Default File System Format
- **32GB / 64GB:** FAT32 (4 GB per-file size limit)
- **128GB:** exFAT (no per-file size limit; supports files up to drive capacity)

To copy individual files larger than 4 GB on 32GB or 64GB models, reformat the drive as exFAT using Windows Disk Management, macOS Disk Utility, or the Linux `mkfs.exfat` command.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does "USB 3.2 Gen 1" mean on this drive?**
USB 3.2 Gen 1 is the official USB Implementers Forum designation for the interface formerly called USB 3.1 Gen 1 and USB 3.0. It provides up to 5 Gbps of bus bandwidth, enabling real-world sequential read speeds of up to 225 MB/s on the X3 Ultra. The spec is backward compatible with USB 3.0 and USB 2.0 ports.

**Is the X3 Ultra faster than the M16?**
Yes. The X3 Ultra reaches up to 225 MB/s sequential read versus the M16's 120 MB/s. Both offer 60 MB/s sequential write. The X3 Ultra is the better choice when transfer speed is the primary requirement; the M16 offers a larger maximum capacity (256GB) and a premium zinc-alloy body.

**Can I copy files over 4 GB onto the X3 Ultra?**
The 128GB model ships as exFAT and supports files of any size up to the drive's total capacity. The 32GB and 64GB models ship as FAT32, which limits individual files to 4 GB. Reformat to exFAT to remove this limitation on smaller capacity models.

**Does this drive work with my MacBook or laptop with only USB-C ports?**
The X3 Ultra uses a USB Type-A connector. A USB-A to USB-C adapter (sold separately) allows use with USB-C-only laptops. Performance is maintained when using a USB 3.x-rated adapter.

**Can I use the X3 Ultra to install Windows or Linux from scratch?**
Yes. Use Rufus (Windows), Balena Etcher (cross-platform), or the Windows Media Creation Tool to write the installation ISO to the X3 Ultra. For Linux, the `dd` command or Balena Etcher are standard options.

**How should I keep the drive clean and safe?**
Store the drive in a protective pouch or case when not in use. Avoid exposing it to liquids, extreme heat, or static electricity. Always eject safely before removing.

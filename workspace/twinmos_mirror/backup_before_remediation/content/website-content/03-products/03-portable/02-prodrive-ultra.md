# ProDrive Ultra Portable HDD

The TwinMOS ProDrive Ultra is a high-capacity portable external hard drive built for users who need to carry, back up, or archive terabytes of data without spending a premium. Available in 1TB, 2TB, and 4TB configurations, it delivers reliable storage in a compact, bus-powered enclosure that requires no external power adapter — making it equally practical at a desk, in a laptop bag, or packed into a field kit.

Hard disk drives are not the fastest portable storage option — that distinction belongs to the [ELITE Drive Pro SSD](/products/portable/elite-drive-pro). But for one critical metric — **cost per terabyte** — hard drives remain unmatched. When the priority is storing as much data as possible at the lowest possible cost, the ProDrive Ultra is the right choice.

## Key Features

### Up to 4TB in a Portable Enclosure

Four terabytes of storage is enough room for approximately 1,000,000 high-resolution photographs, over 2,000 hours of HD video, or a comprehensive multi-year backup archive — all in a drive that fits in a laptop bag, jacket pocket, or desk drawer. No external power supply, no desktop-sized enclosure, no complications.

### USB 3.0 Bus-Powered Convenience

USB 3.0 delivers 900 mA of current to the drive — enough to power the 2.5-inch hard disk mechanism without a wall adapter. You get high-capacity portable storage that works anywhere there is a USB port: coffee shops, airport lounges, classrooms, client offices, or on the move.

USB 3.0 is fully backward compatible with USB 2.0 ports. Speeds on a USB 2.0 connection drop to approximately 25–40 MB/s due to the older interface bandwidth limit; the drive will function correctly but transfers will be significantly slower.

### Plug-and-Play on Windows, macOS, and Linux

The ProDrive Ultra mounts automatically with no driver installation required:

- **Windows** XP, 7, 10, 11 — appears in File Explorer immediately upon connection
- **macOS** — appears on the Desktop and in Finder; compatible with all macOS versions
- **Linux** — kernel 2.6+ supports USB mass storage natively across all major distributions

The drive ships pre-formatted in **exFAT** for cross-platform read/write compatibility without reformatting. Reformat in NTFS for Windows-only use, or in HFS+ / APFS for macOS Time Machine compatibility.

### Compact 2.5-Inch Enclosure

The 2.5-inch hard drive form factor keeps the ProDrive Ultra significantly smaller and lighter than desktop 3.5-inch external drives. Despite housing up to 4TB of storage, it fits flat in a laptop bag or document pouch and travels without drawing attention.

## Use Cases

### System Backup — Windows Backup and macOS Time Machine

The ProDrive Ultra is purpose-made for automated system backups. Connect it, configure Windows Backup or Time Machine, and let your operating system maintain a continuous versioned copy of your entire system. The 4TB model can retain weeks or months of backup history while still leaving room for personal file archives alongside it.

Reformat the drive in NTFS for Windows Backup, or in HFS+ / APFS for Time Machine. Both are straightforward through each operating system's built-in disk utility.

### Media Library and Entertainment Archive

Movie enthusiasts, photographers, and music collectors accumulate data faster than affordable internal SSDs grow. Store an entire video collection, RAW photo archive, and music library on the ProDrive Ultra and access it from any computer. The 4TB model handles even large family or professional media repositories without partitioning.

### Long-Term Archival and Compliance Storage

Businesses, legal practices, medical offices, and accountants must retain documents and records for years — sometimes decades. A ProDrive Ultra kept offline (in a drawer or fireproof safe) provides an inexpensive, air-gapped archive medium. For archival use, the drive should be accessed and verified at least once annually to confirm data integrity.

### Gaming Library Offload

High-end PC games routinely exceed 50–100 GB each. Use the ProDrive Ultra to archive game installations you do not play regularly, then copy them back to your primary SSD when needed — far faster than re-downloading over a broadband connection. The 4TB model handles even the largest Steam, Epic, or GOG libraries.

### Surveillance and DVR Storage Expansion

Some network video recorders (NVRs) and digital video recorders (DVRs) support USB storage for extended recording capacity. The ProDrive Ultra can expand recording time on compatible systems. Verify your DVR or NVR supports the connected capacity, USB power delivery, and file system before use.

### Small Business Shared Storage

In environments without a dedicated NAS, the ProDrive Ultra can serve as a shared file repository accessed through a host computer on a local network. Files already stored and unchanged (sequential access) are well-suited to HDD performance characteristics.

## Important Usage Notes

### Handle with Care During Operation

The ProDrive Ultra contains precision mechanical components: a spinning magnetic platter and a read/write head that floats nanometres above the recording surface. **Do not move, drop, or bump the drive while it is operating.** Physical shock during an active read or write cycle can corrupt data or permanently damage the drive mechanism.

Always use your operating system's **Eject** or **Safely Remove Hardware** function before unplugging. Wait for any drive activity indicator to stop before disconnecting, particularly after write-intensive operations such as backup runs.

### Performance for Active Workloads

The ProDrive Ultra is designed for backup, archival, and sequential large-file transfers. It is **not recommended** for workloads that require fast random access, such as:

- Running applications, virtual machines, or databases directly from the drive
- Video editing with high-bitrate codecs directly from the drive (use the [ELITE Drive Pro](/products/portable/elite-drive-pro) for this)
- Gaming directly from the drive as your primary game storage

For any of the above, the ELITE Drive Pro — or an internal SSD — is the appropriate solution.

### Cold Storage Best Practices

For long-term offline archival, store the ProDrive Ultra in a cool, dry environment away from strong magnetic fields, direct sunlight, and humidity. Power the drive and verify data integrity at least once per year. Hard drives that remain unpowered for extended periods may develop head stiction or bearing degradation; periodic operation helps prevent this.

## Connecting to a USB-C Computer

The ProDrive Ultra's cable terminates in a USB Type-A connector on the host side. To connect to a laptop or desktop that has only USB-C ports, use a standard USB-C to USB-A adapter (sold separately). The drive operates at full USB 3.0 speed through such an adapter. Alternatively, a USB-C to USB Micro-B cable (sold separately) can be used in place of the included cable.

## Getting Started

1. Unbox the ProDrive Ultra and locate the USB 3.0 cable.
2. Connect the Micro-B end of the cable to the drive's USB port.
3. Connect the Type-A end to a USB 3.0 port on your computer (typically marked with a blue insert or the SS symbol for best performance).
4. The drive will spin up and mount within a few seconds. On Windows it appears in File Explorer; on macOS it appears on the Desktop and in Finder.
5. Begin transferring files, or configure backup software to use the drive as its destination.
6. **Always eject the drive safely** before unplugging.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the actual transfer speed of the ProDrive Ultra?**
Sequential read is approximately 125–135 MB/s and sequential write is approximately 110–125 MB/s under typical conditions. These speeds are limited by the 2.5-inch 5,400 RPM hard disk mechanism, not the USB 3.0 interface. USB 3.0 provides far more bandwidth than the drive can use.

**Why is my ProDrive Ultra slower than the spec suggests?**
Hard drives perform best during large sequential file operations (copying a single large video file, for example). Copying thousands of small files — emails, documents, small images — will appear significantly slower because the drive must mechanically seek to a new location for each file. This is normal for all hard disk drives and is not a defect.

**Can I use the ProDrive Ultra for Time Machine on a Mac?**
Yes. Open Disk Utility, erase the drive in APFS or HFS+, then select it as your Time Machine backup disk in System Preferences. The 2TB and 4TB models are the best choices for maintaining multiple backup snapshots with a meaningful retention window.

**Does the ProDrive Ultra need an external power adapter?**
No. The drive is fully bus-powered from the USB connection. No external power supply, no wall outlet, no extra cables.

**Can I format the drive in NTFS for Windows?**
Yes. Use Windows Disk Management to reformat in NTFS if preferred. Note that macOS cannot write to NTFS-formatted drives by default — macOS will mount the drive as read-only unless you use third-party NTFS drivers. exFAT (the factory format) provides full read/write compatibility on both platforms without additional software.

**Is it safe to unplug the drive without ejecting?**
No. Always eject the drive using the operating system's safe-remove function before unplugging, especially after write operations. Abrupt disconnection can corrupt the file system or, in some cases, cause a mechanical head park failure if the drive was in the middle of a write cycle.

**What is the difference between the ProDrive Ultra and the ELITE Drive Pro?**
The ProDrive Ultra is a mechanical hard drive: slower (125–135 MB/s vs. 450 MB/s) but available in capacities up to 4TB at a lower cost per terabyte, and with a longer 3-year warranty. The ELITE Drive Pro is a solid-state drive: faster, more shock-resistant, and more compact. See the full [comparison guide](/products/portable#comparison).

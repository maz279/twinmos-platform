# PCIe Gen 5.0 NVMe SSDs

PCIe Gen 5.0 doubles the available bandwidth of Gen 4.0, enabling a new class of ultra-high-speed solid-state drives. TwinMOS PCIe Gen 5.0 NVMe SSDs leverage this massive headroom to deliver sequential read speeds up to 14,000 MB/s—nearly saturating the x4 lane allocation and transforming how professionals interact with large datasets.

## The Gen 5.0 Advantage

For users working with 8K video, massive 3D scenes, scientific datasets, or high-frequency trading data, storage speed is no longer a bottleneck. PCIe Gen 5.0 SSDs can ingest, process, and export data at rates previously reserved for RAM disks or enterprise SAN arrays.

Key benefits of Gen 5.0 NVMe include:

- **Double the Bandwidth**: Gen 5.0 x4 provides approximately 16 GB/s of raw PCIe bandwidth, compared to ~8 GB/s on Gen 4.0
- **Faster Workflow Completion**: Video editors can scrub 8K timelines in real time; 3D artists can load massive textures instantly
- **Future-Proofing**: Investing in Gen 5.0 storage ensures your system remains competitive as software continues to scale with hardware

## Platform Requirements

PCIe Gen 5.0 SSDs require specific hardware support:

- **Motherboard**: AMD 600-series (X670E, X670, B650E) or Intel 700-series (Z790, B760 with Gen 5.0 M.2) chipsets
- **CPU**: AMD Ryzen 7000 series or Intel 12th/13th/14th Gen Core processors
- **M.2 Slot**: Must support PCIe Gen 5.0 x4 (check motherboard manual)

## Thermal Considerations

Gen 5.0 SSDs generate more heat than previous generations due to their higher controller and NAND throughput. The CoreX Pro M.2 includes a graphene heatsink to manage temperatures. For sustained workloads, ensure adequate case airflow or consider an aftermarket M.2 heatsink.

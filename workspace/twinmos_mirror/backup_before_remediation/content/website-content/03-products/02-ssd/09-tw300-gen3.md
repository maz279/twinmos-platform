# TW300 M.2 PCIe Gen 3.0

The TwinMOS TW300 M.2 is an entry-level PCIe Gen 3.0 NVMe SSD designed to make the transition from hard drives and SATA SSDs accessible to everyone. With sequential reads around 3,500 MB/s and writes around 3,000 MB/s, the TW300 delivers a transformative performance upgrade at a budget-friendly price point.

## Product Overview

The TW300 M.2 focuses on delivering the core benefits of NVMe storage—fast boot times, quick application launches, and responsive file operations—without the premium cost of higher-tier drives. It is an ideal choice for students, families, small businesses, and anyone building or upgrading a PC on a budget.

Available in 256GB, 512GB, and 1TB capacities, the TW300 covers everything from a compact OS drive to a spacious primary storage volume for games, media, and documents.

## Key Features

**Affordable NVMe Performance**
The TW300 M.2 brings NVMe speeds to price points traditionally occupied by SATA SSDs. Users upgrading from hard drives will experience boot times reduced from minutes to seconds, while those moving from SATA will notice faster file copies and application loads.

**Reliable 3D TLC NAND**
Despite its budget positioning, the TW300 uses reliable 3D TLC NAND flash. This ensures adequate endurance for everyday use and protects data integrity over the drive's service life.

**Wide Compatibility**
The TW300 works in any M.2 slot supporting PCIe Gen 3.0 or higher. It is also backward compatible with Gen 4.0 and Gen 5.0 slots, making it a safe choice for mixed-platform environments.

**Easy Upgrade Path**
Replacing a hard drive or SATA SSD with the TW300 is straightforward. The M.2 2280 form factor installs directly on the motherboard with a single screw—no cables, brackets, or drive bays required.

## Use Cases

**Family PC Upgrade**
Older family computers with hard drives feel brand new after a TW300 upgrade. Web browsing, video streaming, homework, and photo management all become smoother and more enjoyable.

**Student Laptops**
Students on a budget can extend the life of an older laptop by swapping its hard drive for a TW300 M.2. The speed improvement makes research, writing, and multitasking far more efficient.

**Budget Gaming Builds**
A 1TB TW300 M.2 provides enough space for the OS, a handful of AAA games, and essential applications—leaving more of the build budget for the CPU and GPU.

**Secondary Storage**
Even on high-end systems with Gen 4.0 or Gen 5.0 primary drives, the TW300 serves as fast, affordable secondary storage for games, media, and backups.

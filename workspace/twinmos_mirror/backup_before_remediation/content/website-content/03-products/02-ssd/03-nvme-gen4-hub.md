# PCIe Gen 4.0 NVMe SSDs

PCIe Gen 4.0 NVMe SSDs represent the mainstream performance tier for modern desktops and laptops. With sequential read speeds ranging from 5,000 MB/s to 7,500 MB/s, TwinMOS Gen 4.0 drives deliver a transformative upgrade over SATA SSDs and mechanical hard drives while remaining compatible with a wide range of platforms.

## Gen 4.0 Ecosystem

PCIe Gen 4.0 doubles the per-lane bandwidth of Gen 3.0, enabling NVMe SSDs to reach speeds that were previously impossible on consumer hardware. AMD introduced Gen 4.0 support with the Ryzen 3000 series and X570 platform, while Intel followed with 11th Gen Core processors and 500-series chipsets. Today, Gen 4.0 is the de facto standard for performance-oriented builds.

## TwinMOS Gen 4.0 Lineup

### Xtreme M.2

The Xtreme M.2 is TwinMOS's high-performance Gen 4.0 drive, offering sequential reads up to 7,500 MB/s and writes up to 6,800 MB/s. Available in 1TB and 2TB capacities, it features a graphene heatsheet, SMI controller, Micron/Hynix TLC NAND, and 2GB of DRAM cache on the 2TB model.

[Learn more about Xtreme M.2](/products/ssd/xtreme-gen4)

### CoreX M.2

The CoreX M.2 delivers solid Gen 4.0 performance with reads up to 5,000 MB/s and writes up to 4,800 MB/s. Available in 1TB and 2TB, this single-sided drive is ideal for laptops and compact systems where space and thermal headroom are limited.

[Learn more about CoreX M.2](/products/ssd/corex-gen4)

### Xtreme Pro M.2

The Xtreme Pro M.2 is tuned for gaming and content creation workloads. With a 1TB capacity and Gen 4.0 performance, it targets users who need fast, reliable storage for game libraries and creative projects without stepping up to Gen 5.0 pricing.

## Platform Compatibility

TwinMOS Gen 4.0 NVMe SSDs are compatible with:

- AMD Ryzen 3000/5000/7000 series with X570, B550, X670, B650 motherboards
- Intel 11th/12th/13th/14th Gen Core with Z590, Z690, Z790, B760 motherboards
- Sony PlayStation 5 (Xtreme M.2 and CoreX M.2 with heatsink)
- Laptops with M.2 PCIe Gen 4.0 slots

## Thermal Management

Gen 4.0 SSDs benefit from adequate cooling. The Xtreme M.2 includes a graphene heatsheet, while the CoreX M.2's single-sided design reduces thermal density. For sustained workloads, ensure your motherboard M.2 heatsink or case airflow is sufficient.

# M.2 2280 SATA III SSD

The TwinMOS M.2 2280 SATA III SSD delivers reliable SATA-level flash storage in a compact, cable-free M.2 form factor. Designed for ultrabooks, slim laptops, and small-form-factor PCs that have M.2 slots without PCIe NVMe support, this drive offers a clean internal upgrade path without the bulk of a 2.5" drive.

## Product Overview

Many laptops and compact desktops from the mid-2010s to early 2020s include M.2 slots that are wired for SATA rather than PCIe. These systems cannot take advantage of NVMe speeds but still benefit enormously from the responsiveness of solid-state storage. The M.2 2280 SATA III SSD is purpose-built for these machines.

With sequential read speeds up to 580 MB/s and write speeds up to 550 MB/s, the drive delivers a significant performance improvement over mechanical hard drives while fitting neatly into the M.2 slot with a single mounting screw.

## Key Features

**Compact M.2 Form Factor**
The 2280 form factor (80mm long, 22mm wide) is the standard size for laptop and desktop M.2 slots. By eliminating SATA cables and drive bays, the M.2 2280 SATA III SSD simplifies installation and improves airflow inside cramped chassis.

**SATA Compatibility**
This drive uses the SATA protocol over the M.2 physical connector (M+B key or B key). It is specifically designed for systems that do not support PCIe NVMe on their M.2 slots. Installing it in an NVMe-only slot will not function.

**Silent, Shock-Resistant Storage**
Like all SSDs, the M.2 2280 SATA III has no moving parts. It operates silently, withstands shocks and vibration, and consumes less power than a hard drive—all critical advantages for portable systems.

**Reliable 3D TLC NAND**
High-quality Micron 3D TLC flash provides a balance of performance, endurance, and cost. The drive is suitable for everyday computing, office productivity, and light media workloads.

## Use Cases

**Ultrabook Upgrade**
Slim laptops with M.2 SATA slots can be upgraded internally without adapters or external enclosures. The M.2 2280 SATA III SSD replaces a slow hard drive or a failed SSD with minimal disassembly.

**Compact Desktop Builds**
Mini-ITX and small-form-factor systems often have limited drive bays. An M.2 SATA SSD provides internal storage without consuming precious chassis space or SATA ports.

**All-in-One PC**
All-in-one computers from brands like HP, Dell, and Lenovo frequently use M.2 SATA SSDs as primary storage. The TwinMOS M.2 2280 SATA III is a direct replacement or upgrade for these systems.

**Secondary Laptop Storage**
Some laptops have a second M.2 slot for storage expansion. If this slot is SATA-only, the M.2 2280 SATA III SSD adds capacity without interfering with the primary NVMe drive.

## Important Compatibility Note

The M.2 2280 SATA III SSD requires an M.2 slot that supports the SATA protocol (typically B-key or M+B-key). It is **not** compatible with M.2 slots that only support PCIe/NVMe (M-key only). Verify your system's M.2 slot type before purchasing. Use our [Compatibility Finder](/products/finder) for assistance.

## Installation

1. Power off the device completely
2. Locate the M.2 slot (refer to your system manual)
3. Remove the mounting screw from the M.2 standoff
4. Insert the SSD at approximately 30 degrees and press down gently
5. Secure with the mounting screw
6. Reassemble and power on
7. Initialize the drive in your operating system's disk management utility

# Alpha Pro M.2 PCIe Gen 3.0

The TwinMOS Alpha Pro M.2 is a performance-oriented PCIe Gen 3.0 NVMe SSD that punches above its weight class. With sequential read speeds up to 3,600 MB/s and write speeds up to 3,250 MB/s, it delivers responsive, reliable storage for mainstream users upgrading from SATA SSDs or mechanical hard drives.

## Product Overview

The Alpha Pro M.2 leverages a proven SMI controller with Micron/Hynix 3D TLC NAND to achieve sustained performance and reliability. Built with quality components, the Alpha Pro M.2 is available in 128GB, 256GB, 512GB, 1TB and 2TB capacities—ideal for OS drives, boot volumes, and budget-friendly primary storage.

## Key Features

**3D TLC NAND**
Three-dimensional TLC NAND stacks memory cells vertically to increase density and endurance. The Alpha Pro M.2 uses high-quality Micron/Hynix 3D TLC flash to ensure data retention and reliability over the drive's lifespan.

**SMI Controller**
The Silicon Motion controller is optimized for power efficiency, thermal management, and broad platform compatibility. It ensures the Alpha Pro M.2 performs consistently across Intel and AMD platforms.

**Compact M.2 Form Factor**
The 2280 form factor installs directly on the motherboard, saving space and eliminating cable clutter. This is especially valuable in small-form-factor builds and laptops.

## Use Cases

**OS & Boot Drive**
A 256GB Alpha Pro M.2 is the perfect size for a Windows or Linux boot drive. Boot times shrink to seconds, and the system feels dramatically more responsive than with a hard drive.

**Budget Gaming**
Install the OS and a few favorite games on a 512GB Alpha Pro M.2 for faster load times without breaking the bank. The drive's performance is more than sufficient for most modern titles.

**Office Productivity**
Business desktops and workstations benefit from the Alpha Pro's low latency and fast application launches. Multitasking across spreadsheets, presentations, and browsers feels snappy and fluid.

**Laptop Upgrade**
Many laptops with M.2 slots ship with slow SATA SSDs or hard drives. Upgrading to an Alpha Pro M.2 breathes new life into these systems with NVMe speeds.

## Compatibility

- AMD Ryzen 1000 through 3000 series
- Laptops with M.2 PCIe Gen 3.0 slots

# CoreX Pro M.2 PCIe Gen 5.0

The TwinMOS CoreX Pro M.2 is a next-generation PCIe Gen 5.0 NVMe SSD engineered for professionals, enthusiasts, and power users who refuse to compromise on storage performance. With sequential read speeds up to 14,000 MB/s and write speeds reaching 10,000–13,000 MB/s, the CoreX Pro redefines what consumer storage can achieve.

## Product Overview

Built around a high-performance PCIe Gen 5.0 x4 controller and premium Micron/Hynix 3D TLC NAND, the CoreX Pro M.2 is designed to eliminate storage bottlenecks in the most demanding workflows. Whether you are editing 8K raw video, compiling million-line codebases, or loading massive open-world game assets, this SSD delivers responsiveness that keeps pace with your creativity.

A dedicated DRAM cache ensures consistent performance under heavy random workloads, while a graphene heatsink manages thermal output during sustained transfers. With capacities up to 4TB, the CoreX Pro provides ample room for large project libraries, game collections, and professional archives.

## Key Features

**Breakthrough Gen 5.0 Speed**
The CoreX Pro leverages the full bandwidth of PCIe Gen 5.0 x4 to deliver roughly double the sequential throughput of flagship Gen 4.0 drives. For content creators, this means faster timeline scrubbing, quicker export renders, and near-instantaneous project loads.

**Premium TLC NAND**
Micron and Hynix TLC NAND flash provides the ideal balance of performance, endurance, and cost. Advanced wear-leveling algorithms extend drive lifespan, while SLC caching accelerates burst writes for everyday tasks.

**DRAM Cache**
A dedicated DRAM buffer stores the drive's mapping tables, ensuring consistent random read/write performance even when the SLC cache is exhausted. This is critical for professional applications with large working sets.

**Graphene Heatsink**
The integrated graphene heatsink dissipates heat efficiently without adding significant bulk. Its low-profile design fits beneath motherboard heatsinks and in compact builds while maintaining thermal stability during extended workloads.

## Use Cases

**8K Video Production**
Professional video editors working with 8K RED, Blackmagic RAW, or ProRes footage need storage that can sustain multi-gigabyte-per-second data rates. The CoreX Pro enables real-time playback and accelerated transcoding in DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, and Final Cut Pro.

**3D Rendering & VFX**
Loading massive texture libraries, simulation caches, and scene files into Blender, Houdini, or Maya becomes significantly faster with Gen 5.0 storage. Artists spend less time waiting and more time creating.

**Software Development**
Large-scale builds, container orchestration, and database operations benefit from the CoreX Pro's low latency and high throughput. Compile times shrink, and local development environments feel more responsive.

**High-End Gaming**
While most games do not yet saturate Gen 5.0 bandwidth, open-world titles with heavy streaming benefit from reduced pop-in and faster level transitions. The CoreX Pro also future-proofs gaming builds for next-generation engines.

## Installation

1. Ensure your motherboard has an M.2 slot supporting PCIe Gen 5.0 x4
2. Remove the M.2 heatsink if present
3. Insert the CoreX Pro at a 30-degree angle and secure with the mounting screw
4. Reattach the motherboard heatsink or use the included graphene heatsink
5. Install the latest NVMe driver and verify link speed in BIOS or CrystalDiskInfo

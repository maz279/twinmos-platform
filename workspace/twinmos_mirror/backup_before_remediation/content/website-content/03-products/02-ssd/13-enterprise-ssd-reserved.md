# Enterprise SSDs

TwinMOS offers enterprise-grade solid-state drives designed for the reliability, endurance, and performance demands of data centers, cloud infrastructure, and industrial applications. Our enterprise SSD portfolio includes U.2, E1.S, and specialized M.2 form factors built to handle 24/7 operation under heavy workloads.

## Product Overview

Enterprise SSDs differ from consumer drives in several critical ways:

- **Higher Endurance**: Enterprise drives are rated for significantly more write cycles (DWPD - Drive Writes Per Day)
- **Power-Loss Protection**: Onboard capacitors ensure data in flight is committed to NAND in the event of unexpected power loss
- **Consistent Latency**: Enterprise SSDs prioritize predictable performance over peak benchmark numbers
- **Hot-Swap Support**: Many enterprise form factors support insertion and removal without powering down the system
- **AES Encryption**: Hardware-based encryption protects data at rest

## Target Applications

- **Hyperscale Data Centers**: High-capacity drives for cloud storage and compute nodes
- **Database Servers**: Low-latency drives for transactional and analytical databases
- **Virtualization Hosts**: High-IOPS storage for VM density and responsiveness
- **Content Delivery Networks**: Reliable reads for high-traffic media serving
- **Industrial Automation**: Ruggedized drives for factory floors and outdoor installations

## Form Factors & Interfaces

TwinMOS enterprise SSDs are available in interfaces and form factors including:

- U.2 (2.5" NVMe) for hot-swap server bays
- E1.S and E1.L for next-generation rack-scale architectures
- M.2 22110 for embedded and edge computing systems
- Custom form factors for OEM and industrial customers

## The 2025–2026 Enterprise IT Refresh Catalyst

Several converging factors are driving the most significant corporate hardware refresh cycle in years:

### Windows 10 End of Life (October 14, 2025)
Microsoft ended support for Windows 10 on October 14, 2025. Organizations still running Windows 10 face increasing security risk, and Windows 11's hardware requirements — including TPM 2.0, Secure Boot, and a minimum of 64GB storage — are forcing infrastructure upgrades. Many HDD-equipped workstations cannot meet these requirements efficiently.

### The HDD-to-SSD Migration Opportunity
- Approximately **60% of SMEs are actively transitioning from HDDs to SSDs**, with SSD adoption in the SME sector growing 22% over the past three years
- All-flash storage deployments have demonstrated **rack count reductions of up to 90%** compared to equivalent HDD arrays, with corresponding power and cooling savings
- Enterprise SSD average pricing reached **approximately USD 60/TB in 2025** — narrowing the cost premium over HDDs to the point where TCO analysis now favors SSDs for virtually all workloads

### NVMe as the New Enterprise Standard
- **78% of enterprise storage upgrades in 2025 deployed NVMe drives**, up from 49% in 2022
- NVMe delivers 5–10× the random IOPS of SATA SSDs, dramatically reducing database query times, application load times, and virtual machine boot sequences

The fastest and most cost-effective way to improve end-user productivity without replacing entire systems:

**HDD → SSD Upgrade:**

## The Business Case for SSD Upgrades

### Productivity ROI
- Employees save an estimated **10 minutes per day** on boot, application launch, and file operations after SSD upgrades
- Across a team of 50 employees: **500 minutes/day = 40+ hours per year per person recovered**
- For a 100-person organization: equivalent to hiring an additional 2 full-time employees in recaptured productivity

### Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)
- SSD-equipped workstations generate fewer helpdesk tickets related to performance and freezes
- HDDs are the most common mechanical failure point in laptops — SSDs have no moving parts and MTBF exceeding 1,000,000 hours
- SSD upgrade extends the useful lifespan of existing hardware by 2–3 years, deferring full PC replacement capex
- All-SSD fleets reduce IT maintenance burden significantly

### Windows 11 Readiness
- Windows 11 requires 64GB minimum storage — many HDD-equipped systems need a storage upgrade to qualify
- Windows 11 performs significantly better on SSD than HDD due to its optimized storage stack

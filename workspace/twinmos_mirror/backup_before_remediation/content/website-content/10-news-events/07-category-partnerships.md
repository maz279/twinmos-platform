# Partnerships

TwinMOS's global success is built on strong, long-standing partnerships with distributors, resellers, system integrators, and retailers across 93+ countries. Our partnership program delivers competitive margins, comprehensive marketing support, technical training, and dedicated account management to help our partners grow.

## Featured Partnership

### [TwinMOS Signs MOU with Supertron for India Distribution](/news-events/2026-q1-supertron-india-mou)
**Q1 2026**
TwinMOS has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with Supertron, a leading IT distribution company in India, to expand the distribution of TwinMOS memory and storage products across the Indian market. This partnership targets national coverage for consumer, SMB, and enterprise segments.

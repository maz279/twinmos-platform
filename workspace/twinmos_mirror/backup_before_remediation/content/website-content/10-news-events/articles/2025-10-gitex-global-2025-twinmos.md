# GITEX GLOBAL 2025: TwinMOS in Dubai

**Published:** October 2025  
**Category:** Events  
**Event:** GITEX GLOBAL 2025  
**Reading Time:** 4 minutes

---

TwinMOS participated in **GITEX GLOBAL 2025**, the world's largest and most influential technology exhibition, held from October 13 to October 17, 2025, in Dubai, UAE. The company hosted strategic partner meetings and product demonstrations at the **Conrad Dubai** in Meeting Rooms 8 & 9, engaging with distributors, enterprise clients, and technology partners from across the Middle East and Africa.

## Why GITEX GLOBAL Matters for TwinMOS

GITEX GLOBAL marked its 45th edition in 2025, cementing its position as the premier technology platform for the Middle East, Africa, and beyond. For TwinMOS, with headquarters in Dubai and deep roots across the MEA region, GITEX represents our most important annual engagement platform for strengthening distributor relationships, exploring enterprise opportunities, and showcasing our latest innovations to a global audience.

## Participation Highlights

### Strategic Partner Meetings
TwinMOS hosted a series of focused meetings with existing and prospective distributors from the GCC, North Africa, and Sub-Saharan Africa. These sessions covered:

- Regional product roadmaps and availability timelines
- Co-marketing and promotional campaign planning
- Technical training and certification programs
- Volume pricing and incentive structures

### Enterprise Engagement
The TwinMOS team engaged with corporate IT buyers, government procurement representatives, and system integrators exploring memory and storage solutions for:

- Data center and cloud infrastructure projects
- Government digital transformation initiatives
- Corporate fleet upgrades and refreshes
- Gaming and esports venue deployments

### Product Demonstrations
Visitors to Meeting Rooms 8 & 9 experienced hands-on demonstrations of TwinMOS's comprehensive product portfolio:

#### Flagship Storage
- **CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSD** — PCIe Gen 5.0 x4 with up to 14,000 MB/s sequential read
- **Xtreme Gen4X4 NVMe Pro** — High-performance Gen 4.0 storage for gaming and content creation

#### Next-Generation Memory
- **VOLTX DDR5 U-DIMM** — 5600MHz desktop memory with PMIC and on-die ECC
- **VOLTX RGB DDR5-6000** — Gaming memory with customizable RGB lighting
- **VOLTX DDR5 SO-DIMM** — High-performance laptop memory

#### Business & Consumer Storage
- **Hyper SSD H2 Ultra** — Reliable SATA III SSDs for mainstream and business use
- **Elite Drive Pro Portable SSD** — USB 3.2 Gen 2 portable storage
- **Mobile Disk X3 USB Flash Drive** — High-speed mobile storage

## About GITEX GLOBAL

GITEX GLOBAL is the world's largest and most influential technology exhibition and conference, held annually in Dubai, UAE. The event gathers technology innovators, enterprise leaders, government entities, and global buyers from around the world. It serves as the premier platform for unveiling emerging technologies, forging strategic partnerships, and exploring new market opportunities across the Middle East, Africa, and beyond.

Key focus areas at GITEX GLOBAL 2025 included AI, cybersecurity, quantum computing, mobility, and sustainable technology — all sectors that drive significant demand for high-performance memory and storage solutions.

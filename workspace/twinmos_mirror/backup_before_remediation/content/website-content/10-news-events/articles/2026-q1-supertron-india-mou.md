# TwinMOS Signs MOU with Supertron for India Distribution

**Published:** Q1 2026  
**Category:** Partnerships

---

TwinMOS has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with Supertron, one of India's leading IT distribution companies, to significantly expand the availability of TwinMOS memory and storage products across the Indian subcontinent. This strategic partnership marks a major milestone in TwinMOS's South Asia expansion strategy, bringing cutting-edge DDR5 memory, PCIe Gen 5.0 SSDs, and portable storage solutions to one of the world's fastest-growing technology markets.

India represents a tremendous growth opportunity for TwinMOS, with the country's PC gaming market, data center investments, and enterprise IT upgrades driving unprecedented demand for high-performance memory and storage solutions. Through Supertron's extensive distribution network, TwinMOS products will be available to consumers, system integrators, SMBs, and enterprise customers across metro cities and tier-2/3 markets.

## Partnership Details

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Partner** | Supertron Electronics Pvt. Ltd. |
| **Market** | India (National Coverage) |
| **Agreement Type** | Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) |
| **Status** | Conditional — Subject to final commercial terms and regulatory approvals |
| **Focus Segments** | Consumer, SMB, Enterprise, System Integrators |
| **Product Categories** | DDR5 Memory, NVMe SSDs, Portable SSDs, USB Flash Drives, Accessories |
| **Expected Launch** | Q2–Q3 2026 |
| **TwinMOS Entity** | TwinMOS Technologies (India) Pvt. Ltd., New Delhi |

## About Supertron

Supertron Electronics Pvt. Ltd. is one of India's premier IT distribution companies, with over three decades of experience in bridging the gap between global technology brands and the Indian market. Headquartered in Kolkata with regional offices across Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, and Hyderabad, Supertron maintains an extensive network of:

- **5,000+** retail partners across India
- **2,000+** reseller and system integrator relationships
- **E-commerce** partnerships with leading online marketplaces
- **Enterprise** direct sales team for B2B engagements

Supertron's portfolio includes leading global brands in components, peripherals, networking, and enterprise solutions, making it an ideal partner to introduce TwinMOS's high-performance memory and storage products to the Indian market.

## About TwinMOS India

TwinMOS Technologies (India) Pvt. Ltd., headquartered in New Delhi, serves as the operational and commercial hub for TwinMOS across the Indian subcontinent. The India office coordinates:

- Regional sales and marketing operations
- Technical support and RMA services for Indian customers
- Partnership management with distributors and channel partners
- Localized marketing campaigns and brand building initiatives

With India's rapidly expanding digital infrastructure, growing gaming community, and increasing enterprise IT investments, TwinMOS is committed to establishing a strong local presence and delivering world-class memory and storage solutions tailored to Indian market needs.

## Partnership Impact

This MOU with Supertron is expected to deliver significant benefits for TwinMOS and the Indian market:

- **National Reach:** Access to Supertron's pan-India distribution network, covering all major cities and emerging tier-2/3 markets
- **Multi-Channel Presence:** Availability through retail stores, e-commerce platforms, system integrators, and enterprise direct sales
- **Local Support:** Faster delivery times, localized technical support, and streamlined warranty services
- **Brand Building:** Joint marketing initiatives, product launches, and participation in Indian technology events
- **Market Education:** Training programs for reseller partners on TwinMOS product features and competitive advantages

## Next Steps

Following the MOU signing, both parties will work toward:

1. Finalizing commercial terms and distribution agreements
2. Completing product regulatory certifications for the Indian market (BIS)
3. Establishing inventory and logistics operations
4. Launching partner onboarding and training programs
5. Planning joint marketing campaigns and product launch events
6. Targeting first commercial shipments in Q2–Q3 2026

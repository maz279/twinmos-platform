# COMPUTEX 2025: TwinMOS Showcase

**Published:** May 2025  
**Category:** Events  
**Event:** COMPUTEX TAIPEI 2025  
**Reading Time:** 5 minutes

---

TwinMOS returned to COMPUTEX TAIPEI 2025, one of the world's premier technology trade shows, to unveil its latest lineup of next-generation memory and storage solutions. Held from May 20 to May 23, 2025, at the Nangang Exhibition Center Hall 1 in Taipei, Taiwan, the event drew industry professionals, buyers, media, and technology enthusiasts from across the globe.

Visitors to the TwinMOS booth **I1431** experienced firsthand the cutting-edge innovations that define the company's 27+ year heritage in memory and storage technology. From blistering PCIe Gen 5.0 SSDs to high-performance DDR5 modules with customizable RGB lighting, TwinMOS demonstrated its continued commitment to pushing the boundaries of performance and reliability.

The flagship of TwinMOS's COMPUTEX 2025 showcase, the **CoreX Pro Gen5** represents a leap forward in consumer storage performance.

| Specification | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Interface** | PCIe Gen 5.0 x4, NVMe 2.0 |
| **Form Factor** | M.2 2280 |
| **NAND** | Micron/Hynix 3D TLC NAND |
| **Controller** | High-performance PCIe Gen5 controller |
| **Heatsink** | Graphene thermal dissipation |
| **DRAM Cache** | Yes |
| **Sequential Read** | Up to 14,000 MB/s |
| **Sequential Write** | Up to 13,000 MB/s (2TB/4TB) |
| **Warranty** | 5 Years |
| **Capacities** | 1TB, 2TB, 4TB |

With sequential read speeds reaching up to 14,000 MB/s, the CoreX Pro Gen5 is engineered for power users, content creators, and gamers who demand uncompromising storage performance.

## Event Types

### Global Trade Shows
Major international technology exhibitions where TwinMOS showcases flagship products and connects with global partners.

- **COMPUTEX** — World's leading technology trade show in Taipei, Taiwan
- **GITEX GLOBAL** — Largest tech exhibition in the Middle East and Africa, Dubai, UAE
- **CES** — Global stage for consumer technology innovation, Las Vegas, USA

### Regional Partner Events
Focused gatherings with authorized distributors, resellers, and system integrators in key markets.

- **Distributor Summits** — Annual partner meetings in Bangladesh, India, and the Middle East
- **Channel Partner Events** — Regional training and product roadmap sessions
- **Launch Events** — Local product unveiling ceremonies with media and partners

### Virtual Events
Online webinars, product demos, and technical training sessions for remote audiences.

## About CEBIT MEA

CEBIT is one of the world's largest and most international computer expos, with a strong heritage in business technology and digital innovation. The Middle East & Africa edition of CEBIT brings together technology providers, enterprise buyers, government representatives, and digital transformation leaders from across the region. The event focuses on digital business, cloud computing, cybersecurity, IoT, and data center infrastructure — all areas that drive significant demand for high-performance memory and storage solutions.

## TwinMOS Evaluation

TwinMOS is evaluating participation in CEBIT MEA as part of our ongoing strategy to strengthen our presence in the Middle East and Africa. With our headquarters in Dubai and established distribution networks across the GCC, CEBIT MEA represents a natural fit for TwinMOS to engage with enterprise clients, government sectors, and regional technology partners.

### Strategic Value

- **Enterprise Engagement** — Direct access to corporate IT buyers and procurement teams
- **Government Sector** — Opportunities in smart city, e-government, and digital transformation projects
- **Data Center & Cloud** — Showcase enterprise-grade storage and server memory solutions
- **Regional Networking** — Strengthen relationships with MEA distributors and system integrators

### Potential Focus Areas

- **Enterprise Storage Solutions** — NVMe SSDs for data center and cloud infrastructure
- **Server Memory** — DDR4 and DDR5 ECC modules for enterprise servers
- **Industrial Applications** — Rugged storage solutions for harsh environments
- **Partner Enablement** — Co-marketing with regional distributors

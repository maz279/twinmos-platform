| Certification | Description |
|---------------|-------------|

| **FCC** | Federal Communications Commission certification (USA) |

## Quality Commitment

Every TwinMOS product undergoes rigorous testing before leaving our facilities:

- **100% product testing** before delivery to customers
- **Environmental stress testing** under extreme temperatures and conditions
- **Performance and benchmark testing** against industry standards
- **Compatibility testing** across major motherboard and system platforms
- **Reliability testing** simulating real-world usage scenarios

Learn more about our quality assurance processes: [Quality Assurance](/about/quality-assurance)

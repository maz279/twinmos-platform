## About Saudi Tech

Saudi Arabia is undergoing a transformative digital revolution under Vision 2030, with massive investments in technology infrastructure, smart cities, and digital services. The Kingdom's technology events attract global technology providers, government entities, enterprise buyers, and investors eager to participate in one of the world's fastest-growing technology markets. With initiatives like NEOM, the Red Sea Project, and widespread digital transformation across government and private sectors, Saudi Arabia represents a significant opportunity for memory and storage solution providers.

## TwinMOS in Saudi Arabia

TwinMOS recognizes the immense potential of the Saudi Arabian market. While we currently serve the Kingdom through regional distributors, direct participation in Saudi Tech events would accelerate our market penetration and brand recognition.

### Market Opportunity

- **Vision 2030** — Massive government investment in technology and digital infrastructure
- **Gaming Market Growth** — One of the fastest-growing gaming markets globally, driving demand for high-performance DDR5 and SSDs
- **Data Center Expansion** — Significant investments in cloud and data center infrastructure
- **Smart Cities** — NEOM and other smart city projects require enterprise-grade storage and memory
- **Youth Demographics** — Tech-savvy population with strong demand for consumer electronics and PC components

### Strategic Objectives

- Establish direct relationships with Saudi distributors and retailers
- Engage with government procurement for smart city and digital transformation projects
- Showcase gaming-focused products (VOLTX RGB DDR5, CoreX Pro Gen5) to the growing gaming community
- Explore partnerships with local system integrators and PC builders

### Potential Showcase Products

- **VOLTX RGB DDR5 Series** — Ideal for Saudi Arabia's booming gaming market
- **CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSD** — High-performance storage for gaming and content creation
- **Enterprise Storage Solutions** — For data center and government infrastructure projects
- **Portable Storage** — Mobile solutions for professionals and students

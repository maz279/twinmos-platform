## Why Meet TwinMOS at Events?

### For Distributors & Partners
- Discuss regional roadmaps and product availability
- Explore co-marketing and promotional opportunities
- Meet with regional account managers and executives
- Access exclusive partner pricing and incentive programs

### For Media & Analysts
- Schedule executive interviews and product briefings
- Access high-resolution product images and press kits
- Receive embargoed information on upcoming launches
- Capture hands-on product demonstrations

### For Consumers & Enthusiasts
- Experience live product demonstrations and benchmark displays
- Get hands-on with the latest DDR5 memory and Gen 5.0 SSDs
- Consult with TwinMOS technical experts
- Receive exclusive event promotions and giveaways

## Event Types

### Global Trade Shows
Major international exhibitions where TwinMOS unveils flagship products and engages with the global technology community.

### Regional Distributor Summits
Focused events for authorized distributors and retail partners, featuring product roadmap presentations, sales strategy sessions, and partner recognition.

### Product Launch Events
Local ceremonies and demonstrations for new product introductions, often co-hosted with regional distributors and media partners.

### Virtual Events & Webinars
Online sessions covering product training, technical deep-dives, and market insights for remote partners and customers.

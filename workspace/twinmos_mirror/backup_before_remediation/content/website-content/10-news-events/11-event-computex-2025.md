## About the Event

COMPUTEX TAIPEI is one of the world's leading technology trade shows, attracting over 1,500 exhibitors and 40,000+ visitors annually. COMPUTEX 2025 focused on AI computing, next-generation connectivity, advanced hardware solutions, and sustainable technology. The exhibition serves as the global launchpad for cutting-edge innovations in computing, consumer electronics, and emerging technologies.

## TwinMOS Participation

TwinMOS showcased its latest memory and storage innovations at booth **I1431** in Nangang Exhibition Center Hall 1. Over four days, visitors from across the globe experienced hands-on demonstrations of next-generation DDR5 modules, breakthrough PCIe Gen 5.0 SSDs, and a comprehensive portfolio of flash storage solutions.

> *"COMPUTEX represents the perfect stage for TwinMOS to connect with our global partners and showcase the innovations that will power the next generation of computing. From enthusiasts building their dream gaming rigs to enterprises seeking reliable high-performance storage, our COMPUTEX 2025 lineup delivers the speed, quality, and reliability that the TwinMOS brand has stood for over nearly three decades."*
>
> — **TwinMOS Executive Team**

## Products on Display

### CoreX Pro Gen5 NVMe SSD
- **Interface:** PCIe Gen 5.0 x4, NVMe 2.0
- **Sequential Read:** Up to 14,000 MB/s
- **Sequential Write:** Up to 13,000 MB/s (2TB/4TB)
- **Capacities:** 1TB, 2TB, 4TB
- **Thermal:** Graphene thermal dissipation heatsink
- **Warranty:** 5 Years

### VOLTX RGB DDR5 DRAM
- **Speeds:** 5600MHz, 6000MHz
- **Capacities:** 16GB, 32GB (single); 32GB, 64GB (kits)
- **RGB Sync:** ASUS Aura Sync, GIGABYTE RGB Fusion, MSI Mystic Light, ASRock Polychrome
- **Compatibility:** Intel XMP 3.0, AMD EXPO
- **Warranty:** Lifetime Product Warranty

### High-Performance Flash Storage
- **Xtreme Gen4X4 NVMe Pro SSDs** — PCIe Gen 4.0 performance up to 7,500 MB/s
- **Hyper SSD H2 Ultra** — Reliable SATA III SSDs up to 580 MB/s
- **Elite Drive Pro Portable SSDs** — USB 3.2 Gen 2 portable storage
- **USB Flash Drives & Memory Cards** — Mobile storage solutions

## Visitor Experience

Attendees at booth I1431 were welcomed into a fully immersive showcase environment featuring dedicated zones for each product category. Gaming stations powered by VOLTX RGB DDR5-6000 and CoreX Pro Gen5 SSDs demonstrated real-world performance in popular titles and creative applications.

The TwinMOS technical team answered questions about compatibility, installation, and optimization. Distributors and system integrators from Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and Asia Pacific held strategic meetings to discuss regional roadmaps, inventory planning, and co-marketing initiatives.

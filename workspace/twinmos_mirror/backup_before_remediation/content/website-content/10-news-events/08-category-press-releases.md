## Press Release Guidelines

TwinMOS press releases follow AP Style guidelines and include:
- Clear, factual headlines optimized for search and social sharing
- Structured content with dateline, lead paragraph, body, quotes, and boilerplate
- Embedded contact information for follow-up inquiries
- Links to related product pages, event listings, and digital assets
- PDF download option for print and distribution

## 3. Scope and Relationship

### 3.1 Master Agreement Framework

This MSA establishes the general terms and conditions governing the relationship between the Parties. Specific projects, deliverables, timelines, and fees shall be detailed in separate Statements of Work (SOWs) executed under this MSA. Each SOW, once signed by authorized representatives of both Parties, shall be incorporated by reference into and governed by this MSA.

### 3.2 Services Overview

Unisoft shall provide software development and professional services for the TwinMOS corporate website rebuild project, encompassing:

| Service Category | Description |
|------------------|-------------|
| **Strategic Discovery** | Technical architecture planning, content modeling, SEO strategy, and technology stack validation |
| **UI/UX Design** | User interface design, user experience flows, responsive design for mobile/tablet/desktop, RTL (Arabic) layout design |
| **Frontend Development** | Astro 5 + React 19 island-based development, Tailwind CSS implementation, component library creation |
| **Backend Development** | Strapi v5 headless CMS configuration, custom controllers, API development, database schema design (PostgreSQL 16) |
| **Search Implementation** | MeiliSearch integration, faceted search, auto-suggest, search analytics |
| **Content Migration** | Migration of 454 markdown content files into Astro Content Collections and Strapi collections |
| **Integration Services** | Third-party integrations (GA4, GTM, Resend, Cloudflare, HubSpot CRM, reCAPTCHA/Cloudflare Turnstile) |
| **Multi-Language Implementation** | i18n framework for 9+ locales (EN, AR, BN, HI, RU, ZH-CN, FR, ES, PT, DE), RTL support |
| **Quality Assurance** | Automated testing (Vitest, Playwright, axe-core), manual UAT support, cross-browser testing |
| **Security Implementation** | TLS 1.3, CSP, HSTS, WAF configuration, OWASP Top 10 mitigation, secure authentication |
| **DevOps & Deployment** | CI/CD pipeline (GitHub Actions), Cloudflare Pages deployment, Hetzner/Coolify backend hosting setup |
| **Documentation** | Technical documentation, API documentation, CMS user guides, runbooks |
| **Training** | CMS training for TwinMOS marketing staff, technical handover sessions |
| **Post-Launch Support** | Hypercare (4 weeks post-launch), bug fixes, performance monitoring, 90-day warranty period |

Unless explicitly included in a signed SOW, the following are excluded:

(a) Ongoing digital marketing, SEO content creation, or social media management  
(b) Translation and localization services (covered under separate Translation Vendor Agreement)  
(c) Penetration testing and security auditing (covered under separate Penetration Test Vendor Agreement)  
(d) Hosting infrastructure costs beyond the initial setup and deployment  
(e) Third-party SaaS subscription fees (e.g., Cloudflare Pro, SendGrid/Resend, BrowserStack)  
(f) Product photography, videography, or creative asset production  
(g) ERP system development or integration beyond the defined API scope  
(h) E-commerce payment processing setup beyond Medusa.js + Stripe technical integration

### 3.4 Standard of Care

Unisoft shall perform all Services:

(a) With the degree of skill, care, diligence, and professionalism reasonably expected of a qualified software development vendor  
(b) In compliance with all Applicable Laws, industry standards (OWASP, WCAG 2.1 AA), and TwinMOS policies  
(c) Using appropriately skilled and experienced personnel  
(d) In accordance with the specifications, requirements, and acceptance criteria set forth in each SOW and the referenced BRD, URD, RFP, and Technology Stack documents

Unisoft may not subcontract any material portion of the Services without the prior written consent of TwinMOS. ## 9. Security, Compliance, and Audit Rights

### 9.1 Security Standards

Unisoft shall adhere to the following security standards:

| Standard | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| **TLS** | TLS 1.3 minimum for all data in transit |
| **HSTS** | max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains |
| **CSP** | Strict Content Security Policy implementation |
| **Secure Cookies** | HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict |
| **Password Policy** | Min 12 chars, mixed case, number, symbol; 90-day rotation for admins |
| **Account Lockout** | 5 failed attempts = 30-minute lockout |
| **Session Timeout** | 30 minutes idle; 8 hours absolute maximum |
| **MFA** | Required for all admin and partner portal accounts |
| **API Auth** | JWT tokens with 1-hour expiry, rotating refresh tokens |

### 9.2 Compliance Requirements

Unisoft shall ensure all Deliverables comply with:

(a) **GDPR** — EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation 2016/679)  
(b) **UAE PDPL** — UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection  
(c) **India DPDP Act 2023** — Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023  
(d) **KSA PDPL** — Saudi Arabia Personal Data Protection Law  
(e) **WCAG 2.1 AA** — Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1 Level AA  
(f) **OWASP Top 10** — Open Web Application Security Project Top 10 (2021)  
(g) **Industry Certifications** — Support for display of ISO 9001:2015, CE, UKCA, FCC, RoHS, REACH, EAC, JEDEC, BIS certifications

### 9.3 Penetration Testing

(a) Annual security audits with 30 days' notice

(c) Review of access logs, change management records, and security policies  
(d) Interviews with Unisoft personnel

Unisoft shall cooperate fully with such audits and provide all reasonably requested documentation.

### 9.5 Regulatory Cooperation

In the event of a data breach, security incident, or regulatory inquiry affecting TwinMOS Data, Unisoft shall:

(a) Notify TwinMOS within **24 hours** of discovery  
(b) Provide a preliminary incident report within 48 hours

## 13. Dispute Resolution

### 13.1 Good Faith Negotiation

The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising under this Agreement through direct negotiation between their respective Project Managers. If unresolved within **fourteen (14) days**, the dispute shall be escalated to the Operational Sponsors.

### 13.2 Mediation

If the dispute remains unresolved after escalation to Operational Sponsors for **fourteen (14) days**, either Party may initiate mediation under the rules of the Dubai International Arbitration Centre (DIAC). The mediation shall be conducted in English in Dubai, UAE.

### 13.3 Arbitration

If mediation fails to resolve the dispute within **sixty (60) days** of initiation, either Party may refer the dispute to binding arbitration under the DIAC Arbitration Rules. The arbitration shall be conducted in English, in Dubai, UAE, by a single arbitrator appointed in accordance with DIAC rules. The arbitrator's decision shall be final and binding.

### 13.4 Injunctive Relief

Notwithstanding the above, either Party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief from a court of competent jurisdiction to prevent irreparable harm pending resolution of the dispute.

### 13.5 Costs

Each Party shall bear its own costs in connection with dispute resolution, except that the prevailing Party shall be entitled to recover reasonable legal fees and arbitration costs from the non-prevailing Party.

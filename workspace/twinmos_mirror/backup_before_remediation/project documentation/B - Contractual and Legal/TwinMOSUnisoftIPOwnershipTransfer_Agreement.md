## 3. Assignment of Intellectual Property

### 3.1 Absolute Assignment

Unisoft hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to TwinMOS all right, title, and interest in and to:

(a) **All Foreground IP** — All Intellectual Property Rights in the Work Product created by Unisoft in the performance of Services under the Project

(b) **All Derivative Works** — Any modifications, enhancements, or Derivative Works of the Work Product created by Unisoft

(c) **All Related Materials** — All documentation, specifications, test cases, user manuals, and other materials related to the Work Product

### 3.2 Scope of Assignment

The assignment in Section 3.1 includes, without limitation:

| Category | Included Rights |
|----------|----------------|
| **Copyrights** | All copyrights and copyright applications, including the right to reproduce, distribute, display, perform, create Derivative Works, and license |
| **Patents** | All patent rights and patent applications, including continuations, divisionals, and reissues |
| **Trademarks** | All trademark rights in names, logos, and branding created specifically for the Project (subject to TwinMOS's existing brand guidelines) |
| **Trade Secrets** | All trade secrets, know-how, and confidential information embodied in the Work Product |
| **Database Rights** | All sui generis database rights |
| **Moral Rights** | All moral rights, to the extent assignable (see Section 7) |
| **Domain Names** | All domain names registered for the Project (transferred to TwinMOS's control) |

### 3.3 Future Rights

Unisoft hereby assigns to TwinMOS all future rights in the Work Product that may arise under any future laws, treaties, or regulations, including any extensions or renewals of existing rights.

### 3.4 Consideration

The consideration for this assignment is the payment of fees by TwinMOS to Unisoft under the MSA and applicable SOWs, which the Parties acknowledge constitutes full and adequate consideration for the rights assigned herein.

## 4. Deliverables and Handover

### 4.1 Deliverables Inventory

A complete inventory of Deliverables is set out in **Annex A** (Deliverables Inventory), which forms an integral part of this Agreement. Annex A shall be updated as Deliverables are completed and accepted.

### 4.2 Handover Process

Upon Acceptance of each Deliverable (or upon termination of the MSA, whichever is earlier), Unisoft shall:

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Provide complete Source Code for all software Deliverables | Within 5 business days of Acceptance |
| 2 | Provide all design files (Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch) in editable format | Within 5 business days of Acceptance |
| 3 | Provide all documentation (technical, user, API) | Within 5 business days of Acceptance |
| 4 | Transfer ownership of all domain names registered for the Project | Within 5 business days of Acceptance |
| 5 | Transfer administrative access to all third-party accounts (Cloudflare, GitHub repos, etc.) | Within 5 business days of Acceptance |
| 6 | Provide build and deployment instructions | Within 5 business days of Acceptance |
| 7 | Provide database dumps and migration scripts | Within 5 business days of Acceptance |
| 8 | Conduct knowledge transfer session(s) | Within 10 business days of Acceptance |

### 4.3 Source Code Requirements

All Source Code delivered shall:

(a) Be complete, compilable, and functional without reliance on Unisoft's internal systems
(b) Include all necessary build scripts, configuration files, and dependencies
(c) Be accompanied by a README file explaining the project structure, build process, and deployment procedures
(d) Include inline comments and documentation sufficient for a reasonably skilled developer to understand and maintain the code
(e) Conform to the coding standards documented in the project's style guide

### 4.4 Repository Transfer

Upon Project completion or termination:

(a) All GitHub repositories shall be transferred to TwinMOS's GitHub organization
(b) All commit history shall be preserved
(c) All branches, tags, and releases shall be preserved
(d) All issues, pull requests, and project boards shall be exported or transferred

### 4.5 Verification Period

TwinMOS shall have **fourteen (14) days** from receipt of Deliverables to verify completeness and functionality. If Deliverables are incomplete or non-functional, TwinMOS shall provide written notice detailing deficiencies, and Unisoft shall remedy within **seven (7) business days**.

## 5. Source Code Escrow

### 5.1 Escrow Arrangement

The Parties agree to establish a source code escrow arrangement with a mutually agreed third-party escrow agent ("**Escrow Agent**"):

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Escrow Agent** | [To be mutually agreed — e.g., Iron Mountain, NCC Group, or similar] |
| **Deposit Schedule** | Initial deposit within 30 days of SOW execution; updates quarterly and upon each major release |
| **Deposit Contents** | Complete Source Code, build scripts, documentation, encryption keys (if applicable), third-party licenses |
| **Verification** | TwinMOS may verify escrow contents annually |
| **Fees** | Shared equally between Parties |

### 5.2 Release Conditions

The Escrow Agent shall release the escrowed materials to TwinMOS upon:

(a) Unisoft's insolvency, bankruptcy, or cessation of business operations
(b) Unisoft's material breach of the MSA that remains uncured for 30 days
(c) Unisoft's failure to provide support as required under the MSA for 60 consecutive days
(d) Mutual written agreement of the Parties

### 5.3 Release Process

Upon occurrence of a release condition:

(a) TwinMOS shall notify Unisoft and the Escrow Agent in writing
(b) Unisoft shall have **ten (10) business days** to dispute the release condition
(c) If undisputed or dispute resolved in TwinMOS's favor, the Escrow Agent shall release materials within 5 business days
(d) TwinMOS may use released materials solely for the purpose of maintaining and operating the Project

## 6. Background IP and Third-Party Components

### 6.1 Background IP Retention

Unisoft retains ownership of all Background IP, including:

(a) Pre-existing tools, libraries, frameworks, and methodologies
(b) General programming techniques and know-how not specific to the Project
(c) Code and materials developed by Unisoft for other clients

### 6.2 Background IP License

Unisoft grants to TwinMOS a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide, non-exclusive license to:

(a) Use Background IP incorporated into the Work Product
(b) Modify and create Derivative Works of such Background IP as part of the Work Product
(c) Sublicense the foregoing rights to TwinMOS's affiliates and successors

This license is limited to use in connection with the Work Product and shall not extend to standalone use of Background IP outside the context of the Project.

### 6.3 Third-Party Components

Unisoft shall maintain an accurate inventory of all Third-Party Components and Open Source Components used in the Work Product in **Annex B** (Third-Party Components Inventory).

### 6.4 Open Source Compliance

Unisoft warrants that:

(a) All Open Source Components are used in compliance with their respective licenses
(b) No Open Source Component imposes copyleft obligations on TwinMOS's proprietary code
(c) All required license notices, attributions, and disclaimers are included in the Work Product
(d) The use of Open Source Components does not infringe any third-party Intellectual Property Rights

### 6.5 Prohibited Licenses

Unisoft shall not incorporate any software licensed under the following licenses without prior written approval from TwinMOS:

- GNU Affero General Public License (AGPL)
- Server Side Public License (SSPL)
- Any license with viral copyleft provisions affecting TwinMOS's proprietary code
- Any license with commercial use restrictions incompatible with TwinMOS's business model

## 7. Moral Rights Waiver

### 7.1 Waiver

To the fullest extent permitted by Applicable Law, Unisoft hereby irrevocably waives all moral rights in the Work Product, including:

(a) The right of attribution (paternity)
(b) The right to integrity (objection to derogatory treatment)

### 7.2 Non-Waivable Rights

In jurisdictions where moral rights cannot be waived, Unisoft agrees:

(a) Not to enforce any moral rights against TwinMOS

(c) To provide any necessary consents to enable TwinMOS to exercise its rights under this Agreement

(b) Use the Work Product in ways Unisoft may not endorse

## 8. Warranty and Indemnification

### 8.1 Title Warranty

Unisoft warrants that:

(a) Unisoft is the sole owner of all Foreground IP or has the right to assign it
(b) The Work Product does not infringe any third-party Intellectual Property Rights
(c) Unisoft has not granted and will not grant any rights in the Work Product that conflict with this Agreement
(d) All employees, contractors, and subcontractors who contributed to the Work Product have assigned their rights to Unisoft

### 8.2 Indemnification

Unisoft shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless TwinMOS from and against any claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising from:

(a) Any breach of the warranties in Section 8.1
(b) Any claim that the Work Product infringes any third-party Intellectual Property Rights
(c) Any unauthorized use of third-party materials in the Work Product

### 8.3 Remediation

If the Work Product is found to infringe third-party rights, Unisoft shall, at its option and expense:

(a) Obtain the right for TwinMOS to continue using the Work Product
(b) Modify the Work Product to be non-infringing while maintaining substantially equivalent functionality
(c) Replace the infringing component with a non-infringing equivalent
(d) If none of the above are commercially feasible, refund the fees attributable to the infringing component

## 9. Ongoing Obligations

### 9.1 Cooperation

Unisoft shall cooperate with TwinMOS in:

(a) Registering Intellectual Property Rights in the Work Product (at TwinMOS's expense)
(b) Prosecuting patent applications (at TwinMOS's expense)
(c) Defending against third-party infringement claims
(d) Executing all documents necessary to perfect TwinMOS's ownership

### 9.2 Further Assurances

Unisoft shall, at TwinMOS's reasonable request and expense, execute all documents and take all actions necessary to:

(a) Perfect, record, or enforce TwinMOS's ownership of the Assigned IP
(b) Assist in obtaining patent, copyright, or trademark registrations
(c) Transfer domain names and third-party account ownership

### 9.3 Power of Attorney

Unisoft hereby grants TwinMOS a limited power of attorney to execute documents on Unisoft's behalf for the purpose of perfecting TwinMOS's ownership of the Assigned IP, should Unisoft fail to do so within thirty (30) days of written request.

## 10. Termination and Post-Termination

### 10.1 Effect of Termination

Upon termination of the MSA for any reason:

(a) All assignments and licenses granted under this Agreement shall remain in full force
(b) Unisoft shall complete the handover process as set out in Section 4.2
(c) Unisoft shall cooperate in transferring all Intellectual Property Rights to TwinMOS

### 10.2 Survival

Sections 3 (Assignment of Intellectual Property), 6 (Background IP and Third-Party Components), 7 (Moral Rights Waiver), 8 (Warranty and Indemnification), and 9 (Ongoing Obligations) shall survive termination of the MSA and this Agreement indefinitely.
